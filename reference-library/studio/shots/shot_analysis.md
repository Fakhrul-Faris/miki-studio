# Motion Design Analysis — "Studio" (~18.0s, 4:5 vertical, 720x900)

Source: `reference-library/studio/source.mp4` (originally `Motion Design/Studio (MD).mp4`)
Extracted via `tools/shot_extractor.py` (7 shots at scene thresh 0.15; the splits are fixed-interval, no real cuts). Re-derived into 11 beats from a 2 fps contact sheet plus spot frames. Beat times are accurate to ~0.5s. Frame refs are approximate: `shot01` = beats 1–3, `shot02` = beats 3–5, `shot03` = beats 5–7, `shot04` = beats 7–8, `shot05` = beat 8, `shot06` = beats 9–10, `shot07` = beats 10–11.

**Reference use:** transition technique (liquid/lens warp), type-on-curve treatment, collage pacing, palette (red / lime green / black / white) and the device-frame presentation. This is a personal design-studio portfolio reel shown as a social-story on a phone. Do not carry over the studio name or logo, the person's face or name, the availability copy, or the photographic/3D artwork. Describe generically (e.g. "a phone held in a dark silhouetted hand shows a story-format portfolio reel").

**Style summary:** one continuous phone-in-hand shot on a dark vertical gradient. The screen plays a story-format reel whose panels never simply cut: each one melts, stretches or tunnels into the next, and type bends along curves as if printed on a rubber sheet. Loud color panels (red, lime green) alternate with stark black and white type cards. The last beat returns to the opening frame, so it loops.

## Beat 1 — 0.00s–1.00s (cover)
**Frames:** `shot01_start`

- **Typography:** A chunky chrome/white wordmark with a small glyph, overlaid on a color collage. Set large, centered, in the lower third of the screen.
- **Animation & transition:** Extreme close-up crop of a red-toned eye at the top, a lime-green shape at the bottom, a thick horizontal seam between them. Content stretches radially (zoom-streak) toward the exit and wipes into Beat 2.
- **Layout & composition:** Phone centered and large in the frame, held by a silhouetted hand from the lower left. Story chrome on screen: a thin progress line, an account row and close mark at the top, a message bar at the bottom.
- **Background & accent motifs:** Outer frame is near-black at the top, fading to a cool blue-gray at the bottom. Screen carries all the color.
- **Pacing:** ~1s.
- **Mood/purpose:** Bold, surreal, brand cover.

## Beat 2 — 1.00s–2.00s (tagline card)
**Frames:** `shot01_start`–`mid`

- **Typography:** A single tiny centered line of white sans on a glowing orange gradient band across the screen's middle.
- **Animation & transition:** The screen darkens to deep maroon and a horizontal orange gradient bar (a "horizon") swells across the middle. The type sits inside it, small. A soft wipe-up bridges into Beat 3.
- **Layout & composition:** Band at mid-height; wide empty dark areas above and below.
- **Background & accent motifs:** Deep red/maroon with orange glow.
- **Pacing:** ~1s. A breath after the loud cover.
- **Mood/purpose:** Quiet, poised mission line.

## Beat 3 — 2.00s–4.00s (portrait warp)
**Frames:** `shot01_mid`, `shot01_end`, `shot02_start`

- **Typography:** None.
- **Animation & transition:** A portrait photo of a person on a brown/red checkered ground enters as a collage, then stretches vertically and warps (as if pulled in a liquid lens) at ~2.5–3.0s, with a small oval "window" of sky at the bottom. Then it breaks into cutout tiles on black.
- **Layout & composition:** Subject filling the screen height, centered; warp is strongest at the head and shoulders.
- **Background & accent motifs:** Warm browns/reds; a pale blue oval accent.
- **Pacing:** ~2s, with the warp as the peak.
- **Mood/purpose:** Introduces the person/face of the studio.

## Beat 4 — 4.00s–5.00s (availability card)
**Frames:** `shot02_start`, `shot02_mid`

- **Typography:** A small centered line of bold caps sitting above a small photo strip, both on a white card; a black oval sits at the top of the card.
- **Animation & transition:** Panel dives into a curved "tunnel" perspective (a dark 3D room with a floor disc, at ~4.0s), then flips to a bright white card with a red-rimmed black oval "portal" at the top.
- **Layout & composition:** Type and strip in the lower middle; oval in the upper half.
- **Background & accent motifs:** White card, black oval, red rim. First high-key panel.
- **Pacing:** ~1s.
- **Mood/purpose:** The practical information beat.

## Beat 5 — 5.00s–7.00s (green/red shapes)
**Frames:** `shot02_end`, `shot03_start`

- **Typography:** Tiny type, mostly hidden by the artwork.
- **Animation & transition:** Black shards fly across, then a lime-green field fills the screen with a red glossy 3D form and the portrait tucked inside. The green wipes out to black scatter, with small tiles drifting.
- **Layout & composition:** Shapes fill the frame edge to edge, cropped by the screen.
- **Background & accent motifs:** Lime green, glossy red, black. Highest saturation in the spot.
- **Pacing:** ~2s.
- **Mood/purpose:** Energy and range.

## Beat 6 — 7.00s–8.00s (3D sculpture)
**Frames:** `shot03_mid`

- **Typography:** A slogan-style line rotated diagonally across a red band, white bold sans, lower-left, partly cropped.
- **Animation & transition:** A green and teal glossy 3D sculpture with a gray dome below fills the screen; the slogan sits on a tilted red strip. Slow drift, no cut.
- **Layout & composition:** Sculpture dominates the upper two-thirds; slogan in the lower left, rotated ~-20°.
- **Background & accent motifs:** Green/teal 3D forms with red accent strip.
- **Pacing:** ~1s.
- **Mood/purpose:** Manifesto beat.

## Beat 7 — 8.00s–10.00s (type on curves)
**Frames:** `shot03_end`, `shot04_*`

- **Typography:** A stack of service labels in wide black bold sans on white, bending as if on a curved surface (bulging in the middle, arcing at top and bottom). Also black cards with white bold caps, warped similarly.
- **Animation & transition:** Black and white cards alternate. Type slides in on a wavy 3D-curved path, letters stretching and rotating with the curve. A small round logo sits at the top-left.
- **Layout & composition:** Text stacked in the lower two-thirds; distortion grows toward the frame edges.
- **Background & accent motifs:** Pure white or pure black; no color except an occasional red/green fragment.
- **Pacing:** ~2s.
- **Mood/purpose:** Communicates the offering with typographic drama.

## Beat 8 — 10.00s–12.00s (portrait with cutout)

- **Animation & transition:** A bearded portrait with a lime-green gloved hand covering half the face and a red rectangular "window" cut out around one eye. Slow push-in. Then the image darkens and a red panel with a small text card slides in.

- **Animation & transition:** Red flows into a collage of tiles, then a white card wipes in with the type stack swelling and rippling.
- **Layout & composition:** Centered stack; wave ring at the top and bottom of the card.
- **Background & accent motifs:** Red → black → white.
- **Pacing:** ~2s.

## Beat 10 — 14.00s–16.00s (3D recap)

- **Animation & transition:** The green/teal sculpture returns, then dissolves into red folded cloth-like 3D forms with a dark square object, then breaks into a black collage.
- **Layout & composition:** Full-bleed 3D forms.
- **Background & accent motifs:** Green → red → black.
- **Pacing:** ~2s.

## Beat 11 — 16.00s–18.00s (logo mark and loop)
**Frames:** `shot07_mid`, `shot07_end`

- **Typography:** None; a symmetrical chrome-white blob mark (a butterfly/ink-blot shape) on black.
- **Animation & transition:** The chrome mark morphs (its lobes swell and fold) in the middle of a black screen. The screen then reopens into a red collage with the tagline band, ending on a frame matching Beat 1, so the reel loops.
- **Layout & composition:** Mark centered, small relative to the screen.
- **Background & accent motifs:** Black with a small red glint at the bottom; red returns at the loop point.
- **Pacing:** ~2s.
- **Mood/purpose:** Sign-off and seamless loop.

## Cross-beat notes
- **No hard cuts.** Every panel change is a wipe, warp, tunnel or morph, so the reel feels liquid. This is the signature device.
- **Type on a bendy surface.** Text is treated as a physical object that warps and rides curves, not as flat overlay.
- **Two registers alternate:** loud color photo/3D panels and stark black or white type cards. Contrast gives the reel its rhythm.
- **Device frame.** The phone and silhouetted hand never move meaningfully; only the screen contents do. The blue-gray floor gradient gives depth without props.
- **Rhythm:** ~1s, 1s, 2s, 1s, 2s, 1s, 2s, 2s, 2s, 2s, 2s. Short breaths (cover, tagline, info card) between 2s set pieces.
