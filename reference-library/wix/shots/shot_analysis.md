# Motion Design Analysis — "Wix" (~49.2s, 16:9, 736x414)

Source: `reference-library/wix/source.mp4` (originally `Motion Design/WIX (MD).mp4`)
Extractor: 57 shots (many real cuts). Re-derived into 10 beats from a 0.6 fps sheet; times ±2s. Frames in `shots/` are named by timestamp.

**Reference use:** customer-testimonial structure: interview footage, kinetic-type overlays, colored-background photo cards, UI screen recordings, colored end-card. Do not carry over brand name, logo, the speaker, product/site content, or copy. Describe generically.

**Style summary:** an interview-led testimonial. A talking-head runs through, cut with b-roll, screen recordings and flat-color panels (cream, pale blue, brick red) carrying tilted photo cards. Type: clean sans, sentence case, left-aligned overlays, one word emphasized with an arrow glyph.

## Cross-beat notes
- **Flat colored panels** (cream, pale blue, brick red) reset the eye between footage and UI.
- **Tilted photo cards** on colored panels are the main motion motif.
- **Arrow-marked word** in overlay type marks the emphasis.
- **Rhythm:** ~1–3s cuts in the middle, longer interview holds at the two ends.
