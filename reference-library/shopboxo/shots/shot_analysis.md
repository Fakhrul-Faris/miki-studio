# Motion Design Analysis — "Shopboxo" (~60s, 16:9, 1280x720)

Source: `reference-library/shopboxo/source.mp4` (originally `Motion Design/ShopBoxo (MD).mp4`)
Extractor: 23 shots. Re-derived into 12 beats from a 0.5 fps sheet; times ±2s. Frames in `shots/` are named by timestamp.

**Reference use:** app-promo kinetic type with one-phrase-per-beat captions, UI-card scatter, gradient panels, dot-burst, CTA ending. Do not carry over brand name, logo, copy or UI. Describe generically.

**Style summary:** a mobile-commerce explainer on mostly white, with three gradient panels (magenta-to-blue, purple-to-pink) for key claims. Short caption beside or under a phone/UI card; one word in a purple accent. Type: clean sans, sentence case, periods at phrase ends. Palette: white, purple, teal-green cards.

## Cross-beat notes
- **Captions extend in place:** "Payment, shipping" → "& product management." keeps position, adds words.
- **Gradient panels** are reserved for claims ("tools", "free forever"); everything else is white.
- **Purple accent word** marks the key noun/adjective.
- **Rhythm:** ~2–4s per beat, with a slower 6s "market" sequence and a longer CTA.
