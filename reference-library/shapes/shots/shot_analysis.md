# Motion Design Analysis — "Shapes" (~67s, 16:9, 736x414)

Source: `reference-library/shapes/source.mp4` (originally `Motion Design/Shapes (MD).mp4`)
Extractor: 77 shots (many real cuts). Re-derived into 10 beats from a 0.5 fps sheet; times ±2s. Frames in `shots/` are named by timestamp.

**Reference use:** geometric-shape kinetic type, shape-to-UI morphs, rotating word lists, dashboard/stat-card scenes, mascot chat scenes. Do not carry over brand name, logo, copy, UI or mascot. Describe generically.

**Style summary:** a SaaS/business-platform promo built from flat geometric shapes (hexagon, octagon, circle, bars) in red, blue, yellow on cream, white, or saturated blue. Type: clean sans, sentence case, small and centered or one rotating word list. UI dashboards sit on blue gradient panels. One small emoji-like mascot appears in chat scenes.

## Cross-beat notes
- **Shape nesting as transition** (octagon → hexagon → circle) carries between scenes.
- **Saturated blue** is the UI backdrop; cream/white hold type cards.
- **Rotating word list** is the signature type device.
- **Rhythm:** ~6s per beat with short (2s) shape bursts inside beats 2 and 3.
