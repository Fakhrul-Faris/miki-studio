## Shot 1 — 0.00s–3.60s (3.60s)
**Frames:** `shot01_*`, `shot02_*`

- **Camera:** Static wide shot, eye-level, locked off (tripod). No pan/tilt — all motion is in-frame (VFX wind gust building over the duration).
- **Location:** NYC brownstone street corner — cream-brick building with red-painted door/window trim and a red half-moon awning over the entrance, wrought-iron fence and stoop, a newsstand awning to the left, a bodega/flower shop with yellow-framed windows to the right, laundry hanging from a fire escape above.
- **Proportion & composition:** Deep symmetrical composition centered on the doorway; foreground taxi cab crossing right-to-left on a diagonal anchors the bottom third; multiple human layers (bike messenger, kid on steps, delivery cyclist, two women on the stoop) create depth across foreground/midground/background. Classic wide "hero establishing" block-shot framing.
- **Mood:** Ordinary, bustling city-morning calm — about to be disrupted. Builds anticipation as wind visibly picks up (laundry, hair, newspaper starting to lift) across the shot.
- **Lighting:** Warm low-angle sun (golden hour / late-afternoon key), strong warm color temperature (~3200K look), soft haze/atmosphere in the air, backlit dust/pollen visible in the light shafts.

**Frames:** `shot05_*`, `shot06_*`

## Shot 4 — 10.32s–15.00s (4.68s)
**Frames:** `shot07_*`, `shot08_*`

- **Camera:** Starts wide/low (near ground level, slight low angle) then the runner closes distance to camera — a push-in/tracking feel achieved via the subject's motion toward a fixed-ish camera, ending on a tighter torso-up framing that moves backward with him (tracking dolly/gimbal following him running toward/past camera).
- **Location:** A different, wider cobblestone SoHo-type street — cast-iron building facades, fire escapes, a bike, a red delivery scooter, a parked NYPD car, pedestrians — much busier and more "urban canyon" than the brownstone block.
- **Proportion & composition:** Wide shot places the runner center-frame with symmetric flanking characters (a man with groceries left, a woman recoiling right) caught in the wind-wake; debris (newspaper pages, leaves, pizza boxes) fills the upper-third airspace for chaos/energy. The closer coverage recomposes to a classic three-quarter hero runner shot, slight low angle, sky visible above the rooftops.
- **Mood:** Explosive, larger-than-life — the runner's speed is visibly "tearing" the street apart (super-hero framing), determined/focused expression.
- **Lighting:** Bright, clear daylight blue sky visible (harder, more overhead sun than the golden-hour brownstone shots — a lighting/time-of-day shift for this "action" beat), strong practical fill from the open sky, sweat visible and lit as a highlight on skin.

- **Mood:** Playful chaos/comedy beat — a bystander reacting to the runner's wind-wake, sight-gag pacing.
- **Lighting:** Warm, lower sun again (matches Shot 1/2/3 palette, softer haze), practical warm highlights on newspaper pages catching the light as they fly.

## Cross-shot notes for prompt-writing

- **Two distinct locations**: (A) a quieter brownstone corner (Shots 1–3, 5–6, warm golden-hour light, ~3200K) and (B) a busier SoHo-style cast-iron canyon block (Shot 4, brighter/cooler daylight, visible blue sky). Any location-generation prompts should keep these two looks separate rather than blending them.
- **Consistent wind-wake VFX language** across Shots 1, 2, 4, 5: paper/laundry/hair/debris lifting in the runner's directional wake — useful as a recurring "disturbance" motif if we borrow this device.
- **Recurring color hydrant/red-accent motif** (red door, red awning, red fire hydrant, red jacket/scooter) against the cream/tan brick — a deliberate accent-color anchor per location.
- **Shot rhythm**: wide establishing → tight reaction → macro product → wide action → medium reaction/comedy → tight reaction → tagline/logo. A clear wide/tight/macro alternation, not same-scale cuts back to back.
