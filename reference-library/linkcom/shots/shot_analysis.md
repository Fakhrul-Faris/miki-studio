# Motion Design Analysis — "Linkcom" (~18.0s, 16:9, 736x414)

Source: `reference-library/linkcom/source.mp4` (originally `Motion Design/linkcom (MD).mp4`)
Extracted via `tools/shot_extractor.py` (12 shots at scene thresh 0.15: 4 real sub-second cuts at the start, then fixed-interval splits). Re-derived into 11 beats from a 2 fps contact sheet plus spot frames. Beat times are accurate to ~0.5s. Frame refs are approximate: `shot01–04` = beats 1–2, `shot05` = beats 3–4, `shot06` = beats 4–5, `shot07` = beats 6–7, `shot08` = beats 7–8, `shot09` = beat 9, `shot10` = beat 10, `shot11–12` = beat 11.

**Reference use:** typography, animation/transition technique, layout, background/accent-motif and pacing DNA only. This is a SaaS marketing-intro template with placeholder copy ("We are introducing our new service that will increase your productivity faster and better than ever before. Check our new product www.link.com"). Do not carry over the brand name, URL, copy, or the stock photos in the shards. The template's shapes and colors are reusable as generic motifs.

**Style summary:** a kinetic-type sentence delivered one phrase per beat on pure black, each beat with its own 3D/flat graphic. The sentence builds "We are introducing / our new service / that will increase / your productivity / faster / better / than ever before / Check our new product / URL." Neon green is the constant accent; orange, red, magenta and purple appear as per-beat highlights. Cuts are fast, and objects fly in and out on ease-out curves.

## Beat 1 — 0.00s–1.00s (scribble open)
**Frames:** `shot01_*`, `shot02_mid`, `shot03_mid`, `shot04_mid`

- **Typography:** A single green "!" mark, then a hand-drawn brush-script "Let's go" in bright green, large, filling the top-center.
- **Animation & transition:** Sub-second cuts between the mark, the scribble and a green shard sweeping in. Hard cuts, no easing.
- **Layout & composition:** Off-center, placed high in the frame; lots of black below.
- **Background & accent motifs:** Pure black. Neon green (#1FE58A-ish) is the only color.
- **Pacing:** ~1s of 3–4 rapid changes. A burst.
- **Mood/purpose:** Energetic hook.

## Beat 3 — 1.50s–3.00s ("We are introducing")
**Frames:** `shot05_mid`

- **Typography:** One line, white, heavy wide grotesque, sentence case, centered.

- **Layout & composition:** Small caption-scale text in the center; ghost type is oversized and cropped by the frame edges.
- **Background & accent motifs:** Black with dim green outline typography as a texture.
- **Pacing:** ~1.5s.
- **Mood/purpose:** Sets up the sentence.

## Beat 4 — 3.00s–5.00s ("our new service")
**Frames:** `shot05_end`, `shot06_start`

- **Typography:** White line with the word "new" in neon green. Small smiley icon under the text.
- **Animation & transition:** Hexagon/polygon-cropped photo shards (a face, ocean surf, a leaf/bird) fly in from the frame edges, each with a thick gradient outline (green→cyan, red→orange, magenta→blue). They drift and tilt. Behind the text, a huge dark-gray ghost "NEW!" repeats.
- **Layout & composition:** Text stays centered; shards crowd the corners and edges.
- **Background & accent motifs:** Black plus dim ghost type. Gradient-outlined photo polygons are the beat's motif.
- **Pacing:** ~2s, busier than Beat 3.
- **Mood/purpose:** Highlights the product name.

## Beat 11 — 13.50s–18.00s (end card)
**Frames:** `shot11_*`, `shot12_*`

- **Typography:** URL in white heavy wide sans, centered, on a solid neon-green shape. A tiny gray credit line sits at the bottom edge.
- **Animation & transition:** A green diamond morphs into a tilted trapezoid/quad in ~1s (fast shape morph, ease-out), then holds still. Text does not animate.
- **Layout & composition:** Shape and URL centered.
- **Background & accent motifs:** Black; one solid green shape, the only flat-fill graphic in the spot.
- **Pacing:** ~4.5s, by far the longest hold.
- **Mood/purpose:** Sign-off and CTA.

## Cross-beat notes
- **One phrase per beat, one graphic per phrase.** The type system is constant (white heavy wide sans, centered, small); all variety comes from the graphic behind it.
- **Black canvas, neon-green anchor.** Green appears in the first and last beats and threads through the middle; other hues are per-beat highlights.
- **Shape language escalates:** ghost outline type → photo polygons → chart line → glossy pills → 3D cylinder/coins → circle + torus → wedges → flat green quad. The visuals become more solid and dimensional, then collapse to one flat shape.
- **Rhythm:** ~1s, 0.5s, 1.5s, 2s, 1.5s, 1.5s, 2s, 1.5s, 1s, 1s, 4.5s. Steady 1.5–2s beats, a fast burst at start and before the CTA, and one long hold.
