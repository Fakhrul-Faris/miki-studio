# Motion Design Analysis — "Tap to Cash" (~14.1s, 1:1, 720x720)

Source: `reference-library/tap-to-cash/source.mp4` (originally `Motion Design/Tap To Cash (MD).mp4`)
Extracted via `tools/shot_extractor.py` (5 fixed-interval splits of 2.82s each — no real cuts detected). Re-derived into 5 beats from a 2 fps contact sheet plus spot frames. Beat times are accurate to ~0.5s.

**Reference use:** layout, header-typography treatment, product-demo pacing and the "static caption + live demo" structure only. This is a phone-feature product-page clip: it shows the source brand's devices, OS screens, feature name and copy. Do not carry over the feature name, the caption text ("Tap to Cash", "send and receive Apple Cash by holding two iPhone devices together"), device design, or on-screen UI. Describe generically (e.g. "two hands bring two phones together on a white background; a light bloom at contact; the screens change to a confirmation state").

**Style summary:** unlike the other motion-design references, this is a locked-off live-action product demo (real hands, real phones) under a fixed text header on pure white. The type never animates. All motion is in the demo: entry, approach, tap, screen response, confirmation. It reads as a website hero/feature-tile loop.

**Type of reference:** closer to a product macro shot than kinetic type. Use for layout and demo-beat rhythm, not for type animation.

---

## Beat 1 — 0.00s–1.00s (entry)
**Frames:** `shot01_start`

- **Typography:** Static two-tier header at top: short bold title (centered), then a lighter two-line subtitle beneath. Dark near-black on white. Present from frame 0 and never changes.
- **Animation & transition:** Two hands slide in from opposite frame edges, each holding a phone, and glide toward center. Left phone (dark screen) enters low-left; right phone enters upper-right tilted, lock screen visible. Motion is quick with ease-out, and a small motion blur is visible on the left phone in frame 1.
- **Layout & composition:** Header occupies the top ~15%. Demo fills the lower-middle band. A small black circular arrow icon sits at the bottom-right corner (a page control, static).
- **Background & accent motifs:** Pure white, no texture or shadow. Devices and hands are the only color.
- **Pacing:** ~1s. Fast entry.
- **Mood/purpose:** Sets up "two people, two devices, coming together."

---

## Beat 2 — 1.00s–4.50s (approach and hold)
**Frames:** `shot01_mid`, `shot01_end`, `shot02_start`

- **Typography:** Header unchanged.
- **Animation & transition:** Phones settle in an offset overlap — the right phone tilted and on top, the left phone flatter and underneath — and hold nearly still with only small hand drift. The left screen shows a ready-to-send state with a large dot-stipple amount. The right shows a lock screen with a large clock.
- **Layout & composition:** Diagonal composition, the devices crossing at the center. Right phone rides higher; hands frame both sides in the lower corners.
- **Background & accent motifs:** White. Screens are the only light sources.
- **Pacing:** ~3.5s hold. Longest still moment, an "anticipation" beat.
- **Mood/purpose:** Shows both states before the action.

---

## Beat 3 — 4.50s–7.50s (tap and light bloom)
**Frames:** `shot02_mid`, `shot02_end`, `shot03_start`

- **Typography:** Header unchanged.
- **Animation & transition:** Phones tap together. At contact (~5.0s) a soft white light bloom flares at the touch point, then a broader glow washes across the left screen for ~1s. The right screen flashes bright and then dims to black by ~7.0s. No camera move.
- **Layout & composition:** Same framing. The bloom is the only new element.
- **Background & accent motifs:** White background, so the bloom reads as a soft highlight on the black phone glass rather than a glow in space.
- **Pacing:** ~3s. Tap is instant; the glow decays over ~2s.
- **Mood/purpose:** The magic moment: contact triggers the transfer.

---

## Beat 4 — 7.50s–10.00s (screens respond)
**Frames:** `shot03_end`, `shot04_*`

- **Typography:** Header unchanged.
- **Animation & transition:** Screens light up with new UI. The right phone shows the stipple amount plus a white pill-shaped confirm control and a dark decline control at its right edge. The left phone shows a small blue ring spinner with a status line under it. The phones reposition slightly to become nearly parallel and level.
- **Layout & composition:** Phones now overlap in a flatter horizontal arrangement, centered.
- **Background & accent motifs:** White background. Blue ring is the first saturated accent in the clip.
- **Pacing:** ~2.5s.
- **Mood/purpose:** Shows the transaction in progress.

---

## Beat 5 — 10.00s–14.10s (confirmation hold)
**Frames:** `shot05_*`

- **Typography:** Header unchanged.
- **Animation & transition:** The confirm/decline controls disappear from the right screen and a small blue ring appears there. By ~13.5s the left spinner turns into a completed state (a check inside the ring). The phones stay still while the state changes.
- **Layout & composition:** Unchanged from Beat 4.
- **Background & accent motifs:** White. The blue ring is the one accent.
- **Pacing:** ~4s. Long, calm hold to end the loop.
- **Mood/purpose:** Payoff: "done."

---

## Cross-beat notes
- **Fixed header, moving demo.** Header and corner icon never animate. The viewer reads the caption once, then watches.
- **Pure-white world.** No environment. Hands and phones are cropped by the frame edges so the demo feels like a cutout.
- **One light event.** The single bloom at contact is the only flashy moment; everything else is calm.
- **Rhythm:** ~1s entry, ~3.5s hold, ~3s tap, ~2.5s response, ~4s confirmation. Slow-fast-slow, ending on a long hold.
