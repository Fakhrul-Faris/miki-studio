# Motion Design Analysis — "Airbnb" (~40.5s, 1:1, 720x720)

Source: `reference-library/airbnb/source.mp4` (originally `Motion Design/AirBnb (MD).mp4`)
Extracted via `tools/shot_extractor.py` (many real cuts, incl. ~10 sub-second ones at 7.7–10.4s). Re-derived into 11 beats from a 1 fps contact sheet; times accurate to ~1s. Frames in `shots/` are named by timestamp; find a beat's frames by its time range.

**Reference use:** product-launch pacing, the "3D icon + label" slam, phone-UI-as-hero staging, full-bleed footage cards with big captions, end-card sequencing. Do not carry over brand name, logo, wordmark, app screens, on-screen copy, celebrity/person names or the footage. Describe generically ("a phone shows a search screen with three tabs").

**Style summary:** a feature-launch spot on a flat off-white background. Two modes alternate: (a) glossy 3D object icons with a centered sans label, (b) an upright phone showing UI. Between them, full-bleed video cards with large white captions add color. Type is one clean rounded sans, dark, centered. Ends on a symbol and a dated lockup.

---

## Beat 1 — 0–2s (headline + phone)
- **Typography:** Two-line centered headline, medium-weight sans, dark on off-white, top third.
- **Animation:** Phone below with a search bar and three tabs; the highlighted tab moves from one to the next (underline slides).
- **Layout:** Headline top, phone bottom, cropped by the frame edge.
- **Pacing:** ~2s. **Purpose:** Premise line.

## Beat 2 — 2–4s (icon slam)
- **Typography:** One word label under each icon, medium sans.
- **Animation:** A glossy 3D bell, then a 3D balloon, each hard-cut in at center, one per ~1s.
- **Layout:** Icon center, label below; empty off-white otherwise.
- **Pacing:** ~1s each. **Purpose:** Name the new categories.

## Beat 3 — 4–5s (title)
- **Typography:** Two-line centered title, larger than the labels.
- **Animation:** Hard cut, static.
- **Pacing:** ~1s. **Purpose:** Announce the release.

## Beat 4 — 5–8s (phone tab tour)
- **Typography:** UI text only.
- **Animation:** Phone shows a category screen with photo tiles; then the 3D bell returns with a small "NEW" badge tilted at its top-right and an underline under its label.
- **Pacing:** ~3s. **Purpose:** Show the UI, then tag it as new.

## Beat 5 — 8–14s (service icon montage)
- **Typography:** Single-word labels under 3D icons.
- **Animation:** A phone card with a person's photo and name, then a rapid run of hard cuts (sub-second at first, then ~1s each): massage table, nail polish, makeup brush and lipstick, food warmer.
- **Layout:** Each icon centered on off-white.
- **Pacing:** Fast; fastest part of the spot. **Purpose:** Range of offerings as rhythm.

## Beat 6 — 14–18s (experiences)
- **Typography:** Label + underline; a big white caption on video.
- **Animation:** Balloon with "NEW" badge, a phone list of photo tiles, then a hard cut to full-bleed dark arena footage with a large centered white caption.
- **Pacing:** ~4s. **Purpose:** Switch from icons to real footage.

## Beat 7 — 18–21s (video cards)
- **Typography:** Large white bold sans caption centered over footage, 2–3 lines.
- **Animation:** Two full-bleed clips back to back (a dance studio, a red-lit music-video look) with the caption already in place.
- **Layout:** Caption centered; footage crops tight to the subject.
- **Pacing:** ~1.5s each. **Purpose:** Energy and color break.

## Beat 8 — 21–24s (second title + objects)
- **Typography:** Same two-line title as Beat 3.
- **Animation:** Title, then a glossy blue grid-textured 3D cushion shape, then a tiny house icon that grows into the next phone screen.
- **Pacing:** ~1s each. **Purpose:** Introduce the homes section.

## Beat 9 — 24–31s (phone: homes, trips)
- **Typography:** UI text.
- **Animation:** Phone builds its home screen from a gray wireframe to filled; then cards slide/crop: a trip card, a trips list, a day-by-day itinerary. Zoom-in crops on the card rather than showing the whole phone.
- **Pacing:** ~7s of steady UI. **Purpose:** Product walkthrough.

## Beat 10 — 31–36s (calendar, originals, profile)
- **Typography:** UI text; big numeral on the calendar icon.
- **Animation:** 3D calendar page-flip icon, phone with a row of video thumbnails, then a profile screen with an avatar and stats.
- **Pacing:** ~5s. **Purpose:** More features, lighter touch.

## Beat 11 — 36–40.5s (end sequence)
- **Typography:** Final lockup: a boxed year + "Summer Release" in dark bold sans.
- **Animation:** Pink gradient ID card, then a layered isometric card stack exploding out, a gold embossed seal, then the lockup, each hard cut, ~1s.
- **Layout:** Objects centered; lockup centered.
- **Pacing:** ~4.5s. **Purpose:** Sign-off with a dated release tag.

---

## Cross-beat notes
- **Off-white canvas, dark type, glossy objects.** Nearly every frame is a centered subject on flat off-white; color comes from the 3D icons and the video cards.
- **Alternation:** icon → label → phone → video, repeated, so no mode lasts more than ~7s.
- **Fast montage in the middle (8–14s)** contrasts with slow UI and hold beats.
- **"NEW" badge** on an icon is a reusable device for flagging a feature.
