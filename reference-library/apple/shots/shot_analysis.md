# Motion Design Analysis — "Apple" (~11.8s, 9:16 vertical, 720x1280)

Source: `reference-library/apple/source.mp4` (originally `Motion Design/Apple (MD).mp4`)
Extracted via `tools/shot_extractor.py` (7 shots at scene thresh 0.15; the short 0.7s, 1.4s and 0.7s shots in the middle are real cuts, the rest are fixed-interval splits). Re-derived into 8 beats from a 3 fps contact sheet. Beat times are accurate to ~0.35s. Frame refs are approximate: `shot01` = beats 1–2, `shot02` = beats 2–4, `shot03`–`shot05` = beat 4, `shot06` = beats 5–7, `shot07` = beats 7–8.

**Reference use:** camera-move/transition technique (whip, slide-wipe), the "macro color run" pattern, device-in-hand staging, and pacing. This is a phone-launch social clip: it shows the source brand's hardware, OS UI and colorways. Do not carry over the brand name, the device design, camera-module shape, colorway names, or the UI screens. Describe generically (e.g. "a hand holds a phone, back facing camera; macro shots of its rear lenses in five colors").

**Style summary:** a locked-off-ish product film in vertical format on a deep-blue gradient studio backdrop. It cuts between a hand-held hero, a side-control interaction, a quick five-color macro run and a return to the hero, ending on the lit screen. Transitions are physical: blurred hand whip, sliding phone bodies, tilting device.

## Beat 1 — 0.00s–0.35s (hero flash)
**Frames:** `shot01_start`

- **Typography:** None.
- **Animation & transition:** A hand holds the phone's back to camera, upper-left crop; the module glints. Hard cut to Beat 2.
- **Layout & composition:** Phone occupies the left-center, angled slightly, hand at top and bottom edges.
- **Background & accent motifs:** Deep navy-to-indigo gradient, lit softly from the left.
- **Pacing:** ~0.35s, a title-card-length flash.
- **Mood/purpose:** Tease the product.

## Beat 2 — 0.35s–2.35s (side-control interaction)
**Frames:** `shot01_mid`, `shot01_end`, `shot02_start`

- **Typography:** None; only tiny UI tick marks.
- **Animation & transition:** Cut to a tight crop of a landscape photo filling the screen (left) and a finger pressing the phone's side edge (right). As the finger slides, a slim tick-mark slider with a yellow indicator appears on the screen edge and a pink glow blooms under the fingertip. The crop is locked; only the finger and slider move.
- **Layout & composition:** Vertical split: image on the left, edge and finger on the right. Slider hugs the edge.
- **Background & accent motifs:** Lavender-purple landscape; navy at the edge; pink glow and yellow tick are the only accents.
- **Pacing:** ~2s, the calmest hold in the spot.
- **Mood/purpose:** Shows a tactile feature in use.

## Beat 3 — 2.35s–2.65s (whip transition)
**Frames:** `shot02_end`

- **Typography:** None.
- **Animation & transition:** Heavy motion blur of the hand sweeping across the frame in a warm blur. Serves as a whip-pan cover to the next macro.
- **Layout & composition:** Full-frame blur.
- **Background & accent motifs:** Blue and skin-tone blur.
- **Pacing:** ~0.3s.
- **Mood/purpose:** Hides the cut.

## Beat 4 — 2.65s–5.90s (color macro run)
**Frames:** `shot02_end`, `shot03_*`, `shot04_*`, `shot05_*`

- **Typography:** None.
- **Animation & transition:** Tight macro of the rear dual-lens module, one colorway per ~0.7s: blue (2.7s) → pink (3.0–3.6s) → teal (3.7–4.4s) → black (4.7–5.3s) → white/silver (5.3–5.9s). Each next phone slides in from the left edge over the previous one (a horizontal slide-wipe with a visible body edge), then settles. Module stays in the same screen position, so the colors "swap" in place.
- **Layout & composition:** Module locked at the same position, filling ~60% of the frame width; body cropped by the frame edges.
- **Background & accent motifs:** Backdrop shifts with each color (blue, magenta, deep green, charcoal, gray) so each beat has a unified monochrome look.
- **Pacing:** ~0.7s each, ~3.25s total. The fastest section.
- **Mood/purpose:** Range of finishes, shown as a rhythm rather than a catalogue.

- **Layout & composition:** Phone landscape-rotated in the lower-middle of the vertical frame, with empty space above (room for a title or end card).
- **Background & accent motifs:** Blue gradient with a white bloom at left; the screen wallpaper echoes the phone's color.
- **Pacing:** ~2.3s hold.
- **Mood/purpose:** Sign-off.

## Cross-beat notes
- **Physical transitions only.** A hand whip, sliding phone bodies and a 3D flip do the work; there are no graphic wipes or dissolves.
- **Macro run as rhythm.** Locking the module in place and swapping the color around it makes five variants read as one continuous move.
- **Color-matched backdrops.** Each finish gets its own background tone, so frames feel designed rather than catalogue shots.
- **Vertical framing leaves headroom.** The end frame keeps the top third empty for text.
- **Rhythm:** ~0.35s, 2s, 0.3s, 3.25s (five ~0.7s hits), 0.8s, 1.3s, 1.5s, 2.3s. Slow-fast-slow, ending on a hold.
