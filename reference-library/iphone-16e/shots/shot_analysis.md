# Motion Design Analysis — "iPhone 16e" (~42.4s, 9:16, 720x1280)

Source: `reference-library/iphone-16e/source.mp4` (originally `Motion Design/Iphone 16e (MD).mp4`)
Extractor: 29 shots (many real cuts). Re-derived into 12 beats from a 1 fps sheet; times ±1s. Frames in `shots/` are named by timestamp; find a beat's frames by time range.

**Reference use:** the repeating "icon + short headline → phone demo" module, left-aligned type, gradient title, black interlude, end-card sequence. Do not carry over brand name, logo, product names, feature names, app screens, or copy. Describe generically.

**Style summary:** a feature-tour spot on pale off-white / light gray. Each feature repeats one two-step module: (1) a small colored line-icon over a 1–2 line left-aligned headline (the last word ends in a period), (2) a hand holding a phone demoing it in close-up. One black interlude and one black claim card break the light palette.

Recurring type: medium-weight sans, sentence case, dark gray/black on off-white, left-aligned around the vertical center. Full-stop endings. Small colored icon above headline.

## Cross-beat notes
- **Module rhythm:** headline (~1–2s) → demo (~2–4s), repeated six times, so the spot never feels like a screen recording.
- **Colored line-icons** give each feature its own hue while type stays neutral.
- **Black is used twice** (interlude and claim) to reset attention.
- **Left-aligned, period-ended headlines** read as confident statements.
- **Rhythm:** slower demos (3–5s) between fast headline cards (~1–2s); claims at the end speed up to ~2s each.
