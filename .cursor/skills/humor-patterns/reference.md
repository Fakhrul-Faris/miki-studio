## Grease Pencil production defaults (from remake notes)

- **Locations:** 1–2 (3 max for romance carry)
- **Cast:** 2 speaking + 1 background status prop (queue, line, extra)
- **Props:** Specific and sad/funny (string cheese, oversized spoon, fake DVD year)
- **Text:** On-screen titles for spells, prices, hooks — odd1sout clarity
- **Hold:** Deadpan faces through the blast / fail; animate the loser
