# EP01 — Stay Strong — Full animation (experiment)

**Goal:** make EP01 entirely as Seedance animation and compare it with the stills version.
Nothing else in this folder changes; the stills pipeline stays the default.

**Source of truth for the story:** `1_script.md` (v2, customer's phone).

**Audio: none from Seedance.** Its generated speech comes out in Mandarin, so every clip is silent.
Characters only *move their mouths* at timed moments; the ElevenLabs lines from `3_voice.md` and all sound
effects are added in the edit.

---

## How it's split

4 segments, each well under the 15s limit. **Every shot inside a segment, and the first shot of every
segment, is a new camera angle.** So any shot boundary is a clean hard cut: if something breaks
mid-generation, cut it there in post and keep the good shots.

| Seg | Length | Shots (each a new angle) | Mouth movement for |
|-----|--------|--------------------------|--------------------|
| **S1** The notifications | 6s | 1 · front medium shot, Miki wiping → 2 · phone close-up | — |
| **S2** Whose phone | 7s | 3 · over Miki's shoulder, customer eating cake → 4 · over the customer's shoulder at the counter | L01 |
| **S3** The comp | 10s | 5 · Miki medium close-up → 6 · customer reverse → 7 · Miki tight close-up → 8 · side two-shot | L02 · L03 · L04 · L05 |
| **S4** Stay strong | 9s | 9 · overhead phone insert → 10 · Miki from the customer's point of view → 11 · front medium shot, empty stand | L06 |

≈ 32s before trimming. Holds can be trimmed in post to ~25s.

**If one shot fails:** keep the good shots. Regenerate only the failed one, using the same segment
prompt with every other shot line deleted, and the duration set to that shot's length (min. 4s, trim in post).

Render L01–L06 from `3_voice.md` **before** generating video, and note each line's length. The talking
windows below assume these lengths — if a rendered line is longer or shorter, change that window's
end time in the prompt so the mouth stops when the line does.

| Line | Shot | Talking window (segment time) | Assumed length |

The prompts never quote the English lines — any quoted dialogue invites Seedance to speak it.

## Fixed blocks (already inside every prompt below — don't reword them between segments)

- **Silent** — no audio of any kind.
- **Talking** — timed mouth movement only, described as shapes, never as words.
- **No subtitles** — no captions or on-screen text (except the phone's notifications in S1).
- **The phone** — S1 shows the notifications on screen (stock iOS lock screen, generated by Seedance). After S1 the screen has gone to sleep: dark.

## S1 — The notifications · 6s
```text
Silent clip: no audio at all — no voices, no speech, no music, no sound effects.
A 6-second vertical 9:16 1080p animated clip, set at @Counter.
Camera: every shot is locked off — no zoom, pan, tilt, orbit or shake. Shots are joined by hard cuts.
A black phone charges on a small stand on the counter, screen facing the camera, dark at the start. No subtitles, captions, logos or extra people, and no on-screen text other than the phone's notifications. Nobody talks in this clip.
Shot 1 (0:00–0:03) — front medium shot across the counter. @Miki wipes the counter with a white rag in small circles, deadpan and half-lidded; the charging phone sits in the lower-right foreground. At 0:00.6 the phone's screen lights up: a standard iPhone lock screen with one stock notification banner from Messages, sender "Babe ❤️", message "We're done." His eyes slide to the phone; by 0:02 his eyebrows rise high and his lips press into a tight wince. The rag stops.
Shot 2 (0:03–0:06) — hard cut to a close-up of the phone on its stand, the lit lock screen filling most of the frame, @Miki's hand and the still rag at the frame edge. The Messages notification is readable. At 0:03.8 a second stock notification banner slides in directly below the first, from the app "PayLater", message "Your payment of $87.40 is overdue." Both notifications stay on screen, still and readable, until the end of the shot. Standard iOS notification design only — no custom cards.
```

## S2 — Whose phone · 7s
```text
Silent clip: no audio at all — no voices, no speech, no music, no sound effects.
A 7-second vertical 9:16 1080p animated clip, set at @Counter, with @Miki and @Customer.
Camera: every shot is locked off — no zoom, pan, tilt, orbit or shake. Shots are joined by hard cuts.
Talking means simple flat sitcom mouth shapes opening and closing, with no sound; outside those times mouths stay closed.

Shot 3 (0:00–0:03.5) — over @Miki's shoulder toward the seating area: @Miki in the foreground in profile, rag paused, brows tilted up in pure pity. In the background @Customer sits alone at a small table, happily eating a slice of cake, chewing and swaying a little with joy, completely unaware.
Shot 4 (0:03.5–0:07) — hard cut to an over-the-shoulder shot from behind @Customer, now standing at the counter: the back of his head and backpack strap in the left foreground, @Miki across the counter looking at him with a careful, sympathetic face, the charging phone on the right of the counter. @Customer turns his head slightly so his cheerful face shows in profile, and talks from 0:04.2 to 0:05.4. @Miki does not talk and keeps the sympathetic look.
```

## Assembly

1. Line up S1 → S4. Joins are hard cuts — no transitions.
2. Trim holds to taste (target ~25s). Cut any broken shot at its boundary and replace it with a regenerated shot.
3. **Notifications:** S1 renders them in-video. If the text came out garbled or non-stock, screen-replace
   the stock lock-screen states from `4_edit.md` (N1, then N1 + N2) onto the phone in shots 1–2 instead.
   Optional: the same composite on the phone in shot 10, so the audience reads it one last time.
4. **Audio (all of it is added here):**
   - ElevenLabs lines L01–L06 from `audio/` on their talking windows (Step 0 table). Slip each line a few frames until the mouth flaps line up.
   - SFX and mix: `3b_sound.md` (use the **Anim** time column).
5. **Subtitles:** if Seedance burned captions in, that shot is a regenerate. Our own burned-in subtitles
   (style in `00_bible/overlays.md`) go on last.
6. Export to `export/EP01_stay_strong_anim.mp4`.
