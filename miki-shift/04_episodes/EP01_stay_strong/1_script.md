## The knife (only this)
A customer's phone, charging at the counter, gets dumped and billed while he's away. Miki reads it all.
The customer comes back cheerful and clueless. Miki comps his bill out of pity and hands the phone
back with **"Stay strong."** We know. Miki knows. The customer doesn't — yet.

## Button (written first)
**MIKI** *(soft, careful, handing over the phone)*  
Oh—here.  
Stay strong.

**VISUAL:** Customer leaves happy. Empty charging stand. Miki watches him go with a soft, pitying
smile. Hold. Never mention the notifications out loud.

---

## Script

**LOC:** Cafe counter. A black phone charging on a stand at the counter the whole first half — the
customer's, left there while he sat down.

**VISUAL:** Miki wiping the counter. The charging phone lights up — stock lock-screen notification:
**Babe ❤️** — We're done.

**VISUAL:** Miki glances at it. Eyebrows up. Oof.

**VISUAL — PHONE CLOSE-UP:** Second notification stacks under it:
**PayLater** — Your payment of $87.40 is overdue.

**VISUAL:** Miki looks from the phone toward the seating area. Pure pity.

**CUSTOMER** *(arriving, cheerful)*  
Hey—can I pay here?

**MIKI** *(gentle, like talking to someone at a funeral)*  
Don't worry about it. It's on me.

**CUSTOMER**  
Wait—serious?

**MIKI** *(grave nod)*  
Serious.

**CUSTOMER**  
Oh my god, thank you.  
Also—my phone? It was charging.

**VISUAL:** Miki unplugs the phone from the stand. Lock screen still showing both notifications,
screen turned toward us — the customer never looks at it.

**MIKI**  
Oh—here.  
Stay strong.

**CUSTOMER** *(no line — a confused, happy half-wave, already walking)*

**BUTTON — VISUAL:** Door bell. Empty charging stand. Miki watches the door, soft pitying smile. Hold. Cut.

**END.**

---

## Why it works
- **Dramatic irony:** the audience and Miki share a secret with a stranger. Every cheerful customer
  line gets funnier because we know what's in his pocket.
- **Wrong-sized kindness:** a free coffee as the cafe's response to heartbreak and debt.
- Notifications do the setup (stock lock screen, English). Dialogue never explains them.
- The button is a face, not a line — reaction-ready for reposts.

## Cold-read test
Cover the notifications: does "Stay strong" still land? (It shouldn't — it needs them. Good.)
Show the notifications: does the customer's "Oh my god, thank you" hurt a little? If not — kill.
If anyone in the audience wonders whose phone it is, the hook failed — fix F01/F03 first.
