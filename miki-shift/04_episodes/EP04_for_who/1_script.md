## The knife (only this)
Tess, late again, asks for "whatever's fastest **for you**." Juno pulls a shot and steams milk — a
beautiful latte comes together and Tess relaxes, sure it's hers. Juno sets it by the machine, fills a
clear glass from the tap and slides **the water** across. "…For who?" "**For me.**"

## Button (written first)
**TESS** *(glass of plain water in hand, looking past Juno at the latte by the machine)*
…For who?

**JUNO** *(picking up the latte, already sipping, not looking up)*
For me.

**VISUAL:** Juno walks off with the latte. Tess still holding the water. Cut to end card.

## Script

**LOC:** Specialty coffee bar. Light-wood counter, espresso machine and water tap behind it.

**VISUAL:** Tess rushes in, phone in hand, coat half on, tote slipping.

**TESS** *(flustered, rushed)*
Oh— hi, sorry, I'm so late. Can I just get whatever's easiest? Honestly, whatever's fastest for you.

**VISUAL:** Juno looks at her for exactly one beat. Small nod.

**VISUAL — THE DECOY:** Juno pulls an espresso shot into a ceramic cup. Steams milk. Pours latte art.
Tess watches, smiles, relaxes — then glances back at her phone.

**VISUAL:** Juno sets the finished latte down **beside the machine, on her side** — not on the hand-off
spot. Fills a **clear glass** at the tap. Slides the water across.

**JUNO** *(deadpan)*
Here.

**TESS** *(taking it without looking, eyes on her phone)*
Thanks so much—

**VISUAL:** Tess looks down at the glass. Plain water. Looks up, past Juno, at the latte by the machine.

**TESS** *(confused)*
…For who?

**JUNO** *(picking up the latte, sipping, not looking up)*
For me.

**BUTTON — VISUAL:** Juno walks off, sipping. Tess stands there holding the water. Cut.

**END** → end card (post).

## What changed in v3
- **Cast and location are new** — Juno (barista) and Tess (customer) at a specialty coffee bar.
- **Tickets removed.** The v2 joke is the decoy, not "slammed"; the tickets were extra props and a mixed message.
- **The two objects read at a glance:** clear glass of plain water vs ceramic cup with latte art.
- **The decoy is visible:** a shot of Tess watching the latte and smiling. If we don't see her assume, "For who?" can't land.
- **"Her side" is a place:** the latte goes beside the machine, not on the hand-off spot.

## Cold-read test
"For me." must get the laugh on its own, with no line after it.
If the latte-making reads as Juno being helpful rather than a decoy, shorten it until it doesn't.
If anyone wonders whether the water was a mistake, Juno's flat, unbothered sip has failed — fix the sip, not the line.
