## The knife (only this)
Staff is mid-crime eating “leftovers.” Customer returns and turns that exact food into a **to-go order**. Punch = saying the dish name with food still in your mouth / hands.

## Button (written first)

Salted-egg chicken. To go.

**VISUAL:** Same crouch. Sauce on lip. Hold 1s. Cut. No apology.

## Script

**LOC:** Cafe pass / kitchen alley. Counter lip in foreground. Miki low behind it.

**ON-SCREEN:** (optional, tiny) leftover protocol

**VISUAL:** Miki crouched with a half-eaten fried bun + steamer. Chewing. Happy crime.

**CUSTOMER** *(OS)*  
Hey—sorry, forgot. Can you pack my food to go?

**MIKI**  
*(chew freezes)*  
…To go?

**CUSTOMER**  
Yeah. The salted-egg chicken.

**VISUAL:** Miki looks at the food in his hands. Then at the empty plate slot. The leftovers *are* the order.

**MIKI**  
*(stands halfway, still crouched energy, mouth full)*  
Salted-egg chicken. To go.

**BUTTON — VISUAL:** Eyes wide. Chew resumes against his will. Cut before he stands fully.

**END.**
