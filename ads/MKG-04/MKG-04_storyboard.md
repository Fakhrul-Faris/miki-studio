# MKG-04 storyboard sheet prompt (human review only)

Style reference: black-and-white pencil and ink film storyboard, 3 columns of 16:9 panels, thick black borders, a short caption under each panel, white outlined arrows for camera and object movement, and a handwritten camera note beside the arrows.

Do not feed this image to the video model. Image generators garble small text: if the captions come out wrong, regenerate with the caption rule at the end set to "leave every caption line blank" and add the captions yourself. If one 15-panel sheet is too crowded, generate two sheets: panels 1-9 (3 by 3, matching the reference) and panels 10-15.

## One-sheet prompt (15 panels, 3 columns by 5 rows)

```
A professional film storyboard sheet for a 30-second product explainer ad, in the style of a hand-drawn black-and-white storyboard: loose graphite pencil and black ink linework with soft grey marker wash, thick black panel borders, white paper, sketchy gestural shading, no colour. The sheet has 15 panels in 3 columns by 5 rows, every panel 16:9 with a small panel number at its top-left corner and a short caption in small italic typed capitals under it. White outlined arrows with a heavy black outline are drawn over the panels to show camera movement and object movement, with a short handwritten camera note beside the arrow. Every device screen is drawn as a flat light-grey tone with fine diagonal hatching to mark a blank green screen. The backdrop in the device panels is a smooth empty gradient, light in the top left and darker in the bottom right. Consistent characters and props across panels: one forearm and hand with a plain long sleeve pushed to mid-forearm, no jewellery; one slab phone, one tablet in a slim case, one open laptop, all without logos.

Panel 1: flat empty paper background, a large simple notebook open at the centre with scribble lines filling its pages, a pen scribbling across the pages, a curved arrow showing the cover flipping open, a short white arrow pointing into the frame with the note "SLOW PUSH IN". Caption: "1 NOTEBOOK FLIPS OPEN".

Panel 2: flat empty background, four chat-bubble icons stacked upward at the centre with small bounce lines, tiny arrows showing them popping in one by one. Caption: "2 CHAT BUBBLES STACK UP".

Panel 3: flat empty background, a paper booking sheet with a pen scribbling a list down its rows, then the sheet crumpled into a small tight paper ball rolling toward the right edge, a white arrow along the roll. Caption: "3 PAPER SCRUNCHES AND ROLLS OUT".

Panel 4: flat empty background, a simple round logo mark at the centre settling with small radiating lines, a white arrow pointing up for the logo lifting out, and a phone silhouette entering from the top edge with a white arrow pointing down. Caption: "4 LOGO LIFTS, PHONE DESCENDS".

Panel 5: medium-close view from slightly above (high three-quarter angle) on the light grey backdrop, an open hand waiting at the lower centre with the palm tilted up and relaxed fingers, a phone gliding down from above toward the palm, a curved white arrow along its path, small motion lines behind it. Caption: "5A PHONE FLOATS INTO HAND".

Panel 6: same framing, the hand now gripping the phone at the centre, tilted about 30 degrees toward the viewer, thumb resting on the side edge and off the screen, a white arrow pointing into the frame and a small curved arrow showing the camera arcing round to face-on, with the note "PUSH IN + ARC TO FACE-ON". Caption: "5B HOLD, PUSH IN TO SCREEN".

Panel 7: extreme close-up, the whole frame filled with the phone screen, the hatched grey tone with simple drawn UI shapes: rounded time-slot chips and a tick, some of them blurred with speed lines flying past, a tall white arrow pointing upward on the right with the note "DIVE, BOTTOM TO TOP". Caption: "5C MACRO DIVE ACROSS SCREEN".

Panel 8: same centred framing, a pulled-back view of the phone held upright with a ghost outline of a wider tablet shape drawn around it, a large curved white arrow turning counter-clockwise around the device, a second faint outline halfway between the phone and the tablet showing the shape stretching. Note: "ROTATE CCW + MORPH". Caption: "6A PHONE ROTATES, MORPHS TO TABLET".

Panel 9: medium-close view, the tablet in landscape held in two hands (one hand at each side) at a three-quarter angle, a curved white arrow showing the tablet gliding in, and a second curved white arrow with the note "ARC TO FRONT-ON" showing the camera swinging round to a symmetric front-on view. Caption: "6B TABLET FLOATS IN, HOLD".

Panel 10: extreme close-up, the whole frame filled with the tablet screen, the hatched grey tone with a simple drawn payment card and a large number, small speed lines, a tall white arrow pointing upward with the note "DIVE". Caption: "6D MACRO DIVE, PAYMENT".

Panel 11: pulled-back view of the tablet with a ghost outline of an open laptop around it, a hinge line opening along the bottom edge, a large curved white arrow turning counter-clockwise, a faint halfway outline. Note: "ROTATE CCW + MORPH". Caption: "7A TABLET ROTATES, MORPHS TO LAPTOP".

Panel 12: medium view, an open laptop on a plain wooden desk that fills the lower third, an index fingertip hooked on its front-right corner with a short white arrow pointing toward the viewer to show the laptop being pulled closer, and a hand coming in from the right edge to rest on the desk with the hand lightly on the trackpad, nothing on the keys, no typing. Caption: "7B FINGER PULLS LAPTOP, THEN TRACKPAD".

Panel 13: same framing, the laptop on the desk with the hand resting on its edge, three vertical columns drawn on the screen filling with simple blocks, tiny rising number ticks above each column, a white arrow pointing into the frame with the note "SLOW PUSH IN". Caption: "7C HOLD, COLUMNS FILL".

Panel 14: extreme close-up, the whole frame filled with a single large number on the hatched grey screen, the camera diving toward it, a solid dark tone flooding in from the edges of the frame, a white arrow pointing into the frame. Note: "DIVE INTO TOTAL, FLOOD". Caption: "7D DIVE INTO TOTAL, COLOUR FLOODS".

Panel 15: flat empty background filled with a solid mid-grey tone, a wide rectangle at the centre marked as a text area with three horizontal lines of different lengths, a small circle placeholder at the bottom centre for the logo, a small line under it for the website. Caption: "8 END CARD: TAGLINE, CTA, LOGO".

Caption rule: write the captions exactly as given, short and clean, in one line under each panel. Panel numbers and camera notes only, no other text, no speech bubbles, no logos, no brand names.
```
