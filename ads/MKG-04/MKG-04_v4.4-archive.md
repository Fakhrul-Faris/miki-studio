## Look

Light neutral grey seamless studio backdrop, no shop, with a soft contact shadow under the desk and laptop. Real hands hold each device at a 3/4 angle, in the Apple reference style. Every screen is flat chroma-key green and filled in post. One clean sans, one short line per beat, each ending in a period. The same right and left hands (same skin tone, nails, cuff) appear across the b-roll clips; the tablet beat uses both.

## Transition grammar (the three moves the whole ad repeats)

1. **Macro dive.** The camera pushes toward a device screen, easing from the 3/4 view to face-on with a slight arc, until the green fills the frame square-on. The UI takes over and the camera keeps moving in extreme close-up, bottom to top, across the UI details: buttons, chips, numbers flying across the screen. Built in post (virtual camera on the UI), so the generated clip only has to deliver a clean push-in to a full green frame.
2. **Rotate and morph.** The camera pulls back out of the macro. The device turns counter-clockwise while its rounded-rectangle outline stretches and reshapes into the next device (phone to tablet, tablet to laptop), with the screen content crossfading. Built in post from the approved device stills, with matching light and grain.
3. **Float into hand.** The new device glides gently, weightless, into the waiting hand and settles into a holding position (the laptop settles onto the desk instead). Generated in Seedance from locked start, mid and end stills.

The same camera behaviour runs through every beat: slow and steady, ease-in and ease-out, no handheld shake, the device always centred.

## Flags

1. **Float into hand is fragile physics.** A device gliding weightless into a grip, with fingers closing, is the riskiest move in the ad. Mitigation: start, mid and end stills for each float-in, the hand already open and waiting in the start frame, and 5 s generations so a failed take is cheap to re-roll.
2. **Laptop settle and hand landing** are two moves in one clip. If it fails, split into a settle clip and a hand-rest clip.
3. **Macro and morph are post.** They need the device stills matched to the generated footage in light, grain and edge softness, or the morph will look pasted on.
4. **Tracking:** the green screen must stay in frame until the push-in fills it, so the UI can be corner-pinned. A pure green frame has nothing to track; the UI takes over only at full fill.
5. **Hand continuity** across three clips and the hand reference.
6. **Green spill** on the hand and sleeve when the camera is close.
7. **Two-handed tablet grip** needs a left-hand reference matched to the right hand, and both hands must stay off the screen. A landscape tablet also loses its edges in a 9:16 crop, so the reframe needs a tighter or re-composed pass for scene 6.
8. **3/4 angles** mean the screen is a perspective quad. Corner-pin it in post, and ease to face-on during each push so the macro dive starts from a square frame.
