# MKG-02 "Split Ends" — Meikigo Commercial

## Context

Meikigo (also written Miki) is a queue/booking/checkout platform for barbershops and other service-sector shops. Its ads follow a fixed grammar: one absurd, magic-realist problem hits a customer or a barber, the pain always ties to queue/booking chaos, and Meikigo appears only as the quiet fix — never explained, never pitched, shown in at most 3 shots. Spots run 15–30s, end on a button gag then a clean end card (logo + tagline, no CTA), and avoid real people, named rivals, and any joke about a customer's looks.

## Logline

A man whose hair grows five inches every time he sighs gets turned away by barber after barber who won't commit to a wait — until a five-second QR booking fixes it for good. Walking away relieved, he steps in a puddle, sighs in irritation, and his hair grows straight back — so without missing a beat, he books again.
