# Queue Barber — 25s vertical ad (v2)

Product: Meikigo, SaaS barber app. Customer joins the barbershop queue from his phone, tracks the live number, and arrives just in time for his turn.

Format: 9:16 vertical, 25s, social.

Setting: Moscow, mid-June, Saturday late morning. Language: Russian (dialogue and end card).

Grade source: `reference-library/accessibility-voiceover-apple-intelligence/shots/`. Grade only. No location, cast or brand carries over from that ad.

Brand mode: none. No brand profile loaded. Grade is the reference's, not Grab's warm-saturated grade.

Screens: every visible phone screen is flat chroma green. All UI (app, game, queue strip, ticket 1038) and the end card are keyed and composited in editing. Never generate text or UI.

---

## STEP 1 — DIRECTOR'S BRIEF (Directive Voice)

**Asset / Character**
ARTYOM. Twenty-two. Russian. Light brown hair two months overdue, fringe past the eyebrows, sides fuzzing over the ears. Faded grey cotton tee, collar stretched and frayed, grey jersey shorts, house slippers (тапочки) at home, white sneakers when he leaves. Phone in a scuffed clear case. Thin stubble on the jaw and chin, uneven skin, a few blemishes, a pillow crease on one cheek. Not a model. Not stressed. He refuses to spend his Saturday on a bench.

BARBER, SERGEI PETROVICH. Fifties. Grey at the temples, reading glasses pushed up on his head, forearms with old clipper nicks. Black apron with cut hair worn into the weave. He does not smile until Artyom is in the chair.

**Location A — Artyom's flat.** A rented one-bedroom in a Moscow panel-block building. Parquet lifted at the seams. Wallpaper with a faded pattern. Tulle curtain on an open window, sun behind thin cloud. Two-seater sofa with a throw that does not match it. Hallway mirror silvered at the corners. Shoes piled at the door. Poplar fluff (тополиный пух) drifting in through the window. Not a showroom. Not a styled Scandinavian interior.

**Location B — the barbershop.** A neighbourhood парикмахерская in a ground-floor unit of a panel block, twenty years old, now sold as a barbershop. Two chairs, one empty. Cracked leatherette chairs mended with tape. A wooden bench along the glass, five men on it. Wall clock, box fan on a shelf, clipper cords on a hook. Daylight through the front window, fluorescent tube overhead. No readable signage in frame. Not a hipster loft. Not neon. Not a set.

**Camera / Lens** — ARRI Alexa, 35mm spherical, T2. Small rooms, real skin. 35mm keeps the room around him and lets the background blur without flattening it.

**Grade / Post** — Cool green-teal shadows, lifted soft blacks, muted mids, one warm wood-and-rust accent. Moderate grain. Protect skin. No saturation boost. No green in wardrobe or props near the phone (spill on the key).

**Refusals**
Not a tutorial. Not a screen recording with a person pasted next to it. Not a slick tech ad. Not a smiling model holding a phone at the lens. No slow motion. No whoosh transitions. No glowing UI floating in air. No Moscow postcard (no Red Square, no onion domes).

## STEP 2 — BEAT SHEET

25.0s total. Cut on action.

**BEAT 1: THE OVERGROWN (0.0–4.0s)** — KEYFRAME (HERO)
Artyom stops at the hallway mirror. Flicks his fringe off his forehead. It falls back. Hard cut, 0.8s: a barbershop bench, five men, one asleep with his head back and mouth open, one staring at the wall clock. Cut back. Artyom exhales through his nose. Decides.
VISUAL: Medium, over his shoulder, his reflection sharper than he is.
EMOTION: Not vanity. Refusal of the bench.
CAMERA MOVE: Handheld push in, slow. Stop on the reflection's eyes.

**BEAT 2: THE TICKET (4.0–9.0s)** — KEYFRAME
Artyom drops onto the sofa. Thumb opens Meikigo. Scrolls the list of barbershops. Taps one. Taps "get queue number". The screen shows 1038.
VISUAL: Over the shoulder, phone low in frame. Screen is chroma green, brightest object.
EMOTION: Small win. He left the line without leaving the sofa.
CAMERA MOVE: Dolly in, slow, parallax preserved. Stop with the screen filling 60% of frame.

**BEAT 3: THE GAME (9.0–14.0s)** — KEYFRAME
Artyom opens a game. Thumbs going. Top of the screen: a live queue strip, "now serving 1029". He does not look up.
ARTYOM: «Пока жду — сыграю в игру.»
VISUAL: Low angle from the coffee table. Phone glow on his chin.
EMOTION: Contented smugness. He owns the waiting.
CAMERA MOVE: Static. Only the queue number changes (composited).

**BEAT 4: THE BUZZ (14.0–18.0s)** — KEYFRAME
Strip flips to "now serving 1035". Phone buzzes on the glass table. He grabs keys off the shoe pile, steps into sneakers, out the door.
VISUAL: Medium, then follow. Door closes on the frame.
EMOTION: Urgency, not panic. Timing is on his side.
CAMERA MOVE: Follow, handheld, medium speed. End on the closed door.

**BEAT 5: THE ARRIVAL (18.0–22.0s)** — KEYFRAME
Doorbell. Bench still full, same sleeper. The barber looks up, glasses on his head, clipper in mid-air.
BARBER: «Номер 1038? Проходите, садитесь.»
Artyom walks past the bench. Two men watch him go.
VISUAL: Wide, low, door-side. Artyom enters frame right. Bench left, chair centre.
EMOTION: Payoff. He is late to nothing.
CAMERA MOVE: Static. He enters the frame.

**BEAT 6: THE CUT (22.0–25.0s)** — KEYFRAME
Cape snaps open. Clippers start. Barber's hand on his crown. Artyom meets his own eyes in the mirror and half smiles. Hold 1s. Hard cut to the end card (composited).
END CARD: «Meikigo. Займи очередь — сохрани субботу.» ("Meikigo. Take a place in the queue — keep your Saturday.")
VISUAL: Close, mirror frame. Cape edge, clipper, his eye.
EMOTION: Warmth, earned.
CAMERA MOVE: Push in, slow. Stop on the half-smile.

Keyframes: 6. The 0.8s bench insert is a cut-only transition.

**Refusals:** Not a comedy of errors. No slapstick. No traffic sprint. No before/after haircut reveal. No pointing at the phone.
