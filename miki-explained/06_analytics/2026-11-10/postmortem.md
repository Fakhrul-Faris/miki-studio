## What the data says

### 1. The first-pool wall: 11 of 13 mature videos stopped at 626–740 views
TikTok shows each new video to a test pool of about 600–700 people. Only two passed it:

| | Views | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| EX14 IKEA maze | **1,973** | a brand Malaysians shop at and search ("ikea", "IKEA maze layout") |
| EX24 McDonald's ice cream machine | **1,078** | a brand + the question everyone has ("Why is it always broken?"); searched as "mcdonald's malaysia" |

Everything else (US banks, Marriott, .99, barbers, Kodak, Costco, the ER, the cinema) ended in the same band, whatever
the style. The only thing the two breakouts share is a **brand Malaysians use and search for, plus a question they
already had**.

### 2. Follows come from one format only
6 of the 8 new followers came from the two videos posted on 8 Nov under the new strategy:

| | Follows | Follows per 1k views | Likes | Avg watched |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| EX25 How is 99 Speedmart so cheap? (Style A) | 3 | **4.1** | **6.6%** | 39% of 32 s |
| EX24 McDonald's machine (Style A) | 3 | **2.8** | 4.1% | **40%** of 35 s |
| EX14 IKEA (iso v4) | 1 | 0.5 | 3.1% | 34% of 46 s |
| EX16 ER (iso v4) | 1 | 1.4 | 3.5% | 26% of 38 s |
| the other 9 mature videos | 0 | 0 | 0.9–4.8% | 19–30% |

**The 7 Nov strategy is working where it was applied:** a Malaysian-known brand, a "why / how is it so cheap" hook, and
Style A at 32–35 s. Follows per view were about 14 times the rest of the account's (3.3 vs 0.23 per 1k views). Two videos is a small sample,
but it is the only positive signal in the data.

### 3. Retention: everyone leaves in the first second
- **Drop-off point is 0:01–0:02 on all 17 videos.** The first second (frame 1 + the first word) decides the video;
  this hasn't changed since the 7 Nov baseline.
- **Average watch is 7–16 s.** As a share of length, the best are the Style A pair (39–40%) and IKEA (34%). Long
  videos hold the least: Kodak about 21% of 66 s, the gym 20% of 61 s, close-door 19% of 48 s.
- **Watched in full** is highest on the short ones: Marriott 13.8% (28 s), barbers 12.3%, the other line 10.5%.
- **30–35 s is the sweet spot for now.** Long-edu (60 s+) hasn't earned its length yet on this account. One exception:
  a timeline story whose hook holds can still be long (see the EX34 note in LESSONS §11).

### 4. Search is a real, small door, and it names the formula
- Search traffic was highest on **Costco 3.1%** ("costco malaysia", "costco in malaysia") and **McDonald's 2.3%**
  ("mcdonald's malaysia"). People search **"<brand> malaysia"**.
- **"meikigo"** is searched (99 Speedmart, banks): some viewers look the account up, but don't follow.
- The barber video is still found by "he made her wait 45 mins part 3 4 5": viewers expect a **series with parts**.

### 5. Engagement is passive
3 comments in 17 videos, all on McDonald's. Saves are low (14). Likes are healthy only on the brand videos. The
comment questions aren't pulling replies.

### 6. Audience (unchanged)
95–99% Malaysia, 57–71% male, 18–34 = 85–90%. 99–100% non-followers on every video: the account has no follower base
to seed new posts yet.

### 7. Style test (A vs B vs C): too early to call
EX26 (C), EX27 (B) and the Style A–C finals EX28–EX34 are either partial or not yet posted. The only mature
comparison is **Style A (EX24, EX25)**, the best on every rate, vs the retired cut-paper and the iso v4 episodes.
Re-pull on 12 Nov and again after EX28–EX34 have 48 hours each.

## What to change (recommendations)

1. **Post the queue in this order, Malaysian brands first:**
   - EX29 Mr DIY (billed as Part 2 of "How is X so cheap?")
   - EX30 Mixue
   - EX31 Grab
   - EX33 Ray-Ban/Oakley (A-Look)
   - EX32 HP printer ink
   - EX28 Apple charger
   - EX34 AirAsia

   Shelve the remaining old stock on US and European topics (EX20 Ronald Read, EX22 freezer hack, EX23 David Jones):
   they fit the pattern that stalls at the first pool.
2. **Every new topic passes a two-part test:**
   - is it a brand or place Malaysians use and **search for**?
   - does the hook ask the **question they already have** ("Why is…", "How is … so cheap?")?

   If either answer is no, don't make it now.
3. **Win the first second.**
   - Frame 1 shows the real logo or product, already moving, with the question as on-screen text at 0.0 s.
   - The voice starts on the brand name: no "In 2001", no throat-clearing.
4. **Length: 30–35 s** for "how it makes money" shorts. Go longer only for a story whose hook has proven itself.
5. **Captions for search:** the first line contains "<brand> Malaysia" (and "Meikigo" stays in the bio and pinned comment).
6. **Series, by name:** "How is X so cheap? Part 2 / Part 3". Put a binary choice in the pinned comment ("Part 3: Daiso
   or KK Mart?"): viewers already search "part 3 4 5".
7. **Cadence (my judgement, the data can't prove it):** while every post stops at the first pool, a second daily post
   from the old stock adds a low-retention signal and no reach. Post 1–2 a day **only from the new-strategy queue**
   until one breaks out.
8. **Re-pull data on 12 Nov.** Compare EX26 (C) and EX27 (B) once they are 72 hours old, and log every post's 24 h and
   72 h numbers in the dashboard (`/stats`).
