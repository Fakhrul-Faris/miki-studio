happens through a breakout. **Every post is aimed at the one pattern that has produced reach and follows.**

## 2. Audience (stable across both exports)
- **95–99% Malaysia**, 57–71% male, 18–34 = 85–90%.
- 99–100% non-followers: there is no follower base to seed a new post.
- English narration works for them (no language complaint, likes in line with local creators' small accounts).

## 3. What the data says works (evidence)

| Finding | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Brands Malaysians use and search for, with the question they already have, break the first pool.** Everything else stops at about 650 views | IKEA 1,973 and McDonald's 1,078 vs 11 of 13 others at 626–740 |
| **The Malaysian-brand "why / how is it so cheap?" Style A shorts are the only ones that earn follows** | EX25 99 Speedmart 4.1 follows/1k, 6.6% likes; EX24 McDonald's 2.8/1k; the rest about 0.2/1k |
| **30–35 s holds best.** Long videos lose most of their audience | EX24/EX25 held 39–40% of their length; 60 s+ videos about 20% |
| **The first second decides everything** | the drop-off point is 0:01–0:02 on every video in both exports |
| **People search "<brand> malaysia" and "meikigo"** | Costco 3.1% search traffic ("costco malaysia"), McDonald's "mcdonald's malaysia"; "meikigo" searched on two videos |
| **Viewers expect series with parts** | "he made her wait 45 mins part 3 4 5" still finds the barber video |
| **Comments don't come by themselves** | 3 comments in 17 videos, all on McDonald's (a "name the branch" question) |
| **A stalled video never recovers** | the same videos 7 → 10 Nov: +3 to +12 views |

## 4. How TikTok ranks (platform research, Nov 2026)
- **Watch time and completion rate are the strongest signals** (about 40–50% of the weight). Watch time in the first
  few seconds matters most; then shares and saves; likes are moderate ([Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/tiktok-algorithm/),
  [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/tiktok-algorithm), [go-viral](https://www.go-viral.app/blog/tiktok-algorithm-2026/)).
- A video is tested on a small pool first and pushed wider only if that pool completes, saves and shares. This is our
  600–700 wall. Small creators break out through completion, not follower count (often quoted: ~70% completion).
  We are at 10–14% full views and 20–40% average.
- **Keywords in the caption and the on-screen text** tell TikTok which first audience to test on, and they feed TikTok
  search ([imark: search signals](https://www.imarkinfotech.com/how-tiktok-search-algorithm-actually-works-in-2026-the-10-signals-that-decide-ranking/)).
- Malaysian TikTok 2026 is dominated by entertainment, food and TikTok Shop LIVE
  ([Favikon](https://www.favikon.com/blog/top-tiktok-influencers-malaysia), [The Rakyat Post](https://www.therakyatpost.com/news/2026/10/29/tiktok-shop-live-becomes-digital-shopfront-for-malaysian-msmes-as-local-sellers-record-140-sales-growth/)).
  English business explainers about local brands are an open lane, not a crowded one.

## 5. The rules for every post (decisions)
1. **Topic test**, both answers must be yes:
   - is it a brand or place Malaysians use and **search for**?
   - does the hook ask the **question they already have**?
2. **First second:**
   - the real logo or product on screen at frame 1, already moving;
   - the question as on-screen text from 0.0 s;
   - the voice starts on the brand's name (no "In 2001…").
3. **Length 30–35 s** for the brand shorts, about 70 spoken words. Longer only once a story has a proven hook.
4. **Caption:** the first line is the searchable question with "<brand> Malaysia"; 3–5 hashtags (brand, #malaysia,
   #bisnes, #businessmodel); "Meikigo" stays in the bio.
5. **Series by name:**
   - "How is X so cheap?": Part 1 99 Speedmart, Part 2 Mr DIY, Part 3 next.
   - The end line promises the next part.
   - The pinned comment is a two-option vote for the next one.
6. **Comment bait that works:** ask the viewer to name something local ("which branch…", "which one next…").
7. **Cadence:** 1–2 posts a day, **only new-strategy episodes**. No more old stock on US or European topics (EX20, EX22,
   EX23 are shelved).
8. **Style:** Style A (EX29 baseline) is the only style with mature, positive numbers. Style B (EX33 baseline) and
   Style C are still being measured.
9. **Voice:** Kokoro am_michael (the user prefers it). Spell brand names so they're said right (e.g. "ringghit").
