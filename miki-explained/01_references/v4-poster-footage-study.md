# V4 study: real footage + constructivist paper poster (r43, r44)

Added 2026-11-01. User: "I really really want Meikigo content to look like this… real video media which are easy to obtain,
and the paper look and motion animation everything in 2.mp4 is really what I want." Status: **idea, awaiting approval.**

## r43 real-footage montage (`r43-real-footage-montage/`, 16:9, 58 s)
- **122 cuts in 58 s (≈ 0.5 s a shot).** News clips, launch events, product B-roll, factory footage, and **film scenes used as
  reactions** (a character's face on "here's the thing", a money scene on "make").
- Black ground; footage full-bleed or as **floating photo cards** that pop in around the caption (1–5 at once, a collage grid).
- **Word-by-word captions**, small, lowercase, centred, building the phrase; a white label card for names ("Elon Musk").
- Feels like a fast, confident essay: every word gets a new picture.

## r44 constructivist morph (`r44-constructivist-morph/`, 9:16, 59 s) — the look
- **Only 2 hard cuts in 59 s.** It's one continuous camera: whip-rotations into deep perspective (rows of desks to a vanishing
  point), push-ins, and **morph / zoom-through transitions**: a skull grows headphones, the headphones open into a hole, the hole
  becomes an auditorium; a dropped book opens to show the next scene.
- **Look:** Russian constructivist / Bauhaus poster, El Lissitzky–Rodchenko: **three colours only (red, black, cream)**,
  diagonals, circles, mass repetition of tiny figures, flat paper planes staged in depth with soft shadows and paper grain.
- **Motion:** paper planes slide and rotate in 3D; objects animate physically (a book falls and opens, rings pulse out of an ear).
  It reads as AI image-to-video morphs between designed keyframes (first frame → last frame), held to one palette.
- **One word at a time, big, centred** (white, heavy, black outline), cut on the voice.

## What the two share
Voice-led, a new image every word or two, single-word captions in the centre, and a strict look held across the film.

## The gap to our current format
Ours: cut-paper collage, ochre accent, mostly still plates moved in EffectCraft/Blender, subtitles in strips, cuts every
2–5 s. Theirs: continuous morphing camera (r44) or a picture every half second (r43), and real footage (r43).
