# r45 — gopcb.ai drone arming-key explainer (Instagram, 95.7 s)

User, 2026-11-03: "i want us to approach this level of render look aesthetic, motion and camera move." Analysed frame by
frame (720 × 1280, 30 fps, 2,866 frames). Contact sheets in `contact_sheets/`.

## Measured
- **No hard cuts**: scene detection (threshold 0.25) found none. Every change is a camera move, a material wipe or a
  fly-through.
- **Motion** (mean absolute frame difference, grey, 180 × 320): median 1.2, mean 3.3. Many seconds sit at 0.3–0.9 (slow
  push or arc under a caption); short spikes of 0.3–1.5 s at turns (3.9 s ring wipe peaks at 32; 15.3–16.5 s fly-through
  at 13–18; 73 s pull-out to the product at 19.7).

## Look
- Perspective camera; short lens close in (copper rails race toward the lens), longer and calmer on wides; shallow depth
  of field on close-ups (foreground copper blurred).
- Realistic materials: brushed dark metal shell, **clear glass shell for x-ray**, frosted translucent "ice" blocks (cyan,
  lit from inside), copper and gold with reflections, detailed PCB.
- Studio set: dark blue-grey gradient backdrop, technical grid floor with reflections, key + rim light, soft shadows,
  bloom on emissive rings.
- Palette: steel blue-grey + one cyan accent + warm copper/gold; red only for warnings.

## Cinematography
- **Shot sizes swing**: extreme close-up (key slot, a capacitor ring) → medium (open body) → wide reveal (whole drone,
  then drone + laptop). New term = close-up; "so what" = wide.
- **Three planes in every frame**: a foreground element (glass edge, propeller, wires) / subject / soft background. The
  moves create parallax between them.
- **Angles**: low three-quarter hero on the product; looking down leading lines in diagrams (bus bars, canal walls run
  diagonally into frame). No flat front-on shots, no dutch.
- **Motivated moves**: the camera goes where the sentence goes ("Pull it out" → pull back as the key leaves; "twelve
  capacitors" → push in, rings light; "cut to a coast" → wide; "a battery that big" → fly through the glass).
- **Rhythm**: held push/arc 1–3 s with an easing tail → one fast swoop at the turn → hold.
- **9:16 composition**: subject in the middle band; top = glowing label, bottom = caption, corner = watermark.
- **Light as direction**: rim separates subject from the set; a glowing outline ring points at the thing; the analogy
  (canal locks) switches to warm sunset light with the same low leading-line camera.

## Transitions (tools)
- **Ring x-ray wipe** (3.9 s): a glowing outline ring grows from the key slot; the dark shell turns to glass behind it.
- **Fly-through** (15.3 s): the camera dives through the glass into the battery pack.
- **Match move to an analogy set** (canal locks, warm light), same camera language.
- **Energy as light**: current runs along copper as a glowing streak.

## Text
Top label: bold white with a soft glow ("50 V", "up to 380 A", "precharge resistors"). Captions: dark rounded box,
current word underlined. Small watermark top right.
