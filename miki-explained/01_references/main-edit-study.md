# Main Edit.mp4 — the clay maquette reference, studied properly

Source: `04_episodes/Main Edit.mp4` (576×1024, 30 fps, 76.1 s). First study (EX20, 2026-11-04) copied framing and colour
only. Second study (2026-11-05, after the user's note) adds what moves, how it cuts, and the camera grammar.

## What moves and what doesn't (the point the first study missed)

- **People never move.** Every figure holds one pose for the whole shot: the woman on the scooter, the red figure in
  the stairwell, the white police figures crouched over the sewer covers. Progress between poses happens **across a
  cut**, or is shown by a **dashed path** drawn to where the next pose stands. Reason (user): believable human motion is
  hard and slides straight into the uncanny valley; a held pose reads as a model, which is the whole aesthetic.
- **What does move:** the camera (slow), dashed route lines drawing on, objects appearing (cars in the garage, rooms in
  the plan), light (a pool of light opening on the sewer covers, a dark frame for "stranger"), and the map's marker.

## Cutting

- **15 hard cuts in 76 s**: a new shot every 4.8 s on average (median 4.5 s, longest 11.9 s). Hard cuts are the main
  transition; there are no long continuous camera journeys between sets.
- Each shot is one idea and one move. The cut lands on the spoken word that changes the idea.
- Opens on a **real aerial photo** of the building, then cuts to the same building as a clay model at the same angle:
  the photo proves it's real, and the model takes over the telling.

## Camera grammar

- **Slow, short moves inside a shot**: a gentle push, a crane of a few metres, a slow drift. Never a swoop.
- **Telephoto frontal elevations** for x-ray buildings: flat, symmetrical, the tall 9:16 frame filled by the tower.
- **Dollhouse high 3/4** for rooms with the roof off; the route drawn on the floor.
- **Vertical shaft / stairwell frames** that use the full height of 9:16.
- **Macro inserts** with shallow focus on the one detail that matters (the key in the scooter).
- **Map mode**: grey satellite-like map, a thin dark route, one red marker, tiny place labels.
- **Exploded view**: the tower's floors pulled apart ("all over again").
- **Light as narration**: darkness, then a soft pool of light on the subject; the frame goes almost black on a dark line.

## Type

Bebas-style uppercase, two to four words a phrase, centred low, one key word in the accent colour.

## Rules carried into the house style

1. Figures: poses only (memory `figures-pose-only`).
2. Shot every ~3–6 s, hard cut on the word, one move per shot, slow.
3. Use the reference's shot types on purpose: photo-to-model, frontal telephoto, dollhouse, shaft, macro, map, exploded.
4. Light and dashed paths carry the action that people would otherwise act out.
