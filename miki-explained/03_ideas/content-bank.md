# Content bank

Every Miki Explained idea in one place: single stories, new series, and series pitches. Pick from here, verify, then
promote to an episode folder (`04_episodes/EXNN_slug/`) and fill the episode column.

**Status key:** 🔍 = a fact still to verify against a primary source (annual report, filing, regulator, original study)
· ☐ not verified · ✓ verified · **next #n** = queued · EXNN = made.

**How an idea becomes an episode:** verify the facts → script on reveal → contrast → urgency → proof → shot list with all
three ingredients (collage, documentary image, diagram) → `how-i-generate.md` → build. See the `miki-explained-episode` skill.

Editing, camera and animation tests (one per episode): `03_ideas/experiments.md`.

## 3. Series pitches

| Series | Idea | Why people watch | Example episodes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Designed on Purpose** | Everyday things that feel random but were designed to change what you do | "Ohh, that's why" | Prices ending in .99; why lift "close door" buttons may do nothing; why casinos have no clocks; why IKEA's path is a maze |
| **The Number** | One number that explains a whole business | Quick, shareable, a single big reveal | Chair-hours a barber sells a year; how many customers a shop needs a day to break even; how much a no-show really costs |
| **Myth or Math** | Popular business "facts", checked | Comments argue, which drives reach; the series earns trust | "The customer is always right" (where it came from); "it takes 5× more to win a new customer"; the milk-at-the-back claim |
| **The Invisible Queue** | Queues you don't see, and how the best places run them | Everyone waits somewhere | Emergency-room triage; airport security lanes; restaurant waitlists; theme-park virtual queues |
| **Before It Broke** | A famous business that failed, told through one fixable system | Drama with a lesson | Blockbuster late fees; Kodak and the digital camera; a chain that grew too fast (verify each) |

Each series keeps the house format (collage + documentary image + diagram), the reveal → contrast → urgency → proof script,
and ends inside a shop.
