# Content bank

Every Miki Explained idea in one place: single stories, new series, and series pitches. Pick from here, verify, then
promote to an episode folder (`04_episodes/EXNN_slug/`) and fill the episode column.

**Status key:** 🔍 = a fact still to verify against a primary source (annual report, filing, regulator, original study)
· ☐ not verified · ✓ verified · **next #n** = queued · EXNN = made.

**How an idea becomes an episode:** verify the facts → script on reveal → contrast → urgency → proof → shot list with all
three ingredients (collage, documentary image, diagram) → `how-i-generate.md` → build. See the `miki-explained-episode` skill.

Editing, camera and animation tests (one per episode): `03_ideas/experiments.md`.
