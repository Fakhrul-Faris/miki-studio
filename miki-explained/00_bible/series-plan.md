# Series plan

## Format

- Under 60 seconds, 9:16, TikTok + Instagram Reels. Each post stands alone; no "Part 1/2".
- No series name. Recognition comes from the look (the cut-paper house format, `visual-system.md`) and the voice.
  The named concept gets its own title card in the takeaway (EX02V3: THE DOORWAY DECISION).
- Every episode mixes collage, 2–3 documentary images and diagram animation, and runs about 40–45s, cut to the voice.
- One new story every post, same skeleton every time. (The Belzona model: never the same repair,
  always the same shape.)
- Meikigo appears only as the problem in the shop turn; a small logo end card is optional (EX02V3 ended on black).
  No product shot, no "download now". The CTA lives in the pinned comment.

## Script formula

See `00_bible/script-formula.md`: **Hook → Stakes → Mechanism → Twist → Payoff → Bridge ("now picture your shop") → Loop.**
Replaces the Hook/Setup/Guess/Reveal/Turn/Takeaway skeleton above (adopted 2026-10-31). Length is a tested variable
(`03_ideas/experiments.md` § 8), not fixed at 55s.

## Why it makes people feel clever

1. Open with a puzzle: something doesn't add up.
2. Let them guess before the twist.
3. Reveal a system, never "a bad person".
4. Connect unrelated things (airport ↔ barbershop).
5. Leave them one sentence they can retell.

## Rules

- **Every story ends inside a shop.** The story can start anywhere; the lesson lands on a service counter.
  Default; whether to keep it is itself tested from EX05 on (`03_ideas/experiments.md` § 9 Bridge presence) — when off,
  the shop tie-in moves to the caption and pinned comment instead of the narration.
- **Verify every fact** and list sources in the episode file. One debunked claim kills the format.
- **No local scandals with names.** Malaysian defamation law and CMA s.233 make it dangerous. Keep
  scandals foreign, historical and well documented; local stories use composites.
- **The villain is a system, never a person** — especially never staff.
- **Alternate tone.** Don't run dark lanes (Incentive Backfires, Autopsy) back to back.

## Instagram extras

- Post the same video as a Reel.
- Make a 7-slide carousel from the same diagrams: hook → stakes → mechanism → twist → payoff →
  bridge (your shop) → loop (question).
- Pinned comment on both: "Want us to check your shop for the Wait Tax? DM 'CASE'." (Adjust the
  keyword to each episode's concept.)

## Sound rules

- **Key moments only:** about 8–10 hits (stamps, a snip, a paper tear, a relay click when a light changes, one swell into
  the title). The voice and a low drone bed carry the rest; music, if any, sits at about −23 dB under everything.
- The user supplies the sound files; trim each to its hit and line the hit up with its frame (`sfx/events.txt`).
- **A Meikigo logo outro, if used, is silent.** No sound effect on the logo, ever.

## Story balance

- **At least every third episode is a Malaysian story** (payments, local shop rhythms, a local business). Foreign stories
  are fine, but owners must feel the series is about them. EX01 and EX02 were foreign, so EX03 is Malaysian.
- Rotate lanes; never two of the same lane in a row.

## Named concepts (the glossary)

Every takeaway coins one term. Keep them in `00_bible/glossary.md`. After 5–6 episodes, turn the glossary into a carousel
or pinned post, so the series is bingeable and owners get shorthand they repeat.

## Before posting (checklist)

1. Every fact has a source in `episode.md`; open questions are resolved or the line is cut.
2. Source credit chip on screen in the hook, and the source in the pinned comment.
3. **Turn on the platform's AI-generated content label** if the episode uses generated photos.
4. Subtitles hidden where the same words are already on screen.
4b. TikTok safe area checked: every word and key subject inside x 60–960, y 160–1440 (`visual-system.md` § TikTok safe area).
5. Watched once at full speed with sound; any logo outro is silent.
