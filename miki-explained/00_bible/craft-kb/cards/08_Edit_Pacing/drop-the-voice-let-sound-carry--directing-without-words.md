## METADATA
08_Edit_Pacing | StudioBinder video essay (Portrait of a Lady on Fire, Beau Travail, Sabotage) | shot list + sound notes

## CREATIVE_INTENT
"Show don't tell is a golden rule", yet since sound arrived "many filmmakers have used dialogue as a crutch". "You can
relay an entire conversation's worth of information with just a look, a shot, or a cut." In Sabotage "when the dialogue
cuts out… the drama really kicks in"; in Portrait of a Lady on Fire, singers give "a rousing diegetic soundtrack"
underscoring the rising feeling; in Beau Travail the score's crescendos lift the tension. "The dialogue is just the
cherry on top."

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Find the one line whose meaning the picture can show alone.
2. Cut that line's words, or open a 0.8–1.5 s gap before it.
3. Fill the gap with a sound that exists in the scene (till drawer, kettle, coin drop).
4. Place a visible event in the gap so the screen keeps moving.
5. Keep only one silent beat per episode.
6. Re-check total runtime and word count after the cut.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Voice text: mark the gap with "…" (user records) or leave it for the edit; shot list row: "VOICE GAP 1.0 s — <event>,
SFX: <diegetic sound>". The gap still counts toward runtime.
### Worked example
Topic: a kedai's daily cash count (illustrative).
BEFORE: L09 "Dan bila dikira, jumlah jualan hari ni ialah RM1,240."
AFTER: L09 "Kira." → 1.2 s no voice: notes flip onto the counter one by one, real note-flick sound; the total "RM1,240"
grows in accent at the end of the gap → L10 "Tapi untung dia… RM180 je."
### Check
- Is there exactly one voice gap, 0.8–1.5 s?
- Is the gap filled with a diegetic sound, not a stinger?
- Does a visible event happen during the gap?
