## CREATIVE_INTENT
Principle (step 5): you're "95 % done" but it can be better. Polish takes a lot of time for a small benefit, yet it's
where higher and lower budget work separates (feature film vs kids' show). Watch frame by frame; fix glitches and
collisions that don't feel right; polish secondary motion; **remove things that don't work**. Then give the finished
shot proper background, lighting, VFX and sound design.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Step through the preview frame by frame at each setup join and each event word.
2. Fix any interpenetration (coin through tray, camera through a wall, tag through the object).
3. Fix any pop, jitter or one-frame flash.
4. Add or refine one secondary motion per setup where the hero is static.
5. Remove any element or move that doesn't work.
6. Finish with the light, material and sound pass (RECIPE-D §5.1).

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Log polish findings by setup and frame in the episode notes (`S7 f412: tag clips the receipt edge → raise 2 mm`). Use the
animatic HUD tag "S<setup> · <time>" to locate frames.

### Worked example
Preview of a how-to on splitting a RM60 dinner bill four ways (RM15 each, plain arithmetic):
- BEFORE: approved preview sent straight to the 60 fps final; S4's RM15 tags clip through the plate rim on two frames.
- AFTER: `S4 f233–234: tags clip plate rim → lift 3 mm; S8: receipt static 3 s → add a slow curl settle; S10: remove stray dust card` → final render.

### Check
- Has every setup join and event word been stepped frame by frame?
- Any collision, pop or one-frame flash left?
- Was at least one non-working element removed or fixed?
