## CREATIVE_INTENT
After storyboard and style frames are approved, the studio sends an **animation test, usually about 5 seconds**, before
"going full blast", because "the last thing you want is you've made about 30 seconds worth of animation only to find out
that the client doesn't like it".

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before animating all rows, pick the 2 consecutive setups that carry the episode's signature move (often the hook).
2. Animate only those ~5 s at final look, timing and sound.
3. Show the test to the user; wait for approval.
4. Apply the approved motion feel (easing, speed, event density) to all remaining rows.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Mark the test rows in the shot list (`test: rows 1–2`). Render a short preview clip of those rows only; log the user's
notes as rules for the rest of the build.

### Worked example
BEFORE: 60 s preview built, user rejects the camera speed in every shot.
AFTER (S16 how-to, gerai QR): test = rows 1–2 — macro QR stand, flight down to phone screen as "Scan je" lands (≈5 s);
user asks for slower landing; landing ease applied to all 22 rows before they're built.

### Check
- Was a ~5 s test of the signature move approved before the full build?
- Is the test at final look (not grey box)?
- Were the user's notes carried into all remaining rows?
