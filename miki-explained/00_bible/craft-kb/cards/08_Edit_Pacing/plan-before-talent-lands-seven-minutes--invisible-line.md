## METADATA

## CREATIVE_INTENT
On a real set, "you've got like 7 minutes of actual work" once the talent actually lands and the lighting is "90%
roughed in" — everything else (getting lamps in place, roughing in the mood) happens beforehand, with no one waiting.
The lesson: front-load every decision you can before the moment that's expensive to redo, and reserve the live/final
pass for tweaking, not for deciding.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Identify the expensive/final step in the pipeline (the moment equivalent to "talent lands" — e.g. the full render,
   or the final preview review).
2. Before that step, rough in every decision as completely as possible (board, camera map, lighting) so nothing is
   discovered only at that final stage.
3. Reserve the final pass for small, reactive adjustments only (tweak a value, nudge a timing) — not for choosing a
   setup or a light from scratch.
4. If the final pass keeps surfacing new decisions (not just tweaks), the front-loaded planning stage was skipped or
   incomplete — go back and fix the gate, don't keep patching live.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Maps directly onto the shot-first gates already in use: everything through the grey-box board and check `.blend`
is the "getting lamps in place" stage; the preview-before-render review is the "7 minutes" — only tweak values there,
never invent a new setup or light at that point.

### Worked example
Topic: any episode's preview review.
BEFORE: the preview review surfaces "this setup needs a completely different light" or "this shot needs a different
angle" — a planning-stage decision discovered too late.
AFTER: those decisions were made and checked at the grey-box board stage; the preview review only adjusts timing,
colour level and minor nudges — no new setups invented at that point.

### Check
- Were all setup/lighting/camera decisions made and checked before the final preview pass?
- Does the final pass only adjust existing values, never invent a new setup?
- If a new decision surfaces late, is the earlier gate fixed rather than patched live?
