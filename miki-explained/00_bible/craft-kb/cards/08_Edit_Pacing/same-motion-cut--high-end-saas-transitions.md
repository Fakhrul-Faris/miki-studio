## CREATIVE_INTENT
The cut transition is a "deliberate flow breaker" for moments when viewers have "mentally checked out" — an abrupt,
intentional cut snaps them back. Same-motion variant: text entered left→right in scene one; after the cut, apply
"that exact same left-to-right motion to the text there". "The shared motion makes your brain feel that this is one
continuous sequence instead of two unrelated scenes." It built intensity before the product reveal.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Find a join where A and B share nothing else.
2. Give A's last moving element a clear direction (e.g., left→right).
3. Give B's first moving element the exact same direction and speed.
4. Cut on the word, with the eye still on the moving element.
5. Use it before a reveal or after a long steady stretch.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Shot list: "join: cut-motion L→R". raea/HyperFrames: copy the same `interpolate` range and easing to both elements;
place B's at the matching screen height.

### Worked example
Topic: "Kenapa kedai tutup walaupun ramai pelanggan" (generic cash-flow arithmetic, illustrative).
- BEFORE: L07 receipt text fades; cut; L08 a bill appears.
- AFTER: L07 "Jualan RM8,000" slides L→R across a receipt; cut on "tapi"; L08 "Kos RM8,500" slides L→R at the same height and speed over a pile of bills, accent red.

### Check
- Is the direction and speed identical on both sides?
- Is the element at the same screen height?
- Is the cut on a word?
