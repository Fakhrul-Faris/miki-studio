## CREATIVE_INTENT
"In *13th*, Ava DuVernay uses silence to give viewers space to absorb devastating information, which makes it land
harder. A sudden drop to silence before a key moment signals that something big is coming." Also: ambient layers (wind,
industrial noise) build atmosphere; "audiences often feel sound before they consciously register it."

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Pick one line per episode as the reveal.
2. Fade the music/ambience bed to silence over ~3–6 frames, ~0.5 s before that line (`~`).
3. Keep the voice and the visual event; nothing else plays.
4. Hold silence ~0.3–0.5 s after the number lands (`~`), then bring the bed back over ~0.5 s.
5. Use it once per episode.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Shot-list sound note on the reveal row: `bed → silence 0.5 s before; back 0.4 s after "RM…"`. Voice processing keeps the
pause natural (voice_fix --plain trims pauses — leave this one at ≥ 0.3 s).

### Worked example
Topic: "Gerai cendol — untung sebenar sebulan" (illustrative).
- BEFORE: bed runs flat under L09 "Untung bersih RM2,400 sebulan."
- AFTER: `| 9 | "Untung bersih…RM2,400 sebulan." | subtle push on the bowl | bed drops to silence 0.5 s before "untung"; counter stops at RM2,400 in silence; bed returns 0.4 s after | silence-drop-before-the-reveal |`

### Check
- Is the silence used once, before the main reveal?
- Does the picture keep an event going during the silence?
- Is the post-reveal pause protected from pause trimming?

- Values in seconds are inferred (`~`); the article gives none.
