## CREATIVE_INTENT
In the "$950 breakdown" video a swallow flies across as a connecting element between two different scenes; "with the
right timing, it feels completely seamless".

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Pick an object that belongs to the story of both setups.
2. Move it across A toward the frame edge (or toward the lens).
3. Cut while it is mid-crossing (it covers the join).
4. Continue its path into B at the same speed and direction, and let it settle on B's subject.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Shot list: "join: connector — parcel travels L→R". raea: same object in both compositions; positions continuous across
the cut frame; settle with `Easing.out`.

### Worked example
Topic: "Kos hantar barang online — siapa tanggung?" (illustrative RM8 postage).
- BEFORE: shop table → cut → customer's door.
- AFTER: a small parcel slides off the seller's table L→R; cut mid-slide; it continues across a map card and lands at a door mat with an "RM8" tag in accent on "pos RM8".

### Check
- Is the connector part of both setups' story?
- Is the cut placed mid-crossing?
- Do speed and direction match on both sides?
