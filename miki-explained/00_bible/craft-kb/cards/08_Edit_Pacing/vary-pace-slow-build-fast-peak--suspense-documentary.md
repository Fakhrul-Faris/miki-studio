## METADATA
08_Edit_Pacing | general (documentary) | shot-list durations

## CREATIVE_INTENT
"Quick cuts in high-stakes moments heighten urgency, while slower editing builds a creeping tension" (*Free Solo*'s fast
cuts during the climbs). "Do vary your pacing. Slower builds make the explosive moments land harder."

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Mark the high-stakes stretch (usually the 2–3 lines before the payoff).
2. Give the build setups ~3–4 s each.
3. Give the high-stakes setups ~2 s each (one per phrase).
4. Hold the payoff setup longer (~3 s) so the number reads.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Shot-list: a `dur` column; check the duration curve: long → short → one long hold.

### Worked example
Topic: "Bayar minimum kad kredit — berapa lama nak habis?" (illustrative arithmetic).
- BEFORE: 24 setups all 2.5 s.
- AFTER: L03–L05 build at 3.5 s; L06–L08 ("RM5,000… bayar RM150 sebulan… faedah makan separuh") at 2 s each; L09 payoff "Hampir 4 tahun" held 3 s.

### Check
- Do setups shorten into the high-stakes stretch?
- Is the payoff held long enough to read?
- Is pace varied by duration only (no flashy transitions)?

## NOTES
- Example arithmetic assumes 18 % p.a. (1.5 %/month): first month interest RM75 = half of RM150; payoff ≈ 47 months ("hampir 4 tahun"). Recompute for the actual rate before use.
