## METADATA
08_Edit_Pacing | general editing (source) | shot-list review + animatic pass

## CREATIVE_INTENT
"When every shot has a crazy transition, none of them stand out — you don't give it room to shine." Learning a new
transition tempts you to use it everywhere; too much feels like "the worst roller coaster". The problem may not be a bad
transition but one you **didn't need**. Plugins, templates and overlays "just made my work feel emptier". Rule (Saint-
Exupéry): "Perfection is achieved not when there's nothing more to add but when there's nothing left to take away" —
if a transition stays, it has to **earn its spot**.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. After the shot list is drafted, count the featured joins (push-through, pull-reveal, object morph, match cut).
2. Keep featured joins to ~1 per 3–4 setups (~); the rest are plain same-set flights or cuts on the word.
3. Give the featured joins to the beats that matter: hook → open loop, the mechanism reveal, the payoff, the loop to frame 0.
4. Never repeat the same featured join on adjacent setups.
5. For each remaining featured join, ask "nothing left to take away?" — remove it if the film loses nothing.
6. Replay the animatic; if a join draws attention to itself rather than the line, downgrade it to a plain flight.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Add a column note "★" to featured joins in the shot list and list the count under the table: `Featured joins: 5 / 20 setups (hook, L03 reveal, L06 morph, L09 payoff, loop)`. In the camera map pass, flag any two consecutive ★.

### Worked example
EX-style how-to, a kedai gunting rambut in Ipoh pricing a RM15 haircut (illustrative):
- BEFORE: 18 setups, 11 joins with a push-through or morph; the RM15 → RM12 reveal is just another push-through and is lost.
- AFTER: `Featured joins: 4 / 18` — hook (push through the price board into the scissors), L04 morph (scissors → coin stack), L08 pull back to reveal the RM12 rival board ★, loop to frame 0; all others plain flights cut on the word.

### Check
- Are featured joins ≤ ~1 per 3–4 setups?
- Does each featured join sit on a key beat (hook, reveal, payoff, loop)?
- Are any two adjacent joins both featured or the same type?
- Can any featured join be removed without losing meaning? If yes, remove it.

## NOTES
- The ~1 per 3–4 ratio is inferred (source gives no number); tune against the animatic.
- Source mentions plugins/templates/overlays as "thousands of dollars wasted" — consistent with our rule to build joins from the scene's own objects.
