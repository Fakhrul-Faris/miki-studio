## CREATIVE_INTENT
"A match cut refers to cutting between two shots with similar compositions." In *Tarzan* a match cut shows "the
universality of a mother's love"; in *Grease* it shows "the stark contrast between fantasy and reality". Similar
composition → the viewer reads the two as related, then sees the difference.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before the cut, name what the two shots equate or contrast.
2. Find a shared shape (circle: coin / plate / clock; rectangle: receipt / phone / banknote).
3. Place the shape at the same screen position and size in both shots (±5 %, `~`).
4. Match the camera angle as closely as possible.
5. Cut on the word that names the second thing.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Shot-list join: `match: coin (A) ↔ clock face (B), centre, 40 % width`. Board check: overlay A's last and B's first frame;
the shape outlines should coincide.

### Worked example
Topic: "Kopi RM12 sehari — berapa setahun?" (illustrative arithmetic).
- BEFORE: `| 8 | "Setahun, RM4,380" | wide shot of a pile of cash |`
- AFTER: `| 7 | "Secawan RM12…" | top-down cup, round rim centred at 40 % width | — |` → `| 8 | "…setahun, RM4,380" | top-down round wall clock, same position and size; hands sweep | "RM4,380" (12 × 365) fades in | composition-match-cut-to-compare |`

### Check
- Is the shared shape at the same position and size across the cut?
- Does the cut land on the word naming the second thing?
- Does the pair say what the line says (equate or contrast)?
