## METADATA
08_Edit_Pacing | Evan Abrams | shot list (cost column)

## CREATIVE_INTENT
"The script is not just direction and description — pre-production is planning." From the video column you should know
how hard each idea is: "How hard will it be to create a three-dimensional Lou character and have the camera orbit around
him? Is orbiting around Lou super important? Maybe we can use a simpler concept." The AV script becomes a map: "we're
gonna have to drive over this mountain… or it'll be a nice leisurely drive." Choose the route that reaches the emotional
impact "with the resources we have on hand", and use it to estimate hours and money. At the word stage you can still go
bigger or smaller.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. For each row, list the assets the Setup cell needs.
2. Tag each row S (existing GLB/kit/past build), M (new library model or new UI screen) or L (custom model, simulation, complex morph).
3. For every L row, ask whether the expensive part is what the line is about.
4. If not, replace it with a simpler setup that shows the same thing.
5. Keep L rows for the hook and the payoff; cap them at ~2 per episode (~).
6. Re-total; list the M/L assets under the table as the build list.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Add a "cost" column to Shot list v1 (removed for the user gate if they prefer). Check past episode build/ folders and
the raea kit (`tools/raea/kit/`: ui.tsx, icons.tsx, Captions.tsx…) before tagging anything M or L.

### Worked example
Topic: a nasi lemak stall's cost per pack (illustrative).
BEFORE: | 6 | "Sambal je makan RM1.20 sebungkus." | camera orbits a full 3D stall with steam sim and moving crowd | L |
AFTER: | 6 | same line | macro: a spoon of sambal on a banana leaf, top light; on "RM1.20" a price tag slides in | S |
(the orbit and crowd weren't what the line was about; the cost moves to L01's hook, where the stall is the subject.)

### Check
- Does every row have an S/M/L tag?
- Is every L row about what its line says?
- Are there ≤ ~2 L rows, placed on hook/payoff?
- Was the kit/past builds checked before tagging M/L?

## NOTES
- The S/M/L scale and the ~2 cap are ours; the source gives the principle, not values.
