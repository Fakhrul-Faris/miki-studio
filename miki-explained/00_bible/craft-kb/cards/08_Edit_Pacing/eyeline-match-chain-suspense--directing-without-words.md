## METADATA
08_Edit_Pacing | StudioBinder video essay (Hitchcock, Sabotage) | shot-list sequence before a reveal

## CREATIVE_INTENT
In Sabotage, once the dialogue cuts out "the drama really kicks in", "told entirely through reaction, eyeline matches,
and shot reverse shots": push in on the wife looking off-screen → close-up of the husband → his stare matched to her
hands reaching for a knife → back to him putting it together → her nervous face → a medium following him closer → a
dolly in on her as he approaches → a two-shot "which holds and holds until the climactic move".

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Name two poles: the cause object (A) and the consequence object (B).
2. Alternate A → B → A → B, each setup ~1–2 s.
3. Link each pair with a pointing element aimed from one at the other (a gaze substitute).
4. Push in a little more on each return to the same pole.
5. Then frame A and B together and hold ~1.5–2.5 s with only a slow drift.
6. Put the reveal event on the next spoken word after the hold.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Shot-list rows tagged "chain 1/5 … 5/5"; each row names the pointing element and the push distance (e.g. 1.25 → 1.05 as
in §14 macro push). The hold row lists "no events until '<word>'". Joins follow RECIPE-D §15.
### Worked example
Topic: a home-based kuih seller checking if one order covers her gas (illustrative).
BEFORE: one wide shot, numbers appear all at once.
AFTER (L07–L08 "Satu tong gas RM26… order ni RM30… jadi untung?")
1/5 gas tank valve (A), its shadow points right · 2/5 order slip on the counter (B) · 3/5 closer on the valve's price tag
"RM26" · 4/5 closer on "RM30" · 5/5 tank and slip together, hold 2 s; on "untung" a "RM4" tag grows between them in accent.
### Check
- Are there two named poles alternating?
- Does each return push closer than the last?
- Is there a held two-shot before the reveal?
- Does the reveal land on a spoken word?

## NOTES
- Visual gaps: shot lengths in the film not stated; the ~1–2 s values are ours.
- No hands/people: the knife-reaching beat becomes an object moving by itself.
