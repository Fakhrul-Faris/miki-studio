## METADATA
08_Edit_Pacing | general editing (source) | shot-list row (setup join) + RECIPE-D §6.2 `tr`

## CREATIVE_INTENT
Edits felt "shallow and amateur" when transitions were used **as decoration** — a glitch, whip pan or film burn "just to
look cool". Fix 1: use them **with direction**. Ask what each transition is trying to accomplish: is this match cut just
cool, or does it show **time passing**? Is this VFX transition for fun, or for **world building**? Transitions are the
glue of visual storytelling; a simpler one where "the camera followed the direction of the transition" felt more
intentional than the flashy sequence. A kept transition must **build rhythm, connect scenes, or add meaning**.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before you choose a join, write its job in ≤ 6 words (time passes / connects A to B / reveals new info / rhythm on the word).
2. If you cannot name a job, use the plain default join (same-set flight or hard cut on the word).
3. Make the camera travel in the direction the next setup's content lies.
4. Ask of every match cut: does it show time passing or a change of state? If it is "just cool", drop it.
5. Ask of every effect-based join: does it build the world or the idea? If it is "for fun", drop it.
6. Delete any join that does not build rhythm, connect scenes, or add meaning.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Add the job to the shot-list's "Setup and camera" cell for every setup that does more than a same-set flight, and map
it to RECIPE-D §6.2 `tr`:
- "goes inside / closer look at the cause" → `tr: "in"` (push through the object).
- "reveals new information / the bigger picture" → `tr: "out"` (pull back).
- "next item in a list / step N" → `tr: "side"` along the line of objects.
- "time passes / state changes" → match cut: same framing, object in a new state, on the word.
Never: whip pan, glitch, film burn, generic wipe, flash, dissolve.

### Worked example
S16 how-to, a nasi lemak stall in Bangsar deciding whether a "50 % off" bundle is real (illustrative):
- BEFORE: `| 5 | "Step 2: tengok harga asal" | whip pan to the menu board | glitch on "harga asal" |`
- AFTER: `| 5 | "Step 2: tengok harga asal" | tr: "out" — pull back from the RM8 tag to reveal the menu board's old RM6 price (job: reveals new info) | RM6 lights in accent on "harga asal" | transition-purpose-test |`
- Match cut kept: `| 9 | "Seminggu lepas…" | same top-down on the bungkus, now three bungkus (job: time passes) | counter 1 → 3 on "seminggu" |`

### Check
- Does every non-default join name its job (time / connect / reveal / rhythm / meaning)?
- Does the camera move toward where the next content is?
- Is any join from the banned list (whip pan, glitch, film burn, wipe, flash, dissolve)?
- Would removing this join lose anything? If no, remove it.
