## METADATA
08_Edit_Pacing | Live-action | principle-only (sound design brief)

## CREATIVE_INTENT
"Ambient sounds, nature sounds, room sounds... without them... there's just these like silences between the sound effects that feels very digital. It doesn't feel very natural." A continuous low bed (birds, room tone) "acts as glue in the silences and brings all these sounds together" and implies a believable space around the hero object.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Identify the single shot that will hold longest (usually the payoff/end beat).
2. Pick one ambient texture that matches the implied space (street hum for a shop counter, quiet room tone for an indoor macro shot).
3. Lay it continuously under that shot at low level, under the music and any SFX.
4. Do not add a second ambient texture to the same shot.
5. Keep it off every fast-cut run; it is for the one held setup only.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Frame-brief "Feeling" field notes the implied space; audio pass adds one low ambient layer under the held end/payoff shot only.

### Worked example
Topic: a kedai runcit's closing-time till count (illustrative).
BEFORE: payoff shot (till drawer closing on the day's total) scored with music only, feels sterile.
AFTER: add a faint shop-hum ambient bed under the held shot → "ambient-room-tone-glues-silence" tag in shot-list.

### Check
- Is the ambient bed used on exactly one held shot, not the whole episode?
- Does it sit below the music and any SFX, never competing?
- Does the space it implies match what's on screen?
