## CREATIVE_INTENT
"Pair voiceover lines with matching visuals, lower thirds, and on-screen text (SUPER:) so timing and graphics stay
aligned", "putting each beat on its own row with an estimated duration." The workflow is a disciplined sequence:
research → outline → script → visual planning → voiceover → sound design.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before recording, put each beat on its own row.
2. Fill four cells: voice words, visual, SUPER (on-screen text, ≤ 8 words), duration.
3. Estimate duration as words ÷ 3.4 s.
4. Sum the durations; adjust lines until the total is 55–90 s.
5. Replace estimates with real word timings after the voice is processed.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Extend the RECIPE-D row: `| # | Line / words | Setup and camera | State change and events | SUPER | est. s | cards |`.
Keep the order of work: claim ledger → script → shot list → voice (user records) → timings → sound.

### Worked example
BEFORE: `| 4 | Step satu… | receipt | screenshot appears | |`
AFTER: `| 4 | "Step satu. Sebelum sale start, screenshot dulu harga barang tu." (10 w) | phone face-up beside the tag, top-down |
on "screenshot" the phone screen fades up to a capture of the tag over 0.4 s | SUPER: "1. Screenshot harga" | 2.9 | beat-row |`

### Check
- Does every beat have voice, visual, SUPER and duration?
- Is SUPER ≤ 8 words and not a copy of the caption?
- Does the duration sum land in 55–90 s?

## NOTES
- Captions are separate (EX15 capBlock); SUPER = designed step labels/numbers only.
