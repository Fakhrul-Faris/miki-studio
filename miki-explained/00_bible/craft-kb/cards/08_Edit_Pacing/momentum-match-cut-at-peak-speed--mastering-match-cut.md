## CREATIVE_INTENT
"The cut happening when the bubble is moving at its fastest is an example of a momentum match cut." In the summer
travel edit, the camera moving left is synced with the motion blur outside the bus — "a seamless match cut".

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Give A a camera or object move in one direction, eased in.
2. Cut at the move's speed peak.
3. Start B with motion already running in the same direction at similar screen speed.
4. Let B's motion ease out to rest on the subject.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
raea: A's camera `interpolate` with `Easing.in` ending at the cut frame; B's scene element (e.g. a conveyor of items)
moving at the matching screen speed from frame 0, easing out.
### Worked example
Topic: "Kedai runcit — kenapa barang laku letak paras mata" (phrase as the shop's layout choice; no sales-uplift numbers).
- BEFORE: shelf wide → cut → product macro, both static.
- AFTER: camera pans left along a shelf, accelerating; cut at peak speed on "paras mata"; B: a row of packs sliding left on a counter at the same speed, easing to stop with the eye-level pack centred, price tag in accent.
### Check
- Is the cut at the speed peak?
- Same direction and similar screen speed on both sides?
- Pan within ~130°/s, no whip?
