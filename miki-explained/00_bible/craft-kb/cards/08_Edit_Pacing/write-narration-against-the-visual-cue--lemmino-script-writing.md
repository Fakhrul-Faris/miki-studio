## METADATA

## CREATIVE_INTENT
"Scripts are written tightly around the visual timeline. Every sentence is paced to give custom 3D maps, layered archival
documents and self-composed ambient music room to breathe, turning data points into navigable visual spaces." "Draft
your narration alongside visual cues (maps, documents, timelines) so the voice-over guides the viewer's eye rather than
over-explaining what is already shown."

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before you finalise a line, write its visual cue next to it.
2. Delete words that describe what the visual already shows.
3. Keep words that point the eye ("tengok angka bawah tu").
4. Leave ~0.5–1 s without voice after a dense visual (a chart, a full receipt).

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Draft the script table with a "Visual" column filled at the same time. Mark breathing gaps in the shot list (`hold
0.8 s, no voice`) and tell the user where the pause goes in the voice text ("…").

### Worked example
- BEFORE: L06 "Resit ni ada nama kedai, tarikh, senarai barang, dan jumlah RM56.40 di bawah sekali."
- AFTER: L06 "Tengok baris paling bawah." … (0.8 s hold on the receipt, RM56.40 lights in accent) L07 "Tu je yang
  penting."

### Check
- Does each line have its visual written beside it?
- Did you cut words that only describe the visual?
- Is there a breath after each dense visual?
