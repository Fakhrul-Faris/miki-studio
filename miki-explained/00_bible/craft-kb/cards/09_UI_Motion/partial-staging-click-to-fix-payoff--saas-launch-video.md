## CREATIVE_INTENT
"Very important staging here: we are showing a part of the drawing, we are showing a part of the error. Perfect use of
the screen." Then: click one error → its summary appears → an **Autofix** button → click → the error is gone. "This is
the killer transition of the video": the cut lands on "tolerance updated, notes clarified" to show it is solved. The
product is shown working in real time, not as icons.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before you board the demo, crop to one part of the object and one problem — not the whole screen.
2. Highlight the problem (accent outline) for ~1 s.
3. Open its detail panel on the next word.
4. Show the fix button; press it as a UI reaction (button depresses, ripple), never a hand.
5. Cut or morph to the solved state on the word that names the result.
6. Confirm the solved state with a short status line (≤ 4 words).

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Four rows, ~1–1.5 s each: crop+highlight → detail panel → button press → solved state. raea: button press = scale
1 → 0.96 → 1 over ~12 f, eased (no spring bounce); panel grows in 12 f; solved state replaces the error mark with an
accent tick that fades in over 0.4 s. Keep the camera on the cropped region; the solved cut is the episode's payoff.

### Worked example
EX46 step 2 "tengok resit betul-betul".
- BEFORE: full receipt and full shelf in one wide frame, a red X then a green tick.
- AFTER: crop to two receipt lines; "RM12.90" outlined in accent next to the shelf tag "RM9.90" → a panel slides in:
  "Beza RM3" → the panel's "Bayar harga murah" button presses → cut on "lagi murah": receipt line now reads RM9.90,
  status "Harga betul".

### Check
- Is the frame cropped to one problem, not the whole screen?
- Is every press shown as the UI reacting, with no hand?
- Does the solved state land on the result word?
- Is the solved state confirmed in ≤ 4 words?
