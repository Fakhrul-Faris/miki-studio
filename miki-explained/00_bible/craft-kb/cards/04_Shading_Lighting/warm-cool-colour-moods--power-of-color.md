## CREATIVE_INTENT
Warm tones feel softer, happier, make a room feel smaller and cosier and an artwork more ecstatic (our eyes resolve more warm hues). Cool tones feel harder, moodier, more corporate, detached and calm.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Choose warm for cosy/happy/close; cool for serious/corporate/calm.
2. Apply it to the light colour first, the set materials second.
3. Keep the opposite temperature only as a small accent for contrast.
