## CREATIVE_INTENT
Colour = hue (tint), saturation (pastel → vibrant), value (dark → light). Colours carry learned associations: finance brands use blue for trust, dating apps warm reds for love; an alien world goes red/green/purple, not Earth blue-green. Meanings vary by culture (red: love/danger/urgency in the West, luck and New Year in China, purity in India), though globalisation pushes toward marketing-common meanings. Knowing the rules lets you break them (romance in cool tones) — harder, but possible. Matters most for stills, which lack context.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Name the feeling or meaning the beat must carry.
2. Pick the hue whose association matches (trust → blue, urgency/danger → red, etc.).
3. Set saturation for energy (pastel calm, vivid loud) and value for weight.
4. Check the hue against the audience’s cultural reading before locking it.
5. Break a convention only on purpose, and support it with light and story.
