## CREATIVE_INTENT
Monochromatic = harmonious, undivided (one hue, lighter/darker). Complementary = opposition, tension, drama (two opposite hues; the most common). Analogous = sympathy, realism (neighbours on the wheel, found in nature). Triadic = balanced but vibrant, can look cartoonish (three evenly spaced hues). Happier images lean warm; sadder or alien ones cooler and stranger.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Ask: what mood should this image/animation have?
2. Ask: what story am I telling?
3. Ask: which colour will pop, and which won’t be noticed?
4. Ask: can I emphasise something with more colour or light?
5. Pick the scheme that answers them; apply it to set dressing, lights and the accent.
