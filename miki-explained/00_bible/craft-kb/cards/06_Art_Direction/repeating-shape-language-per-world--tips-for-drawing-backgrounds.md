## METADATA
06_Art_Direction | 2D background art (general) | frame brief (Decision, applied across a world's setups)

## CREATIVE_INTENT
Repeat one family of shapes across a world's buildings/props — rectangles and straight lines for a city, triangles
and curves for an organic/rural place — to unify that world's visual feel and distinguish it from another world in
the same episode. "Repeating shape elements help unify the feeling of a design."

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before designing a recurring location, pick one shape family (angular/rectilinear vs curved/organic) for that
   world only.
2. Apply that shape family to every prop and surface edge in that world's setups.
3. Switch shape family only when the episode cuts to a genuinely different kind of place.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Note the chosen shape family once in the episode's shot-list header or in each relevant frame brief's Decision, so
every setup in that world stays consistent.

### Worked example
Topic: an episode contrasting a modern mall kiosk vs a traditional pasar stall (illustrative).
BEFORE: both locations use the same prop shapes.
AFTER: Decision for mall setups: "rectilinear, straight edges throughout"; Decision for pasar setups: "rounded
baskets, curved awning lines throughout" — the two worlds read as different places at a glance.

### Check
- Does every prop/surface in one world share the same shape family?
- Is the shape family distinct between the episode's different locations, if there is more than one?
