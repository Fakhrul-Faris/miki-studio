## CREATIVE_INTENT
The close-up is "the most powerful visual weapon for highlighting a change in emotion or dramatic beat", usually at
eye level, a "front-row seat for a character's thoughts". "The close-up is about empathy" and is most effective "in a
time of decision or anxiety".

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Find the decision or flip line.
2. Frame the object's most telling side at eye level, filling ~50–60 % of the frame.
3. Hold the CU for the whole line; let only one element change.
4. Save CU size for decision lines; use wider sizes elsewhere.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Shot list: `CU · eye-level on the receipt total`. raea: camera at subject height, front-on, shallow DoF.

### Worked example
Topic: "Beli pukal RM90 atau beli kecil-kecil?" (plain arithmetic: 10 × RM10 = RM100 vs RM90 bulk).
- BEFORE: `| 10 | "Jadi korang pilih mana?" | WS of two shopping bags | — |`
- AFTER: `| 10 | "RM90 sekali… atau RM100 sikit-sikit?" | CU · eye-level on two price tags side by side | the RM90 tag lights accent green on "RM90" |` → `| 11 | "Korang pilih mana?" | same CU | both tags hold |`

### Check
- Is the CU on the decision/flip line?
- Eye level, front-on, ~50–60 % fill?
- Only one element changes during it?
