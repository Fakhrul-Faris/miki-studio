## METADATA

## CREATIVE_INTENT
The wide shot "positions subjects far from the camera to visually represent their relationship to their
environment" — about the subject's scale, not the location. Use it to make subjects appear lost, lonely or
overwhelmed, or to comment on their relationship to the environment (Phantom Thread ends on Alma and Reynolds dwarfed
by a messy ballroom, "together yet isolated"). Use it to make statements with distance, depth or size.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Decide what the subject is small against (debt pile, rows of competitors, a big number).
2. Frame the big thing to fill the 25–60 % band; place the subject small at a third.
3. Put the accent only on the subject so it reads at once.
4. Hold ~2–3 s; do not cut in to a close-up on the same line.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Frame brief: Feeling = "outnumbered"; Decision = "subject at 6 % frame height, lower third, accent rim". raea: wide lens
from far, top light pool on the subject only.

### Worked example
Topic: "Bayar hutang RM50 sebulan, hutang RM6,000 — berapa lama?" (no interest, plain arithmetic: 120 months = 10 years).
- BEFORE: CU on an RM50 note.
- AFTER: `| 8 | "RM50 sebulan…" | WS · one RM50 note at the foot of a stack of 120 notes, stack fills 55 % of frame | note rim-lit in accent |` → `| 9 | "sepuluh tahun" | — | stack counter 120 |`

### Check
- Is the subject visibly small against a named big thing?
- Does the big thing fill the 25–60 % band?
- Is the accent on the subject only?
