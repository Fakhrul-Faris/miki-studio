## CREATIVE_INTENT
"It's so important to have beautiful product shots; these product shots really make or break your commercial. You can
have really nice shots all over the place but without product shots it's just going to look like a video." The Apple
spot drops in clean phone shots between the story shots: the perfectly smooth back of the phone with one clean reflection.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before you finalise the shot list, mark which product or brand the episode is about.
2. Reserve 1–2 setups as pure hero shots of it: no text over it, one clean reflection, controlled background.
3. Place one hero shot early (frame 0–3 s, with the logo) and one at the payoff line.
4. Light the hero shot separately from story shots: one key, one long clean reflection across the surface.
5. Hold a hero shot ≥ ~1.5 s so the surface reads; still within the 2–3 s setup rhythm.
6. Check the hero shot alone looks like an ad still.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Shot-list: tag hero rows `HERO` in the cards column. Frame brief: Decision "product at its best"; Eye "the logo or
the product edge"; Not generic because "the real product/logo, its real material". raea: a long thin `RectLight`
(with `range` ≈ 2× light-to-subject distance) placed so its reflection runs along the product surface; see
`cards/04_Shading_Lighting/side-strip-reflection-lights--perfect-product-animations.md`.

### Worked example
Topic: "Terima QR di gerai: duit masuk terus" (how-to; DuitNow QR is a real Malaysian standard).
- BEFORE: 11 setups, all story (stall, customer phone, receipts); the QR standee only appears blurred in two.
- AFTER: row 1 `| 1 | frame-0 text | HERO: macro of the stall's QR standee on the counter, one strip reflection across the acrylic, logo sharp | reflection slides across over 1.2 s eased | hero-product-shots |` and row 10 `| 10 | "RM30 sehari tak payah cari duit kecil" (illustrative) | HERO: same standee, low angle, accent on the QR frame | RM30 counts up beside it | hero-product-shots |`.

### Check
- Do 1–2 rows exist that are pure hero shots of the product/brand?
- Is there no text over the hero in those rows?
- Is one clean reflection or edge light planned on the product surface?
- Would the hero still alone work as an ad image?
