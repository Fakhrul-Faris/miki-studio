## CREATIVE_INTENT
Burton's **hyperreal suburbia** is "more than real": conformity, plainness, simplicity, obedience — bright and orderly,
exaggerated "typically to provide greater contrast with the fantasy world". The other world is the exact opposite:
dark, random and jagged lines, mechanical and industrial, surreal yet playful, physics as "suggestions". Edward's and
Kim's bedrooms show they are "worlds apart" from design alone. Even the normal world is exaggerated, in the direction
that maximises the contrast.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before designing, name the two worlds the script contrasts (e.g. shelf price vs real cost).
2. List 4 traits for world A (e.g. bright, orderly, straight, plain).
3. Give world B the opposite of each trait (dark, random, jagged, mechanical).
4. Exaggerate world A past realistic toward its traits — don't leave it neutral.
5. Keep the same subject object in both, so the contrast reads as one thing changing.
6. Check two stills side by side: they must read as opposites at a glance (< 1 s).

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Frame brief: add a "World" field: `A (surface): <4 traits> | B (mechanism): <4 opposites>`. raea translation of traits:
order = aligned objects, even top light, clean straight edges; disorder = rotated/scattered instances, hard raking
side light, darker floor, more grain. Reach world B with an object state change (x-ray, open, slice), never a flash
transition. Accent stays the brand colour in both; in B it marks the hidden piece.
### Worked example
Topic: "Promosi 'Beli 1 Free 1' — siapa sebenarnya bayar?" (a kedai runcit, illustrative numbers).
- BEFORE: both setups the same dark macro of the bottle; the "hidden cost" reads as a caption only.
- AFTER: World A (shelf): bottles in a perfect row, soft even top light, clean tag "B1F1" — over-tidy. Shot-list row
  `| 5 | "siapa sebenarnya bayar?" | camera dollies down through the shelf edge; bottle x-rays | B: inside, jagged stack of
  cost slips, hard side light, slips tilted at random; RM 4.20 (accent) on the slip that says margin on "bayar" | two-worlds |`.
### Check
- Do the two worlds differ on at least 3 named traits (light, line, order, density)?
- Is world A exaggerated, not neutral?
- Is the same object present in both, joined by a state change?
- Is there still only one accent colour?
