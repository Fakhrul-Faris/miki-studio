## CREATIVE_INTENT
Medium close-ups (mid-chest to just above the head) are "about reducing distraction and prioritising story and
character details", getting intimate "without losing physicality". In Avengers: Endgame the MCU has room for the
gauntlet and Thanos's satisfaction — and when the snap fails, the same framing captures his reaction.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Frame the working part (the action) and the place the result appears.
2. Crop out everything else.
3. Play the action, then the result, in the same setup without cutting.
4. If the result fails/changes, keep the framing so the change reads.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Shot list: one row, events "action on word X, result on word Y". raea: 85–100 mm equivalent, shallow DoF between the
two points kept in focus by aperture or a rack.

### Worked example
Topic: "Tap kad kat terminal — kenapa kadang-kadang minta PIN" (generic: shows the PIN prompt; no limit values claimed).
- BEFORE: CU card tap → cut → CU screen.
- AFTER: `| 6 | "Tap… tapi skrin minta PIN" | MCU · card corner touching the terminal, screen in the top of frame | card taps on "tap"; screen changes to "Masukkan PIN" on "PIN", accent outline |`

### Check
- Are action and result both in frame?
- Is the background cleared?
- Does the result happen without a cut?
