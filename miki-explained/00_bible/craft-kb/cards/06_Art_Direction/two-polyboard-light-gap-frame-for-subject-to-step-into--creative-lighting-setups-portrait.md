## CREATIVE_INTENT
Use a harder light aimed at the backdrop with two flags (polyboards) placed either side of the beam, close enough
to leave only a gap for light to pass through, so the backdrop shows a defined rectangle/frame of light for the
subject to "step into" rather than an even wash.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Use a harder light source (no softbox) aimed at the backdrop.
2. Place two flags either side of the beam, close enough together to leave only a gap.
3. Rig the light overhead if a stand puts the gap's shadow too low on the backdrop.
4. Check the gap frames where the subject will actually be placed, not just anywhere on the backdrop.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
raea: a SpotLight with a narrow `outer_angle` plus a static occluder (or a masked cutout on the light) so only a
rectangular patch of the backdrop lights up, timed to appear just before the hero's own light comes up.

### Worked example
Topic: a small kedai's new signboard "reveal" beat (illustrative).
BEFORE: backdrop evenly lit; the signboard just fades in.
AFTER: shot-list row — a narrow lit rectangle appears on the dark backdrop first (the gap-frame look), then the
signboard slides into that lit frame.

### Check
- Is the backdrop light shaped into a defined gap rather than an even wash?
- Does the gap's position match where the hero element will appear?
