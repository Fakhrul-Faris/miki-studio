## CREATIVE_INTENT
Research has **two parts**: study the product, and look for inspiration. The lead designer pins inspiration; "the most
obvious is to look for other explainer videos… but it's not only limited to that" — illustrations, posters, movies,
series, magazine layouts, film composition: "you could get inspiration from anything". Pins are compiled into a mood
board; the whole research phase took ~1 week at studio scale.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Keep two separate lists: product facts (claim ledger) and look references (mood board).
2. Collect refs from at least 3 non-explainer sources (film still, poster, magazine spread, product photo).
3. Limit explainer/TikTok refs to ≤ 1 in 4 of the board (`~`).
4. For each kept ref, write one ref_<shot>.md naming the single trait borrowed.
5. Drop refs that only repeat a trait already on the board.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Board file `refs/moodboard.md`: one row per ref with source type (film / poster / magazine / product photo / explainer)
and the trait to borrow. Each frame brief's "Ref" field cites one row. Run the reference-review method on each ref
individually.

### Worked example
BEFORE: mood board = 6 other finance TikToks.
AFTER (kedai runcit cashflow episode): film still of a late-night counter lit by one bulb (trait: single top light pool);
a 1960s Malaysian product poster (trait: one saturated red against cream); a magazine spread of a receipt shot macro
(trait: paper texture at 1:1 scale); one explainer (trait: counter that ticks on the word).

### Check
- Are product facts and look refs in separate files?
- Do at least 3 refs come from outside explainers?
- Does each ref name one specific trait borrowed?
