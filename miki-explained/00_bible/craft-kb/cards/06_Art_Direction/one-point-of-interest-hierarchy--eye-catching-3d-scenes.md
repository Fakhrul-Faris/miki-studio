## CREATIVE_INTENT
Before any effects are added, the scene needs exactly one point of interest; everything else placed around it "should
be significant but not significant enough to overshadow the main point of interest" — present, but never distracting.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Identify the single object the setup is about before placing anything else.
2. Place supporting elements (icons, props, background layers) only after the hero is locked.
3. Keep supporting elements smaller, dimmer, or further back than the hero — never matching its scale, brightness, or
   sharpness.
4. Check the still: does the eye land on the hero first, without hunting?

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Frame brief "Eye" field states the hero object and confirms every other element is subordinate (scale/brightness/
focus). Board check: reject a still where a supporting prop is as bright, as sharp, or as large as the hero.

### Worked example
Topic: a barbershop's loyalty-stamp card (illustrative numbers).
BEFORE: the stamp card, a mirror, and a row of scissors all lit and in focus at once — no single read.
AFTER: the stamp card stays sharp, bright, centred; scissors and mirror sit soft, dim, at the frame edges, out of
focus — the eye lands on the card first, every time.

### Check
- Is there exactly one object that reads first in the still?
- Are all other elements dimmer, softer, or smaller than the hero — never equal?
- Does removing every non-hero element still leave the idea intact?
