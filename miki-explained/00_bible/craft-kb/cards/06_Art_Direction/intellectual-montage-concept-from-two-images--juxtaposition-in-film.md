## METADATA

## CREATIVE_INTENT
"Perhaps the most powerful use of juxtaposition in editing" — coined by Sergei Eisenstein. In *October* he juxtaposes
deities and military "to heighten the absurdity of blind faith"; *V for Vendetta* juxtaposes protest footage with falling
dominoes, "indicating that everything is interconnected and that this is just the beginning."

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Confirm the script line is itself a metaphor; otherwise stop and show the line literally.
2. Pick image A = the literal subject; image B = an everyday object that embodies the idea.
3. Cut A → B on the metaphor word; ≤ 2 s on B.
4. Return to A (or the RM number) on the next line so the metaphor is anchored.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Shot-list: two rows, B tagged `[metaphor — line is one]`.

### Worked example
Topic: "Bila satu pembekal naik harga, semua kedai kena" (illustrative).
- AFTER: `| 6 | "Satu pembekal naik harga…" | macro supplier invoice, +RM0.20 per unit in accent |` → `| 7 | "…macam domino" | row of dominoes on the same counter, the first one marked +RM0.20 tips the rest | tips on "domino" |` → `| 8 | "Kedai runcit, gerai, kafe — semua naik RM0.20" | back to the invoice, 3 shop tags appear |`

### Check
- Is the line itself a metaphor?
- Is image B a real everyday object on our surface?
- Does the next line return to the literal subject?
