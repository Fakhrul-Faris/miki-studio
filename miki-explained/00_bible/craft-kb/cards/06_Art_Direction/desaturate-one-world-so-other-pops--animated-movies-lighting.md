## CREATIVE_INTENT
Desrumaux used colour scripts to contrast Earth with the moon kingdom Lunaria: "by making the colors on Earth less
saturated, the colors of Lunaria, with all of its light-emitting structures and characters, would look even bolder."
On arrival the shot is filled with "bright, glowing yellow light emitted from the structure around them" to create
"a sense of awe" — she kept "a space for the audience to be amazed".

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before the shot list is locked, split setups into world A (problem/before) and world B (solution/after).
2. Lower world A's saturation: grey surfaces, neutral light, no accent except the line's number.
3. Hold back all strong colour and glow until world B's first setup.
4. Give world B emissive sources (screens, signs) and warmer light.
5. Make the first B setup the brightest, most saturated frame so far.
6. Check A and B side by side: B must look bolder at thumbnail size.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Shot-list `colour` column: `A-grey` / `B-glow`. raea: world A materials low `base_color` saturation, key `#e8e8e8`;
world B adds emissive materials and `<Bloom>`, warm key `#ffe2c0`. Keep `<Grade>` constant (it holds for the whole
piece); the contrast lives in materials and lights. The brand accent appears in both worlds on the line's subject only.

### Worked example
Topic: "Buku 555 vs app akaun: kedai runcit kira untung" (how-to; arithmetic: profit = sales − cost).
- BEFORE: both the paper-notebook setups and the app setups are lit the same warm and colourful; the app doesn't feel like a relief.
- AFTER: L02–L05 (world A) — grey paper 555 notebook, neutral top light, accent only on the handwritten "RM?" · L06 "Kalau korang nak tahu untung tiap hari, ni caranya. 3 step je." (world B starts) — the phone screen glows, warm key, bloom on the app UI; accent on "Untung hari ni: RM142" (illustrative).

### Check
- Is world A visibly less saturated than world B?
- Is the first world-B setup the boldest frame so far?
- Does the accent stay one colour in both worlds?
- Is the change carried by a setup change, not a flash?
