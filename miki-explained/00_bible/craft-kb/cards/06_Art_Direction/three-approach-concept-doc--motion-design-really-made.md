## METADATA
06_Art_Direction | general (studio concept phase) | frame brief, plan for user approval

## CREATIVE_INTENT
After research, the studio offers 1–3 concept approaches (depending on budget; assume three). Each concept contains:
(1) a **sample script** — not the script, the structure ("in the intro we explain this topic, next section the product");
(2) a **sample story** — not the storyboard, just **three frames**, because there are many ways to present it visually:
metaphorical or literal, characters or shapes, voice-over or text/typography only; (3) the **design** — concept art
that dictates the look: colour palette, a sample illustration, or fonts if typography-heavy. The client picks one, and
that approach is applied to the whole storyboard.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before script v1, draft 2–3 approaches that differ on at least one axis: literal/metaphor, object/UI-screen, VO/text-led.
2. For each, write a 4–6 line beat outline (structure only).
3. For each, describe 3 frames: hook frame, mechanism frame, payoff frame.
4. For each, give a palette (neutral + one accent hex) and the hero object or screen.
5. Present all approaches to the user; wait for a choice before writing the full script.
6. Apply the chosen approach to every row of the shot list.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
In episode.md add `## Concepts` with A/B/C blocks: `Outline`, `Frame 1/2/3` (frame-brief "Idea" + "Decision" only),
`Design` (palette hexes, hero object). After the user picks, delete the losers from the plan and note the choice.

### Worked example
Topic: "Kenapa kedai makan suka bundle air + makanan" (illustrative).
BEFORE: one approach drafted and built straight away.
AFTER: A (literal, object): tray with nasi + teh ais; frames: price tags separate → bundle tag → margin counter; red accent.
B (UI-led): POS screen; frames: two line items → combo button → profit field ticks up; green accent.
C (text-led over macro receipt): receipt lines strike through into one combo line; amber accent. User picks A.

### Check
- Do the approaches differ on at least one named axis?
- Does each have outline + 3 frames + palette?
- Did the user choose before the full script was written?
