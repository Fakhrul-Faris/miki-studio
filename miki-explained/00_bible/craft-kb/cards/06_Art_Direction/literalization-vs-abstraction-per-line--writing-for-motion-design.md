## CREATIVE_INTENT
For each audio block choose **literalization** (show what the words say) or **abstraction** (a representation of the
themes). Lou Gehrig's "for the past two weeks you have been reading about a bad break I got" literally = a newspaper
unfolding with the diagnosis headline, then a photo of Lou on the diamond; abstract = inside Lou's brain as ALS takes
hold, a hospital bed, a fan's tear smudging face paint. Showing a phone with the app interface and a finger tapping is
the everyday literal case — "totally valid". Often you need a combination, and you may try many times. Check that
neighbouring choices flow: the diamond photo can push in, go black-and-white to colour, and become the live speech.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. For each line, write the literal picture first.
2. Keep it if it is showable with the hero, a UI screen or a number.
3. Switch to abstraction only if the line is a metaphor or the literal picture needs people/hands.
4. When abstract, use a real object that shares the line's mechanism, not a mood image.
5. Read each pair of neighbouring rows; mark rows whose pictures don't lead into each other.
6. Fix a marked pair with a joining move from the scene's own objects (push into a photo, a screen filling the frame).

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Tag each shot-list row [LIT] or [ABS] in the Setup cell; target ≥ ~80 % [LIT] (~, inferred from house rule). Each [ABS]
row names why in the cards column ("line is a metaphor").

### Worked example
Topic: expired stock at a kedai runcit (illustrative).
L06 "Kedai ni buang RM2,000 barang expired sebulan."
BEFORE [ABS]: banknotes melting into a drain.
AFTER [LIT]: macro over a bin: yoghurt cups and bread loaves with dated labels slide in one by one; a counter on the bin
rim climbs RM0 → RM2,000 on "RM2,000". L07 joins by flying the camera from the bin label to the shelf-date label of the
next line.

### Check
- Does every [ABS] row have a reason (metaphor line, or literal needs people)?
- Is each [ABS] image a real object with the line's mechanism, not a mood picture?
- Does each row's picture lead into the next one?
