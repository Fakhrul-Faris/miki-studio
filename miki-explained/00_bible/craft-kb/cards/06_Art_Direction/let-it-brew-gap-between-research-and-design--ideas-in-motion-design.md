## CREATIVE_INTENT
"Let your ideas brew": let the unconscious work — "leave a sleep or two and then come back to your project". Good ideas
take time; working fast suits supplied assets and bread-and-butter jobs, not idea generation. He worked on the piece over
about a week, jotting ideas and adding to sketches and mind map as they came. Buffer time between researching and
designing frames is "free time where your subconscious is working for you for free".

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. When planning an episode, finish research and the claim ledger in one session.
2. Schedule frame briefs for a later session (ideally after one night, ~).
3. In between, log any idea in a parking list in episode.md, unsorted.
4. Start the frame-brief session by reading the parking list.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Propose to the user: research/claim ledger session N, frame briefs session N+1. When the calendar can't allow a gap, at
least separate research and frame briefs by the script step, and re-read the research before briefing.

### Worked example
Topic: car wash pricing (illustrative).
BEFORE: research, script, shot list and frame briefs in one sitting; the frame briefs repeat the first ideas.
AFTER: Mon: claim ledger + outline; parking list gains "foam rising like a bar chart" overnight; Tue: frame briefs start
from the parking list; L07's foam-chart setup replaces a generic counter.

### Check
- Is there a session gap (or at least the script step) between research and frame briefs?
- Was a parking list kept and read at the start of briefing?

## NOTES
- For an agent the "sleep" is a session boundary; the user's own overnight review often supplies it.
