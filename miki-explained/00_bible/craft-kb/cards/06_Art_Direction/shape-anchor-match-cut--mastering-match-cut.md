## CREATIVE_INTENT
"All it requires is taking at least one similar attribute from two different sources and lining them up." Favourite
types: shape, momentum, colour. In the bubble animation, a paint-like bubble transitions into a macro shot: the end
of shot one syncs with the start of shot two, "centering around the shape of the bubble".

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Name the silhouette at the end of A (circle, rectangle, ring).
2. Find or design the same silhouette at the start of B, at a different scale or medium.
3. Line up its centre and size on screen across the cut.
4. Cut on the word; let B's camera then reveal what the shape really is.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Frame brief: Handover = "circle at centre, 38 % width". raea: set B's camera so the matching object's projected size
equals A's on the cut frame, then pull back with `Easing.inOut`.
### Worked example
Topic: "Satu syiling 10 sen sehari, setahun?" (10 × 365 = RM36.50).
- BEFORE: a chart → cut → a coin on a table.
- AFTER: a round pie-chart slice-ring at centre 38 % width on "setahun"; cut to a macro 10 sen coin at the same centre and size; camera pulls back to show 365 coins in rows, "RM36.50" in accent.
### Check
- Same silhouette, centre and size across the cut?
- Does B then reveal the shape's real identity?
- Is the cut on a word?
