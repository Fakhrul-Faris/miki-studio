## CREATIVE_INTENT
Once a shoot develops a strong presentation idea (images displayed on a physical CD-ROM lookbook, in the source's
example), extending that same idea to other brand touchpoints (pricing pages, size charts, manuals) makes the one idea
work harder and reinforces brand consistency, instead of confining it to a single hero image.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. After an episode's hero visual concept is locked, list the episode's other deliverables (cover still, post caption, calendar thumbnail).
2. For each, ask whether the episode's specific visual device (not just its general house style) could carry over.
3. Apply the device only where it genuinely fits — don't force it onto a deliverable it doesn't suit.
4. Note in the episode.md which other assets reused the device, so it's traceable.
5. Check: does the cover/post feel like it belongs to this specific episode's idea, not a generic template filled in after the fact?

## AGENT_EXECUTION
In `episode.md` § Post, add a one-line note when the cover still or caption deliberately reuses the episode's hero
visual device (e.g. the cover still is literally a frame from the oversized-object beat, not a separately composed shot).

### Worked example
Topic: an episode whose hero device is "the invoice tears open to reveal the hidden fee" (illustrative).
BEFORE: the cover still is a generic close-up of the invoice, chosen separately from the episode's visual idea.
AFTER: the cover still IS the torn-open invoice frame mid-reveal, and the caption echoes it ("the bill was hiding
something — swipe to see").

### Check
- Does the cover still or caption use the same specific device as the episode's hero frame, not just the same general look?
- Was the reuse noted in episode.md so it's traceable?
- Was the device skipped anywhere it genuinely didn't fit, rather than forced everywhere?
