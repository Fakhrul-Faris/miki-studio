## METADATA

## CREATIVE_INTENT
"The hook as the first domino": not yet the main conflict, but related — "that first thread that gets pulled". Best way:
"get them to ask a question… 'what is that? what's that about?' or simply 'what's going to happen next?'" Often in medias
res (Fast Five opens on a jailbreak before any exposition), and "a taste of what's to come". Jurassic Park's opening hides
the animal in the cage: "the very fact that they're not showing us the dinosaur… is a fantastic hook". By the hook's end
the audience "may still have questions, but they know enough that they are invested".

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Start frame 0 mid-action: the hero already moving or changing.
2. Show only part of the hero (macro detail, shadow, inside a container).
3. Make the hidden part the question the script answers.
4. Hint at the payoff (a taste of the later state).
5. Reveal the full hero by L02–L03, once the viewer is invested.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Frame brief `Idea:` what is withheld; `Eye:` the visible detail; `Moves:` the reveal on a named word in L02–L03 (pull-out,
rack focus, lid opening). Board check: frame 0's subject in the 25–60 % band even when partial; logo legible.

### Worked example
Topic: why a RM1 kuih costs a stall more than RM1 to make (illustrative).
- BEFORE: frame 0 = the whole kuih on a plate, centred, static.
- AFTER: frame 0 = extreme macro of a banana-leaf fold, slowly unwrapping, the stall's logo on the tray edge; on
  "sebiji" (L02) the camera pulls out to show one kuih and a tiny receipt "kos RM1.10".

### Check
- Is frame 0 already in motion?
- Is part of the hero withheld, and is that the script's question?
- Is the full reveal on a named word by L03?
