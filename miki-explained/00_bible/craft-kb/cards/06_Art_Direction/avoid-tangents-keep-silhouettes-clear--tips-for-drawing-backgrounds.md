## CREATIVE_INTENT
A tangent — two silhouette edges just touching or barely overlapping — reads as a confusing, unsettled join. Keep
objects' edges clearly separated or clearly overlapping, never just grazing.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. At board-check, scan each setup for two edges (object-to-object, object-to-frame-edge, object-to-horizon) that
   just touch or barely clip.
2. If found, move the camera or object slightly so edges either clearly separate or clearly overlap.
3. Re-check after any camera move lands on its final frame, not just the starting frame.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Add to the board-check pass (RECIPE-D §7): "no tangents — no two edges just grazing/touching" alongside the
existing no-clipping, one-accent checks.

### Worked example
BEFORE: a coin's edge just grazes the frame's bottom edge in the final held frame.
AFTER: camera adjusted so the coin either clears the bottom edge with a gap or is deliberately cropped by it, not
grazing.

### Check
- Does the board-check scan include a tangent check (edges just touching) at every setup's final held frame?
- Were any grazing edges fixed by a small camera/object move, not ignored?
