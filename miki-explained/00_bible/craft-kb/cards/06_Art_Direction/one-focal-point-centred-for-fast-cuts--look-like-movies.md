## CREATIVE_INTENT
"A good shot has one place where the eye lands first." The best wide shots "hold so little: sky, ground and a rider —
the eye has no other choice." Placement: rule of thirds (subject on a crossing) is the best-known rule, but dead centre
gives an inescapable gaze, and in *Fury Road* George Miller kept the subject "almost always in the middle": "cut after
cut flies by, but after each your eye doesn't have to search again. He's right there."

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before you write a setup, name its one focal point (the line's subject).
2. Remove or darken anything that competes as a second landing spot.
3. In a run of setups under ~3 s each, keep the focal point inside the centre ninth of the frame.
4. Use a thirds crossing only for a setup held ≥ ~3 s or where unease/space is the point.
5. Across a cut, keep the outgoing and incoming focal points within ~10 % of frame width of each other.
6. Check each still: where does the eye land first? It must be the named subject.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Shot-list "Setup and camera" cell: add `focal: <thing> @ centre` or `@ thirds-UL` etc. Board check: overlay the stills of
consecutive setups; focal points should stack near centre. raea: aim the camera with `lookingAt` the subject's
position so it projects to frame centre; offset later only on purpose. In 9:16 keep the focal point above the EX15
caption panel (lower ~25 %); see `masters/05_Camera_Raea_Pipeline/composition-guides.md`.

### Worked example
Topic: "Kalau korang nak buka akaun simpanan anak, ni caranya. 4 step je." (how-to; illustrative amounts).
- BEFORE: L03 MyKid card at lower-left third; L04 bank app button at upper-right; L05 RM50 note centre-left — the eye hunts every 2.5 s.
- AFTER: `| 3 | "Step 1: bawa MyKid" | macro MyKid card, focal: card chip @ centre | card slides in 0.4 s | one-focal-point |` · `| 4 | "Step 2: isi borang" | form on counter, focal: the signature box @ centre | pen line draws | one-focal-point |` · `| 5 | "Step 3: masuk RM50" (illustrative) | RM50 note, focal: the '50' @ centre | note settles, accent on 50 | one-focal-point |`.

### Check
- Does each setup name exactly one focal point?
- In fast runs, is the focal point in the centre ninth?
- Do focal points across each cut sit within ~10 % of each other?
- Is the focal point above the caption zone?
