## METADATA
06_Art_Direction | general (Wes Anderson, Kubrick) | frame brief (Decision, Feeling)

## CREATIVE_INTENT
"Balance at its extreme is symmetry." Wes Anderson puts the hero dead centre and "the world around him becomes a
dollhouse, everything in its place." Kubrick uses the same symmetry "and it makes you uneasy — perfect order with
nothing alive in it." "One device, two opposite feelings. Symmetry doesn't choose the emotion, it amplifies it."

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before choosing symmetry, write which feeling the line already has: calm order or cold unease.
2. Centre the hero on the vertical axis and mirror the surrounding elements.
3. For calm: warm soft light, gentle colours, small props neatly placed.
4. For unease: cold flat light, hard edges, nothing moving except one element.
5. Keep the accent on one side-free element (the centre hero), never mirrored.
6. Break symmetry only on the word that breaks the order.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Frame brief: Decision "symmetry"; Feeling "calm" or "unease" with the lighting that matches. raea: camera on the axis
(`x = 0`, `lookingAt([0, y, 0])`); mirrored props by duplicating at `±x`. A symmetry break = one prop eases off-axis
over ≥ 0.4 s on the named word.

### Worked example
Topic: "Kenapa harga sama di semua kedai 'RM2' (kedai harga tetap)" (illustrative; a fixed-price shop is plain arithmetic: every item one price).
- BEFORE frame brief: "shelf with products, three-quarter view".
- AFTER: Decision "symmetry, calm-dollhouse"; Ref a perfectly ordered shelf; camera on axis; 5 identical RM2 tags in a row mirrored around the centre item; Feeling "everything in its place"; Moves "on 'tapi' one tag tilts 8° off-axis over 0.5 s — the order breaks"; accent on the centre tag only.

### Check
- Is the feeling (calm or unease) chosen before symmetry and supported by the light?
- Is the camera exactly on axis?
- Is the accent on one element only, not mirrored?
- Does any break land on a named word, eased?
