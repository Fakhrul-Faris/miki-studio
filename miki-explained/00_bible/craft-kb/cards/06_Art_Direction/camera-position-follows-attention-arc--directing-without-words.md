## METADATA
06_Art_Direction | StudioBinder video essay (Sound of Metal) | shot-list "Setup and camera" sequence

## CREATIVE_INTENT
In Sound of Metal "where the camera is located creates a small emotional arc". Camera in front of Ruben while he is in
his own head; the kid bangs the slide, still front. Second bang → new angle behind Ruben, "signalling that he is now
focused on the child", with a more mobile camera for his burst of energy. Then closer angles on the kid and on Ruben,
"strengthening the bond". Then a cut to a wide: "we are reminded of how small it is, just two people playing on a slide."

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Write the attention arc for the beat in 3–4 words (ignored → noticed → closer → small).
2. Start in front of the hero while the line is about the hero itself.
3. When the line turns to a second thing, move the camera behind the hero, looking at it.
4. Raise camera mobility (a longer eased move) for the energy peak.
5. Go closer on both things as the link tightens.
6. End on a wide to show scale when the line says how small or common it is.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Shot-list rows: "front eye-level (§14 eye-level frontal) → over-the-hero to target (look-at past the hero) → closer pair
(§14 descend to a pair) → pull out into a void / wide". Keep moves ≤ ~130°/s and eased.
### Worked example
Topic: a laundromat's coin box vs the phone QR sticker beside it (generic, illustrative).
BEFORE: four random angles on the machine.
AFTER
- L03 "Mesin ni dulu terima syiling je." front, eye-level on the coin slot.
- L04 "Sekarang ada QR kecil kat tepi." behind the coin slot, looking past it to the QR sticker.
- L05 "Setengah pelanggan dah scan." closer on sticker and slot together; a counter reaches 50 %.
- L06 "Satu sticker. Tapi tu separuh duit kedai." wide: the whole row of machines, one sticker each.
### Check
- Is the attention arc written before angles are chosen?
- Does the camera move behind the hero when attention shifts?
- Does the closing wide match a line about scale?
