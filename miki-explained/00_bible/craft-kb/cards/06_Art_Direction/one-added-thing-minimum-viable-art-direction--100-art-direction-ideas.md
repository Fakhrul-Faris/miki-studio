## CREATIVE_INTENT
The gap between great and mediocre creative is whether a shot has an idea: "is this just a person standing there... or
do we have an idea". The minimum requirement for art direction, when there's no budget or oversight, is to add exactly
one thing to an otherwise normal shot — a glove on the hand modelling the jewelry costs nothing extra over a standard
shoot, but transforms the photo from generic to intentional.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before approving a frame brief, check whether the setup is "just" the hero object on a plain surface with nothing added.
2. If so, pick exactly one addition: a prop, a material swap, a framing choice, or a context the object doesn't normally appear in.
3. Confirm the addition costs nothing beyond the normal setup (no new budget line, no new shoot).
4. Write the addition into the frame brief's "Not generic because" field in one sentence.
5. Check: without that one thing, would this be an identical shot to every other explainer's version of the same subject?

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Frame-brief field "Not generic because": state the single added element plainly, e.g. "the receipt curls out of the
machine already torn at the total" rather than "a receipt printer on a counter". Use this as the first gate before a
board still is approved — a still with nothing added fails Reference review and Test-stills-too-basic checks.

### Worked example
Topic: a laundromat's RM0.50 dryer-minute fee (illustrative).
BEFORE: a generic macro shot of a coin slot on a dryer.
AFTER (one thing added): the coin is mid-drop, caught exactly at the slot's edge, with the accent-coloured digital
timer already ticking down behind it — one addition (the suspended coin) turns a plain coin-slot shot into a shot
about time literally draining with the coin.

### Check
- Does the frame brief name exactly one added thing, not a list of unrelated embellishments?
- Does removing that one thing make the shot generic again?
- Did the addition avoid any new budget, shoot, or asset beyond what the setup already needed?
