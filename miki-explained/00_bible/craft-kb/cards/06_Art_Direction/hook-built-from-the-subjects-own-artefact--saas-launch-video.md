## CREATIVE_INTENT
The hook line was "Even a small drawing error can cost thousands" — "the hook of the whole video… I'm trying to keep
viewers as long as possible." The director used one of the client's own engineering drawings as inspiration and coded
the words of the line into boxes like the drawing's cells: the hook is shown in the visual language of the thing it is
about.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before you brief frame 0, name the real artefact the hook line is about (receipt, tag, invoice, app screen).
2. Get a real reference of that artefact from the user or the episode research.
3. Place the hook words inside the artefact's own structure: its cells, fields, rows, price slots.
4. Keep the artefact's real type class (thermal mono, price-tag bold, app UI sans).
5. Put the one word or RM number that carries the cost in the brand accent.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Frame brief: **Ref** = artefact photo; **Idea** = "the line lives inside the receipt/tag"; **Decision** = which field
holds which word; **Eye** = the RM figure in accent; **Not generic because** = only this topic has this artefact. In
raea build the artefact as a 3D card/plane with the text in its texture or a 2D layer locked to it. EX15 captions still
run; the in-artefact words are set dressing that repeats the frame-0 text (≤ 8 words).

### Worked example
Frame-0 text: "Salah satu digit. Rugi RM2,000." (kedai hardware in Klang, illustrative)
- BEFORE: dark background, the line in big white sans, an invoice icon above.
- AFTER: macro on a printed invoice at a low grazing angle; the words sit in its columns ("Kuantiti: 10" with the 0
  smudged, "Jumlah: RM2,000"); "RM2,000" is the only accent-red text; the camera is already pushing toward it.

### Check
- Is the hook text inside a real artefact the topic is about, not floating on a void?
- Was the artefact recreated from a real reference?
- Is exactly one word/number in the accent colour?
- Does no text cover the hero's key detail?
