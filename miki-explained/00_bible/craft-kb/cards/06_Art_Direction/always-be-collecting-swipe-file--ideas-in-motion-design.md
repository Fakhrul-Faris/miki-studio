## CREATIVE_INTENT
Noticing the many circular shots in the shower scene, he remembered an animation he'd made a while ago and used it as a
transition. He keeps a project full of random experiments and folders of interesting images and animations. From a time
point of view, don't rely only on what you gather during a project: if you're always collecting and tinkering, you can
draw on it when you're on the clock. He also borrowed a bit from an Adobe animation that fit thematically (Norman's
duality).

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before designing a new transition or setup, search past episode build/ folders and the raea kit for a matching shape or move.
2. Match on the motif's shape (circle, lines, grid), not on the old topic.
3. Re-fit any reused piece to this episode's hero object, light and accent colour.
4. After each episode, note any unused experiment in episode.md so it can be found later.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Search `04_episodes/*/build/` and `tools/raea/kit/` (ui.tsx, iso.tsx, icons.tsx, BlendShot.tsx) before building.
Assets reused from libraries stay logged in `00_bible/ASSET-SOURCES.md`.

### Worked example
Topic: DuitNow QR at a gerai.
BEFORE: build a new "squares assemble" transition from scratch for L05.
AFTER: an earlier episode's grid-reveal (tiles flipping into a screen) is found in its build folder; re-fitted so the QR
modules flip into the bank app's success screen, in this episode's accent; saves the build and stays object-based.

### Check
- Were past builds and the kit searched before building new?
- Does the reused piece match the motif's shape?
- Was it re-fitted to this episode's hero, light and accent?
