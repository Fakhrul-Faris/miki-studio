## CREATIVE_INTENT
Harris separates "evidence" — "you have to see this piece of evidence to prove the fact" — from "soul moments, non-evidence
moments… the visual vibes", e.g. "for soul moments before chapter titles, find or create simplified versions of these
[cryptic] symbols". Soul visuals give each video its own texture without carrying facts.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Tag every shot-list row E (shows the proof of the line) or S (mood, no fact).
2. Make every RM number, rule or step an E row showing the literal thing.
3. Place S rows at step breaks, before the zoom-out, and at the loop.
4. Keep S rows ≤ ~1 in 4 setups (~ inferred for 55–90 s).
5. Build S visuals from the episode's own motif (one recurring object or symbol), simplified.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Add an E/S marker to the shot-list "Setup and camera" cell; S rows get a "Feeling" line in the frame brief.
### Worked example
Topic: a laksa stall in Ipoh, 3 steps to set a price (numbers illustrative).
BEFORE: every row a literal ingredient close-up; step breaks feel flat.
AFTER: rows 5, 9, 13 = S: the stall's steaming ladle, simplified and lit in the accent, rises over the pot as the step number "1/2/3" appears; all other rows E (noodles RM0.80 on a scale, broth cost counter).
### Check
- Is every row tagged E or S?
- Is every fact on an E row?
- Do S rows reuse one episode motif and stay ≤ ~1 in 4?
