## CREATIVE_INTENT
A low horizon line emphasises tall shapes and depth and is simpler to draw; a high horizon line leaves more empty
ground and makes scale/perspective harder to read, but suits shots with something to look down at.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before setting camera height for a wide/establishing setup, decide: does the line need depth/scale drama (low
   horizon) or a look-down view of something on the ground (high horizon)?
2. For drama/scale, keep the camera near the ground, horizon low in frame.
3. For something best seen from above, raise the camera; accept this makes perspective/scale cues harder and
   compensate with scale props.

### Worked example
BEFORE: an establishing shot of a row of shoplots picked at an arbitrary camera height.
AFTER: Decision: "low horizon (camera near ground) so the shoplots feel tall and the street recedes dramatically,
matching the line's 'this used to be a whole street of them'."
