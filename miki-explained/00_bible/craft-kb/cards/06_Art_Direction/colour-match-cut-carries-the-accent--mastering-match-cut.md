## METADATA

## CREATIVE_INTENT
A cyclist's red jacket cuts to a woman with a very similar coloured umbrella. Found by coincidence — "by looking at my
footage with a certain eye, looking for matching traits I found these two and synced it up".

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before briefing B, note where A's accent sits on screen at the cut.
2. Place B's accent object at the same screen region, same hue and saturation.
3. Keep everything else neutral on both sides.
4. Cut on the word naming B's accent object.
5. When reviewing a board, scan all stills for matching colour spots and reorder joins to use them.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Frame brief: Eye = "accent at upper-left third". ref_<shot>.md: same accent hex in both palettes.
### Worked example
Topic: "Tag SALE RM89 — resit pun sama ke?" (illustrative price).
- BEFORE: a red sale sign → cut → a receipt.
- AFTER: the red "SALE" tag at upper-left third; cut on "bayar"; the receipt's total "RM89" printed in the same red at the upper-left third; rest neutral.
### Check
- Same hue at the same screen region across the cut?
- Is the accent on what the line is about in both setups?
- Only one accent colour in each frame?
