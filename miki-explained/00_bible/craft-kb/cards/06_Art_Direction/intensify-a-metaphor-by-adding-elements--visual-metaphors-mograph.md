## METADATA

## CREATIVE_INTENT
Showing beats saying "I'm very sad". The article escalates one image: a character's tears grow until they become a sea;
because the sadness is deep, add storms, lightning, a tiny raft adrift among huge waves, sharks — "intensifying more and
more that feeling of suffocation."

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before you plan, decide how deep the feeling is (mild / growing / severe).
2. Setup 1: the metaphor at small scale.
3. Setup 2: the same image grown (scale, count).
4. Setup 3 (severe only): add one element that raises the stakes, from the same world.
5. Tie each step to a spoken word.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
2–3 shot-list rows, each ~1.5–2 s, same hero, escalating state.

### Worked example
Line: "Hutang kad kredit ni membesar sendiri." (illustrative; balance growth by interest is real)
- AFTER: row 1 a RM1,000 statement on a desk; row 2 the statement's pages multiply into a stack, counter RM1,180;
  row 3 the stack's shadow swallows the wallet beside it, counter RM1,400 in accent.

### Check
- Does every escalation step stay in one metaphor?
- Does each step land on a word?
- Is the extra element from the same world?
