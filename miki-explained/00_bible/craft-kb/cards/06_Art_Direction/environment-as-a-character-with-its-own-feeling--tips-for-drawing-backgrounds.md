## METADATA
06_Art_Direction | 2D background art (general) | frame brief (Feeling / Decision)

## CREATIVE_INTENT
An environment makes the viewer feel something — fun or sterile, inviting or intimidating — even when it seems
undesigned; bright hard-edged rooms read "intense/technical", dim worn rooms read "relaxed". Treat the surroundings
as a character, not neutral backdrop.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before dressing a setup, name the feeling the surroundings should give (clinical, warm, risky, safe), matching
   the line.
2. Pick hard/clean vs soft/worn surface treatment, and bright vs dim lighting, to match that feeling — not by
   default.
3. Gather 2–3 real reference photos of the place-type before designing, rather than inventing from memory.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Frame brief "Feeling" names the surroundings' mood word; "Decision" states the surface/material/lighting choice
that delivers it.

### Worked example
Topic: a loan shark's office vs a legit bank counter (illustrative).
BEFORE: both shots use the same neutral dark macro surface.
AFTER: Feeling "risky, cold" → Decision "harder, colder surface reflections, tighter framing" vs Feeling "safe,
orderly" → Decision "cleaner, evenly lit surface, more breathing room".

### Check
- Does every setup's surface/lighting choice trace to a named feeling, not a default?
- Was real reference gathered before deciding the look?
