## CREATIVE_INTENT
A long concert sequence was "broken down into manageable sections to each have their own clear color statement,"
so a single continuous beat doesn't blur into visual sameness even though it's one scene.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. For a multi-step script (3–5 numbered steps), treat each step as its own section.
2. Give each section one clear, distinct light statement (not a new hue — a distinct level/ratio/angle).
3. Make sure no two adjacent sections share the same statement.
4. Check the sequence read as a strip of thumbnails: each step should be identifiable by its lighting alone.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Shot-list: add a light-ratio note per numbered step (e.g. "step 1: flat even light," "step 2: hard side light,"
"step 3: backlit silhouette") so steps are visually distinct even muted, paired with "vary light ratios per setup"
(06_Art_Direction, invisible-line card) for the actual values.

### Worked example
Topic: "3 cara elak kena scam QR palsu" (how-to, 3 steps).
BEFORE: all three steps lit the same flat top light.
AFTER: step 1 (spot the fake QR) flat even light; step 2 (check the merchant name) hard side light picks out the
mismatch; step 3 (report it) backlit silhouette on the phone being put down — three sections, one accent colour
throughout, each with a different light statement.

### Check
- Does each numbered step have a distinct light statement from its neighbours?
- Does the accent colour stay the same hue throughout (no new hues per section)?
- Does the strip of steps read as distinct even as stills?
