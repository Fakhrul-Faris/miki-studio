## CREATIVE_INTENT
Once a campaign (or here, a video series) lands on a working idea, the same creative formula and the same visual
ingredient should be reused across every touchpoint, "threaded across these channels" — so the audience recognises the
series from the ingredient alone, not just the logo.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. For a recurring series, pick one ingredient (a recurring prop type, a camera opening move, a graphic device) that
   can appear in every episode regardless of topic.
2. Reuse it in the same position/role every time (e.g. always the frame-0 object, always the same counter style).
3. Check it survives topic changes — it must work whether the episode is about a warung or a phone bill.
4. Don't introduce a second, competing recurring device in the same series.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Add the recurring device to the series bible (e.g. `RECIPE-D.md` or the series-portfolio tracker) as a fixed
ingredient, separate from any one episode's own ingredients (which still follow the 2–3-ingredients rule per episode).

### Worked example
Series: "Everyday Owners." Recurring device: every episode opens on the same macro object type (a cash drawer, in
some form) before cutting to that owner's specific business — so viewers recognise the series within the first second
even before the logo appears.

### Check
- Is there one ingredient reused across every episode of the series?
- Does it still work when the topic changes?
- Is it distinct from, and doesn't compete with, that episode's own 2–3 ingredients?

## NOTES
- Visual gaps: the source's own "touchpoints" (popup, out-of-home ad, social ad) don't map 1:1 to our single-platform
  series, so it's adapted to mean "across episodes" instead of "across channels."
