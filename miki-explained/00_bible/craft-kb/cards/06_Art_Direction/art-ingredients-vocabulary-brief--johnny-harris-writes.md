## METADATA
06_Art_Direction | Google Docs "art ingredients" tab → mood board by a visual producer | art direction lines, ref_<shot>.md

## CREATIVE_INTENT
"Every single video has its own art direction that suits the story" — no fixed channel branding package. While writing,
Harris notes ideas in an "art ingredients" tab: how archival stills are treated ("mysterious cryptic symbols", framed a
certain way), shooting vibe ("cozy, warm vibes", A-cam and B-cam), animation/text style ("Chernobyl fuzz": the HBO
*Chernobyl* 1980s Soviet-TV titles, with fuzz on the text). The visual producer turns the words into a mood board (palette,
title templates, set design: "similar to <past video>, a bit moody, scientific posters, brains, practicals, analog tech,
Natural History Museum clutter", animation style); he reacts. A shared vocabulary ("cozy", "moody") made the team work;
without it "there was disconnect". He screenshots references as he writes (old yellowed paper, map styles, Pinterest).

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Open an "Art ingredients" section while drafting the script, not after.
2. Write each idea as a named vocabulary phrase (2–3 words) plus one reference screenshot.
3. Cover: hero object treatment, surface/set, accent use, text/number treatment, props/clutter.
4. Reuse existing house vocabulary words before inventing new ones; define any new word in 1 line.
5. Turn the ingredients into the five-line art direction (hero, accent, what is grey, font, sets).
6. Build a 6–9 image mood board from the screenshots; get the user's reaction before the board.

### Worked example
Topic: a goldsmith shop in Kota Bharu pricing a 916 ring (numbers illustrative).
BEFORE: "Dark room, gold ring, accent gold." (generic)
AFTER ingredients: "jeweller's hush" (black velvet tray, one top light, dust in the beam) · "scale truth" (the digital
scale's green LCD is the only accent) · "receipt ink" (numbers appear as thermal-printer type) · "glass case clutter" (soft-
focus trays behind). Five-line AD: hero = ring on a velvet tray; accent = LCD green; grey = everything else; font = house; sets = velvet tray, scale pan, counter glass.
