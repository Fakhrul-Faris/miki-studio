## METADATA
06_Art_Direction | Adam Bennett | pre-board ideation

## CREATIVE_INTENT
Sketch while researching — the swinging light bulb in the basement scene, shapes and motifs in the research. Sketches
"don't have to be amazing, you're not sending these to a client — they're just for you, as long as you can make out
what they are". Mind-mapping and sketching run together and continue even after animating starts. Some links only
appear pictorially: he sketched Hitchcock next to taxidermied birds, then tried an owl (unused, but the mind map alone
wouldn't have produced it). He drops collected images into a rough mood board (PureRef) to move them around and see what
pairs: putting two images of the Bates house together gave him the idea for a 2.5D camera move.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before the grey-box board, drop 10–20 reference images into one rough board.
2. Move images next to each other in pairs; note any pair that suggests a move or transition.
3. Write each idea as a one-line thumbnail note ("two shelf angles → parallax push").
4. Keep notes rough; never polish them for the user.
5. Promote only ideas that serve a script line into frame briefs.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Work in the episode's scratch/ref folder; grey-box stills or quick contact-sheet composites stand in for hand sketches
(no hand-drawn art in deliverables). Record promoted pairs as **Ref** in the frame brief.

### Worked example
Topic: QR payments at a gerai.
BEFORE: one ref per setup chosen in isolation.
AFTER: on the board, a top-down shot of a laminated QR stand sits beside a phone-camera viewfinder image → idea: L04
camera starts top-down on the stand, the phone frame slides over it and the QR squares snap into the viewfinder grid
(transition from scene objects). Promoted to the L04→L05 frame brief.

### Check
- Was a rough pairing board made before the grey-box board?
- Did at least one transition or move come from a pair?
- Are the rough notes kept out of user-facing deliverables?
