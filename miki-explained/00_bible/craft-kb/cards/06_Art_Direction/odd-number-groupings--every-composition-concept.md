## CREATIVE_INTENT
"If you have multiple similar objects in your scene, odd numbers tend to be more visually pleasing than even numbers."

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before dressing, count similar objects in the frame.
2. If the count is free (not stated by the script), make it odd: 3, 5, 7.
3. If the script states a number, show that exact number.
4. With an odd group, put the middle one (or the hero) at the group's focal position.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Board check row: "similar-object groups odd unless a scripted count". Shot-list Setup column: write the count ("3 cups").
### Worked example
Topic: "Kedai kopi kecil: berapa cawan sehari baru untung?" (illustrative).
- BEFORE: two cups and four sugar sachets dressing the hero kettle.
- AFTER: three cups (middle one sharp under the key light), five sachets in soft focus behind. The L05 line "120 cawan
  sehari" is shown literally (counter ticks to 120) — not forced odd.
### Check
- Is every free-count group odd?
- Does every scripted number appear exactly?
- Is the hero at the group's centre or otherwise clearly first?
