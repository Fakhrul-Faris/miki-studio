## CREATIVE_INTENT
The master "confirms the location and geography of the scene" and "clarifies which characters are in the scene and
where they are in relationship to each other". The Godfather Part II dinner plays mostly in the master to emphasise
the family's closeness — until Michael says he enlisted; then the framing "makes Michael's split from the family
visual. From this… to this. And we feel the emptiness around him." The master also gives the editor something to cut
out to.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Hold the group in one master setup while the line describes them as a set.
2. On the word that names the odd one, cut or fly to a frame where it is alone with empty space around it.
3. Give the isolated item the accent colour.
4. Optionally return to the master to show the gap it left.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Shot list: row A `MS/master · all items`; row B `MCU · the odd item alone, 60 % negative space`.

### Worked example
Topic: "Satu kos kecil yang makan untung kedai" (illustrative monthly costs).
- BEFORE: a list graphic of costs.
- AFTER: `| 6 | "Sewa, bil, gaji — semua dah kira" | master · five labelled jars on a shelf in a row | each jar label lights on its word |` → `| 7 | "Tapi **langganan app** RM180 sebulan…" | MCU · that one jar alone at frame right, empty shelf left | jar label turns accent red on "langganan app" |`

### Check
- Is the group shown together first?
- Is the odd one framed alone with clear empty space?
- Is the accent only on it?
