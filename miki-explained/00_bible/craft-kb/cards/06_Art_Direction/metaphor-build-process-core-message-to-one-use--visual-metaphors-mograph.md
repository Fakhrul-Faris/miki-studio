## METADATA

## CREATIVE_INTENT
Four steps: (1) **Understand the core message** — main message, central conflict and emotion, and whether it applies
to the character, the world or both. (2) **Associate ideas** — brainstorm visual elements; "don't settle for the first
idea that comes to mind". (3) **Choose metaphors people will understand** — easy to relate, reinforcing, not confusing.
(4) **Use them creatively and in moderation** — overuse exhausts the viewer; pick the most appropriate one and make it
powerful.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before you brainstorm, write the line's core message in ≤ 8 words.
2. List ≥ 5 candidate images; cross out the first one.
3. Keep only images a 12-year-old in Malaysia recognises in 1 s.
4. Pick one; use it once in the episode.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Write the candidate list in the frame brief's "Not generic because" field (why the chosen one beats the first idea).

### Worked example
Line: "Duit korang bocor sikit-sikit."
- BEFORE: first idea, a leaking tap, used again at L06 and L09.
- AFTER: candidates — tap, tyre, sieve, torn pocket, wallet seam; first (tap) crossed out; chosen: wallet seam (the
  hero, readable in 1 s); used only at L02.

### Check
- Were ≥ 5 candidates listed and the first rejected?
- Does it read in 1 s on a phone?
- Is it used only once?
