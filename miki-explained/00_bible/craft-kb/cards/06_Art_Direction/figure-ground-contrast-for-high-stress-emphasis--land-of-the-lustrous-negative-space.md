## CREATIVE_INTENT
Strongly separating the subject from its ground (a very dark object on a light field, or the reverse — "white on
black is known to be visually stressing on the eyes when applied") sharpens emotional stress at a scene's most
intense beat; it is not used as a default look.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before the episode's single most stressful/shocking line, check the planned frame isn't visually busy.
2. Strip the setup to the hero object against one flat, strongly contrasting ground (very dark object on a light
   field, or vice versa).
3. Reserve this maximum figure-ground contrast for the one or two sharpest beats — not every setup.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Frame brief Decision for that one beat: hero silhouette against a flat, opposite-value background (no gradient, no
secondary props); board check for that row should confirm the hero is fully separated in value from its ground.

### Worked example
BEFORE: the "scam revealed" line uses the same dark-room-with-bloom look as every other beat.
AFTER: for that one line only, the counterfeit note is shown stark black against a flat white surface (or the
reverse), nothing else in frame.

### Check
- Is this treatment used on at most the episode's one or two sharpest beats?
- Is the hero fully separated in value (not blended in brightness) from its background for that beat?
