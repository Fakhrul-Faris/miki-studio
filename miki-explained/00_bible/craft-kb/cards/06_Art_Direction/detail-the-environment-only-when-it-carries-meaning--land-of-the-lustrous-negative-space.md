## CREATIVE_INTENT
A minimal, undetailed environment is a deliberate choice, not laziness, when the setting carries no narrative
information that beat needs — "if it's not adding anything significant... you don't need it." Detail is spent only
where it serves the line, not evenly everywhere.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before adding set-dressing to a setup, ask whether the environment itself is part of what the line is saying.
2. If the line is about the object only, keep the surroundings dark/empty and detail nothing beyond the hero.
3. If the line is about a place or context (a crowded counter, a street), spend the detail there.
4. Don't add ambient props "to fill space" without a reason tied to the line.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Frame brief "Decision" states explicitly whether this setup is object-only (dark room, no dressing) or
environment-matters (named set-dressing items), guarding against over-dressing a macro-object shot that doesn't
need it.

### Worked example
Topic: a shot of a receipt on a counter (illustrative).
BEFORE: a fully dressed counter (till, bags, other products) even though the line is only about the receipt's
total.
AFTER: frame brief Decision: "object-only: dark surface, no other props — the line is about the number on the
receipt, not the counter."

### Check
- Does every piece of background detail trace to something the line needs?
- Is a line about the object-only kept free of unrelated set-dressing?
