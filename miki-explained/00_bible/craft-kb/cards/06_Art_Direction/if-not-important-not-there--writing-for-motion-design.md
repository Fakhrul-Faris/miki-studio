## METADATA
06_Art_Direction | teleplay/commercial script convention | frame brief (Decision, Eye), shot-list Setup cell

## CREATIVE_INTENT
A 30 Rock shooting draft says "Jack and Liz are there, this is on Jack's computer" — "we don't describe the potted
plants on the desk or what's in the background… if it's not important it's not there." In motion design the video
column works the same: "It's not enough to say a game of chess is starting because that could mean something to
anybody. What's in the scene, what's important that we see? If it's not important, consider removing it." If something
important must be in frame, describe it there, because the storyboard will be drawn from that cell.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before writing a Setup cell, list what the viewer must see to understand the line.
2. Keep that list to the hero plus at most ~2 supporting items (~).
3. Write each kept item with its state ("receipt, total RM45.80, crumpled").
4. Delete every item that doesn't serve the line.
5. Replace generic scene words ("a shop", "a game starting") with the specific visible items.
6. At board stage, remove any prop that appeared without being listed.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Frame brief: **Decision** = the kept list; **Eye** = the one item the eye lands on first. Shot list Setup cell = the
same list. Board check (RECIPE-D §7): unlisted props are clutter; remove them in the build.

### Worked example
Topic: a laundry shop's per-kg pricing (illustrative).
BEFORE: Setup "Inside a laundry shop, customer at counter, machines, posters, plants."
AFTER: Setup "macro, three-quarter top: digital scale reading 3.0 kg (hero); folded towel stack on it; price card
'RM4/kg' at the edge, out of focus." Nothing else in frame.

### Check
- Does every listed item serve the line?
- Is the hero plus ≤ ~2 supporting items all that is listed?
- Are generic scene words replaced by specific items with states?
- Did the board add no unlisted props?

## NOTES
- The "~2 supporting items" cap is inferred from Style D, not from the source.
