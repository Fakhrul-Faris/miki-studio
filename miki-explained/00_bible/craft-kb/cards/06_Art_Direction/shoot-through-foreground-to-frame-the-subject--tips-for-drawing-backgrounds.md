## METADATA
06_Art_Direction | 2D background art (general) | frame brief (Decision) + raea shallow DoF

## CREATIVE_INTENT
Place a dramatic foreground element (partially out of focus, framing the edges) between camera and subject so the
viewer's eye is drawn through it to the subject, rather than shooting the subject in a clear open frame — "we use
this in cinema all the time... to highlight a story beat or... create a sense of tension."

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before finalising a wide setup's camera, check whether a nearby real object could sit between camera and hero.
2. If so, move the camera so that object partially frames the edges of the shot (corner, top, or one side), kept
   soft/out of focus.
3. Keep the hero itself fully readable in the remaining open frame.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
raea: place a secondary prop closer to camera than the hero, outside the DoF focal plane so it blurs per Style D's
shallow DoF; frame brief Decision notes which real prop frames the shot.

### Worked example
BEFORE: an open, unframed wide shot of a shuttered shop.
AFTER: Decision: "shoot through a blurred stack of unsold stock in the foreground, camera-left, framing the
shuttered sign in the clear two-thirds of frame."

### Check
- Is the foreground element a real object from the scene, not an invented prop?
- Does it stay soft/out of focus so it doesn't compete with the hero for sharpness?
