## CREATIVE_INTENT
In Phantom Thread's omelette scene, the audience knows the omelette is poisoned. When Reynolds takes the bite, Alma's
grin vanishes; Anderson moves to a close-up "to get the extreme nuances of her reaction", and "in this context, a subtle
tilt of the head feels enormous". A simple swallow then "acts as an agreement". Context set up beforehand plus a
close-up makes a small change read as a big event.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before the beat, set up what the viewer knows (the stake) in the previous setup.
2. Cut or fly to a macro close-up on the one object that will change.
3. Hold the frame still-ish: drift ≤ ~13 cm, no other events.
4. Make one small change on the key word (one digit, one edge, one light).
5. Keep the change small: ≤ ~10 % of the object's size or one character of text.
6. Hold ~0.5–1 s after the change before the next setup.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Shot list row: "Macro push (RECIPE-D §14), fov 26, f/2.8; on '<word>' only <one change>; nothing else moves." Frame brief
Eye = the changing part; Feeling = the stake from the previous setup.
### Worked example
Topic: a kedai makan's electricity bill after adding a second freezer (illustrative).
BEFORE row: "Wide of the kedai, bill appears, numbers fly in, arrows, freezer, icons."
AFTER rows: L05 "Bil bulan lepas: RM480." (wide on the bill on the counter) → L06 "Bulan ni…" macro on the bill's total;
on "RM720" only the first digit rolls 4 → 7 in accent; hold 0.8 s.
### Check
- Was the stake set in the setup before?
- Is there exactly one change, and is it small?
- Does nothing else move during the change?
- Is there a hold after the change?
