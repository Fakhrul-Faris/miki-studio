## CREATIVE_INTENT
"Two visually similar objects in an environment that is different, where the composition is similar — the subjects
match." In the source's boxed-wine explainer the pouring scene matches "between all of the heads to show the passing
of time as our drinker ages", with a subtle rotation matching the head tilt.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Choose one subject that recurs (same object type or the same object in new states).
2. Lock its screen position and size across the sequence (same centre, same 25–60 % band).
3. Change the environment or state between cuts (age, wear, amount, place).
4. Add one small motion that continues across every cut (source: head tilt rotation).
5. Cut on the words that name each time step.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Frame brief: "Eye" = same screen point in every setup; "Moves" = the carried tilt/rotation; "Handover" = next state.
raea: reuse the camera transform across the setups and swap the scene/state; carry a 3–5° rotation with
`Easing.inOut` across cuts. ⚠ board check "no two stills from the same angle": vary the lens height slightly or the
background, keep the subject's screen position.

### Worked example
Topic: "Simpan RM100 sebulan — 1, 5, 10 tahun" (plain arithmetic, no interest: RM1,200 / RM6,000 / RM12,000).
- BEFORE: three unrelated shots — a piggy bank, a calendar, a bank card.
- AFTER: the same glass jar centred at 40 % frame height in three setups: on a student desk ("setahun, RM1,200"), on a kedai counter ("lima tahun, RM6,000"), on a home shelf ("sepuluh tahun, RM12,000"); notes fill it more each time; one slow 4° turn carries across both cuts; the amount label in the accent colour.

### Check
- Is the subject at the same screen position and size in every setup?
- Does the environment/state change on each cut?
- Does one small motion carry across the cuts?
- Do the shown numbers add up?
