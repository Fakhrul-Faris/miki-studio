## CREATIVE_INTENT
The line of action is "your best friend" for a dynamic pose: the longest unbroken line through the pose, kept as
C-shaped as possible. Complex poses may use two lines, never more. A **positive** line of action (arching up/forward)
reads as confidence; a **negative** one (sagging, curling in) reads as sadness or shyness. Pick the one the concept
needs, then use it strongly (exaggerate the limbs' curves) or as a soft suggestion.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before posing, decide from the concept: positive (confident) or negative (sad/shy) line.
2. Draw the longest unbroken line through the pose; keep it C-shaped.
3. Use at most 2 lines of action for a complex pose.
4. Pick the library pose whose spine follows that line.
5. Decide strength: exaggerate along the line, or keep it a soft suggestion.
6. Prefer C and S curves; avoid many sharp straight lines in the silhouette.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Frame brief **Feeling** field → positive/negative line. Style A/C: pick the MPFB pose by spine curve (chest up = positive,
chest caved = negative); check on a still by tracing the spine. Style D (no people): apply to object chains — a row of
coins rising in a C-arc reads as gain (positive), sagging toward the floor as loss (negative); place items along a curve
`x = a·sin(t)` rather than a straight row.
### Worked example
Line: "Untung RM1,200 sebulan bila stok laku cepat." (illustrative)
- BEFORE: coins in a flat straight row on the counter.
- AFTER (shot-list row): `| 6 | "untung RM1,200" | low 3/4 on the counter, 85 mm | coin stack row rises in one upward C-arc toward the till drawer; counter ticks to RM1,200 on "RM1,200" | line-of-action |`
  For the loss line the same coins sag in a downward C toward the floor edge.
### Check
- Is there one (max two) clear C-shaped line through the subject or object chain?
- Does its direction (up/positive, down/negative) match the line's feeling?
- Is the figure static?
