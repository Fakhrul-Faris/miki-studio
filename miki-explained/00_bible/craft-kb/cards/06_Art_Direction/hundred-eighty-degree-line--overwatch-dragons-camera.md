## CREATIVE_INTENT
"As soon as the other character is established … there's a line between them. There's a right side from this line and a
left side. As soon as you establish the line between the two characters that are interacting, you always want to stay on
the same side." In the trailer, "Genji is always on the right and Hanzo is always on the left."

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. When the first setup shows both subjects, draw the line between them in the shot list.
2. Note which side the camera is on and which subject is screen-left.
3. Keep every later camera position on that side of the line.
4. Check each board still: subject A stays screen-left, B screen-right.
5. To cross the line, move the camera across it on screen in one continuous flight (never cut across).

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Shot-list: add `A left / B right` to the camera cell of every comparison row. raea: compute the line from A to B; reject a
camera pose whose position lies on the other side (sign of the 2D cross product of `B−A` and `cam−A` must not change).

### Worked example
Topic: "Bayar tunai vs QR — mana lagi murah untuk peniaga" (illustrative fees).
- BEFORE: `| 3 | cash tin left, QR stand right |` → `| 5 | reverse angle: QR stand now left |` (viewers confuse which is which)
- AFTER: `| 3 | "Tunai kiri, QR kanan" | wide low angle, cash tin left, QR stand right | labels fade in | hundred-eighty-degree-line |`
  `| 5 | "QR caj 0.5 % (illustrative)" | close on the QR stand, camera still on the front side, stand stays screen-right | "0.5 %" tag fades in | hundred-eighty-degree-line |`

### Check
- Is the A–B line written in the shot list?
- Does every board still keep A on the same screen side?
- Is any line crossing done by a visible camera flight, not a cut?
