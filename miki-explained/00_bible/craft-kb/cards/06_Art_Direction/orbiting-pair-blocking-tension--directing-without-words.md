## CREATIVE_INTENT
In Beau Travail, rivals Galoup and Sentain "circle each other like boxers in a ring, getting closer and closer". The
movement "creates compelling coverage, letting the desert spin around them and revealing subtly that they are being
watched by the rest of the legionnaires". Blocking states the relationship (neither backs down) and the score's
crescendos elevate the tension.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Place the two compared objects facing each other on the hero surface.
2. Orbit the camera around their midpoint, or rotate both on a turntable, eased in-out.
3. Close the gap between the objects ~20–40 % over the setup (~).
4. Let the background turn past to reveal one context element (the shop, the crowd of products).
5. Keep orbit speed ≤ ~130°/s; use ~60–120° over 2–4 s (~).
6. Resolve on the next word: one object takes the accent, the other dims.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Shot-list row: "Orbit (RECIPE-D §14, look-at on the pair's midpoint), objects slide closer; on '<answer word>' winner
lit with accent, loser to 40 % light." Sound: rising room tone, not a music crescendo stinger (sparse SFX rule).
### Worked example
Topic: 1 kg rice bag vs 5 kg rice bag per kilo (illustrative).
BEFORE: split screen, two bags, a "VS" graphic.
AFTER: L04 "Beras 1 kilo RM3.60. Beras 5 kilo RM15." Both bags on a kedai counter, camera orbits 90° over 3 s, bags
slide closer, the shelf behind turns into view. L05 "Sekilo, RM3 je." on "RM3" the 5 kg bag's tag lights accent, the
1 kg bag dims.
### Check
- Do the two objects close the gap during the orbit?
- Is the orbit eased and ≤ ~130°/s?
- Does the background reveal one context element?
- Does the answer word resolve to one accent?
