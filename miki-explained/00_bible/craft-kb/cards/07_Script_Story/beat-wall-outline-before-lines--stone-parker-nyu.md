## CREATIVE_INTENT
The South Park writers' room has one whole wall split into three acts, with different coloured markers; they place
beats ("this would be a funny scene if we had this") on the wall before writing, then test the joins between beats.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before you write lines, split a beat outline into 3 acts: setup (And/hook), complication (But), resolution (Therefore/end).
2. Write each beat as one short phrase, one beat per row.
3. Use a distinct marker per act (in markdown: a column "act 1/2/3").
4. Test the joins with the but/therefore rule before writing any caption.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
In `episode.md`, add "Beats v0" above "Script v1": ~6–8 beat phrases with an act column and a join column. Only after
the joins pass, expand into the ~11 lines.

### Worked example
Topic: a kedai runcit's free plastic bags (numbers illustrative).
BEFORE: start straight on L01 wording and fix structure later.
AFTER (Beats v0): act 1 "beg plastik nampak percuma" → but → act 2 "setiap beg kos kedai 5 sen" → therefore →
"300 pelanggan sehari = RM15 sehari" → but → "sebulan dah RM450, sama macam sewa satu rak" → therefore → act 3
"patut ke kedai caj beg?"

### Check
- Is there a beat outline with acts before Script v1?
- Does each act have at least one beat?
- Have the joins been tested before writing lines?

## NOTES
- Coloured markers on a physical wall are visual only; their meaning (per act / per writer) is not stated in speech.
