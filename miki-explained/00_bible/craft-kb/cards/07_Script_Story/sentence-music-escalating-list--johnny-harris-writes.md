## CREATIVE_INTENT
"Joseph Smith, the leader of 26,000 Mormons, father of 14 children, husband to 40 wives" — "I like the music of that
sentence, it has a rhythm", and the last item hits hardest. Harris writes "for the ear as much as for the eye" (people
watch while making dinner); he "speaks it in my mind" while writing. Alliterated concrete pairs ("undetonated bombs and
abandoned bones") carry the poetic voice.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. For an intro or end line, list three facts about the subject with numbers.
2. Order them from expected to surprising; the last item is the hook.
3. Use the same grammatical shape for each item (noun + of + number).
4. Keep each item ≤ 5 words.
5. Read the line aloud at ~3.4 words/s; cut any syllable that trips.
6. In the voice text, put a comma pause between items and "…" before the last item.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Script line + voice text row (Eleven v3: numbers as words, ≤ 2 tags/line).
### Worked example
Topic: a car wash in Klang (illustrative).
BEFORE: "Kedai cuci kereta ni dah lama beroperasi dan ramai pelanggan."
AFTER caption: "Satu kedai cuci kereta. 12 tahun. 80 kereta sehari. Satu je pekerja."
Voice: "Satu kedai cuci kereta, dua belas tahun, lapan puluh kereta sehari… satu je pekerja."
### Check
- Do the items share one shape and rise to a surprise?
- Is each item ≤ 5 words?
- Does the line read cleanly aloud at ~3.4 words/s?
