## CREATIVE_INTENT
Of the 7 sub-minute stories, "4 of 7 opened on a named buyer problem. 1 of 7 opened on a general threat. 2 of 7 opened on
a setting." "Under a minute, open on the problem. You don't have time to build a world and then break it." The product
appears "after the problem and before the result… If your product appears in the first frame, the viewer has no reason
yet to care about it." End "on the changed state, not the logo" (EU carousel stopping, Lendi's "SOLD" home). For software:
draw the mess as shapes first, "but show the interface before the end. Buyers want to see what they'd actually use."
Measure: "A drop before the change lands means the setup is too long."

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Make L01 name the viewer's problem, not a setting or a general threat.
2. Hold the fix/feature until the problem has landed (not before L03).
3. Keep the brand logo visible on the hero from frame 1 as identity only (house rule).
4. For app/UI topics, show the real interface before the end, after the problem shapes.
5. End on the changed state of frame 0's object, then the side-taking question.
6. After posting, if retention drops before the change lands, shorten the setup.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Script: L01–L02 problem; L03 fix promise (S16: "Kalau korang nak…, ni caranya. N step je."); L04–L08 fix; L09–L10 changed
state + number; L11 question on frame-0 framing. Post-mortem: compare the retention curve's drop point with the payoff
line's timestamp.

### Worked example
Topic: a small clothing shop on Instagram losing sales to slow replies (illustrative).
- BEFORE: L01 "Instagram ni platform yang besar untuk bisnes." (setting) … L11 "Follow untuk tips lagi!"
- AFTER: L01 "12 orang DM tanya harga semalam. 3 je dapat reply." L03 "Kalau korang nak reply laju, ni caranya. Dua step
  je." L09 "Minggu ni, 12 tanya, 12 dapat reply." (phone inbox in after-state) L11 "Customer tunggu berapa lama… sebelum
  dia lari?"

### Check
- Does L01 name a problem (not a setting)?
- Is the fix held until after the problem lands?
- Is the logo on screen from frame 1 without being the fix?
- Does the video end on frame 0's object, changed, then a question (no follow request)?
