## METADATA
07_Script_Story | short-form hook tactics | script L01–L03 (context lean), analogy lines

## CREATIVE_INTENT
Hearing something familiar unlocks comfort; hearing only unfamiliar things makes people "feel dumb" and bounce (like an
advanced physics lecture with no physics). For complex or niche topics, wrap the unknown idea in a known layer: a brand,
celebrity, movement or cultural reference used as a metaphor or comparison (e.g. how Taylor Swift's financial advisor
would plan her estate around the Eras Tour earnings, or naming her among the elite wealth you serve). It needn't carry
the whole video; it lets a small creator "draft off" existing popularity and credibility.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before L01, list the one idea a viewer won't know (the unknown).
2. List 3 references nearly every Malaysian 18–34 knows (a sale event, a national habit, a famous product).
3. Pick the reference whose shape matches the unknown (same mechanism, smaller scale).
4. Write the comparison in one line: "<known>, tapi versi <unknown>".
5. Make no factual claim about the reference beyond what is common knowledge.
6. Use it once in the hook or mechanism; don't build the episode around it.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Use in the context lean (L01–L02) or the first Mechanism line. Shot list: show the topic business's object, not the
reference brand's logo (one brand on screen); the reference lives in the voice and caption. Claim ledger: mark the
reference "common knowledge — comparison only".

### Worked example
Topic: a kedai runcit in Kajang running a one-evening flash sale (illustrative).
BEFORE: L01 "Flash sale ialah strategi diskaun yang dihadkan kepada tempoh masa tertentu."
AFTER: L01 "Korang tahu sale 11.11 online? Kedai runcit pun boleh buat versi kecil." L02 "Dua jam je, satu rak je."
L03 "Tapi kalau salah kira, kedai rugi." (Mechanism then counts: 40 tin × RM1 off = RM40 given away.)

### Check
- Is the reference known to nearly every viewer without explanation?
- Is it used as a comparison only, with no new claim about the brand/person?
- Is the topic business still the only logo on screen?
- Does the reference appear once, not as the episode's subject?
