## METADATA
07_Script_Story | general | script L02

## CREATIVE_INTENT
Fern hammers the open home: "It is an absolute miracle that no one died during this incident. Had it happened just hours
earlier…" then "How could this have happened?" "Essentially all they're doing here is amping up the stakes and asking
the question how did this happen."

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before you write L02, find the real worse outcome that almost happened (time, amount, scale).
2. State it as "Kalau … sikit lagi, …" / "Had it … just …, …" with the real number.
3. Follow immediately with the how/why question.
4. Log the counterfactual's facts in the claim ledger as verified or inferred.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Script L02 (one line, ≤ 15 words) + the question as L03 or its second half. Shot list: show the near-miss literally —
the counter or clock rolls to the worse value, then rolls back.

### Worked example
Topic: a stall owner whose QR payment went to a scammer's sticker (illustrative case).
- BEFORE: "Benda ni boleh jadi kat sesiapa."
- AFTER: "Dia rugi RM600. Kalau tak perasan seminggu lagi, RM4,000. Macam mana sticker tu boleh ada?"
  Shot: the RM600 counter rolls on to RM4,000 (accent), freezes, rolls back to RM600.

### Check
- Is the worse outcome real and logged in the claim ledger?
- Is it stated with a specific number or time?
- Is the how/why question right after it?
