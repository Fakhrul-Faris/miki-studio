## METADATA
07_Script_Story | YouTube packaging (VidIQ-style "outlier" tools, ViewStats; A/B thumbnail test) | frame-0 text, cover, caption

## CREATIVE_INTENT
"A video has to be marketable… a couple million people have to be interested." Harris writes 7 or more titles; all but one
start with "How" or "Why", "because we're in the business of answering questions and explaining processes." He checks
comps (an outlier tool shows a topic video that did 50× its channel's normal views and how it was packaged) and, since A/B
testing, prepares very different thumbnail directions (map vs "Illuminati energy") instead of refining one.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Write ≥ 7 frame-0/title options before choosing.
2. Start most options with How/Why (BM: Macam mana / Kenapa), or a contradiction that implies one.
3. Search the topic on TikTok; note the top 3 outlier videos (views ÷ account's usual views) and their first frame text.
4. Write down why each outlier was packaged that way (one line each).
5. Make 2–3 cover directions that differ in kind (object close-up vs number vs UI screen), not in wording.
6. Pick one for frame 0; keep the others for the cover test or a repost.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
In `episode.md` add "Packaging options": 7 lines, the comps table (link, views, multiple, frame-0 text), and 2–3 cover
directions. Frame 0 still obeys ≤ 8 words + contradiction; the How/Why list is the brainstorm, not the final form.
### Worked example
Topic: how to price nasi lemak at a stall in Kuantan (illustrative).
BEFORE: one option, "Tips harga nasi lemak".
AFTER: 1 "Kenapa nasi lemak RM3 boleh rugi?" 2 "Macam mana gerai kira harga sebungkus?" 3 "Laku 200 bungkus. Untung kosong?" 4 "Kenapa telur je makan separuh untung?" 5 "Macam mana nak tahu harga betul?" 6 "Jual habis, duit tak ada?" 7 "Kenapa RM3 tak cukup?". Frame 0 = 3 (contradiction, 6 words). Cover directions: A macro of the egg with "RM0.70?" ; B the receipt-total counter; C a phone calculator screen.
### Check
- Are there ≥ 7 options, most How/Why or contradiction?
- Is there a comps note with at least one outlier multiple?
- Do the cover directions differ in kind, not just wording?
- Does the chosen frame 0 still fit ≤ 8 words?
