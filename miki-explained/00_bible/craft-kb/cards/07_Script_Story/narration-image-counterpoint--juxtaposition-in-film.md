## METADATA

## CREATIVE_INTENT
"Placing narration over visuals can provide a third separate meaning that wouldn't be inferred from just one or the
other." In *The Godfather* a priest's baptism vows ("Do you renounce Satan?" "I do renounce him") play over the
executions Michael ordered — dark irony. In *Trainspotting*, "Choose life. Choose a job. Choose a career…" while the
visuals show he has made none of those choices. In *Adaptation*, "saying one thing … and doing the opposite" for comedy.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before writing the line, write the common belief in the viewer's own words.
2. Let the voice say that belief plainly (≤ 15 words).
3. Show the same object in the state that contradicts it.
4. On the next line, name the contradiction with the RM number.
5. Use it at most twice per episode.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Script: mark the line `[counterpoint]`. Shot-list: the visual cell names the contradicting state and the word it lands on.

### Worked example
Topic: "Free delivery tu betul ke free?" (illustrative).
- BEFORE: L03 "Free delivery buat korang jimat." / visual: a delivery box with a "FREE" sticker.
- AFTER: L03 "Free delivery. Jimat, kan?" / visual: the same order's two receipts side by side — the in-app price RM12.90 vs the shop-counter price RM10.90; accent on the RM2 gap on "jimat".
  L04 "Sebenarnya RM2 dah masuk dalam harga makanan." / visual: the RM2 slides out of the menu price into the box.

### Check
- Does the voice state a belief the viewer holds?
- Does the picture show the same object contradicting it?
- Does the next line resolve it with a number?
- Is it used ≤ 2 times?

## NOTES
- The worked example's prices are illustrative; whether a given platform marks up menu prices varies — present as one example order, not a rule.
