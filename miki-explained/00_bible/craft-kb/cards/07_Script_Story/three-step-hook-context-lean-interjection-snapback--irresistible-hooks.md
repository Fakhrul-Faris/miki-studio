## METADATA

## CREATIVE_INTENT
The viewer is "driving down the highway at 70 mph": the hook must make them see something, stop, turn round and come
back. It does that in 3–4 sentences by opening a curiosity loop, "the domino effect" where each line makes the next one
unmissable. Formula: (1) **context lean** — topic clarity + lean-in; (2) **scroll-stop interjection** — one "stun gun"
line built on a contrast word; (3) **contrarian snapback** — "the haymaker", a line that goes the opposite way of the
lean but stays on topic. "The bigger the shock, the bigger the snap." Shape: "if you want X, don't do Y, do Z" — and Z
must be genuinely compelling, or viewers churn and "you're a fraud".

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before you write L01, name the topic in ≤ 5 words so a viewer can self-select in or out.
2. Write the context lean (1–2 lines): the topic plus one lean-in device.
3. Pick the lean-in device from four: common ground, a benefit or pain point, a simplifying metaphor, a mind-blowing fact.
4. Write the interjection as one line opening with a contrast word: but, however, yet, although, on the other hand (Malay: "tapi", "masalahnya").
5. Make the interjection deny or downgrade what the lean just built up ("the screen is the least impressive part").
6. Write the snapback: one line that turns to a different answer on the same topic.
7. Check that the snapback's answer is paid off later in the script with a real fact or number.
8. Cut the hook to 3–4 lines total (~10–14 s at ~3.4 words/s, ≤ 15 words per line).

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Map onto RECIPE-D §3: L01 = context lean (topic + wow fact, RM number where possible), L02 = interjection ("Tapi…"),
L03 = snapback, which becomes the open loop the Mechanism/Steps answer. Frame-0 text (≤ 8 words) compresses lean +
interjection into one contradiction. Shot list: the interjection word ("tapi") is an event — the hero changes state on
it (the impressive thing dims or shrinks), and the snapback names the new object the camera flies to.
Source examples: Vegas Sphere ("biggest screen ever built, 20× bigger than an IMAX / but get this, the screen is the
least impressive part / because the most impressive part is the audio"); mortgage ("3 massive mistakes… average person
pays an extra $122,000… most think it's interest rates / but it has nothing to do with that / the biggest waste is…").

### Worked example
Topic: real profit of a burger stall in Shah Alam (numbers illustrative).
BEFORE: L01 "Hari ni kita cerita pasal untung gerai burger." L02 "Ramai nak tahu berapa dia dapat."
AFTER: frame-0 text "RM600 masuk. Untung? Bukan RM600."
- L01 (lean, wow fact): "Gerai burger ni jual 100 biji semalam. RM600 masuk."
- L02 (interjection): "Tapi RM600 tu bukan duit dia."
- L03 (snapback): "Yang tinggal dalam poket? RM150 je. Ni ke mana RM450 lagi."
L04 onward answers the loop: buns + patties RM250, sos + bungkus RM60, gas RM40, tapak RM100 (total RM450).

### Check
- Is the topic clear from L01 alone (≤ 2 s of audio)?
- Does L02 start with a contrast word and push against L01?
- Does L03 turn to a different answer on the same topic, not a new topic?
- Is the snapback's promise paid off later with a real number?
- Are the hook lines 3–4 lines, each ≤ 15 words?
