## METADATA
07_Script_Story | general | Script L01–L02, pinch lines

## CREATIVE_INTENT
The inciting event (~12 %) is "the moment when they brush up against the conflict for the first time", and "is a choice…
something they could still walk away from". "For an inciting event to have sufficient impact, stakes need to be built
beforehand": Doctor Strange first establishes Strange as a brilliant surgeon, so when his hands are injured "we understand
that this has changed his life". Pinch points later "are specifically about showing what is at stake… what are they
possibly going to lose if they continue on this course".

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before the problem line, show what the owner/viewer has (income, savings, a full shop) in one line.
2. Hit that exact thing with the inciting event in the next line.
3. Keep it a choice: the owner could still walk away.
4. At pinch lines, restate what could be lost, with its RM figure.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Script: L01 shows the asset (with number); L02 the threat to it. Shot list: L01 row shows the asset whole (full till, stack
of notes); L02 row changes that same object (notes thinning). Pinch rows return to it.

### Worked example
Topic: a tuition centre owner and late fees (illustrative).
- BEFORE: L01 "Yuran lewat boleh rosakkan bisnes tuisyen."
- AFTER: L01 "Cikgu Aina ada 40 student, RM150 sebulan — RM6,000 masuk." L02 "Tapi bulan ni, 12 orang belum bayar.
  RM1,800 tergantung." (12 × RM150.)

### Check
- Is the asset shown, with a number, before the problem?
- Does the inciting line hit that same asset?
- Do later pinch lines restate the amount at risk?
