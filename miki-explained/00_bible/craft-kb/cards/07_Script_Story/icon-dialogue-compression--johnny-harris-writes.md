## CREATIVE_INTENT
Harris takes the icon and "gives it dialogue… very reductionist, simplistic dialogue that captures big complicated events":
"Hey, we know you didn't like us settling in Missouri or Illinois, but… please let us have our own state" — a modern,
conversational interpretation of what was really a four-year lobbying process.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Identify a process that takes months/years or many documents.
2. Write what the agent effectively said, in ≤ 15 casual words, modern language.
3. Open with a casual marker ("Hey", "Eh", "Okay so") so it reads as paraphrase.
4. Attribute it to a generic agent or a voiced object, never to a named real person.
5. Show the dialogue as a speech bubble or UI message on the agent, not as a caption restyle.
6. Follow with one plain line giving the real duration/fact.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Script: a line in the voice text with a [casual] tag. Shot list: the agent object/UI shows the line as an on-screen chat
bubble or notification (UI-heavy topics: a phone message). The next line states the real fact.
### Worked example
Topic: a home baker in Johor Bahru joining a food delivery app (illustrative).
BEFORE: "Platform mengenakan komisen dan syarat yang ketat kepada peniaga."
AFTER: L06 (app notification on the phone): "Okay, kami hantar order. Tapi 30 % untuk kami, ya." L07: "Untuk kek RM50, kakak ni simpan RM35 je."
### Check
- Is the dialogue ≤ 15 words and casual?
- Is it clearly paraphrase (no quote marks around a real company's words)?
- Is the real fact stated on the next line?

## NOTES
- ⚠ Never put invented words in a real brand's mouth inside quote marks; label "illustration" on frame if a real brand UI is shown.
