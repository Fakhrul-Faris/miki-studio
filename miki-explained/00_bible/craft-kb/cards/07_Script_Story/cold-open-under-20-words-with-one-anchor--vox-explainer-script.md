## METADATA
07_Script_Story | general (Vox style summary, cites a 50-video study by Seiji Yushin) | frame-0 text, L01

## CREATIVE_INTENT
A Vox script "follows a mystery-solving arc rather than a textbook lecture". The first 7–12 s: no intro, no channel
greeting; start inside the story or pose a focused question ("Why are shipping containers all the same size?"); keep
the opening to roughly **15–20 words before the title card**; pair it with "a striking map, surprising number, or single
bold visual". The pattern was found across 50 Vox videos.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before you write L01, delete any greeting, name or "hari ni kita nak cerita".
2. Choose one opening mode: inside the story, or one focused question.
3. Keep spoken L01 ≤ 15–20 words; frame-0 text ≤ 8 words.
4. Pair L01 with exactly one anchor: a surprising RM number, a map, or one bold object.
5. Put the anchor on screen in frame 0.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Script L01 + frame-0 text. Our "title card" equivalent is the end of L01 (~4–6 s at ~3.4 words/s). Shot-list row 1 =
the anchor in macro, moving from frame 0.

### Worked example
Topic: why every nasi lemak bungkus at a stall costs the same whatever the size.
- BEFORE: "Assalamualaikum korang, hari ni Miki nak share pasal harga nasi lemak yang ramai tak tahu."
- AFTER: L01 "Bungkus besar, bungkus kecil. Dua-dua RM3. Kenapa makcik tak rugi?" (11 words); frame-0 text "Besar
  RM3. Kecil RM3." over a macro of two bungkus side by side on a scale (illustrative prices).

### Check
- Is there zero greeting or self-introduction?
- Is spoken L01 ≤ 20 words and frame-0 text ≤ 8?
- Is there exactly one anchor (number, map or object), on screen in frame 0?
