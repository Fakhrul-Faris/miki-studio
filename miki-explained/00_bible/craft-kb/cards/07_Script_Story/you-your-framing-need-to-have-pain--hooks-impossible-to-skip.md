## CREATIVE_INTENT
Irrelevance: the viewer knows the topic but isn't confident what follows will solve a problem of theirs. Two fixes.
(1) Say **you/your** instead of **I/me**: "I've struggled with skin problems my whole life" makes viewers ask whether
they are like the creator; "If you've struggled with skin problems your whole life…" leaves no question. (2) Frame the
hook around **expected value**: entertainment solves boredom, education solves a specific problem; agitate a pain point
they already have to tee up the solution. "These are three common trends in skincare" is a nice-to-have; "If you
struggle with acne, try these three things" is a need-to-have.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before you write L01, delete "saya/I/me" from the hook.
2. Address the viewer as "korang" (or "you") in L01.
3. Name a pain the viewer already has, in their words.
4. Test it: nice-to-have (trends, facts) or need-to-have (losing money, wasting time)? Keep only need-to-have.
5. Follow with the promised fix and its size ("3 step", an RM number).

## AGENT_EXECUTION
L01 = "Kalau korang <pain>…" or a question naming the pain; L02 = "ni caranya, N step je". Post copy uses the same framing.

### Worked example
Topic: stock expiring at a small shop (illustrative).
BEFORE: L01 "Saya dulu selalu buang stok expired." / "Ni 3 trend pengurusan stok kedai runcit."
AFTER: L01 "Kalau stok kedai korang selalu expired atas rak…" L02 "…buat 3 benda ni. Boleh jimat RM300 sebulan."
(illustrative) L03 "Satu: barang lama depan, baru belakang."

### Check
- Is there no "saya/I" in the hook?
- Is the pain one the viewer already has?
- Is it need-to-have, not nice-to-have?
