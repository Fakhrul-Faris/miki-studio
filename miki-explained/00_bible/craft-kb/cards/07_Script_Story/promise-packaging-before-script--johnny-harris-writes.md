## CREATIVE_INTENT
"The marketing — why should you click — is defined early on", even when the story itself is still open. The packaging is
"a straight line", a billboard: one clear promise the video is guaranteed to answer (e.g. "Why Saudi Arabia is building a
$1 trillion city in the desert"). The story can then "fold in the vegetables" (wider ideas the viewer didn't know they
wanted), but the promise must be reinforced in the first minute and satisfyingly answered by the end, or viewers "feel
betrayed".

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before you write any script line, write 2–3 different packagings (title + image idea) for the topic.
2. Test each: is it one straight line a stranger understands in 1 s?
3. Keep the packaging whose promise you can answer with a verified fact.
4. Write the promise as one question the video will answer.
5. Reinforce that promise inside the first ~1/6 of the runtime (Harris: first minute of ~20; ours: by L03, ~10 s).
6. Do not answer the promise in the first 10 s; answer it fully by the end.
7. Before locking, check every line still serves the promise; cut lines that serve only the "vegetables".
8. If the package and the payoff differ, rewrite the package, never stretch the payoff.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
- Frame-0 text (≤ 8 words, contradiction) = the packaging. Write it first, log the 2–3 alternatives in `episode.md`.
- L01–L03 = reinforce (restate the contradiction in plain words; S16: "Kalau korang nak …, ni caranya. N step je.").
- Mechanism/steps = the vegetables (the wider "how it works").
- L09–L10 = the satisfying answer, with the RM number. L11 = end question taking a side on that answer (not "follow").
- Cover text = the same promise as frame 0; never a different hook.
### Worked example
Topic: a kedai runcit in Shah Alam selling RM1 kuih from a supplier (numbers illustrative; plain arithmetic).
BEFORE: frame 0 "Tips untuk kedai runcit" → L01 "Hari ni kita cerita pasal kuih." (no promise; nothing to pay off)
AFTER: packagings tried — (a) "Kuih RM1. Untung berapa sen?" (b) "Habis 100 biji. Untung RM10?" (c) "Kenapa kedai jual kuih juga?".
Pick (b) (answerable by arithmetic). Frame 0: "Habis 100 biji. Untung RM10 je?" L02 reinforce: "Kuih tu RM1, tapi kedai
beli dari pembuat 90 sen sebiji." L09 pay off: "100 biji × 10 sen = RM10 sehari… tapi kuih tu bawa pelanggan beli air RM2."
L11: "Korang rasa berbaloi ke jual kuih untung 10 sen?"
### Check
- Were 2–3 packagings written before line 1, and is the chosen one logged?
- Is the promise restated in plain words by L03?
- Is it answered with a concrete (RM) fact by L10, not in the first 10 s?
- Does the cover text promise exactly what frame 0 promises?

## NOTES

- "Folding in vegetables" is a long-form luxury; we have one idea per episode (RECIPE-D §3), so keep only one vegetable.
- Visual gaps: thumbnails shown on screen; their design is not recoverable from speech.
