## METADATA
07_Script_Story | script voice | L02–L03, reveal lines

## CREATIVE_INTENT
Journalists often write as "a detached observer… an on-high expert", even "for their peers, not their audience". Harris
writes as "a friend who's really curious": "I want to show you what this map says…". It "equalizes the journalist and the
viewer", invites people in instead of "this is only for the smart people", and "lets defences down" — invoking a name the
viewer doesn't know makes them pretend, defensive, "not curious anymore". YouTube is "the original show-and-tell platform".

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before a reveal, write a show-line: "Tengok ni / Jom tengok / Let me show you…" (≤ 6 words).
2. Address the viewer as "korang/you", never "penonton" or "one".
3. Remove every name, acronym or term the viewer must already know; explain it in ≤ 6 words or cut it.
4. Remove words that signal expertise rather than meaning.
5. Keep the hard content; change only how it is invited in.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Script; voice text with a [curious] or [conspiratorial] tag on the show-line; the shot cue on "tengok" triggers the
eye-direction event (see 08_Edit_Pacing/look-at-this-eye-direction--johnny-harris-writes.md).
### Worked example
Topic: reading a supplier invoice for a laundromat in Melaka (numbers illustrative).
BEFORE: "Analisis invois menunjukkan kos tambahan memberi impak signifikan kepada margin."
AFTER: "Jom tengok invois sabun ni sama-sama. Nampak baris kecil ni? Caj hantar RM25, tiap minggu. Setahun, RM1,300."
### Check
- Is there a show-line before each reveal?
- Is every unfamiliar term explained in ≤ 6 words or cut?
- Is the viewer addressed directly as korang/you?
