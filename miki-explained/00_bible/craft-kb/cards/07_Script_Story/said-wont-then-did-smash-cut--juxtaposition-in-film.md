## CREATIVE_INTENT
"Smash cuts use juxtaposition to highlight extreme differences, and usually use a contrast in sound to further heighten
the cut … often used for comedic effect." *Thor: Ragnarok*: "We are not doing 'get help'" → next shot, they are doing
exactly "get help". Irony from intention vs action.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Write line A as a confident intention the viewer has said themselves (≤ 8 words).
2. Write line B as the action that breaks it, with a number (≤ 10 words).
3. Cut A → B on B's first word; no pause between.
4. Show the same object in both shots, opposite states.
5. Keep sound flat across the cut (no hit).

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Script: lines A and B adjacent; frame 0 can be A's text. Shot-list: same framing, object state flips on the cut word.

### Worked example
Topic: "Kenapa gaji habis sebelum hujung bulan" (illustrative).
- BEFORE: L01 "Ramai orang belanja lebih daripada yang mereka sangka."
- AFTER: L01 (frame 0 text) "Bulan ni aku tak beli air manis." / macro: a fresh coffee loyalty card, 0 stamps.
  L02 "Hari ke-12: 9 cawan, RM58." / same card, same framing, 9 stamps; "RM58" fades in on the word.

### Check
- Is line A a believable intention in the viewer's own voice?
- Does line B contradict it with a number?
- Is the cut on B's first word, with no sound hit or flash?
