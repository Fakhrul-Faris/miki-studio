## CREATIVE_INTENT
"The most powerful tool … is the gap between what a character wants and what they need." Wants are "immediate, stated
goals"; needs are "the deeper truths or growth they must experience, often something they resist or can't yet see".
*Senna* wants to be the best F1 driver; he needs to rediscover the joy of racing.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Write the want in the viewer's words (≤ 10 words).
2. Write the need they don't see yet (≤ 10 words).
3. Open with the want; reveal the need at the payoff with the RM number.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Script: hook line = want; payoff line = need; the steps bridge the gap.

### Worked example
Topic: "Nak kedai makan ramai pelanggan" (illustrative).
- BEFORE: "Ni 3 tips tarik pelanggan."
- AFTER: want (L01): "Korang nak kedai penuh setiap hari?" → need (payoff): "Sebenarnya korang perlu untung RM2 sepinggan, bukan kedai penuh. 100 pinggan × RM0.50 = RM50; 60 × RM2 = RM120."

### Check
- Is the want in the viewer's own words?
- Is the need something they don't yet see?
- Does the payoff prove the need with arithmetic?
