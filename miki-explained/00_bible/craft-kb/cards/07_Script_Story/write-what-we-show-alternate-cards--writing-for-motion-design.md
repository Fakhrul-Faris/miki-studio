## METADATA
07_Script_Story | Evan Abrams, AV script in Milanote | shot-list cells + an Alternates list

## CREATIVE_INTENT
"What's important with writing here is we want to write what we are showing." In the chess piece, "player B panics" was
the most ambiguous cell: what does panic look like? Flip the card and write options: sweat forms on the head, narrowing
eyes, gritted teeth, lip biting — or take "a whole other tack": a POV shot as the hand hovers from piece to piece.
"Decisive move" becomes "slam down the piece, slap the clock". You end up with several cards saying the same thing;
choose one but **save all of them** — later you may go back to an earlier idea.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Scan every video cell for a feeling, judgement or state verb (panic, decisive, grows, confused, expensive).
2. For each, write 3–5 concrete visible alternatives on separate lines.
3. Make at least one alternative a different tack (another viewpoint or object), not a variant of the first.
4. Choose one; write it into the cell as object + action + the word it lands on.
5. Move the unchosen ones into an Alternates list under that row; never delete them.
6. If a chosen alternative fails at board stage, promote the next one before inventing new ones.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
In `episode.md` Shot list, add "Alternates (L04): …" lines below the table. Style D alternates use the hero's own
states (x-ray, open, slice, multiply with a counter, become a chart), the light, or a UI screen.

### Worked example
Topic: a kedai runcit owner running out of a best-seller (illustrative).
BEFORE: | 5 | "Stok habis, owner panik." | shelf | owner panics |
AFTER: | 5 | "Stok habis, owner panik." | macro, low angle along an empty shelf row | on "habis": the last tin slides
off the end, the shelf light flickers once to dim (~0.4 s) |
Alternates (L05): supplier invoice pages riffle by themselves · phone face-up, five missed-call cards stack in ·
price label of the empty slot tips forward · counter on the shelf edge ticks 12 → 0.

### Check
- Does any cell still name a feeling instead of a visible action?
- Does every vague cell have ≥ 3 alternates, one of them a different tack?
- Are the unchosen alternates saved under the row?
- Do all alternates obey "no people/hands" in Style D?
