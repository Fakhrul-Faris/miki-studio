## CREATIVE_INTENT
"For each point in the body you want to include these three things. I call it the value loop": (1) **context** — "say
what it is and explain it as simple as possible", "clear, concise and digestible"; (2) **application** — "say how to do
it" with "as many relevant examples" as possible, easy enough that viewers "can begin taking action"; (3) **framing** — "say
why this point matters and how it fits into the overall puzzle", so when viewers zoom out "they can see why it's worth
continuing to watch". It mirrors how the brain processes information: "base facts first, surface level, then slowly
deepening". The rhythm is "a constant zoom in and zoom out… big picture, tactics… like a wave".

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Open each step with what it is, in ≤ 10 words.
2. Give the how with one concrete example the viewer can copy today.
3. Close with why it matters, linking back to the episode's problem or RM number.
4. Alternate zoom: big picture (why) → tactic (how) → big picture.
5. Fit each step into ~2 lines (~30–36 words).

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Script: step line A = context + application ("Step dua. Kat kaunter, tengok resit betul-betul."); line B = framing ("Kalau
harga lain, korang bayar yang lagi murah."). Shot list: context/application rows are close macro on the action object;
framing rows pull out to the wider picture (camera zoom-out mirrors the "wave").

### Worked example
Step: record daily sales (illustrative).
- BEFORE: "Step satu, rekod jualan. Ini penting."
- AFTER: L04 "Step satu: tulis jualan setiap malam. Satu baris je — tarikh, jumlah." (context + application) L05 "Sebab
  bila Jumaat selalu RM300 dan Isnin RM90, korang tahu hari mana nak masak lebih." (framing)

### Check
- Does each step say what, then how with an example, then why?
- Can a viewer do the step today from the example alone?
- Does the framing link to the episode's problem or number?
