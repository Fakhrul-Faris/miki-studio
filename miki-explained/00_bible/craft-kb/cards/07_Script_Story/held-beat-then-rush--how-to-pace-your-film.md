## CREATIVE_INTENT
Performance changes tempo: actors slow down to show inner emotion (Frances McDormand's silences in Nomadland);
Ralph Fiennes in The Grand Budapest Hotel takes "a long beat or dramatic pause for comedy", then he and Tony Revolori
speed through their dialogue to ramp the scene up. The contrast between the hold and the burst creates the laugh or
the punch.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Pick one line where the reveal lands.
2. Before the reveal word, write one "…" (a ~0.5–0.8 s pause after voice processing, inferred).
3. Write the next 1–2 lines as a fast burst (short fragments, no tags).
4. In the shot list, hold the camera slow during the pause and cut to a new setup on the reveal word.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Voice table: "…" before the reveal; check voice_fix.py pause trim does not remove it (mark it as a kept pause).
### Worked example
Topic: "Jual air balang RM2 secawan — untung ke?" (plain arithmetic, illustrative costs).
- BEFORE: `L09 | Selepas tolak kos gula, cawan dan ais, untung setiap cawan cuma 30 sen.`
- AFTER: `L09 | Jual RM2 secawan… [wry] untung 30 sen je.` / `L10 | Gula 60 sen, cawan 40, ais 30, sirap 40.` (held beat before "30 sen", then a burst; 60+40+30+40 = RM1.70, so RM2 − RM1.70 = 30 sen).
### Check
- Is there one pause right before the reveal word?
- Is the line after it a fast burst?
- Does the setup keep moving during the pause?

## NOTES
- Silences in film can be long; in FYP shorts keep the pause under ~1 s (inferred from retention, not the source).
