## METADATA
07_Script_Story | YouTube explainer on ABT | Script end lines (RECIPE-D §3)

## CREATIVE_INTENT
After the problem is solved and the characters return home, a story can end with a teaser (cliffhanger): a new fact is
revealed but not explained ("the cat has a sinister motive … but we are not told what"). The main problem stays solved;
only a new, smaller strand is left open so the audience keeps thinking.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before you write the end, confirm the Therefore has fully solved the episode's main problem.
2. Choose one new, smaller doubt that the solution itself creates.
3. State the doubt as a fact or question; do not explain it.
4. Phrase it as a question with two sides the viewer can pick (≤ 7 words on screen).
5. Do not reopen the main problem and do not promise a next video.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Applies to L10–L11 and the end text. The source's teaser is the "but" that sits after the Therefore. In our format the
open strand must be answerable by the viewer in the comments (side-taking), and the last frame returns to the frame-0
object (loop), so the teaser also invites a replay.

### Worked example
Topic: QR at a gerai (illustrative).
BEFORE: "Jadi tu je cara nak terima QR. Follow untuk part dua!" (request to follow — banned)
AFTER: L10 "Sekarang duit masuk terus, kira sendiri." L11 (end text) "Tapi pelanggan warga emas… nak ke scan?"
— main problem (lost cash) solved; new doubt (older customers) left open for the viewer to take a side.

### Check
- Is the main problem fully solved before the teaser?
- Is the open strand new and smaller, not the main problem again?
- Is the end text ≤ 7 words and a question with two sides?
- Is there no "follow", "part 2" or "next video" in the end?

## NOTES
- Source example (Chris, the PhD, pirates, the fireball cat) is narrative fiction; only the structure is kept.
