## CREATIVE_INTENT
"Every assertion in every single video has a fact check." Each claim starts red; once checked it gets a citation tag and
turns green. Clicking the citation shows "a little blurb that proves the fact" plus the source; some claims have 3–4 checks
(academic articles, census data). "There are more words in comments than in the script." They learned "the hard way that
things fall through the cracks", and publish the sources as a doc viewers can scrutinise.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Mark every number, name, cause and rule in the script red (unverified) as you write.
2. For each, open a source and paste the exact sentence that proves it.
3. Turn the claim green only with that quote + source link attached.
4. Add a second source for any RM number or legal rule.
5. Do not show the script while any claim is red; cut or soften it.
6. Copy the green list into the post caption's sources or a pinned comment.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Use the claim-ledger skill; its status column = red (unverified) / green (verified, quote + link). The script table marks
red claims with ⚠ until cleared.

Topic: price-tag vs receipt mismatch at a supermarket (EX46 style).
BEFORE: L07 "Kalau harga resit lain, korang bayar yang murah." — no source.
AFTER: L07 red → opened the KPDN price-display rules; quote + link pasted; second source a news explainer → green. Post copy lists both links.
### Check

- Are RM numbers and legal rules double-sourced?
- Is no red claim left in the script shown to the user?
