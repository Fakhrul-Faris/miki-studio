## METADATA
07_Script_Story | Live-action (short-form loop) | script (End beat) + raea audio cue at the loop seam

## CREATIVE_INTENT
"We've slowed down, but because it's for short form, the video wants to loop... paying a little bit of attention to how your videos end and start can go a long way." The end shot carries a sound (a camera-flash/shutter rise) chosen specifically because, looped back to the start, "it sounds like a camera shutter goes off" — the SAME sonic event that could plausibly open the video, so the seam disappears.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before writing the End line, re-read the Hook line/frame-0 object and framing.
2. Choose the end frame's object/framing to match frame 0 (already required by RECIPE-D).
3. Choose ONE sound for the exact last frame that could also plausibly be the first sound of frame 0 (a click, a chime, a snap).
4. Place that sound at the very last frame, not earlier.
5. Play the loop seam (last frame → first frame) in isolation and confirm the sound reads as one continuous event, not two.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
In `episode.md`, End beat note: "loop sound: <X>, matches frame-0's <Y>". In raea, place the sound cue at the final frame's timestamp so it rides across the render's loop point.

### Worked example
Topic: a bengkel kereta's RM50 oil-change upsell (illustrative).
BEFORE: End line "Jadi, servis asas ke servis penuh?" over a static shot of an oil can, no loop sound.
AFTER: frame-0 opens on the oil can lid snapping shut (soft click); End line "Jadi, servis asas ke servis penuh?" over the SAME lid snapping shut from the other side → loop-rhyme-start-end-sound-and-frame; the click plays once, at the seam, read as both the end and the next start.

### Check
- Does the end frame match frame 0's object and framing (already required)?
- Is there exactly one sound at the loop seam, not two competing ones?
- Played back-to-back, does the seam read as one continuous event?
