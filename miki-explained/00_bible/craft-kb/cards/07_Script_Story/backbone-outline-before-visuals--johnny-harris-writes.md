## METADATA
07_Script_Story | outline step | between research and script v1

## CREATIVE_INTENT
"Knowledge is one thing; storytelling is the curation of chaotic information about the world into a very firm linear
presentation." After reading the info doc, Harris writes an outline: "not thinking about visuals, just backbone and
chronology and flow and promise". History stories come with a backbone (chronology), which removes "fighting structure is
brutal"; then the puzzle is "what am I curating and how am I explaining it in a new way".

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before script v1, decide if the topic has a built-in order (time, process steps, money flow).
2. If yes, use it as the backbone; if no, pick one (money in → money out is a good default).
3. Write the outline as 5–8 bullets, one per beat, no visuals.
4. Mark each bullet as serving the promise or not; cut those that do not.
5. Only then write lines and the shot list.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
`episode.md` gets an "Outline" (5–8 bullets mapped to Hook / Open loop / Mechanism or Steps / Proof-Payoff / End) before Script v1.
### Worked example
Topic: how a burger stall in Shah Alam spends RM1,000 of sales (illustrative).
BEFORE: script written straight from notes; jumps between rent, buns, permits, profit.
AFTER backbone = money flow: 1 RM1,000 comes in (hook: "untung" vs real) · 2 buns + patties RM420 · 3 gas + minyak RM90 · 4 tapak + permit RM60 · 5 helper RM200 · 6 left: RM230 · 7 question: worth 6 nights a week?
### Check
- Is there an outline before script v1?
- Does it follow one backbone order?
- Does each bullet serve the promise?
