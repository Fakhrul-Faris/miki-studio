## CREATIVE_INTENT
The explanation is **50–70 % of the script**. "One clear throughline: build ideas step by step so each beat adds
clarity." "Concrete scale: use specific numbers, named examples and familiar comparisons instead of vague abstractions."

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before you draft, write the throughline in one sentence.
2. Give the Mechanism 50–70 % of the words: ~90–140 of 175–200.
3. Make each line add exactly one new piece toward the throughline.
4. Put one concrete number, named thing or familiar comparison in every Mechanism line.
5. Cut any line that repeats or side-steps the throughline.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Count words per beat in `episode.md` (Hook / Open loop / Mechanism / Proof / End). Shot list: each Mechanism line's
concrete number is the line's on-screen event (counter, stack, chart from the object).

### Worked example
Throughline: a RM3 nasi lemak's cost adds up from small parts (all numbers illustrative, plain arithmetic).
- BEFORE: "Kos bahan mentah, operasi dan overhead semua naik, jadi margin makin nipis."
- AFTER: L04 "Beras, santan, sambal: RM1.20." L05 "Telur separuh: 30 sen." L06 "Daun, kertas, getah: 20 sen."
  L07 "Gas dan sewa tapak: 50 sen." L08 "Tinggal 80 sen untung. Sebungkus."
  (1.20 + 0.30 + 0.20 + 0.50 = 2.20; RM3 − 2.20 = 0.80.)

### Check
- Is the Mechanism 50–70 % of spoken words?
- Does every Mechanism line add one new step?
- Does every Mechanism line hold a concrete number/name/comparison?
- Do the numbers add up?

## NOTES
- "Margin" and "overhead" are analyst words — replaced with plain ones.
