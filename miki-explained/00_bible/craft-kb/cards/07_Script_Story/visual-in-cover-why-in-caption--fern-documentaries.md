## CREATIVE_INTENT
"The visual part of the hook is in the thumbnail because it's a picture, but… they set up this deeper why question right
in the title. They don't ask the question explicitly but… implicitly promise to answer it inside the video." The
picture makes them click; the implicit question makes them stay.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before you write the caption, pick the cover: the frame that shows the visual hook most clearly.
2. Write the caption/title so it promises an answer without asking the question outright.
3. Keep the visual and the promise in different places: picture shows, words promise.
4. Check the cover text does not repeat the caption word for word.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Cover = a still from the film's frame 0–L01 (the hero object). Caption first line = the implicit promise. On TikTok the
first second is the "thumbnail": frame 0 shows the object, frame-0 text carries the contradiction.

### Worked example
Topic: "dari RM299, sekarang RM149" (EX46).
- BEFORE: caption "Kenapa harga sale kadang-kadang tipu? Jom tengok!"
- AFTER: cover = macro of the red tag with RM299 struck through; caption "RM299 tu pernah wujud ke? 3 cara nak tahu"
  — the "how do they fake it" is promised, not asked.

### Check
- Does the cover show the visual hook, not text alone?
- Does the caption promise an answer rather than ask a bare question?
- Do cover text and caption say different things?
