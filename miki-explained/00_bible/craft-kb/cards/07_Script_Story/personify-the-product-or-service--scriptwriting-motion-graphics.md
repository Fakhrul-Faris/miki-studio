## CREATIVE_INTENT
For Jixo (smart homes) in a crowded market, the "eureka moment" was "personifying the brand": Jixo is "like an invisible
friend" that turns homes into "living homes". The client changed its tagline from "Smart Homes" to "Living Homes". A
role word gives a simple story to a jargon-heavy product.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. List what the product/service does for the viewer in ≤ 3 verbs.
2. Name a human role that does those verbs (pembantu, jaga, kawan, kerani).
3. Write the product as that role in ≤ 8 words.
4. Show the role through the object acting by itself, never a figure.
5. Drop the role if it needs explaining.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
One line in the open loop or turn: "<Product> ni macam <role> yang …". The shot list shows the object doing the role's
action on the verb (a POS screen totting up by itself).
### Worked example
Topic: a POS app for a small kedai runcit (generic).
BEFORE: "Sistem POS ni ada fungsi inventori, laporan jualan dan amaran stok."
AFTER: "POS ni macam kerani yang tak pernah tidur — stok habis, dia bagitau." Shot: the shelf's last tin slides out; the
tablet on the counter lights a "stok: 0" alert on "bagitau".
### Check
- Is the role one familiar word?
- Is it shown only through objects acting by themselves?
- Does the line still say what the product literally does?
