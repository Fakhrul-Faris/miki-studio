## METADATA
07_Script_Story | short-form hook tactics | script L01–L02

## CREATIVE_INTENT
People like hearing about what they already care about and are open to new perspectives if those unlock a benefit or
solve a pain. "You should be taking magnesium because it's one of 21 core building-block minerals" needs prior knowledge
and offers no benefit; "If you want better sleep, you need to be taking magnesium" leads with the benefit and introduces
the solution second. Even sceptics wait to hear you out, because the pain is strong.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before you write L01, write the viewer's pain or wanted result in ≤ 8 words.
2. Put that pain or result first in L01.
3. Name the solution (tool, step, habit) after it, in the same line or L02.
4. Delete any definition or category fact that needs prior knowledge ("one of 21…").
5. Keep the benefit concrete: an RM amount, time saved, a problem gone.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
S16 template: L01 = the pain in the viewer's words; L02 = "Kalau korang nak <benefit>, ni caranya. N step je." The
solution's name may wait until step 1. Shot list: L01's hero shows the pain literally (the empty till, the expired
stock), not the solution's logo.

### Worked example
Topic: separate bank account for a small shop.
BEFORE: L01 "Akaun bank bisnes ialah akaun yang berasingan daripada akaun peribadi."
AFTER: L01 "Kedai laku, tapi hujung bulan korang tak tahu duit lari ke mana?" L02 "Kalau korang nak nampak duit kedai
jelas, ni caranya. 3 step je." L03 "Step satu: buka akaun kedua, untuk kedai je."

### Check
- Does L01 open with the viewer's pain or wanted result, not the solution's name?
- Is there zero definition that needs prior knowledge?
- Is the benefit concrete (RM, time, a visible problem)?
