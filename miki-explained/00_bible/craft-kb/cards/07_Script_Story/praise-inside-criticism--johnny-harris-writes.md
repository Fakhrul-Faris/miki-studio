## METADATA
07_Script_Story | script device | critical lines, zoom-out line

## CREATIVE_INTENT
"Weave in unexpected praise to things I'm criticising, to show I'm not just purely on a one-sided rant — that's too easy and
people are tired of it." Example: "in this beautiful valley surrounded by massive mountains, feeling close to the specific
sugar-coated version of this history I was taught" — "a juxtaposition in one sentence… life is complicated." His editor
also pushed the opposite way: don't lean too hard into apologising; the hard part must be in the conclusion, "next to an
understanding that it has beauty to it as well."

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Find every line that criticises something.
2. Write one true, specific good thing about the same subject.
3. Put the praise in the first clause and the criticism in the second, in one sentence.
4. Keep both concrete (objects, RM, actions), no adjectives alone.
5. Check the criticism is not softened away: the hard fact stays in the sentence.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Script lines with criticism; the shot can show both halves (object at its best, then the flaw revealed: x-ray, flip, price
tag behind).
### Worked example
Topic: Buy Now Pay Later for a RM1,200 phone (illustrative).
BEFORE: "BNPL ni perangkap, jangan guna."
AFTER: "BNPL bagi korang bawa balik phone RM1,200 hari ni… tapi lambat sehari, caj lewat dah menunggu." Shot: phone box opens (good), then flip to the back showing a small red late-fee tag.
### Check
- Does every critical line also hold one true, specific positive?
- Are both halves in one sentence?
- Is the hard fact still stated plainly?

## NOTES
- Only praise that is verified; it is a fact claim like any other (claim ledger).
