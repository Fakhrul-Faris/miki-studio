## CREATIVE_INTENT
"Conflict comes from what your subject stands to lose." External stakes: physical or material (*Free Solo*). Internal:
emotional (*Jane*: relationship vs passion). Philosophical: moral or societal (*An Inconvenient Truth*). "Most successful
documentaries combine all three." Ask: what could your subject lose or gain? why does it matter so much to them? what
would it cost them to fail?

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before drafting, answer the three questions: lose/gain? why does it matter? cost of failing?
2. Write the external stake as an RM number (loss, profit, saving).
3. Write the internal stake in one plain clause (malu, penat, risau).
4. Write the philosophical stake as a fairness question.
5. Put the external stake in the payoff line and the philosophical one in the end question.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
`episode.md` stakes box: `external: RM… | internal: … | philosophical: …`. Script: the end question (≤ 7 words) comes
from the philosophical line.

### Worked example
Topic: "Peniaga online kena bayar caj platform — adil tak?" (illustrative).
- BEFORE: end question "Korang setuju tak?"
- AFTER: stakes — external: "RM1,200 sebulan hilang pada caj" (illustrative); internal: "penat kerja, untung tipis"; philosophical: "siapa patut tanggung caj — penjual atau pembeli?" → payoff line uses RM1,200; end text "Pembeli patut tanggung caj?"

### Check
- Are all three stakes written down?
- Is the external stake an RM number in the payoff?
- Does the end question come from the philosophical stake?

## NOTES
- Caj amount illustrative; platform fees vary.
