## CREATIVE_INTENT
For mysteries, he "systematically presents the facts and debunks popular myths before laying out the remaining plausible
scenarios"; the middle acts "methodically test major theories against the established timeline", highlighting
contradictions or impossibilities "to narrow down the possibilities".

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before you write the Mechanism, list the 2–3 explanations viewers already believe.
2. Test each one against one sourced fact.
3. Give each myth one line: the belief, then the fact that breaks it.
4. Leave the true cause last, with its RM number.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Shot list: the myths are labels on the hero that get struck out one by one on the fact's word; the survivor lights in
accent.

### Worked example
Topic: why a nasi lemak price went from RM2.50 to RM3 (illustrative numbers; the costs are the stall's own).
- BEFORE: "Ada banyak faktor kenapa harga naik."
- AFTER: L05 "Makcik tamak? Untung dia sama je, 80 sen." L06 "Sewa naik? Sewa tapak tak berubah." L07 "Yang naik:
  santan dan telur, 50 sen sebungkus." Labels "tamak" and "sewa" strike out; "santan + telur" lights in accent.

### Check
- Are 2–3 popular beliefs tested, each with a fact?
- Is the true cause left for last?
- Is every fact in the claim ledger?
