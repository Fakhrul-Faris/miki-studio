## CREATIVE_INTENT
Callaway writes every script in one order: **packaging → outline → intro → body → outro**. The outline comes before the
intro because "anybody can learn how to write a compelling intro… the real sauce… is having something actually unique,
differentiated and novel to say in the body." Uniqueness "can come from the actual tip itself, the process of
distillation, how you actually say the tip, or the example that you use." A "quick and dirty bulleted list" is the gut
check; "if you don't have something worth saying… go research more". For each point layer "the what, the why and the
how". "If I don't have unique points in the outline I'm not moving forward. I'm not making the video." It's "the measure
twice, cut once of content". "If expectations are existing base knowledge and you're just repeating existing base
knowledge, you will not be able to beat expectations."

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Fix the frame-0 text (packaging) first.
2. Before writing any line, bullet the 3–5 points/steps.
3. Mark each point's novelty source: tip, distillation, wording, or example.
4. Strike points the viewer already knows; research replacements.
5. Under each surviving point write what it is, why it matters, how it fits the whole.
6. If fewer than 2 points are novel, stop and return to research or a new topic.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
In `episode.md`, add `Outline` before Script v1: one row per point with `novelty:` and `what / why / how`. The fresh
critic or user can veto at this gate. S16 steps that are common knowledge ("compare prices") need a novel example or
distillation (a specific screenshot trick, an RM rule) to pass.

### Worked example
Topic: "Cara elak kena tipu masa sale" (generic).
- BEFORE outline: 1) bandingkan harga 2) baca review 3) jangan terburu-buru (all known → fails).
- AFTER: 1) screenshot the price a week before the sale (novel: example) — why: proves whether the "original" price
  existed; how: step 1. 2) check the receipt against the shelf tag before bagging (novel: moment) — why: the error is
  found while you can still fix it at the counter. Passes with 2 novel points.

### Check
- Is there a bulleted outline before L01?
- Does each point name its novelty source?
- Does each point have what / why / how?
- Are at least 2 points not common knowledge?
