## CREATIVE_INTENT
Conclusions are "strictly grounded in verifiable records, primary sources and documented timelines rather than unfounded
theories". "Build a factual timeline first and strip out filler or speculation." The core of the script unfolds
step-by-step along a strict timeline, "using timestamps and spatial mapping… minute-by-minute or year-by-year so the
viewer feels like they are examining the evidence alongside him."

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before you write the script, build a dated timeline in the claim ledger from primary sources.
2. Strip every row that is speculation or has no source.
3. Pick 3–5 dated beats that carry the story.
4. Give each beat one line, starting with its date or time.
5. Show the date on screen as an event on the hero (a calendar page, a dated receipt).

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Claim ledger first (claim-ledger skill), then script. Shot list: a recurring timeline device (dated tag/stamp) that
advances per beat — the through-line motif.

### Worked example
Topic: one item's price across a sale season (EX46-type, using the user's own screenshots as the source).
- BEFORE: "Harga naik dulu sebelum sale, lepas tu turun."
- AFTER: "1 Nov: RM149." / "8 Nov: tiba-tiba RM299." / "11 Nov: 'sale' RM149." Each beat = the same tag macro, the
  date stamp ticking forward, the price changing on the date.

### Check
- Is every beat sourced in the claim ledger?
- Is speculation removed?
- Are there 3–5 dated beats, each with its date on screen?

## NOTES
- ⚠ Minute-by-minute reconstruction is long-form; compressed to 3–5 beats here.
