## CREATIVE_INTENT
"How to seamlessly transition between the points… while maintaining max retention is called re-hooking": "set up these
mini hooks between the end of one point and the beginning of the next one", e.g. "that point was super important, but if
you don't also couple it with this one, then the magic is lost." It works because "people's brains are on an attention
delay": interest peaks when they see something "and that will slowly start to decay"; without re-hooking "they will
eventually just bounce".

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. At the end of each step, add ≤ 10 words that make the next step necessary.
2. Tie the bridge to a gap: "step 1 alone fails because…".
3. Never announce structure ("next point", "Part 2"); say the stakes instead.
4. Use at most one bridge per join.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Script: append the bridge to the step's framing line or open the next step with it. Shot list: put a state change on the
bridge's key word (the step-1 object shows its flaw — a crack, a missing piece) so the visual also re-hooks.

### Worked example
Topic: screenshot the price before a sale (generic).
- BEFORE: L05 "…simpan dalam phone." L06 "Step dua. Kat kaunter…"
- AFTER: L05 "…simpan dalam phone. Tapi screenshot je tak cukup kalau kat kaunter lain pula." L06 "Step dua. Kat kaunter,
  tengok resit betul-betul." Visual on "tak cukup": the saved screenshot's price and the receipt's price sit side by side,
  not matching.

### Check
- Does every join have a ≤ 10-word bridge that makes the next step needed?
- Is there no structural announcement in the voice?
- Does the bridge word carry a visual state change?
