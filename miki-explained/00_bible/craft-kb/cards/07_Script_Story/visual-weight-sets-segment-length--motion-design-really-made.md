## CREATIVE_INTENT
The studio prefers writing the script itself for "full control of the flow". A script from a non-animator is "usually
well written" but has segments where "the voice-over is just way too long and it's just one concept". When the animator
writes and "the concept is just not that big of a concept to express visually, I'm gonna make that segment a bit
shorter".

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before locking the script, draft one setup per line beside it.
2. For each line, count the setups it needs to be shown (1 setup ≈ 2–3 s ≈ 7–10 words at ~3.4 words/s).
3. If a line has more words than its setups can carry, cut words.
4. If one idea spans 2+ lines but needs only one visual, merge into one line.
5. Give extra words only to ideas that need several distinct visuals.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Add a "setups" column to the script table: words ÷ setups must be ≤ ~10. Flag any line > 10 words per setup, and any run
of lines sharing one visual.

### Worked example
BEFORE (15 words, 1 visual): "Bila korang nak bayar, korang cuma perlu buka app dan scan QR kat kaunter tu."
AFTER (5 words, 1 setup): "Buka app, scan QR, siap." — the longer version had one image (phone → QR stand) and
lost the 2–3 s rhythm.

### Check
- Is every line ≤ ~10 words per setup?
- Is any single visual stretched over 2+ lines?
- Do the longest lines belong to the ideas with the most distinct visuals?
