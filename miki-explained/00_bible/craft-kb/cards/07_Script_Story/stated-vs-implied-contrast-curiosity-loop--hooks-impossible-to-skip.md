## METADATA
07_Script_Story | Kallaway | script hook L01–L03, frame-0 text

## CREATIVE_INTENT
Disinterest is the curiosity problem. A curiosity loop: the viewer sees something, asks a question in their head, gets
context that answers it, which spurs a new question, and so on through the video; the hook opens the first loop. Build
it with **contrast**: the distance between the viewer's current belief (A) and your alternative (B) that solves their
pain faster, better or cheaper ("this one trick will clear up all your acne in 45 minutes without side effects" vs the
baseline of 6 months of Accutane and side effects). Two types: **stated** — say both A and B ("Most people solve acne
with Accutane, but I have an herbal remedy that does it 3× faster"), blunt and impossible to miss; **implied** — say only
B and let the field of known options be A ("this herbal supplement is 8× more effective"), subtler. Tactically, topic
clarity comes in sentence 1 and the contrast in the next 1–2, so a hook is really 2–3 lines; when clarity and contrast fit
one line, that hook is the easiest to reuse.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before L02, write A: what most viewers believe or do now, in ≤ 8 words.
2. Write B: the episode's answer, and how it is faster, better or cheaper.
3. Choose stated (A and B both spoken) when A isn't common knowledge.
4. Choose implied (only B) when every viewer already knows A.
5. Put topic clarity in L01 and the contrast in L02–L03, or both in one line if it fits ≤ 15 words.
6. Plan the next loop: the question B leaves open, answered later.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Frame-0 text = the contrast in ≤ 8 words. In the claim ledger, B must be verified or plain arithmetic, never hype.

### Worked example
Topic: unit price at a pasaraya (how-to; prices illustrative).
- Stated: L01 "Orang ingat pek besar mesti lagi murah." L02 "Kira harga per 100g dulu. Kadang pek kecil menang."
- Implied: L01 "Pek kecil ni lagi murah daripada pek besar." (A = "big pack is cheaper" is known to everyone.)
Frame-0 text: "Pek besar. Lagi mahal?" Proof later: 500g RM8.50 = RM1.70/100g vs 200g RM3.20 = RM1.60/100g.

### Check
- Are A and B both clear (spoken or obviously implied)?
- Is B faster, better or cheaper in a way the script proves?
- Is the hook 2–3 lines with clarity first?
- Does B open a question the script answers later?
