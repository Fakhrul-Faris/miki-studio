## METADATA
07_Script_Story | Bijy Benny, WowMakers (Tralexho fish delivery) | mechanism outline + shot list

## CREATIVE_INTENT
For Tralexho, a fish delivery app with traceability from sourcing to delivery, the team told "the story of a customer
who is taken on a journey once she places an order", to convey "that it is as good as seeing the whole journey in
person". One subject's journey turns a process into a story.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Pick one physical item the process moves (a fish, a parcel, a ringgit note).
2. List its stops in order, 3–5 stops.
3. Give each stop one line and 1–2 setups with the same item.
4. Change the item's state or number at each stop (price added, label added).
5. End on the item where the viewer meets it, matching frame 0.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Shot list: the hero is the item in every row; each stop is a new setup (RECIPE-D §14 "scale travel" for distance,
`tr:"out"`/`"in"` joins by scale). A running RM counter on the item can sum costs per stop.
### Worked example
Topic: why a RM12 ikan kembung pack costs that much at the pasar (illustrative numbers).
BEFORE: "Harga ikan naik sebab kos pengangkutan, orang tengah dan peruncit."
AFTER: one fish, four stops — bot (RM5/kg) → jeti, ais + lori (+RM2) → pemborong (+RM2) → gerai pasar (+RM3) = RM12.
Each stop: same fish, new surface (deck, ice crate, steel scale, wet-market tile), tag grows on the number word.
### Check
- Is the same item in every setup of the chain?
- Are there 3–5 stops, each with a state/number change?
- Does the last setup return to the frame-0 object?
