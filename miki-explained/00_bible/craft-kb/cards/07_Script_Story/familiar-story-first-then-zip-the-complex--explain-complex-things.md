## CREATIVE_INTENT
"Have you ever been to a Mexican restaurant?… If they brought only hot salsa and mild salsa, what would you do if you
wanted medium?" — "Mix them together." "That's just like how we make the three grades of gasoline… We only make a high
grade and a low grade. To get the middle grade, we mix them." Steps: (2) **determine something similar and familiar**
to this audience ("if we don't, we'll only lose people"); (3) **tell the familiar story first**, highlighting the steps
you'll connect back to; (4) **connect the complex step by step**, "like the teeth in a zipper" — each complex step
linked to its familiar step, which also stops you drifting into detail.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before you write, pick a familiar story your Malaysian 18–34 viewer has lived (mamak, pasar malam, Grab, kenduri).
2. Check it shares the same steps as the complex system, in the same order.
3. Tell the familiar story first, in ≤ 2 lines.
4. Pair each complex step with its familiar step, one line per pair.
5. Drop any complex detail with no familiar twin.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Script: L03–L04 familiar story; L05–L07 zipper pairs. Shot list: each pair = the familiar object morphing into the real
one in the same framing (object morph, Style D native).

### Worked example
Topic: why savings left untouched grow faster each year (compound growth — plain arithmetic, no product named).
- BEFORE: "Compounding bermaksud faedah dikira atas pokok dan faedah terkumpul."
- AFTER: L03 "Bayangkan pokok rambutan. Tahun ni berbuah, korang tanam biji dia." L04 "Tahun depan, ada dua pokok
  berbuah." L05 "Duit sama. RM1,000 dapat 5%, jadi RM1,050." L06 "Tahun depan, 5% dikira atas RM1,050: RM1,102.50."
  Shots: one rambutan tree → two trees; the coin stack in the same framing grows 1,000 → 1,050 → 1,102.50.

### Check
- Is the familiar story one this audience has lived?
- Is it told first, before the complex system?
- Is each complex step paired with a familiar step?
- Is any unpaired detail cut?

## NOTES
- Arithmetic: 1,000 × 1.05 = 1,050; 1,050 × 1.05 = 1,102.50. The 5% rate is illustrative.
