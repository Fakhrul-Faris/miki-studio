## METADATA
07_Script_Story | script (The Economist reporting model) | proof lines, zoom-out line

## CREATIVE_INTENT
Most reports say a leader "has taken power and is trying to unify the country" — "I can't see anything". The Economist wrote
that the palace, "five times larger than the White House, echoes with emptiness and there wasn't even staff to serve
coffee." That human detail lets you "see" the situation; "you have to be brave to include that, because it might fall flat."
Same idea in the poetic voice: pair two concrete, unexpected images ("undetonated bombs and abandoned bones") rather than an
abstract claim. Finding these details "is a lot more work".

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Mark every line whose meaning is abstract (struggle, success, pressure, growth).
2. For each, find one verified physical detail that proves it (an object, a count, a room, a sound).
3. Replace the abstract clause with the detail; keep the abstract word only if needed for clarity.
4. Prefer a detail with a number or an absence ("no one to serve coffee").
5. Optionally pair two concrete images that are rarely seen together, ≤ 6 words.
6. Make the detail the hero object of that setup.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Script proof line + shot-list row whose hero object is the detail; ref_<shot>.md "unique features" records it.
### Worked example
Topic: a mamak in Petaling Jaya short of workers (illustrative).
BEFORE: "Restoran ni sedang menghadapi masalah kekurangan pekerja."
AFTER: "Pukul 2 pagi, 40 meja penuh… dan satu je orang yang buat teh tarik." Shot: macro of a single steel mug pouring, 40 order chits stacking on the spike behind (counter 1→40).
### Check
- Does every abstract line now carry a physical detail?
- Is the detail verified (or the example marked illustrative)?
- Is the detail the hero of its shot?
