## CREATIVE_INTENT
The studio's first step is a 1–2 hour discovery call to find out **why** the video is needed (educate? company overview?
one specific feature? sell?) and **who** watches it: "is that person warm towards the company… ready to buy… is it for
selling or just explaining or educating". Everything after (concept, script, style) follows from those two answers.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before line 1, write the purpose in one line: educate / show one how-to / change one belief / sell.
2. Write the viewer's warmth: cold (never heard of us), warm (follows), hot (ready to act).
3. If cold, assume zero context: the hook must work with no brand knowledge and the payoff must be useful on its own.
4. Name the one action or belief the viewer should leave with (≤ 12 words).
5. Reject any line that serves a different purpose than step 1.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Add three lines to the top of episode.md under RECIPE-D §0.1: `Purpose:`, `Viewer warmth:` (default `cold — FYP`),
`Leaves with:`. The script's end question must test the "Leaves with" line, not ask for a follow.

### Worked example
BEFORE: `Purpose: explain e-wallets. Viewer: everyone.`
AFTER (S16 how-to): `Purpose: one how-to — check your e-wallet transaction history every night. Viewer warmth: cold,
18–34, scrolling FYP, has an e-wallet. Leaves with: "RM8 a day unnoticed is RM240 a month" (8 × 30). End
question: "Korang rasa sehari habis berapa?"` (amounts illustrative).

### Check
- Is the purpose one of educate / how-to / belief / sell, in one line?
- Is viewer warmth stated (cold by default)?
- Does every line serve that purpose?
- Does the end question test the "Leaves with" line rather than ask for a follow?
