## METADATA

## CREATIVE_INTENT
The Rules of Attraction's four-minute Europe montage is fast because both the edit and the propulsive narration race
("Saw the Tate, saw Big Ben, ate a lot of weird English food…"). The Wolf of Wall Street montage uses fewer cuts but
speeds through more narrative: the voice compresses the story, not the cutting.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before writing the list, cap it at 3–4 items.
2. Write each item as 2–4 words in one line, comma-separated, verb first.
3. Keep one setup (one object) and change its state on each item word.
4. End the line with the consequence in ≤ 6 words.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Script: one line, items as fragments. Shot list: one setup row with one event per item word.
### Worked example
Topic: "Buka gerai burger — modal awal" (illustrative numbers).
- BEFORE: L04 "Mula-mula korang beli dapur." L05 "Lepas tu beli peti sejuk." L06 "Lepas tu kanopi." (3 lines, 3 setups).
- AFTER: L04 "Dapur RM400, peti RM900, kanopi RM300 — dah RM1,600." → one top-down setup on a receipt that prints a line on each item word; the total lands in the accent colour on "RM1,600".
### Check
- Is the list ≤ 4 items in one line?
- Does each item get its own on-word event?
- Does the arithmetic add up exactly?
