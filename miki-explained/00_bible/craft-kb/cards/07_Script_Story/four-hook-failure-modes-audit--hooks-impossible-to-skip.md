## CREATIVE_INTENT
A hook has one job: help the viewer decide to opt in and stay. It needs only two things: **topic clarity** (crystal-clear
what the video is about) and **on-target curiosity** (they believe it's for them, it will give value, and they want what
comes next). A hook fails through one or more of "the four horsemen": (1) **delay** — the topic arrives too late
(13-line script, topic first appears in line 3: lines 1–2 are fluff; cut them so the topic lands in the first 1–2 s;
short-form retention is exponential decay, steepest in the first 2 s; vague openers like "this is one of the craziest
things I've ever seen" give zero context); (2) **confusion** — comprehension loss from unclear words/order;
(3) **irrelevance** — they know the topic but not that it's for them; (4) **disinterest** — no curiosity loop. Fix in
that order.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before you show script v1, read L01–L03 alone, without the rest.
2. Delay: find the line where the topic first appears; if not L01, cut the lines above it.
3. Delete any opener that could fit any video ("korang takkan percaya…").
4. Confusion: run the clarity proof (see hook-clarity-proof card).
5. Irrelevance: rewrite with "korang/you" and a need-to-have pain (see you-your card).
6. Disinterest: add an A-vs-B contrast in the next 1–2 lines (see stated-vs-implied card).
7. Confirm the topic word is spoken within ~1–2 s once timings arrive.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Write a 4-line audit under Script v1: "Delay ✓/✗ · Confusion ✓/✗ · Irrelevance ✓/✗ · Disinterest ✓/✗" with the fix
made. Frame-0 text gets the same audit (it must carry topic clarity even muted).

### Worked example
Topic: QR payments at a gerai (S16 how-to).
BEFORE: L01 "Korang, benda ni memang gila." L02 "Saya tak sangka langsung." L03 "Gerai pun dah boleh terima QR."
(delay: topic in L03; L01–L02 vague.)
AFTER: L01 "Gerai korang masih cash je?" L02 "Pelanggan tak bawa duit, terus jalan." L03 "Kalau korang nak terima
QR, ni caranya. 3 step je."

### Check
- Is the topic named in L01, within ~1–2 s?
- Could L01 open any other video? (Must be no.)
- Is each of the four modes marked ✓ with its fix?
