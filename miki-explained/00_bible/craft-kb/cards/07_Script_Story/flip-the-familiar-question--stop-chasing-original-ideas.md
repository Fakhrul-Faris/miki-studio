## CREATIVE_INTENT
"Every story's already been told." Park Chan-wook kept Oldboy's premise (a man imprisoned for years, released, told to
find out why) "but then he flipped the question. Not why was he locked up, but why was he let go?" Leone remade Yojimbo
as A Fistful of Dollars in a new language; Tarantino took the last 10 minutes of City on Fire and "made an entire movie
about it". Originality is interpretation: keep the premise, change the question or the zoom.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Write the usual question the topic answers ("macam mana nak jimat?").
2. Write 3 flips: reverse it, ask from the other side, or zoom on its last step.
3. Keep the flip whose answer is true and verifiable in the claim ledger.
4. Put the flipped question in frame-0 text (≤ 8 words) or L02.
5. Check the premise and facts stay unchanged; only the question moved.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Topic gate in `episode.md`: "Usual question → flipped question". The flip drives the open loop; the mechanism answers it.
### Worked example
Topic: pasar malam traders (generic, illustrative).
BEFORE (usual): "Macam mana peniaga pasar malam buat untung?"
AFTER flips: reverse "Kenapa ramai peniaga pasar malam berhenti?" · other side "Kenapa korang beli lebih bila ada
lampu?" · zoom on last step "Apa jadi pada makanan yang tak habis pukul 11?" → keep the zoom. Frame 0: "Pukul 11.
Makanan ni nak ke mana?"
### Check
- Are 3 flips written and one chosen?
- Is the flipped question's answer verifiable?
- Is the premise unchanged?
