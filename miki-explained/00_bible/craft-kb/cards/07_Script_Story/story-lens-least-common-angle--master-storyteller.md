## CREATIVE_INTENT
A cool topic isn't enough; dozens of others cover it. A **story lens** is your unique angle — like a prism in front of a
white beam: everyone sees the naked beam the same way; the lens changes what they see. Taylor Swift at the Super Bowl:
most common lens = what she wore, when she arrived, her reactions; less common = a prediction of what happens; least
common = the business impact she drove for the NFL. He took that one to be "a category of one"; it got ~1 M views.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before the claim ledger, write the topic in ≤ 6 words.
2. List the 3 most common angles others already post.
3. List 2 less common angles (a prediction, a comparison).
4. List 1–2 least common angles: the money mechanism behind it, for a small business or a viewer's wallet.
5. Pick the least common angle that can be proven with real numbers or plain arithmetic.
6. Write the chosen lens as one line in the topic gate: "Lens: …".

## AGENT_EXECUTION
`episode.md` topic gate gets "Lens ladder: common / less / least → chosen". The chosen lens drives the frame-0
contradiction.

### Worked example
Topic: Ramadan bazaar stalls (illustrative numbers).
Common: best food to buy; prices this year; crowded bazaars. Less common: which stalls sell out first. Least common: how
much a kuih stall keeps after 30 days of tapak rent, ingredients and helper pay.
Chosen lens: "RM15,000 jualan sebulan. Tinggal berapa?" (worked out from count × price − costs, illustrative).

### Check
- Are common, less common and least common angles listed?
- Is the chosen lens not one of the 3 most common?
- Can it be proven with real figures or plain arithmetic?
