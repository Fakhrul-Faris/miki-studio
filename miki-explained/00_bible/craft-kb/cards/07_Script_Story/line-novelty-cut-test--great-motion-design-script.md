## METADATA
07_Script_Story | Dan Neale, Motion Story (blog) | script v1 → v2 edit pass

## CREATIVE_INTENT
"Every word in a motion design script needs to justify its existence." Neale's test: read every sentence and ask "what
does the viewer learn or feel from this that they didn't learn or feel from the previous sentence?" If the answer is
"nothing new", cut it. His pair: "Our innovative, cutting-edge platform leverages advanced technology" (nine words that
communicate nothing) versus "See which campaigns are working before your budget runs out" (eleven words with urgency,
specificity and a reason to care).

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Number every script line (L01…L11).
2. For each line from L02, write in ≤ 8 words what the viewer newly learns or feels.
3. If that cell is empty or repeats the previous cell, mark the line CUT.
4. Delete every CUT line; do not merge its words into a neighbour.
5. Before keeping an adjective, check it carries a fact (a number, a place, a time); else delete it.
6. Replace any line made only of abstract words ("innovative", "efficient", "advanced") with a specific outcome plus a stake.
7. Re-run steps 2–3 on the shortened script until no line is CUT.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Add a temporary third column "new for viewer" to the script table in `episode.md` during the v2 edit; delete the column
once every line passes. Run it before RECIPE-D's word count, so the count measures a script with no dead lines.
### Worked example
Topic: a nasi lemak stall in Shah Alam pricing its egg (numbers illustrative).
BEFORE
- L03 "Telur ni kos dia RM0.50 sebiji."
- L04 "Jadi kos telur memang penting untuk peniaga." ← new for viewer: nothing (restates L03)
- L05 "Kalau jual RM1.50, untung RM1 sebiji."
AFTER
- L03 "Telur ni kos dia RM0.50 sebiji."
- L04 "Jual RM1.50. Untung RM1 sebiji."
- L05 "Seratus pinggan sehari? RM100 dari telur je."
Each line now adds a fact: cost → margin → daily total.
### Check
- Does every line from L02 have a non-empty "new for viewer" cell?
- Is any cell a restatement of the cell above it?
- Is every adjective carrying a fact or deleted?
- Does no line consist only of abstract words?
