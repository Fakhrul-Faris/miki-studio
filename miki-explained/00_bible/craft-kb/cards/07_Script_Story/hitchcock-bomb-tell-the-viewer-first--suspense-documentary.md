## METADATA

## CREATIVE_INTENT
Hitchcock's "bomb theory": two people talk and a bomb explodes — "fifteen seconds of surprise". Tell the audience a bomb
is under the table and will go off in five minutes, and the same mundane conversation becomes "unbearably tense". In
documentary: withhold information from subjects while sharing it with viewers; release it strategically "through
narration, text cards or intercutting"; foreshadow through details — "showing a risk or obstacle early that pays off
later". It "turns passive watchers into active participants".

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before writing L02, name the hidden cost (the "bomb") and the moment it "goes off" (the RM payoff).
2. Show the bomb on screen early (L02–L03) in accent, with a short text label, before the voice explains it.
3. Write the middle lines from the unaware point of view ("Korang tekan 'bayar'…").
4. Keep the bomb visible in each of those setups (corner of frame, still in accent).
5. Detonate on the payoff line with the RM number.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Script: mark the plant line `[bomb]` and the payoff `[goes off]`. Shot-list: the bomb object appears in every setup
between them (`bomb visible: yes`).

### Worked example
Topic: "Beli Raya ansuran 0 % — betul ke takde kos?" (illustrative).
- BEFORE: L06 "Rupanya ada caj lewat RM10 sebulan." (surprise only at the end)
- AFTER: L02 (visual): the checkout screen, a tiny "lewat bayar: RM10/bulan" line glows in accent while the voice says "0 % faedah, senang je." L03–L05 follow the buyer's unaware steps; the line stays lit in the corner. L06 "Lewat dua bulan? Tambah RM20." — the line expands to fill the frame.

### Check
- Is the hidden cost on screen before the voice mentions it?
- Is it visible through the middle setups?
- Does the payoff line "detonate" it with a number?

## NOTES
- The worked example's fee is illustrative; check the real terms of any named provider before use.
