## CREATIVE_INTENT
"I'm always empathizing with the viewer… who's watching this and what is happening in their minds as this information hits
them — the most important sensibility." Harris weighed "husband to 40 wives": it could turn off believing viewers (a known
anti-Mormon trope), but "most of the audience will find that fascinating… it will hook them", so it stayed. "Every word is
thought through in that way." His style comes from "the way I wish it had been described to me".

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. List the 2–3 audience groups watching (e.g. owners, customers, workers of that trade).
2. For each charged detail, write each group's likely reaction in ≤ 6 words.
3. Keep the detail if most viewers gain interest and no group is insulted by a false or one-sided frame.
4. If a minority will switch off, soften the wording, not the fact.
5. Read the script once as the most sceptical group; mark every line where they would stop.
6. Fix marked lines before showing the script.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Add a short "Viewer reaction" note under the script in `episode.md`: groups, charged details, keep/soften decision.
### Worked example
Topic: why pasar malam traders pay "duit tapak" (illustrative).
BEFORE L02: "Peniaga pasar malam kena bayar macam-macam, kesian betul."
Groups: traders, shoppers, council staff. Charged detail: "RM15 satu malam untuk tapak". Traders: "betul, mahal"; shoppers: "oh, patutlah harga naik"; council: "itu yuran sah". Keep, neutral frame.
AFTER L02: "Setiap malam, peniaga ni bayar RM15 untuk tapak sebesar meja ni."
### Check
- Are the audience groups listed?
- Was each charged detail checked against each group?
- Is no group insulted by a one-sided frame?
