## METADATA
07_Script_Story | Dan Neale, Motion Story (blog) | frame-0 text + L01

## CREATIVE_INTENT
"You have about five seconds, maybe 15 words, to earn the viewer's attention. If the opening line doesn't create
recognition, tension, or curiosity, nothing that follows matters." Failing openers: "Welcome to [product name]" (nobody
cares yet), "[Company] is the leading platform for…" (self-congratulation), "In today's fast-paced world…" (generic).
Working openers create "a specific scenario. The viewer either recognises themselves or doesn't. Both outcomes are
useful" — the right viewers stay, the wrong ones self-select out. Examples: "Three spreadsheets, two time zones, and
nobody knows which version is current."

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before writing L01, write the one moment the target viewer has lived (place, object, number).
2. Keep L01 within ~5 s spoken: ≤ 15 words (our frame-0 text ≤ 8).
3. Reject the line if it opens with a greeting, a brand claim, or "zaman sekarang"/"in today's world".
4. Make the line create recognition, tension or curiosity; name which one.
5. Use counts and concrete objects ("tiga resit, dua akaun") over categories ("pengurusan kewangan").
6. Read it as an outsider: would a non-target viewer know at once it isn't for them? Keep it if yes.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Frame-0 text (≤ 8 words, the contradiction) and L01 (the spoken scenario, ≤ 15 words) are written as a pair; note beside
them which of recognition / tension / curiosity they create. The hero object on frame 0 is the scenario's object.
### Worked example
Topic: a small kedai runcit owner who mixes shop and personal money (generic shop).
BEFORE: "Hai semua! Hari ni kita belajar pasal pengurusan kewangan kedai."
AFTER — frame 0: "Kedai untung. Duit tetap tak cukup." · L01: "Satu laci duit, untuk kedai, untuk dapur, untuk
anak." (recognition: the owner sees their own drawer; a salaried viewer self-selects out.)
### Check
- Is L01 ≤ 15 words and frame-0 ≤ 8 words?
- Is there no greeting, brand claim or "in today's world" opener?
- Does the line contain a concrete object or count?
- Is it labelled recognition, tension or curiosity?
