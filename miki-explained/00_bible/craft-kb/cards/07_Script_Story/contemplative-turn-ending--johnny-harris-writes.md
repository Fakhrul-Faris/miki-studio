## CREATIVE_INTENT
After the "who did what to whom" action, Harris always has a moment "usually towards the end" to "zoom out and reflect and
contemplate what this means" — "conclusion energy": the music hits, a slower pace, a more emotional tone; "less who did
what to whom", more "the postscript at the end of a documentary". This "poetic voice" is "less precise, more human… raising
questions, showing complexity". Switching from the cognitive tone to a feeling tone "makes it more engaging… people
remember what they felt" — a "sweet and sour" experience. It grounds technical topics in the fact "this is about humans".

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before the end line, write one "zoom-out" line: what this means for ordinary people today.
2. Make it less precise than the mechanism lines: no new numbers except the one RM wow number.
3. Slow the voice: add one "…" pause and drop to ~2.8–3.0 words/s for this line (~ inferred).
4. Mark a music change at this line (thinner, warmer, or a held chord), no stinger.
5. Pull the camera back to a wider or calmer setup here, held ≥ 3 s.
6. End with a question the viewer can take a side on; it must come out of the zoom-out line.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Script L09–L10 = zoom-out; L11 = question. Shot list: the zoom-out row is the calmest setup (slow eased push-out, longer
hold), then the loop back to frame 0's object/framing. Voice text: [thoughtful] tag, one "…".
### Worked example
Topic: a tailor shop in Kota Bharu losing Raya orders to online baju (illustrative).
BEFORE L10–L11: "Jadi itulah sebab kedai jahit makin kurang. Follow untuk lagi."
AFTER L10: "Dulu, satu baju Raya maksudnya tiga kali datang kedai… sekarang, satu klik, RM89." (slow, held wide macro of tape measure on an empty cutting table). L11: "Korang pilih yang mana — muat sempurna, atau sampai esok?"
