## CREATIVE_INTENT
Fern went from 0 to ~2.2 million subscribers in ~2 years with ~60 videos; every best performer shares "an effective
visual hook". "YouTube is an inherently visual medium": people click because there is something in there they want to
**see** — a dark-web criminal, the inside of a CIA base, the inner workings of a strange gun, the world's largest
aquarium breaking. It can be "a person, a place, an object, an action, an event", but "the video's topic has to be seen
to be understood". The Aquadome open sets a dated, calm scene (15 November 2022, a wintry afternoon in Berlin, guests
lounging under the aquarium) and then delivers the event: 1 million litres of water rush through the lobby.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before you pass the topic gate, name the one thing viewers will want to see (object, place, action, event).
2. Reject the topic if it can be fully understood without seeing anything.
3. Put that thing on screen in frame 0, already in motion.
4. Set the scene in one line of concrete facts (date/place/object) before the event, when the event is the hook.
5. Deliver the visual event early, inside the first ~10–20 % of the runtime.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Add "Visual hook: <thing>" to the topic gate in `episode.md`. Frame-0 brief Idea = the visual hook; it is also the
episode's hero object (RECIPE-D one hero). In a 60 s episode the hook pays off by ~L02 (≤ ~8 s), as the source paid off
~1:30 into 12 min (~12 %).

### Worked example
Topic: why a pasar malam stall prices "3 for RM10".
- BEFORE: frame 0 = text "Kenapa 3 untuk RM10?" on a dark background.
- AFTER: visual hook = the stall's pile of keychains under one bulb; frame 0: macro, three keychains slide together on
  the tray as a hand-written "3/RM10" card tips into frame (no hands — the card tips by itself). Frame-0 text: "Satu
  RM4. Tiga RM10. Siapa untung?"

### Check
- Can the topic's hook only be understood by seeing it?
- Is the visual hook on screen in frame 0, moving?
- Is the hook's event delivered by ~L02?
