## CREATIVE_INTENT
A refinery engineer explained gasoline grades to a non-engineer in under a minute. There is "a lot more complexity" to
mixing grades, but the listener "only needed the overall perspective to answer my question." "It's tempting to wow
people with our knowledge… what really wows people is our ability to zero in on just what they need."

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before you draft the Mechanism, write the viewer's question in their own words.
2. List everything you know that touches it.
3. Keep only what answers that question; target 1 mechanism, ≤ 3 facts.
4. Move the rest to a "Not claimed / left out" note in `episode.md`.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Script header: "Viewer's question: …" and "Left out: …". Every Mechanism line must answer the question; the claim
ledger's "Not claimed" list holds the dropped detail.

### Worked example
Topic: "Kenapa kena bayar caj bila guna kad kat kedai kecil?" (card payments do carry merchant fees — true mechanism;
rate illustrative)
- BEFORE: lines on interchange, acquirer, scheme fees, MDR tiers, card network rules.
- AFTER: viewer's question "Siapa ambik duit tu?" Kept: "Setiap kali korang tap kad, bank ambik sikit dari kedai —
  contoh 1%. Jual RM50, kedai dapat RM49.50." Left out: interchange/acquirer split.

### Check
- Is the viewer's question written in their words?
- Does every Mechanism line answer it?
- Is the dropped detail listed, not squeezed in?
