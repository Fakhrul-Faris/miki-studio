## CREATIVE_INTENT
Confusion = comprehension loss: the topic isn't delayed, but the words or their order are unclear, so the viewer can't
judge if it's for them. Bad: "These guys built a $30 million empire and the online money they made is most difficult to
earn if you don't develop a journaling practice like they did." Good: "These guys built a $30 million empire and their
secret for earning money online was their insane journaling practice." Tactics: fewer words, but enough that the viewer
can't misunderstand; simpler words, ideally a sixth-grade reading level; direct active voice ("the dog jumped", not "the
jump of the dog"). Final proof: read the 1–2 hook sentences in isolation and ask whether they can be understood in the
wrong way or more than one way; if so, rewrite to remove the alternative paths. He also suggests an LLM prompt: keep the
meaning exactly the same, rewrite at a sixth-grade reading level so there's no misunderstanding.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Put the subject first, then the verb, then the payoff noun ("Gerai ni untung RM300 sebab…").
2. Replace every word a 12-year-old wouldn't use with an everyday word.
3. Rewrite passive or noun-heavy phrases as active verbs.
4. Remove words until meaning would break, then stop.
5. Read the hook alone and write every other way it could be understood.
6. Rewrite until only one reading is left.
7. Apply the same pass to the frame-0 text.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
You are the LLM in his tip: run the rewrite instruction on your own hook ("same meaning, sixth-grade level, no
misunderstanding") and keep both versions in the draft until the user picks. Malay: prefer everyday words (untung, rugi,
modal) over formal ones (keuntungan bersih, perbelanjaan operasi).

### Worked example
Topic: a car wash's monthly profit (illustrative).
BEFORE: "Pendapatan kedai cuci kereta ni mungkin nampak tinggi tapi selepas ditolak perbelanjaan operasi yang dibuat
setiap bulan ia tak banyak." (passive, long, two readings: is the revenue high or not?)
AFTER: "Kedai cuci kereta ni dapat RM12,000 sebulan. Simpan? RM1,800 je."

### Check
- Is the hook subject → verb → payoff, in active voice?
- Would a 12-year-old know every word?
- Is there only one way to read it?
