## CREATIVE_INTENT
The outro is not an afterthought: "you can add retention and shares and likes and rewatches at a much higher clip" by
leaving viewers "with a high note": wrap up cleanly, "summarize those points and remind them of the pain point solved",
"the final push to remind them that we did in fact have reality beat expectations." It's "like the free chocolate that the
restaurant gives you when they bring the check" — a reminder of the good service. Likes, comments, shares and rewatches
"all kind of come from leaving them with a high note."

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before the end question, write one recap line: the pain from L01 now solved, with its RM number.
2. Name the steps in ≤ 6 words total, or skip them if the number says enough.
3. Put the recap on the hero object in its after-state.
4. Follow with the ≤ 7-word side-taking question; no follow request.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Script: L09 or L10 = recap; L11 = question. Shot list: the recap row returns toward frame 0's object, after-state, with
the number; the last row lands on frame-0 framing (loop).

### Worked example
Topic: checking whether a sale is real (generic; RM figure illustrative).
- BEFORE: L10 "Okay itu je untuk hari ni." L11 "Follow untuk lagi!"
- AFTER: L10 "Screenshot, semak resit, report. Tiga benda, RM40 tak hilang bulan ni." L11 "Korang pernah semak resit
  sendiri?"

### Check
- Is there one recap line naming the solved pain and a number?
- Is the last line a side-taking question, not a follow request?
- Does the final frame return to frame 0's object/framing?
