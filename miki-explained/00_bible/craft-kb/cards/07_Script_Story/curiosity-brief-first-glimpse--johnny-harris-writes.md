## CREATIVE_INTENT
Harris starts with a "reporting brief: this is what I want to know about this thing" — a little search, then "a million
questions" for the researcher, who builds a 60–80 page "info doc" over 3–4 weeks. At the start he has "fractured visions" of
what it could feel like. Then "you descend into reality… this is not nearly as cool as it was when we started" — the curse of
knowledge, "you forget what it feels like to be curious and to not know". "The work of storytelling is to remember what it
was like at the beginning… hold it, guard it, push it up the other side of the valley." His hardest days: day 2–3 of
writing without having "cracked the satisfying explanation".

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before research, write the reporting brief: 8–15 questions a curious outsider would ask.
2. Write the first-glimpse note: what made the topic exciting, in ≤ 3 lines, plus the image you see.
3. Research only to answer the brief; log answers in the claim ledger.
4. After script v1, reread the first-glimpse note.
5. If the script no longer delivers that feeling, rewrite the hook and end toward it.
6. If after 2 drafts you can't state the satisfying explanation in 1 sentence, report more before drafting again.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
`episode.md` sections "Reporting brief" and "First glimpse" above the claim ledger; checked at script v2.
### Worked example
Topic: why a RM1 kuih stall in Bangi sells out by 9 a.m. (numbers illustrative).
Brief (excerpt): How many pieces a day? What does one cost to make? Who buys before 9? Why not make more? ...
First glimpse: "300 kuih gone before the office crowd even arrives — the stall wins by stopping." Script v1 drifted into
ingredient prices; v2 restores the hook: "Pukul 9 dah habis… dan makcik ni memang tak nak buat lebih."
### Check
- Is there a brief of ≥ 8 questions written before research?
- Is the first-glimpse note on file and reread at v2?
- Can the satisfying explanation be stated in 1 sentence?
