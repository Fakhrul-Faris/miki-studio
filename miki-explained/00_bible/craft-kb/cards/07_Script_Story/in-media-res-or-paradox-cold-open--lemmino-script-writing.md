## METADATA
07_Script_Story | general (LEMMiNO style summary) | frame-0 text, L01

## CREATIVE_INTENT
LEMMiNO videos "frequently start in media res — either dropping the viewer directly into a pivotal moment of the
timeline or presenting a stark, intriguing paradox before the title card appears."

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before you write L01, list the topic's pivotal moment and its paradox.
2. Choose in media res when the moment is visual and dated (a closing day, a price jump).
3. Choose paradox when the topic is a rule or system with no single moment.
4. Write frame-0 text ≤ 8 words; context comes after, never before.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Frame-0 text + L01. In media res: frame 0 shows the hero mid-change (already moving). Paradox: two true facts side by
side on the hero.

### Worked example
Topic: why a kedai makan with a long queue can still close down (plain arithmetic, illustrative).
- BEFORE: "Hari ni kita bincang kenapa kedai makan tutup."
- AFTER (paradox): frame-0 text "Beratur panjang. Tapi tutup kedai." L01 "Jual 300 pinggan sehari, tapi akaun dia
  merah." Frame 0: macro of a tray of order slips piling up while a shutter's shadow slides down over them.

### Check
- Does L01 open on the moment or the paradox, with no background first?
- Is frame-0 text ≤ 8 words?
- Is the hero already moving in frame 0?
