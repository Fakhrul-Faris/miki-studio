## CREATIVE_INTENT
After the hook ("Even a small drawing error can cost thousands"), the script stacks the pain in three growing time
units: "Engineers spend **hours** checking drawings, **days** chasing feedback, and **weeks** fixing what should have
been caught in **minutes**." The fourth, small unit (minutes) is the payoff the product delivers.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before you write L02, pick one pain the hook named.
2. Write three clauses, each with a bigger unit (hours → days → weeks, or RM10 → RM100 → RM1,000).
3. Give each clause a different concrete action (checking, chasing, fixing).
4. End with the small unit the fix costs ("…in minutes").
5. Keep the whole triad ≤ ~20 words so it fits one line.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Script L02 or L02–L03. Shot list: one setup per clause (3 setups, ~1.5–2 s each); the unit on screen grows each time
(counter or stacking object), the accent sits on the biggest unit, and the small final unit lands on the fix's hero.

### Worked example
Topic: a kedai runcit owner who never records stock (illustrative).
- BEFORE: "Tak rekod stok ni banyak masalah, rugi masa dan duit."
- AFTER: "Sejam kira stok. Seminggu cari barang hilang. Sebulan rugi RM800. Padahal boleh settle lima minit."
  Shots: clock hand spins one turn → calendar flips 7 pages → coins stack to RM800 (accent) → phone screen shows a
  stock list, timer "5:00".

### Check
- Do the three units grow in size?
- Is each clause a different concrete action that can be shown?
- Does the triad end on the small unit the fix costs?
- Is the triad ≤ ~20 words?
