## CREATIVE_INTENT
Three acts = 25 % / 50 % / 25 %; structure is "a guideline for creating a resonant psychological transformation" — a gift
box whose contents can be anything. Weiland's 8 parts: **Hook** (opening; "why should the audience keep watching", the
first domino) · **Inciting event** (~12 %; first brush with the conflict, still a choice) · **First plot point** (~25 %;
threshold of no return) · **First pinch point** (~37 %; shows the antagonistic force and what's at stake) · **Midpoint**
(50 %; "moment of truth", reactive → proactive) · **Second pinch point** (~62 %; stakes higher than ever, sets up the
third plot point) · **Third plot point** (~75 %; false victory followed by the low moment) · **Climax** (final 10 %;
decides whether the goal is won) · **Resolution** (lets the climax land).

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Map the beats to lines: Hook L01 · Inciting L02 · Plot point 1 L03 · Pinch 1 L04 · Midpoint L05–L06 · Pinch 2 L07 ·
   Plot point 3 (false win → low) L08 · Climax L09–L10 · Resolution + question L11.
2. Make L03 a point of no return: after it, the owner can't go back.
3. Make L04 and L07 show what could be lost (the RM at risk).
4. Make L08 a win that costs more than expected, then a low moment.
5. Let L11 land the climax's result on the hero before the question.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Write the beat name beside each line in Script v1's table for story episodes. Shot list: the midpoint row gets the
biggest state change (RECIPE-D "return to the hero" in the middle); pinch rows show the risk object (debt, rival).

### Worked example
Everyday Owners-style story of a generic burger stall (illustrative numbers):
L01 Hook "RM9,000 sebulan… dari satu gerai burger." · L02 Inciting: rent doubles to RM1,600. · L03 PP1: he quits his
office job; no going back. · L04 Pinch: a franchise opens across the road. · L05–06 Midpoint: he stops reacting, starts
selling only 3 burgers, done right. · L07 Pinch 2: patty supplier raises price 30 %. · L08 PP3: queue doubles (win) but
he's out of stock by 9 pm (low). · L09–10 Climax: pre-orders by WhatsApp; RM9,000 month. · L11 "Kurang menu… lagi
untung?"

### Check
- Is there a point of no return by L03?
- Does the midpoint flip the owner from reacting to acting?
- Is there a false victory before the climax?
