## METADATA
07_Script_Story | Dan Neale, Motion Story (blog) | beat outline

## CREATIVE_INTENT
"Before writing a single sentence, I map the structure." Neale's explainer sequence: Opening hook (5–10 s), Context
(10–15 s, why the problem matters, what it costs), The turn (5 s, the solution), How it works (25–35 s, two to four key
mechanisms), Proof (5–10 s), Call to action (5–10 s). "The timing is flexible, but the sequence matters. Rearranging
these sections almost always makes the script worse." The viewer must feel the problem before valuing the solution, and
understand how it works before trusting the proof. The most common mistake is feature listing ("We offer X, Y, Z"); the
fix is to start with the customer's problem.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before writing lines, list six beats with a target second range each.
2. Assign hook 5–10 s, context 10–15 s, turn ~5 s, mechanism 25–35 s, proof 5–10 s, end 5–10 s.
3. In the mechanism beat, name 2–4 mechanisms; no more.
4. Check that the context beat states a cost (an RM number or time lost).
5. Keep the order; if a beat seems better earlier, rewrite it, don't move it.
6. If the outline starts with a feature or product name, replace beat 1 with the viewer's problem.
7. Replace the call to action with a ≤ 7-word question the viewer can take a side on.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Mapping to our beats: Hook = hook; Context = open loop + cost; Turn + How it works = mechanism (S16: "Kalau korang nak …,
ni caranya. N step je." + 3–5 steps, which fits the 2–4/3–5 mechanism count); Proof = proof↔payoff (a real RM number);
CTA = End question. At ~3.4 words/s: hook ≈ 17–34 words, context ≈ 34–51, turn ≈ 17, mechanism ≈ 85–119, proof ≈
17–34, end ≈ ≤ 7 words on screen.
### Worked example
Topic: "Jual kek dari rumah: harga ikut kos, bukan ikut kawan" (illustrative numbers).
BEFORE (feature-first): "Ada tiga cara kira harga kek: kos bahan, kos masa, kos packaging…"
AFTER outline:
- Hook (0–6 s): "Kek RM60 ni… sebenarnya rugi."
- Context (6–18 s): bahan RM38, kotak RM4, gas + elektrik RM5, 4 jam kerja.
- Turn (18–23 s): "Kalau korang nak harga yang tak rugi, ni caranya. Tiga step je."
- Mechanism (23–50 s): 1 tambah semua kos (RM47) · 2 letak upah RM10 sejam (RM40) · 3 tambah untung 20 %.
- Proof (50–56 s): RM87 × 1.2 = RM104.40 → "harga sebenar dalam RM105."
- End (56–60 s): "RM60 atau RM105 — korang pilih mana?"
### Check
- Does the outline have six beats in this order with second ranges?
- Does the mechanism hold 2–4 (S16: 3–5) items only?
- Does the context beat name a cost?
- Is the end a side-taking question, not a request?
