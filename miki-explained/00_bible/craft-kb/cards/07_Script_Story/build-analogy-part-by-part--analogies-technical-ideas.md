## CREATIVE_INTENT
Walk the audience through the analogy in steps, mapping each part: in a house analogy for code, the foundation is the
basic code structure and building walls is adding functionality.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. List the concept's parts in order (2–4 parts).
2. Map each part to one part of the analogy object.
3. Give each mapped part its own line or half-line.
4. Show each part as one state change on its word, in the same order.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
One shot-list row per mapped part; the event column names the word and the part that appears.

### Worked example
Topic: what a kedai's monthly cost is made of (illustrative numbers).
BEFORE: "Kos bulanan kedai ada sewa, gaji, bil dan stok."
AFTER: "Bayangkan satu bekas nasi lemak. Nasi tu sewa, RM1,500. Sambal tu gaji. Telur tu bil. Baru ada untung — ikan bilis je."
Rows: `| 9 | "Nasi tu sewa, RM1,500" | top-down macro on the packet, opened | rice layer lights in accent, RM1,500 counter on "sewa" |` …

### Check
- Does each concept part map to exactly one analogy part?
- Does each mapping get its own line/word and its own event?
- Is the order of parts the same in script and shot list?
