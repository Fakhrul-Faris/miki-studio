## CREATIVE_INTENT
"Introduce a detail or question early, then connect it later when the mechanism clicks into place."

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before you lock L01, pick one visible detail of the hero that the payoff will explain.
2. Show it in frame 0–L02 without explaining it (≤ ~1 s on screen, in focus).
3. Do not mention it again until the payoff line.
4. In the payoff line, return to the same object and framing and name the detail.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Shot list: mark the plant row `plant: <detail>` and the payoff row `payoff: <detail>`; same camera angle in both
(the loop rule). Frame brief "Eye" for the payoff row = the planted detail, in accent.

### Worked example
Topic: why the "dari RM299" price can be fake (EX46).
- BEFORE: the tag just shows RM149; the price-history screenshot appears out of nowhere at L05.
- AFTER: frame 0: the tag's small print "harga asal" is in focus for ~0.8 s next to RM299 (plant). L05: the screenshot
  from before the sale shows the item at RM149 the whole time; cut back to the same tag macro, "harga asal" lights in
  accent (payoff).

### Check
- Is the planted detail visible early without being explained?
- Is it untouched between plant and payoff?
- Does the payoff return to the same object and framing?
