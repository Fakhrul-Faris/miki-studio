## CREATIVE_INTENT
"Let me show you how China uses cartography to assert power" also "promises there's a lot more going on — that's a hook
device… stick around." Harris says it "constantly, without ever saying it explicitly". Good storytelling is "always
foreshadowing, letting you see a little bit ahead, so you are compelled to stick around and be engrossed in the now."

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. At each reveal line, add a clause that hints at a further layer not yet shown.
2. Hint, never announce: no "tunggu sampai habis" / "wait till the end".
3. Make the hint specific to an object or number the viewer can picture.
4. Pay off each hint within the next 2–3 lines (≤ ~10 s).
5. Limit to one open hint at a time.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Script: open-loop line and step transitions. Shot list: show a partly hidden element (an unrevealed price tag, a blurred row
on a UI) on the hint word; reveal it on the payoff word.
### Worked example
Topic: checking a used car's price at a dealer in Kajang (illustrative).
BEFORE L03: "Tunggu sampai habis untuk tip paling penting!"
AFTER L03: "Harga kat cermin RM38,000… tapi ada satu nombor lain kat geran yang dealer tak sebut." (macro of windscreen tag; the geran corner stays out of focus). Payoff L05: geran racks into focus, year circled.
### Check
- Does each hint name something picturable?
- Is there no explicit "wait till the end" request?
- Is each hint paid off within ~10 s?
