## METADATA

## CREATIVE_INTENT
The narration "avoids hyperbole, jump-scares or melodramatic vocal inflections", with occasional "subtle, dry humor or
brief personal observations". "Use simple, declarative sentences and let the weight of the facts — paired with
deliberate pauses — create tension."

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before handover, delete hype words: gila, gempak, shocking, "korang takkan percaya".
2. Turn each line into a plain statement of fact.
3. Put one "…" before the heaviest number.
4. Allow at most one dry aside in the whole script.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Script + voice text. Voice tags stay calm (≤ 2 per line). The paused number is the shot's accent event.

### Worked example
- BEFORE: "Korang takkan percaya berapa banyak duit hilang sebab scam QR ni, memang gila!"
- AFTER: "Satu sticker QR palsu. Seminggu. … RM600 masuk akaun orang lain." (illustrative amount)

### Check
- Are there zero hype words?
- Is the heaviest number preceded by a pause?
- Is there at most one aside?
