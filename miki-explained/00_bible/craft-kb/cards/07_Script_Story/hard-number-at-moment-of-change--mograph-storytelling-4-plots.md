## CREATIVE_INTENT
Of 7 short stories, only Ownr "puts a number on its promise: incorporation in less than 10 minutes"; Lendi names "FEES,"
"TAX" and "RATES" without figures. "1 of 7 gave a specific number." "If you have one number your buyer can check, put it at
the moment of change. A drawn after-state plus a number is a promise. Without the number, it's a mood." Expertise also
"has numbers" (PharMerica: 30 years, 24/7 pharmacists). A months-long story's destination should be "a number your buyer
cares about" (Recruit CRM).

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before writing the payoff line, pick one number the viewer could check (RM, minutes, count).
2. Place it in the same line as the change, not in a separate stats line.
3. Show it on the changed object on the word that says it.
4. Use one number only at that moment; move others earlier or cut them.
5. Log it in the claim ledger as verified or illustrative.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Script: payoff line contains the change + the number. Shot-list event: "`tick` to RM… on 'word'" on the object in its
after-state. raea: `<Animate enter={tick({ to: 240, prefix: "RM", frames: 24 })} at={word("240")} />`, fading in ≥ 12 f
(house: no pop — set the fade).

### Worked example
Topic: a kedai runcit switching from a paper hutang book to a simple spreadsheet (illustrative).
- BEFORE: L09 "Lepas tu, semua jadi lagi senang dan teratur."
- AFTER: L09 "Sebulan je, RM180 hutang yang dah lupa tu… masuk balik." The notebook closes; the till drawer slides out
  and RM180 counts up on its display on "RM180".

### Check
- Does the payoff line carry exactly one checkable number?
- Does the number appear on the changed object on its word?
- Is it in the claim ledger?
