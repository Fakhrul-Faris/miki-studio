## CREATIVE_INTENT
Once the mechanism is laid out, Vox adds friction: "highlight bottlenecks, hidden costs, trade-offs, or unintended
consequences", and raises the stakes: "show why the topic still matters or where the system breaks down."

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before the payoff, ask: where does this system break or cost more than it seems?
2. Pick one friction type: bottleneck, hidden cost, trade-off or side effect.
3. State it in one line with an RM number or count.
4. Follow with why it still matters to the viewer this week.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
One line (L07 or L08). Shot list: the hero object shows the friction literally (a crack, a coin leaving, a stack
toppling) with the accent on the hidden part.

### Worked example
Topic: buy-now-pay-later for a RM1,200 phone (illustrative numbers, plain arithmetic).
- BEFORE: "BNPL memudahkan pengguna membeli barang."
- AFTER: L07 "Tapi lambat bayar sekali, kena caj lewat RM10." L08 "Lambat tiga bulan? RM30 hilang, phone sama je."
  Shot: three coins slide off the phone, one per month, each lighting in accent.

### Check
- Is there one named friction, not a vague "ada risiko"?
- Does it carry a number?
- Does the next line say why it matters now?
- Is any fee or rule in it verified in the claim ledger before use?

## NOTES
- The RM10 late fee is a placeholder; a real episode must quote the actual provider's fee from its terms.
