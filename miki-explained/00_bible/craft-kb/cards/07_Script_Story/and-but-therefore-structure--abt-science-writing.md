## CREATIVE_INTENT
Most stories run: characters in their normal day-to-day life → a problem spurs the action → a journey to solve it →
return to normal life with new insight (and sometimes a teaser). Randy Olson, marine biologist turned filmmaker,
distils this into **And, But, Therefore**: *And* states generally known facts, *But* establishes a tension, conflict or
problem, *Therefore* gives the solution and ties all narrative strands together. Writing that consciously uses ABT
becomes "easier to understand and more interesting".

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before you write any line, write the whole episode as one ABT sentence: "X and Y, but Z, therefore W."
2. Fill the **And** part with facts the viewer already accepts (their normal day). Keep it to ~1–2 lines.
3. Introduce the **But** part: one tension, conflict or problem. Only one.
4. Make the problem block the normal day, so the viewer must follow the "journey" (steps / mechanism).
5. Write the **Therefore** part as the solution that answers the But directly.
6. Check that Therefore ties every strand opened in And and But; cut any strand it does not close.
7. Show the "return": the normal day again, now with the new insight (our loop to the frame-0 object).

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Map onto the script formula and S16 how-to:
- **And** = L01 (hook context the viewer recognises) — still needs the frame-0 contradiction, so the And line already
  carries the seed of the But.
- **But** = L01–L02 (open loop: the problem). S16: "Kalau korang nak …, ni caranya" sits at the But→Therefore hinge.
- **Therefore** = L03–L09 (mechanism / steps / RM proof). The "journey" is the steps.
- **Return with insight** = L10–L11, ending on the end question and the loop back to the frame-0 object.
Write the ABT sentence at the top of the script draft in `episode.md` as a comment so the critic can test it.

### Worked example
Topic: a kedai runcit in Shah Alam choosing between cash and QR (illustrative numbers).
BEFORE (and-then list): "Kedai runcit terima cash. Ada juga QR. QR ada caj. Ada juga kad. Ni beza dia."
AFTER (ABT sentence): "Pakcik kedai runcit terima cash setiap hari **and** rasa duit tu selamat dalam laci, **but**
setiap bulan RM300 hilang sebab salah kira dan duit palsu, **therefore** dia buka QR — duit masuk akaun, app kira sendiri."
- L01 (And): "Kedai ni terima cash je. Pakcik kata paling selamat."
- L02 (But): "Tapi bulan lepas, RM300 hilang. Tak tahu ke mana."
- L03–L09 (Therefore): the QR steps and the RM0 fee.
- L11 (return): "Laci cash ke QR — korang pilih mana?"

### Check
- Can the whole script be read as one "And …, but …, therefore …" sentence?
- Is there exactly one But (one problem)?
- Does the Therefore answer that But directly, not a different question?
- Does every strand opened before the Therefore close by L11?
