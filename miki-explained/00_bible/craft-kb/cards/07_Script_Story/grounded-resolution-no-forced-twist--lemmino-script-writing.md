## CREATIVE_INTENT
"Rather than forcing a dramatic twist, his endings zoom out to reflect on why the event endures, what remains genuinely
unknown, or the broader human perspective behind the history."

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before you write the end, check the payoff is fully supported by the claim ledger.
2. If it isn't, don't invent a twist; say what is still unknown.
3. Zoom out one level (one shop → every shop like it).
4. Turn that zoom-out into a ≤ 7-word side-taking question.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
L09 = the grounded answer; L10 = the zoom-out; L11 = question. Camera: the pull-out into a void (RECIPE-D vocabulary),
then back to the frame-0 hero for the loop.

### Worked example
Topic: the kedai makan with a queue that still closed (illustrative).
- BEFORE: "Rupanya owner dia lari dengan duit!" (unsupported twist)
- AFTER: L09 "Untung 30 sen sepinggan tak cukup bayar sewa RM4,000." L10 "Banyak kedai beratur panjang pun sama." L11
  "Korang sanggup bayar 50 sen lebih?"

### Check
- Is there no claim beyond what the ledger supports?
- Does the end zoom out one level?
- Is the end a ≤ 7-word question?

## NOTES
- Arithmetic of the example: 300 plates × RM0.30 × ~26 days ≈ RM2,340 < RM4,000 rent.
