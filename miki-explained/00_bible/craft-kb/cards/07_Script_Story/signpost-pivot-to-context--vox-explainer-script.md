## CREATIVE_INTENT
After the cold open, Vox "zooms out" from the specific hook to the broader history, system or mechanism behind it,
using clear signposts — "To understand how, you have to go back to…" or "But that's not the whole story" — and tells
the viewer what puzzle the video will unpack next.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before you write L03, name the system behind the hook (pricing, fees, a rule, a supply chain).
2. Open the turn line with one signpost phrase.
3. State the puzzle the next lines will solve, in ≤ 10 words.
4. Use one signpost per video; don't stack them.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Signpost bank (casual BM): "Nak faham, kena tengok dulu …" · "Tapi tu bukan cerita penuh." · "Rahsia dia kat …".
How-to: "Kalau korang nak …, ni caranya. N step je." Shot list: the signpost line is the camera's scale travel (object
→ store → system), one of RECIPE-D's required camera moves.

### Worked example
Hook: "Bungkus besar, bungkus kecil. Dua-dua RM3."
- BEFORE: L02 "Jadi, harga nasi lemak ni sebenarnya macam ni…"
- AFTER: L02 "Nak faham, kena tengok dulu dapur makcik pukul 5 pagi." L03 puzzle: "Apa yang dia kira, korang tak
  nampak?" Camera pulls out of the bungkus, through the stall, into the kitchen.

### Check
- Does the turn line start with one clear signpost?
- Is the next puzzle named in ≤ 10 words?
- Is there only one signpost in the script?
