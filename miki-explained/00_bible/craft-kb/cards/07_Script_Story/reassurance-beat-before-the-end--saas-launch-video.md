## CREATIVE_INTENT
"This part of the script is sort of reassurance building up to a call to action." After the demo, the script lists
results in a three-beat rhythm — "Faster reviews, fewer errors, happier customers" — before the close.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before the end line, write one reassurance line of three short results (2–3 words each).
2. Make one result an RM number.
3. Follow it with the end question (≤ 7 words), not a request.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
L09 or L10. Shot list: the three results as three quick state changes of the hero (~1 s each), then the loop back to
frame 0's object for the question.

### Worked example
- BEFORE: "Jadi guna cara ni, follow untuk tips lagi!"
- AFTER: "Kurang salah kira. Kurang stok hilang. Jimat RM300 sebulan." → end: "Korang still kira guna kepala?"
  (kedai runcit, illustrative)

### Check
- Are there exactly three short results, one with RM?
- Does the video end on a question, with no follow/sign-up line?
