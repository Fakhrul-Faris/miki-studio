## Extra steps / variants
- Variant "motif across time": when the next setup is later in time (next day, next month), carry the scene's motif
  object or its glow into the same screen position of the new setup, so the jump reads as continuity of the idea
  (e.g. a stall's lamp glow at night → the same glow position as the morning sun on the shutter, "esok pagi").
- Choose a motif that already means something in the line (fire = passion/danger in the source); no generic flash.
