## Extra step (not in the master's STE_PROCEDURE)
- Keyframe Focus Distance/Object at the shot's start on Subject A (in focus), add more keys holding A in focus while
  the camera moves, then at the point the story's attention shifts, swap Focus Object to Subject B and key again —
  the pull is a *reassignment* of which object is tracked, not just a distance slide. Key the F-Stop at the same frame
  if the depth characteristics should change too (e.g. tightening to isolate B).
