## Extra steps
- Before placing any rim/key light, check its angle relative to the camera axis; if it sits close to the camera's own
  view direction and is acting as the KEY (not a fill), move it — this is the same failure mode the master already
  warns about ("light from the camera's direction throws every shadow behind the objects and flattens form"), now
  with the explicit "amateurish / onboard flash" framing to cite when rejecting a front-lit draft.
- Front light is acceptable only as a FILL (secondary, not the key) or as a stated, deliberate retro-flash style —
  never as an unexamined default key placement.
