## New values/variant this source adds
- **Subtractive variant for shadow-side control.** The master card carves form with an additive side spot. This
  source instead uses a single key light (softbox + grid, camera right) plus a polyboard flag on the opposite side
  (camera left) with no added light there at all — the flag alone creates the shadow side. Add as a noted variant:
  "shadow-side control can also be a flag/polyboard blocking ambient bounce, not only an added side spot."
- **Workflow discipline: max three lights, one variable at a time.** "I tried to keep them all really simple. Max
  three lights... having setups that you can really easily adapt to, make small changes that have big effects." Add
  as a STE_PROCEDURE note: cap a rig at ≤3 lights; iterate by changing one light's colour/position/output at a time
  to generate variations from the same rig, rather than rebuilding.
