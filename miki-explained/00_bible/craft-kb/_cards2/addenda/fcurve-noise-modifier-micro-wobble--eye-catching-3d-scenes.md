## New value / variant
AE expression equivalent of the same "subtle organic wobble" technique, applied to a stationary two-node camera
rather than an object's rotation: hold Alt, click the position stopwatch, type `wiggle(1,8)` — frequency 1/second,
amplitude 8 (AE position units/pixels) — "especially... when the camera's stationary," for a little life/shake.

## NOTES

  otherwise fully static — never combined with an eased move.
