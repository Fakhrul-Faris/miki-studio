The target card covers how much space and where it sits (alone/crushed/heading/cornered). This source adds a
distinct technique: clear open space between two elements reads as unobstructed communication, and inserting a third
element into that space reads as a block or interruption — without moving either original element.

## Extra step for the target's STE_PROCEDURE
- When a line is about two things that relate clearly (a cost and its total, a before and after), leave the space
  between them clean.
- When the line introduces a complication or obstruction, add one element into that exact gap rather than
  restaging the frame — the interruption reads from the gap closing, not from anything else changing.
