## Extra steps
- Before placing a two-item comparison (two prices, two plans), use an asymmetric layout so the eye compares sides.
- For a calm or "done" beat, return to a symmetric layout.
- Tips are "tools to tell the story you want to tell", not hard rules (05:09).
