## Extra variant
- **Depth checkerboard** (the set-1 card is vertical: top vs bottom). Alternate lit and dark bands receding from
  the camera so each layer separates from the next; the hero sits in one lit band.

## Extra steps
1. Lay out 3–5 depth bands; light the odd ones, leave the even ones dark.
2. Put the hero in a lit band with dark bands in front of and behind it.
3. Use a long lens so the bands stack.
