## Extra variant
- **Move the camera instead of the light**: when a setup reads flat, keep the key where it is and orbit the camera
  to the key's shadow side. Fits Style D's camera-led changes between setups.

## Extra steps
1. Note which side of the object the key lights.
2. Place the camera on the opposite side so the visible face is partly in shadow.
3. Compare a still from each side; keep the shadow-side one unless the beat needs plain readability.

## Notes
- The readable side still wins when the line needs a label or number read (break-physics-for-clarity card).
