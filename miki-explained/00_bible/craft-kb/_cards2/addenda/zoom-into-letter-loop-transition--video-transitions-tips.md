## Extra variant
- **Pull out to reveal**: the reverse of the letter dive — start tight on one detail, pull back so the next line's
  information enters the frame (e.g. from one RM price tag back to the whole menu board). Ease in-out; the reveal lands on
  the word.

## Extra steps
1. Before choosing push vs pull, write the join's job: "go inside / closer" = push; "show the bigger picture / new info" = pull.
2. Move the camera in the direction the new content lies; never against it.
