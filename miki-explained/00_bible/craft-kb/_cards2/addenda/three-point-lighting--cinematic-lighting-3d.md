## Extra steps (build order shown in the demo)
1. Turn on the back light alone and check the separation it gives.
2. Add the fill and check the shadow softness.
3. Add the key last; then move the camera to the key's shadow side (see short-vs-broad addendum).
4. Add the motivating source and a background last (see motivated-lighting cards).
