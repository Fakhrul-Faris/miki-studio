The 3D camera has many options available
to it. Zoom, like in a real camera,

away.
Depth of field brings the image pixels
in and out of focus.
Enable depth of field by switching it on
under the options drop-down or by

Focus distance is the distance from the

Aperture helps to bring more or less

Blur level is the amount of blur in the
