## CREATIVE_INTENT
Before any script line is written, two decisions have to be made: what promise is the video making, and who is it
for.
- Johnny Harris Reveals: "the marketing — why should you click — is defined early on," even while the story itself
  is still open. The packaging is a straight line, a billboard — one clear promise the video is guaranteed to
  answer. The story can "fold in the vegetables" (wider ideas) around it, but the promise must be reinforced in the
  first minute and satisfyingly answered by the end, or viewers "feel betrayed."
- Motion Design How It's Really Made: the studio's first step is a discovery call to find out *why* the video is
  needed (educate? one specific how-to? change a belief? sell?) and *who* watches it — their warmth toward the
  subject (cold/warm/hot) — because everything after (concept, script, style) follows from those two answers.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before line 1, write the purpose in one line: educate / show one how-to / change one belief / sell.
2. Write the viewer's warmth: cold (never heard of us — our default, FYP), warm (follows), hot (ready to act); if cold, assume zero context: the hook must work with no brand knowledge and the payoff must be useful on its own.
3. Write 2–3 different packagings (title + image idea) for the topic; test each: is it one straight line a stranger understands in 1 s?
4. Keep the packaging whose promise you can answer with a verified fact; write the promise as one question the video will answer, and name the one action or belief the viewer should leave with (≤ 12 words).
5. Reinforce that promise inside the first ~1/6 of the runtime (Harris: first minute of ~20; ours: by L03, ~10 s) — do not answer it in the first 10 s; answer it fully by the end.
6. Before locking, check every line still serves the purpose and the promise; cut lines that serve only the "vegetables" or a different purpose.
7. If the package and the payoff differ, rewrite the package — never stretch the payoff.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Add three lines to the top of `episode.md` under RECIPE-D §0.1: `Purpose:`, `Viewer warmth:` (default `cold — FYP`),
`Leaves with:`. Then: Frame-0 text (≤ 8 words, contradiction) = the packaging — write it first, log the 2–3
alternatives in `episode.md`. L01–L03 = reinforce (restate the contradiction in plain words; S16: "Kalau korang nak
…, ni caranya. N step je."). Mechanism/steps = the vegetables (the wider "how it works"). L09–L10 = the satisfying
answer, with the RM number. L11 = end question taking a side on that answer (not "follow"), testing the "Leaves
with" line. Cover text = the same promise as frame 0; never a different hook.

### Worked example
Topic: a kedai runcit in Shah Alam selling RM1 kuih from a supplier, and checking e-wallet transaction history
(numbers illustrative; plain arithmetic).
- BEFORE: `Purpose: explain e-wallets. Viewer: everyone.` Script: frame 0 "Tips untuk kedai runcit" → L01 "Hari ni
  kita cerita pasal kuih." (no promise; nothing to pay off.)
- AFTER (S16 how-to): `Purpose: one how-to — check your e-wallet transaction history every night. Viewer warmth:
  cold, 18–34, scrolling FYP, has an e-wallet. Leaves with: "RM8 a day unnoticed is RM240 a month" (8 × 30). End
  question: "Korang rasa sehari habis berapa?"` Kuih example: packagings tried — (a) "Kuih RM1. Untung berapa sen?"
  (b) "Habis 100 biji. Untung RM10?" (c) "Kenapa kedai jual kuih juga?" Pick (b) (answerable by arithmetic). Frame
  0: "Habis 100 biji. Untung RM10 je?" L02 reinforce: "Kuih tu RM1, tapi kedai beli dari pembuat 90 sen sebiji."
  L09 pay off: "100 biji × 10 sen = RM10 sehari… tapi kuih tu bawa pelanggan beli air RM2." L11: "Korang rasa
  berbaloi ke jual kuih untung 10 sen?"

### Check
- Is the purpose one of educate / how-to / belief / sell, in one line, and is viewer warmth stated (cold by default)?
- Were 2–3 packagings written before line 1, and is the chosen one logged?
- Is the promise restated in plain words by L03, and answered with a concrete (RM) fact by L10, not in the first 10 s?
- Does every line serve that purpose, and does the end question test the "Leaves with" line (never ask for a follow) while the cover text promises exactly what frame 0 promises?
