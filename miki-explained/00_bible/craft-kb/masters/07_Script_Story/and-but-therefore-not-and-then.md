## CREATIVE_INTENT
Two complementary forms of the same rule: an episode's shape should compress to one sentence with real causation,
and every join between its beats should be checkable word by word for that same causation.
- ABT science writing adds the whole-episode form: distil the episode into one sentence — "X and Y, but Z, therefore
  W." **And** states facts the viewer already accepts (their normal day); **But** introduces one tension, conflict
  or problem that blocks that normal day; **Therefore** gives the solution and ties every strand opened in And/But
  back together; the ending returns to the normal day, now with the new insight (our loop to the frame-0 object).
- Stone & Parker (NYU) adds the line-by-line check: write the literal joining word between every pair of beats. If
  the word that belongs there is "and then", the beat is boring — rewrite it so the previous beat causes it
  ("therefore") or complicates it ("but"), or cut it if neither works. South Park's writers room does this
  literally, word by word, on the whiteboard.

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Before writing any line, write the whole episode as one ABT sentence: "X and Y, but Z, therefore W."
2. Fill the **And** part with facts the viewer already accepts (their normal day). Keep it to ~1–2 lines.
3. Introduce the **But** part: exactly one tension, conflict or problem, blocking the normal day so the viewer must follow the journey (steps/mechanism).
4. Write the **Therefore** part as the solution that answers the But directly; check it ties every strand opened in And and But — cut any strand it does not close.
5. Show the "return": the normal day again, now with the new insight (loop to the frame-0 object/framing).
6. List the script lines (or beats) one per row; between each pair, write the joining word literally — "but", "therefore" or "and then".
7. Mark every "and then" join as a failure; rewrite the beat so it is caused by the previous beat (therefore) or complicates it (but); cut the beat if neither rewrite works.
8. Check the joins alternate somewhat — roughly one "but" for every 1–2 "therefore" (inferred, no source ratio) — and re-read the whole chain end to end as cause and complication.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Map onto the script formula and S16 how-to:
- **And** = L01 (hook context the viewer recognises) — still needs the frame-0 contradiction, so the And line already carries the seed of the But.
- **But** = L01–L02 (open loop: the problem). S16: "Kalau korang nak …, ni caranya" sits at the But→Therefore hinge.
- **Therefore** = L03–L09 (mechanism / steps / RM proof). The "journey" is the steps.
- **Return with insight** = L10–L11, ending on the end question and the loop back to the frame-0 object.
Write the ABT sentence at the top of the script draft in `episode.md` as a comment so the critic can test it. While
drafting, add a join column to the script table (removed before the user sees the final):
`| L | Caption | join to next |`. For S16 how-tos, steps naturally read "and then" (step 1, step 2…); fix by making
each step the answer to a problem the previous step leaves ("screenshot dulu → **but** kedai boleh tukar harga asal
→ **therefore** compare bila sale start").

### Worked example
Topic: a kedai runcit in Shah Alam choosing between cash and QR, told as a laundry shop owner's pricing-per-kg steps
(illustrative numbers).
- BEFORE (and-then, whole episode): "Kedai runcit terima cash. Ada juga QR. QR ada caj. Ada juga kad. Ni beza dia."
- BEFORE (and-then, steps): L04 "Step satu, timbang baju." → and then → L05 "Step dua, kira harga." → and then →
  L06 "Step tiga, bagi resit."
- AFTER (ABT sentence): "Pakcik kedai runcit terima cash setiap hari **and** rasa duit tu selamat dalam laci, **but**
  setiap bulan RM300 hilang sebab salah kira dan duit palsu, **therefore** dia buka QR — duit masuk akaun, app kira sendiri."
  - L01 (And): "Kedai ni terima cash je. Pakcik kata paling selamat."
  - L02 (But): "Tapi bulan lepas, RM300 hilang. Tak tahu ke mana."
  - L03–L09 (Therefore): the QR steps and the RM0 fee.
  - L11 (return): "Laci cash ke QR — korang pilih mana?"
- AFTER (join check, steps): L04 "Step satu, timbang baju depan pelanggan." → **but** → L05 "Tapi timbang je tak
  cukup, pelanggan still rasa mahal." → **therefore** → L06 "Jadi, tulis RM4 sekilo besar-besar kat kaunter." →
  **but** → L07 "Tapi baju tebal selimut? Lagi berat, lagi untung RM12."

### Check
- Can the whole script be read as one "And …, but …, therefore …" sentence?
- Is there exactly one main But (one problem) at the episode level?
- Does the Therefore answer that But directly, not a different question, and does every strand opened before it close by L11?
- Is every join between lines literally "but" or "therefore" (tapi / jadi / sebab tu), never "and then"?
- Do a how-to's steps each solve a problem raised by the line before, with at least one "but" after the mechanism starts?
