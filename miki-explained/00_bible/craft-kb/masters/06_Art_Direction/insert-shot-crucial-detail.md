## CREATIVE_INTENT
An insert shot or ECU looks like it is just showing a detail, but its real job is a decision about attention: framing
only that one detail tells the audience "this is what matters right now, everything else disappears" — "the most
intimate, dramatic and potentially startling of all shot sizes."
- Shot Sizes Explained adds the framing/timing discipline: name the single detail the line depends on, hold it only
  ~1–2 s landing on the word that names it, and ration inserts (≤ 2 per episode) so they stay startling.
- Framing Every Shot Wrong adds the narrative mechanism: the cut also plants a question the audience didn't choose
  to ask ("why are we seeing this?") and carries it forward until a later beat answers it — Hitchcock's "you don't
  have to show the explosion, you just have to show the bomb."

## STE_PROCEDURE
1. Name the single detail the line depends on, and the question it should plant in the viewer's mind before cutting to it.
2. Frame only that detail; nothing else readable — crop tight enough that nothing competes for attention.
3. Keep it short (~1–2 s) and land it on the word that names it.
4. Do not explain it immediately — let the planted question sit for at least one more beat.
5. Pay off the question later in the episode (the mechanism/payoff beat), not in the same setup.
6. Use at most 2 inserts per episode so they stay startling.

## AGENT_EXECUTION
Frame brief: Eye = the detail; Decision = "ECU, detail 70% of frame (insert exception)". Shot-list row: mark the
setup "insert — plants: <question>" in the events column; the later row that answers it gets "pays off L0X's
insert." raea: macro lens, very shallow DoF, top light raking across the detail; isolate the object with DoF and a
tight crop, hold slightly longer than its line needs before cutting away.

### Worked example
Topic: "Tarikh luput vs 'best before'" (generic labels; no regulation claims), and a small clinic's consultation fee
going up by RM5 (illustrative numbers).
- BEFORE: `| 5 | "Tengok kat tin" | MS of a tin on a shelf | — |`; fee simply stated by the voice over a wide shot of the counter.
- AFTER: `| 5 | "Tengok **tarikh** kat bawah tin" | ECU · embossed date on the tin's base, raking light | date edge lit accent on "tarikh", 1.5 s | insert-ecu |`. Fee example: L02 — insert, tight on the printed fee card with the old
  number still showing, held a beat longer than the line ("plants: why show us this card now?"); L07 — the same
  card's number updates to the new fee in the accent ("pays off L02's insert").

### Check
- Is only one detail readable, and does the shot-list name the question it plants and which later row pays it off?
- Does it land on the word that names it, ≤ 2 s, framed tight enough that nothing else competes for attention?
- Are there ≤ 2 inserts in the episode, and does the payoff happen later, not in the same setup as the plant?
