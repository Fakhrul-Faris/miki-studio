# STUDY-D — the three reference videos, frame by frame (2026-11-12)

Why this exists: the user rejected EX38 preview v1 ("only move, pan and zoom … 1 setup … subtitle not animated … lazy work …
learn from the reference videos frame by frame, take note everything"). I had analysed the references from 1 frame/second
sheets and a cut detector, and concluded "one continuous camera move, almost no cuts". That was wrong in the way that mattered:
there are almost no *hard cuts*, but there is a **new setup every 2–3 seconds**, joined by camera flights and object morphs.

Method this time: video B (TV boxes, the main target) at 5 frames/s for all 83 s (416 frames); its caption area at 30 frames/s
for 0–1.4 s and 1.85–3.3 s; videos C and A at 3 frames/s for their full length (272 and 169 frames); full transcripts.
Files: `miki-explained/Video from Facebook (1).mp4` = B, `(2).mp4` = C, `Video from Facebook.mp4` = A.

Every setup also has 1–4 **events** inside it (a label lands, a number counts, a part lights, an object opens), so something
changes on screen about every 0.5–1 s, always on the spoken word.

## 3. Camera vocabulary (all three)

Used constantly: push-in · pull-out into a void · **rack focus** between two depths · tilt · **orbit to top-down** · crane up/down ·
eye-level frontal · low angle along a surface · macro with readable print · fly from one place to the next.
C adds: Dutch tilts (horizon 15–40° off), skimming a surface a few centimetres up, whip moves with motion blur, spotlight
pools that travel across paper. A adds: 3D flattening into a 2D diagram, then UI windows that type, scroll and stack.
No setup is held still: even a "hold" drifts or pushes slowly.
What v1 used: push, pan, pull, one whip. Missing: rack focus as a story beat, orbit to top-down, eye-level frontal, macro,
low angle, scale travel.

## 4. Composition

- Picture band: subject centred between about 25 % and 60 % of the frame height; the bottom ~28 % fades to near-black
  (space for the app's own captions); the top ~12 % is dark behind the subtitle.
- One subject per setup, large; neighbours blurred or dim. In voids the ground is a soft dark gradient, not pure black.
- B's colour: near-black + neutral greys + **one green**; warm wood appears only in the "your home" setups. C: monochrome
  warm grey + one magenta late. A: indigo/gold, then dark teal UI.
- Labels: spaced capitals (small), a bigger value under them, a thin leader line ending in a dot on the object, placed on the
  dark side of the frame. Tiny grey disclaimers bottom-left ("imagined scenario", "simplified test…").
- Counters are big numerals top-left with a small unit under them, and they always count up, never just appear.

## 5. Object behaviour (why it never feels like one setup)

Objects **change state**: go x-ray, open, lose their lid, slice into layers, multiply (with a counter), shrink into a
diagram header, turn into chart points, get covered by padlocks/boxes. A new setup is often the same object in a new state
from a new angle, not a new place.

## 6. Subtitle animation, measured at 30 frames/s (B)

- Position: centred, first line at ~9.5 % of the frame height, second line directly under; never more than 2 lines.
- Type: rounded geometric sans, about 46 px on a 720-wide frame (≈ 68 px at 1080). Current word **bold white**; every
  earlier word in the phrase drops to **light weight, grey**.
- Phrase = 2–5 words (a clause). Words are added one at a time; the line re-centres with a short ease (~3 frames) as it grows.
- **Word in (2 frames ≈ 66 ms):** frame 1 the word is blurred, grey and a few px low; frame 2 sharp, white, in place.
- **Key-word box (draws on over ~4 frames, starting 1 frame after the word):** first the two corner dots (top-left,
  bottom-right); then the top and bottom edges draw from left to right; then the side edges close and a dark-blue fill
  fades in. About one box per phrase at most, only on the word that carries the fact (a name, a number, the verb).
- **Phrase out (≈ 3 frames):** the whole phrase dims in one frame and is gone; 2–3 empty frames; the next phrase begins.
- v1 had: words switching on with no motion, a static box, no phrase exit.

## 7. Sound and voice

Voice ~3.5 words/s, almost no pauses over 0.4 s, a pause of ~0.5 s only before a turn ("Now imagine…", "But…").
(I have not analysed the sound design: I can't hear it. The user's ear decides that.)

## 8. What this means for a build

1. Plan **setups, not a camera path**: at 3.5 words/s a 58 s film needs about 24–28 setups, each with its events on the words.
2. Every script line needs 2–4 setups; no setup longer than ~4 s unless it keeps building (the chart).
3. Model for state changes: the hero must open, slice, multiply; props need readable macro detail.
4. Use the whole vocabulary: at least rack focus, orbit to top-down, eye-level frontal, macro, low angle and one scale travel.
5. Captions as in § 6, as a component.
6. Check before showing: count setups and events per line against this file.
