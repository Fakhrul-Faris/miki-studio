### A-cam-044 · ★4 · worm's eye — "Untitled Image" (Drooker, businessman among towers)
Idea: a businessman striding over the camera as the city's towers converge above him.
Decision: painted; camera at pavement level under his stride; towers lean to a cross-shaped sky gap; a single bird in the gap; he holds a briefcase, tie red, warm ochre towers vs blue sky.
Eye: 1 the man's face → 2 the sky cross → 3 the bird → 4 the towers.
Feeling: ambition, a man owning the city (or being swallowed by it).
Not generic: the towers and the man share the same perspective lines, so he becomes a tower himself.
Steal: from below, align the figure with the buildings' vanishing lines so he reads as part of the city's power.
Moves: a held striding pose; the camera turns slowly under him.

### A-cam-045 · ★4 · worm's eye — "YR3 Cinematography Pin" (boy with hose, from the grass)
Idea: a small boy becomes a giant from a bug's point of view.

Moves: the grass sways at the lens; he holds the pose.

## C07 digest
**★5 (4):** people-014 (the grey clay street is a look in itself: form and light, no colour) · people-033 (the clay street turned into the coloured street with the camera locked, brand as a shopfront) · places-012 (a Japanese shop street at golden hour, every sign and wire true, tilt-shift) · props-003 (Braun pedal: warm-white matte, one orange button where your foot goes).
Strong ★4s: people-012 (linen figure with crossed arms), places-006 (empty chairs tell "waiting").
**Convictions forming:**
- **Clay first, colour on the beat** (locked camera): people-014 → 033. A ready-made transition for our clay house look.
- **Faceless people that work**: smooth head + real fabric (people-001, 006, 012); identity by silhouette props (cap, beard, hard hat: people-003, 030); shot from behind or 3/4 back (people-008).
- **True local clutter makes a toy street real**: places-012, C06 cam-010, C03 To Binh Binh.
- **Props: matte, warm white, one accent on the part you touch**: props-003, 002; unseen angles (props-005).
- **Empty furniture tells the beat without figures**: places-006.
- **Brand inside the world** (shopfront, wall band, building sign): people-033, places-003, 011.
**Tensions:** the Sony film is full character acting (faces, dance) — we can't use the acting, only its light (golden low sun, raking shadows) and its clay → colour street. Most iso street/room refs are AI-generic with colour everywhere; they show the format, not the taste.
**Couldn't read:** none.
