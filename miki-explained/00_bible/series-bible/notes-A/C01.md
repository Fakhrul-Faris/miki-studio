# C01 — Style A notes (A-core-001 … A-core-058)

Series: **Bizone** = BIZONE technology frames (fire-suppression product film, 001–024).

### A-core-001 · ★5 · Bizone — "BIZONE technology-10"
Idea: the one tank that is in trouble, shown as the only one you can see inside.
Decision: x-ray only the problem. The burning tank is glass; its two neighbours stay opaque black steel. The fire is the only warm colour in a cold world.
Eye: 1 fire → 2 red alarm ring above it → 3 "02"54" timer bottom left (and the artist kept everything else dark and blue so orange wins).
Feeling: urgency with control: a crisis already being watched.
Not generic: the average version cuts every tank open or shows a flat diagram; this keeps the real object and opens only the one that matters.
Steal: reveal the inside of exactly one object, the one the line is about; everything else stays solid and dark.
Moves: slow push-in; the tank wall fades to glass on the word; the timer counts; the alarm ring pulses once.

### A-core-002 · ★3 · Bizone — "BIZONE technology-11"
Idea: the whole territory before the story starts, a title over a place.
Decision: the plant is tiny on a dark horizon with a huge empty sky; the title floats in that sky like a hologram over the site.
Eye: 1 title → 2 the plant's silhouette on the horizon → 3 wet ground reflections.
Feeling: calm, serious, a chapter opening.
Not generic: a stock title card would be text on black; this anchors the words over a real place, at real scale.
Steal: a chapter title sits in the empty space above the world it introduces, not on a separate card.
Series: the wide establishing beat that the close-ups (001, 003) zoom into.
Moves: drone drift toward the plant; the title draws in line by line, then the camera passes under it.

Series: **Davines** = character and prop modelling boards (025–029), sculpted figures with hand-made surfaces.

### A-core-025 · ★5 · Davines — "1-Davines" (Spirits character modelling, process board)
Idea: people as carved household objects: a body that is also a vessel, a head that carries a bowl.
Decision: the figures are not humans scaled down; they are pottery. Tiny faces (two dots), huge simple bodies, a carved line pattern over the whole surface, one terracotta accent (the bowl). A hole through the body holds a small thing, like a niche in a wall.
Eye: 1 the terracotta bowl → 2 the tiny face under it → 3 the carved texture → 4 the hole with something inside.
Feeling: warm, quiet, folk-tale; you want to touch it.
Not generic: the average 3D character is a smooth toy with big eyes; this one is a sculpture with almost no face, and the body shape does the character work.
Steal: make figures as objects (pottery, wood), with faces almost absent; let the silhouette and the surface carry who they are.
Moves: held poses suit it; a slow turntable, the light sweeping across the carving, is all the motion it needs.

### A-core-026 · ★4 · Davines — "2-Davines"
Idea: the same figure dressed four ways, a wardrobe test.
Decision: wooden mannequin heads and hands with real cloth materials (tweed, knit, wool), tiny noses, no eyes, on a warm grey seamless.
Eye: 1 the jacket textures → 2 the faceless wooden heads → 3 the red boots on the last one.
Feeling: tailored, adult, calm.
Not generic: generic stylised people get bright plastic clothes; here the fabrics are real, and the faces stay carved wood. The mix of real textile and unreal person is the point.
Steal: real-world materials on simplified, faceless figures; the material makes them believable, the face stays out of the uncanny valley.
Series: moves from sculpture (025) to dressed people.
Moves: held pose; light passes across the fabric to show the weave.

### A-core-028 · ★4 · Davines — "4-Davines"

### A-core-029 · ★5 · Davines — "5-Davines"
Idea: the sculpted people and props inside their world, lit like a film.
