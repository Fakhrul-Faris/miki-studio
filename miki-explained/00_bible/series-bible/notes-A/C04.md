### A-core-071 · ★5 · synaos — "Factory processes-1"
Idea: a warehouse as a white model where only the people and machines that work are red.
Decision: everything structural is matte white (floor, walls, racks, conveyors, robots); cardboard boxes stay their real brown; workers, forklifts and robot carts are red. Three tones: white = the place, brown = the goods, red = the workers. Soft daylight, gentle contact shadows, high iso-ish camera.
Eye: 1 the logo top left (in the empty white) → 2 the red forklift → 3 the red workers scattered → 4 the brown box stacks.
Feeling: calm, organised, easy to follow; a factory you can understand.
Not generic: the average warehouse render uses every real colour and becomes noise; here colour is a job description.
Steal: colour by role: the place is white, the goods keep their natural colour, everything that acts is one accent.
Moves: high glide across the floor; red carts move along routes; workers hold poses at stations.
