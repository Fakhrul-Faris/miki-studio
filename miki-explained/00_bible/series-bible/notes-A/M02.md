# M02 — Style A motion notes (iso animation, Buck, transitions, title sequences, Oddfellows / Ordinary Folk films)

### A-motion-008 · ★5 · iso animation — "Video 7595d45c.mp4" (11.2 s, 0 cuts, loop)
Idea: a street block builds itself from flat tiles: buildings drop in from the sky and stack into place, then everything un-builds and repeats.
Decision: a single street strip on a flat sage-green void; flat footprint tiles first; then roofs, floors and shopfronts fly in tumbling from above and lock together; the red brick building is the hero; awnings and roof slabs arrive last.
Eye: always the piece in the air → where it lands.
Feeling: playful, satisfying, a toy assembling.
Not generic: build/unbuild as the whole animation; parts arrive rotated and settle with a clean ease (no wobble), so it feels physical but tidy.
Camera: locked iso.
Handover: build → hold → unbuild to flat tiles → rebuild (loop) — usable as a scene's entrance and exit.
Timing: snappy drops (~0.3–0.5 s each) in a quick stagger; a ~1 s hold when complete; the unbuild is faster than the build.
Entrances/exits: pieces fall from above with a tumble and settle; exit by flattening back to footprint tiles.
Steal for Style A: introduce the business's building by dropping its parts in from above on the beat (footprint → walls → roof → sign last); end the scene by unbuilding to tiles. Our "shrink/fade in" rule is respected because nothing pops — everything travels.

### A-motion-025 · ★5 · Oddfellows — "Google – Password Manager" (118 s, 9 cuts + extra strip 16–40 s)
Idea: passwords are coloured marbles; the product is the machine that sorts, stores and fills them.
Decision: an all-white, soft 3D world (matte white, frosted glass, thin line rings); the "password" is a row of five marbles in real materials (wood, stone, metal); they roll on tracks, drop into a glass bowl that fills pink (danger/leak), sit on a white plinth, slot into the login field's pill, pass through a ring and turn into the brand colours (yellow, blue, red, green); the product is a white cabinet full of coloured marbles; the end shows four Google-colour marbles in a row, then the URL.
Eye: always the marbles — the one moving thing in a white world.
Feeling: calm, clever, trustworthy, satisfying (like a marble run).
Not generic: an abstract service (passwords) made physical as one object type that travels through every scene; the white world keeps the marbles the only focus; colour change at the ring = "secured by Google".
Camera: locked or slow, mostly front-on; a few top-downs (the tray at ~32 s).
Handover: object-led — the marbles carry you from scene to scene (roll out of one set into the next); match on the row of five (plinth → login pill); pass-through-a-ring for transformation.
Timing: calm, physical: balls roll with real weight; 3–8 s holds; the danger beat (pink bowl) is the one tense moment.
Entrances/exits: marbles roll in/out of frame, drop, slot; UI panels slide in as solid white objects.
Steal for Style A: choose one physical token for the episode's invisible thing (money = coins, customers = marbles, orders = boxes) and let it travel through every scene of a white clay world; pass it through a ring/door to change its colour when the business changes it; a glass bowl filling red for the danger beat. This is the closest motion reference to our "white world + one accent" bible.

## M02 digest
**★5 (3):** motion-008 (a street that builds itself from tiles, parts dropping in and settling, then unbuilding) · motion-015 (a case laid out as objects on a dark floor, the camera gliding along white tape lines, ending on the full connected map) · motion-025 (passwords as marbles travelling through an all-white world; the object carries every handover).
**Motion convictions:**
- **One physical token travels through the whole episode** and carries the handovers: motion-025 (marbles), 028 (sphere), C08 light-042 (orange ball). The strongest motion idea in Style A.
- **Build / unbuild as entrance and exit**: motion-008 (parts drop in, then flatten), C04 synaos, model → real (motion-009).
- **Line-led camera**: the camera follows a line on the floor (tape, track, route) from subject to subject, then pulls up to the whole map (motion-015, M01 motion-003).
- **Graphic match handovers**: shape → shape across scenes (motion-016, 027 recurring shape), fly-through nested frames (013, 020).
- **Colour key shifts with the arc**: cool → warm (motion-019), bookend day → dusk (024).
- **Calm physical timing**: things roll, drop and settle with weight; no bounce; holds of 3–8 s (025, 008, 015). Fast-cut title sequences (012, 021, 144 cuts) are the opposite pace — not for us.
**Tensions:** most of M02 is 2D character animation (Oddfellows, Ordinary Folk, Buck) — its handover devices transfer, its acting does not. Several pieces are retired-adjacent (paper looks: 014, 016) — techniques only.
