Series: **To Binh Binh** = Hong Kong shop dioramas, stylised 3D (122–130).

### A-core-122 · ★4 · To Binh Binh — "1-To Binh Binh"
Idea: the owner's life on the shop roof, above the sign that says whose shop it is.
Decision: a cropped corner of a tiny building on a flat lavender void; the big red-and-yellow shop sign anchors the bottom right, and above it the owner reads his newspaper among laundry, a lemon tree and an aircon unit.
Eye: 1 the red sign → 2 the man with his paper → 3 the hanging yellow hoodie.
Feeling: warm, nosy, everyday; you know this man.
Not generic: the average shop render shows the storefront; this shows the owner's private rooftop, crowded with real clutter (aircon, antenna, laundry), which is what makes it Hong Kong.
Steal: the business is the person: show the owner's small private life right above the sign.
Moves: crane up from the sign to the rooftop; laundry sways; the owner holds his reading pose.

Decision: a 2D poster (two men, a jar, flowers) is mapped flat onto the red wall; real 3D clutter (stacked plastic baskets and stools) sits in front of it, sharp, while the corner falls out of focus.
Eye: 1 the poster's two faces → 2 the jar → 3 the plastic baskets.
Feeling: nostalgic, local, proud.
Not generic: a flat, culturally specific graphic on a 3D wall gives the toy world a real place and era.
Steal: put a real local graphic (poster, signage) flat on a 3D wall; it gives a toy world its culture.
Series: the side wall of 122's shop.
Moves: slow slide along the wall, baskets passing in the foreground.
