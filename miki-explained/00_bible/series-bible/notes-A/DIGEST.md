## C01 digest
**★5 (5):** 001 (x-ray only the problem tank, fire as the only warm colour) · 017 (problem and solution on one line, the small truck wins through the foam) · 025 (people as pottery: silhouette and surface instead of faces) · 029 (sculpted figures cropped like a live-action film, a hand on a wall acts) · 054 (a whole industry as object tiles on white with gaps as arrows)
**Convictions forming:**
- **Open only what the line is about** (x-ray, cutaway, glass box): 001, 003, 019, 031, 046.
- **Locked camera, change one thing** for before/after or suspense: 001→005, 011→012, 020→017→021, 057→058.
- **The problem owns the light; the hero stays small and cool**: 006, 017, 023.
- **Paint meaning onto the object's surface** instead of arrows: 011, 007, 050.
- **Systems as tiles of real objects on white**: 039, 041, 054.
- **Faceless figures made of real materials** (pottery, wood, wool): 025–029.
- **Isolate the story character with empty space**, even when small: 017, 056, 057.
**Tensions:** Bizone is dark, wet, photographic and HUD-heavy; Dzimitry is a white, shadowless toy board; Davines/Gary are warm, handmade, filmic. Night-tech drama vs daylight craft is the big split so far.
**Couldn't read:** 042, 043, 048, 051, 052 look identical (or nearly) to earlier plates at 768 px; noted as repeats.

## C02 digest
**★5 (6):** 067 (a phone lid over a mall: a metaphor made physical by scale, no colour) · 070 (sunlight stripes on a loud carpet are the composition) · 078 (one red house in a white city) · 080 (white foam-model world, the subject dipped in terracotta) · 087 (a slice pulled out of a house and set beside it, joined by dotted lines) · 089 (the subject in real brick, the city in white model).
**Convictions forming:**
- **One subject in colour or real material; the world in white model** (strongest rule so far): 078, 080, 089, 082, 079, 084; echoes C01's 001.
- **Show the inside next to the whole**: 087, 076, 067 (and C01's cutaways).
- **Hard light as composition**: 070, 095 (edge-lit roofs), 117.
- **Pointers without colour**: line weight 088, solid vs outline 079, an odd shape 116, particle density 106, an out-of-place object 119.
- **People as texture and as owners** (colour people like the thing made for them): 090, 085, 068.
- **Hide to show feeling**: 066 (eyes in the shell), 120.
**Tensions:** Thomas's dark monochrome city has mood but most frames are coverage (many ★2) — the darkness needs one lit subject to land. Architecture diagrams (Tahahojohati) are line drawings, not 3D renders; their rules transfer, their technique doesn't. Gary is character animation we can't do; its staging (isolation, hiding, foreground props) transfers, the acting doesn't.
**Couldn't read:** none unreadable; 097/102/120 are too dark to judge fine detail at 768 px.

## C03 digest
**★5 (2):** 124 (a tiny tofu shop: 3–4 true details + the owner on her phone) · 129 (warm light inside the shop, cool street outside: the doorway is the invitation).
**Convictions forming:**
- **Small businesses as collectible toy buildings, full of true local details** (closest to our subject matter): 122–130.
- **The brand built into the set** (signs, awnings, posters), so the logo is there from frame 1: 130, 124.
- **A meaning colour that carries across scales and through the story** (pink = polluted from map to chimney; red → blue = damage → fixed): 162–163, 167–170.
- **Lock the place, swap the graphic layer** for the next sentence: 151/152, 155/159, 156/161.
- **Desaturate the world, one saturated line or ring for what matters**: 135, 151, 173.
- **Graphics on the ground plane** (rings, arrows, names), never floating: 141, 166, 173.
**Tensions:** To Binh Binh is warm, handmade and human; Vorontzov is cold cartography and HUD — useful grammar (colour codes, layering, zoom from map to object) but the dark-tech look is the genre default, so most frames sit at ★3–4. Several Vorontzov frames are "slides on a map" (140, 156) — avoid.
**Couldn't read:** 159–161 are GIFs judged on the first frame only; motion goes to M01/M02 if they are in those chunks.

## C04 digest
**★5 (5):** 071 (colour as a job description: white place, brown goods, red workers) · 073 (draw the route first, then a real fire truck uses it in the same frame) · light-001 (crop until the one lit feature rules; one tiny orange square) · light-009 (sound made physical as yellow ribbons, in the product's own colour, around a still centre) · light-018 (the inside of an everyday object as a ghostly x-ray).
**Convictions forming:**
- **Colour by role, not by reality**: 071, 184 (people green, danger red), light-001 (one accent square). Echoes C02's "one subject in colour".
- **Set up a graphic, pay it off with a real object in the same frame**: 073, 152, 170.
- **Blur the world, one sharp idea per beat**: 072, 074, 075, light-007, light-027.
- **The invisible made physical in the accent colour**: light-009/010/020 (sound), 181/186 (underground), 196 (plate edges as walls).
- **X-ray the everyday object**: light-018, 019, 030 (echoes C01's 001 and C02's 087).
- **Light as the animation** (sweep a shadow across a still object): light-029, light-024, light-016.
- **Timeline/data placed in the world**: 187 (arc over the site), 186 (depth ruler under the site).
**Tensions:** Nothing's white-studio product language (crop, macro, one accent, x-ray) vs Vorontzov's cartography; both are "Style A", but one is intimate, the other god's-eye. They meet in synaos (a white model world seen from above with one accent).
**Couldn't read:** light-017 first frame is near-black (needs motion); GIF ids (light-006/007/011/012/014–017/020/023/027/029, core 159–161) judged on first frame only.

## C05 digest
**★5 (3):** light-071 (the camera as a pure black silhouette; the reveal is a light switching on, see 072) · color-001 (colour follows emotion: pastels in control, saturation at the turn) · color-039 (one pink accent is the story's heart, growing inside a cold green world).
**Convictions forming:**
- **Reveal by light, not by camera**: light-071→072, 077 (raking light), C04 light-029.
- **Colour is planned across time, per beat, for emotion**: color-001, 003, 008, 040, 042. One dominant key per section; the twist gets its own block.
- **The accent colour carries meaning, and its area tracks the story**: color-039, 003 (hero's colour constant), C04 071.
- **Test palettes across the whole sequence on locked frames**: color-004, 041.
- **Palette with proportions and jobs**: color-012, 009.
- **One ordinary human prop gives a figure a role without acting**: light-079, 095 (name badge) — fits our "figures pose only".
- **Change of material along one object = transformation**: light-055, 062, 064 (wood → glass).
**Tensions:** Endress is lush iridescent fantasy (rich materials everywhere) — the opposite of the restraint the earlier ★5s reward; its storytelling (material change, seam between worlds) transfers, its surfaces don't. Kriket is a strong character sheet but mostly coverage (many ★3).
**Couldn't read:** color-007 is too dense at 768 px; colour boards were judged as boards (the decision is the colour plan, not a frame).

## C06 digest
**★5 (8):** color-025 (light splits a sofa: one woman in warm pink, one in cold blue; a thread joins them) · cam-001 (straight-down fields: one red roof and one white van are the story) · cam-005 (a tiny boat beside a giant whale under the surface: hidden scale) · cam-009 (trade as parallel lanes on one diagonal, clouds over it) · cam-010 (an ordinary street of small shops with true everyday clutter, soft dusk) · cam-024 (a grey city, one pink road, numbered magnifier circles for the details) · cam-026 (three-level surface code: gold subject, hatched neighbour, white rest) · cam-038 (looking up from under her chin to a warm sky strip between cold walls).
**Convictions forming:**
- **Neutrals + one bright colour** (stated outright in color-036, used in 018, 022, 017): the house rule most ★5s follow.
- **Light splits the frame into emotional zones**: color-025, 028, 022; the warm opening as the goal: cam-038, 041.
- **Wardrobe/subject in the complementary colour, world in one family**: color-019, 020, 027.
- **One series palette, each episode a subset**: color-021, 029.
- **Straight-down and impossible camera positions as hooks**: cam-001, 005, 043.
- **Wide + numbered close-ups in one frame** (magnifier bubbles, numbered stops): cam-024, 019.
- **Hierarchy by surface** (accent / hatched / white): cam-026, 022 (cool building, warm life), C02's 078/089.
- **Everyday proof in a toy street** (aircon, scooters, boxes): cam-010 (and C03's To Binh Binh).
**Tensions:** Several camera refs are AI-generated anime/stock (cam-013–015, 018, 027) — saturated everywhere, no hierarchy; they show camera angles only. Palette cards are utilities, not pictures; their value is rules (ratio, jobs, one accent).
**Couldn't read:** none.
