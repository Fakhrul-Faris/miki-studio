## C01 digest
**★5 (5):** 001 (x-ray only the problem tank, fire as the only warm colour) · 017 (problem and solution on one line, the small truck wins through the foam) · 025 (people as pottery: silhouette and surface instead of faces) · 029 (sculpted figures cropped like a live-action film, a hand on a wall acts) · 054 (a whole industry as object tiles on white with gaps as arrows)
**Convictions forming:**
- **Open only what the line is about** (x-ray, cutaway, glass box): 001, 003, 019, 031, 046.
- **Locked camera, change one thing** for before/after or suspense: 001→005, 011→012, 020→017→021, 057→058.
- **The problem owns the light; the hero stays small and cool**: 006, 017, 023.
- **Paint meaning onto the object's surface** instead of arrows: 011, 007, 050.
- **Systems as tiles of real objects on white**: 039, 041, 054.
- **Faceless figures made of real materials** (pottery, wood, wool): 025–029.
- **Isolate the story character with empty space**, even when small: 017, 056, 057.
**Tensions:** Bizone is dark, wet, photographic and HUD-heavy; Dzimitry is a white, shadowless toy board; Davines/Gary are warm, handmade, filmic. Night-tech drama vs daylight craft is the big split so far.
**Couldn't read:** 042, 043, 048, 051, 052 look identical (or nearly) to earlier plates at 768 px; noted as repeats.

## C02 digest
**★5 (6):** 067 (a phone lid over a mall: a metaphor made physical by scale, no colour) · 070 (sunlight stripes on a loud carpet are the composition) · 078 (one red house in a white city) · 080 (white foam-model world, the subject dipped in terracotta) · 087 (a slice pulled out of a house and set beside it, joined by dotted lines) · 089 (the subject in real brick, the city in white model).
**Convictions forming:**
- **One subject in colour or real material; the world in white model** (strongest rule so far): 078, 080, 089, 082, 079, 084; echoes C01's 001.
- **Show the inside next to the whole**: 087, 076, 067 (and C01's cutaways).
- **Hard light as composition**: 070, 095 (edge-lit roofs), 117.
- **Pointers without colour**: line weight 088, solid vs outline 079, an odd shape 116, particle density 106, an out-of-place object 119.
- **People as texture and as owners** (colour people like the thing made for them): 090, 085, 068.
- **Hide to show feeling**: 066 (eyes in the shell), 120.
**Tensions:** Thomas's dark monochrome city has mood but most frames are coverage (many ★2) — the darkness needs one lit subject to land. Architecture diagrams (Tahahojohati) are line drawings, not 3D renders; their rules transfer, their technique doesn't. Gary is character animation we can't do; its staging (isolation, hiding, foreground props) transfers, the acting doesn't.
**Couldn't read:** none unreadable; 097/102/120 are too dark to judge fine detail at 768 px.
