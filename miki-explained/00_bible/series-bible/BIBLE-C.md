# BIBLE-C — Style C: the lit plate

Built 2026-11-09 from one-by-one art-director notes on 431 handpicked images and 4 GIFs (`notes-C/C01–C08.md`,
`DIGEST.md`). Style C = illustrated plates the user generates (Seedream) and I bring alive (depth camera, light
breathing, overlays) — method in `00_bible/plates-playbook.md`. Convictions, not averages. **Draft, not yet tested.**

---

## 1. Point of view

**Style C believes the business is the light in the frame.** Each plate is a cinematic still where the world sits in
one cool hue and the only warmth comes from the story's source: the shop's sign, its doorway, a lamp, a phone, a stall's
bulbs. People are present but never need faces: they are silhouettes, back views and shadows, often tiny against the
place. One idea per plate, told by a single pun, a single shape of light, or one exception in a pattern.

## 2. Convictions

**C1. The business is the light source.** Signs, doorways, lamps, stall bulbs and screens are the only warm light; the
world stays one cool hue. Ids: light-083, 115, places-013, light-001, color-012. On screen: every plate names its light
source, and it belongs to the business.

**C2. Light is a shape that holds the subject.** A wedge, a pool, a window patch, a cone; the subject sits inside it or on
its edge. Ids: light-126, core-055, cam-062, light-063, light-105. On screen: prompt the light as a hard shape; animate it
(slide, widen, flicker) with overlays.

**C3. One idea per plate, as a single pun or hidden truth.** An everyday object recast (basket = UFO, screen = pool,
reflection = tower block, paperwork = wave). Ids: core-008, 051, 094, 122, 037. On screen: one metaphor per plate, only
when the line is a metaphor (house rule: visualise the script literally first).

**C4. One among many.** The subject as the single exception in a full-frame pattern: one lit window, one occupied seat,
one coloured figure in a crowd. Ids: cam-046, 025, color-007, color-011, people-022. On screen: the "this shop / this
customer" beat.

**C5. People without faces.** Back views, silhouettes, void faces, brim shadows, reflections, shadows; identity by one
item (tudung, songkok, chef's cap, hard hat). Ids: core-047, light-064, cam-035, people-003, 045, 052. On screen: plates
show people in held poses, faces hidden — never moved (house rule).

**C6. Shadows and reflections as a second character** (EX26 rule, confirmed). Ids: cam-066, people-048, cam-035,
core-073, core-113. On screen: the shadow shows ambition/threat; the reflection shows the hidden truth.

**C7. The local place told truthfully.** Red/white plastic stools, zinc tables, the teh tarik pour, roti, fluorescent
tubes, the shop's painted or neon sign, scooters, wires, aircon units, crescent moon. Ids: places-016, 033, 013, 001,
024, 027, light-082, light-064. On screen: Malaysia is the home key of C; collect the true detail list per episode.

**C8. Scale: tiny people, big place.** Ids: core-027, cam-021, light-069, people-005, core-036. On screen: the business
or its problem is big; the person small; negative space leaves room for captions.

## 3. The eye
- The warm light is the first stop; place the subject inside or at its edge.
- Frames within frames: doors, windows, mirrors, keyholes, stairwells (cam-005, 016, 018, 035).
- Paths lead to the goal: zig-zag paths, aisles, stairs (cam-021, 013, light-109).
- Foreground darks for depth (leaves, stacks, door edges) — the depth warp loves them (cam-003, light-045, light-064).
- 9:16 composition: subject low or mid, huge sky/wall/negative space for EX15 captions (cam-004, people-008).

## 4. Light as storytelling
- Night/dusk default for hooks and payoffs (warm source, cool world); daylight for heritage/morning (places-033).
- Two-tone light (one colour room, one colour light): light-018, 021, 027, 035, 126.
- Reflections on wet ground double the light (light-083, 001, color-012).
- Light connects two things (light-063 shaft; light-076 pool) and reveals (light-089 torch).
- Lock the plate, change the light = time passing (light-117).

## 5. Colour as meaning
- One cool hue for the world (teal, navy, plum, slate); one warm for the business (amber, orange, coral, red).
- Brands keep their true colours when they appear (color-007) — logo from frame 1.
- The accent from the brand and the story's meaning (user decision, all styles).
- Avoid neon multi-colour city palettes; one or two warm notes only.

## 6. Surface and texture
- Painterly flat with grain/stipple (most north stars); watercolour for heritage (places-033); screen-print for poster
  beats (places-019). One texture family per episode.
- Avoid: AI "animated movie still" gloss (core-005, light-096–102), anime fisheye rooms, holographic gradients.
- Never cut out or trace plates (retired paper look); used whole.

## 7. Scale and people
- People allowed in plates, held poses only, faces hidden (C5).
- Crowds as texture; one figure in the accent colour (people-005, 022).
- Posture tells it: slumped, leaning back, hands in pockets (core-073, people-008).

## 8. Camera and motion
- Depth camera: slow pushes into the light, tilts from sign to action, parallax through foreground darks.
- Light breathing: flicker of signs and bulbs, steam, smoke, rain, ripples, moving light patches (GIFs light-089, 065).
- The subject in the accent holds while the grey world changes around it (light-116, people-040).
- Overlays live on the plate's own surfaces: prices on menu boards, numbers on screens, labels on signs.

## 9. Handovers
| handover | how | refs |
|---|---|---|
| Push into the light | push into the doorway/screen until its light fills the frame, cut to the next plate's light | light-066, core-007 |
| Plate-in-plate | push into an object that holds the next scene (hourglass, bite, silhouette) | core-007, 066, people-019 |
| Light slide | the light patch slides to the next subject | light-018, 012 |
| Tilt sign → action | open on the sign, tilt down to the action | places-033, light-016 |
| Match on shape | sun disc → coin → clock; a reflection becomes the next plate | cam-043, core-094 |
| Silhouette window | the next scene appears inside a figure's silhouette | people-031, 019 |
| Bookend | open and close on the same plate at a different light (day → dusk) | light-117 |

## 10. Never
- Faces as the subject (expressions, close-ups) — even in plates; character sheets and expression studies are not plates.
- AI-glossy animated-feature stills, fisheye rooms, generic neon cyberpunk.
- Film-still look-alikes (we take their light, not their frames).
- Cut-paper, traced or cut-out plates (retired).
- More than one metaphor per plate; metaphor when the line is literal.
- Slide-like frames (anti-slop).

## 11. Adopt / Adapt / Drop
| device | verdict | how |
|---|---|---|
| Business as the light source (C1) | **Adopt** | prompt the source explicitly; overlay flicker/breathing |
| Light shapes (C2) | **Adopt** | prompt hard light shapes; animate with masked overlays |
| One pun per plate (C3) | **Adopt** | only on metaphor lines; literal otherwise |
| One among many (C4) | **Adopt** | prompt grids/rows; overlay highlight on the one |
| Faceless people (C5) | **Adopt** | prompt back views/silhouettes; held poses |
| Shadow/reflection second character (C6) | **Adopt** | prompt the shadow shape explicitly |
| Malaysian truth list (C7) | **Adopt** | a reusable prompt glossary of local details (stools, pour, signs, scooters) |
| Moving people in plates | **Drop** | figures never move (house rule; raea test needed) |
| Painterly character close-ups | **Drop** | faces off-limits |
| Fast montage | **Drop** | plates need 3–6 s each |

## 12. Decisions (user, 2026-11-09)
Carried over (2026-11-08): accent from brand + meaning; match cut allowed in identical framing; day to explain, dusk
for payoff; styles share techniques.
1. **Malaysia is the default setting** for Style C (local places, true detail).
2. **Night by default** for F&B and street businesses; day for heritage/morning ones.
3. **Painterly grain** is the default texture; watercolour for heritage episodes.

## 13. Test plan (not built)
Same business as the A/B tests.
- **Plate 1 — hook:** the shop at night, its sign the only warm light, reflected on wet ground; one customer silhouette.
- **Plate 2 — mechanism:** inside, back view of the owner at the counter/till, lit by its screen or lamp; the number
  overlaid on the screen.
- **Plate 3 — payoff:** one lit window/stall among dark ones from above (one among many).
- **10-second test:** push from the sign down to the door (3 s) → push into the doorway light, plate 2 (3 s) → pull up
  to plate 3, the one lit window (4 s); steam/flicker overlays throughout.

## 14. Shared with A and B
**C gives A and B:**
- The business as the light source (A: emissive windows/signs on clay; B: flat light shapes from the shop).
- One among many (A: one lit box among white ones; B: one coloured window in a grid).
- The Malaysian truth list (all three styles).
- Shadows as a second character (A: shaped gobo shadows; B: flat shadow shapes).
**A and B give C:**
- One physical token through the episode (A-C4) — the same coin/marble overlaid across plates.
- Words on the world's objects (B4) — overlay numbers on menus, tags, screens.
- Lock and change one thing (A-C5) — same plate, light changes.
**Keep different:**
- A = modelled clay world, B = flat geometry in SVG, C = painted plates with depth camera. C alone has painterly
  texture, real-feeling people (faceless) and atmospheric weather; A and B never imitate painting.
