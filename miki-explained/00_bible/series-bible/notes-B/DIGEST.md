## C01 digest
**★5 (10):** 003 (an invented machine whose screen speaks the line) · 009 (court shadows tell the heat) · 010 (a trade-off as two objects on a see-saw) · 028 (a plan as a road that ramps up to take-off) · 031 (9:16 top-down: ball, racket and one big diagonal shadow) · 033 (chips and a card spell "007") · 035/036 (a trade map in one colour family; one red roof as the subject) · 043 (a giant Jenga tower kept up by one worker) · 049 (huge storks over a tiny map) · 050 (the story told by what's packed in a suitcase). 10 of 54 (19 %) — a strong chunk; Julian Burford and Josh Patterson are north-star candidates.
**Convictions forming:**
- **Invent one object for the mechanism** (machine, suitcase, see-saw, Jenga): 003, 010, 043, 044, 050, 052, 014.
- **Shadows of unseen things set the place and the time**: 009, 031, 041, 021.
- **Top-down map in one colour family, one subject coloured**: 035, 036, 049, 047.
- **Spell the brand/number with props**: 033, 008, 042.
- **Roads/paths as timelines**: 028, 011, 005.
- **Limited palette (3–4 colours) + grain + hard flat shadow**: 006, 019, 003 family.
**Tensions:** two styles inside B: (a) line-art iso objects on a flat ground (Burford: thin black line, hard black shadow) and (b) textured top-down/perspective scenes with light and grain (Patterson, 009, 031). Several are crypto/stock (004, 015, 038, 048, 054) — avoid.
**Couldn't read:** GIFs (017, 023, 026, 052) judged on the first frame; motion in M01/M02.

## C02 digest
**★5 (12):** core-061 (money in, product out through an arch) · core-062 (a shadow crosses the third lemon) · light-001 (warm light as two flat shapes in a blue world) · light-003 (pub at dusk: warm windows, name on the façade) · light-012 (a desk that exists only inside the moonlight patch) · light-016 (a street told in two colours by shadows on a wall) · light-018 (a dog exactly inside the sun patch) · light-024 (a spiral stair's one big curve, four colours) · light-028 (one cone on the running track at the shadow edge) · cam-003 (a façade of lit windows, each a life) · cam-005 (a city as a cross-section). 12 of 54 (22 %) — the Light folder is the richest set in B.
**Convictions forming:**
- **Light is a flat shape, and it is the composition**: light-001, 012, 014, 016, 018, 020, 022, 028; core-009, 031. Light patch = the stage; the subject sits inside it; objects split at the light edge.
- **Two to four colours, one for light, one for shadow**: light-016, 017, 022, 024, color-004.
- **Warm windows in a cool world = the business is alive**: light-001, 003, 027, cam-003 (matches A-C7).
- **One small object on a big pattern / at a shadow edge**: light-028, core-062, light-027.
- **Invented machines and conveyors for transactions**: core-061 (continues C01).
- **Palette as ground/figure pairs**: color-004, color-011.
- **Cross-section to show the hidden system**: cam-005 (matches A-C3).
**Tensions:** many flat-light pieces rely on human figures (Malika Favre) — we can only use tiny silhouettes; objects must carry the light idea. Long-shadow 2014 flat icons (light-004, 010, 015) and cut-paper (color-006) are explicitly out.
**Couldn't read:** none.

## C03 digest
**★5 (2):** cam-006 (a small brand's 3-floor cutaway: shop, workroom, office) · cam-022 (a landmark in two colours: lit side = the sky colour, shadow draws the building). 2 of 54 (4 %) — stricter rating, and the People folder is mostly not transferable.
**Convictions forming:**
- **Cutaway building, one stage per floor** (cam-006, cam-010; C02 cam-005): the business's process stacked vertically.
- **Two-colour light, lit side melts into the ground** (cam-022, confirms C02 light-016).
- **Reuse the ground colour inside the subject** (cam-012, people-010) — figure and ground tie together.
- **People, if ever: primitive shapes, no faces, no hands, back views, one held posture, identity by one item** (people-007, 018, 019, 026, 030). This is the buildable answer to the people rule.
- **The accent goes on the topic/goods, not the people** (people-008, 027, 031).
- **A token's motion as a translucent trail** (people-015).
**Tensions:** the People folder is mostly character illustration (faceted sports stars, Jun Takahashi, stock "Humaaans") — style admired, technique off-limits. Several Camera images are stock/AI (cam-002/009/013/014/021/023/025).
**Couldn't read:** none. (cam-023 is visually the same as cam-002.)

## C04 digest
**★5 (3):** places-007 (a façade split by a horizontal light line: red family above, blue below) · places-010 (a high street where a new coffee sign hangs over an old jeweller's lettering: business history in signs) · type-004 (a product's hidden cost printed on its own swing tag, one orange bar). 3 of 51 (6 %).
**Convictions forming:**
- **Words and numbers live on the world's own objects** (signs, tags, bus boards, shop names): type-004, places-009, 010, 016, 024 — the strongest B idea of this chunk, same as A-C8.
- **Flat front elevations of shop rows** as the "street" beat; the subject is the only saturated façade: places-010, 011, 021, 001.
- **Light changes the colour family, not just the value** (red in sun, blue in shade): places-007, 022.
- **True local clutter** (signboards, wires, bikes) on South/East Asian streets: places-003, 006, 012.
- **Prop kits in the episode's 2–3 colours**, black as shadow, accent on one part: props-001, 003, 016, 009.
- **Lists as filled-vs-outlined words or sign rows**: type-002, type-009.
**Tensions:** much of Props is generic icon packs (Streamline, fintech 3D, holographic) — stock; only the system rules transfer. Several Places are AI or painterly-traced (places-004, 018, 023).
**Couldn't read:** GIFs (props-001, 007, type-002) judged on the first frame.

## M02 digest
**★5 (1):** motion-017 (black/cream/red, the red shape carries every handover, bookended on the same phone message).
**Motion convictions (with M01):**

- **Object/scale grows into the next background** (motion-012, 022).
- **Bookend on the same object** (motion-017, 007).
- **Brand fills frame 1, then the camera pulls back** (motion-019).
