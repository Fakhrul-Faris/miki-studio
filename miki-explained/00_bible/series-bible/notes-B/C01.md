### B-core-001 · ★4 · "10x18" (Janelle Monáe — Dirty Computer, album poster series 7/10)
Idea: an album about being a "dirty computer" shown as an old computer whose parts are breaking out of it.
Decision: a beige CRT is the base; a cursor, a keyboard slab, a sphere and a floppy-like bar stack and tumble out of the screen like a sculpture; one warm pink-to-yellow gradient ground with grain; small flat squiggles and asterisks as punctuation.
Eye: 1 the cursor arrow (darkest, centre) → 2 the yellow sphere → 3 the CRT screen.
Feeling: playful, a little chaotic, retro-warm.
Not generic: the object literally comes apart into its symbols; the stack has balance and tension.
Steal: let the subject's own parts burst out of it and stack into a sculpture to say "it's overflowing / breaking".
Moves: parts pop out of the screen in sequence and settle into the stack (no bounce); the gradient breathes.
