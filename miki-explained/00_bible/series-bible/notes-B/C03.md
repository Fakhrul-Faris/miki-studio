### B-cam-006 · ★5 · "Editorial Illustration Projects III" (three-storey shop building cut open)
Idea: one small business's whole life in a dollhouse cross-section: shop, workshop, office.
Decision: front wall removed; ground floor is the shopfront (striped awning, suit in the window, owner at the door), middle floor the sewing/stock room, top floor the office with a logo on the wall; neighbours are flat textured façades; a brand flag hangs off the side; flat colour + grain.
Eye: 1 the shop window (red suit) → 2 the owner at the door → up to 3 the workroom → 4 the office.
Feeling: proud, understandable, "this is how a small brand works".
Not generic: each floor is one stage of the business (make → stock → sell → manage), stacked so the eye climbs.
Steal: a business as a 3-floor cutaway, one stage per floor; the brand flag/sign on the façade.
Moves: camera rises floor by floor on each beat; the shop sign lights first.
Shares: A-C3 + A-C8, nearly identical to A-light-032 in flat form.

### B-cam-022 · ★5 · "Towers of San Francisco by Coen Pohl" (Ferry Building tower, pink/blue)

Decision: 9:16, low angle; the lit faces of the tower are pink (the same pink as the sky), the shaded faces are cobalt; detail is carved purely by the light/shadow split; only the flag breaks out.
Eye: 1 the flag → down the shaded edge → 2 the clock face.

Steal: two-colour landmark: lit side = background colour, shadow side = one dark colour; the business's building as a 9:16 icon.
Moves: the shadow side rotates as the sun moves (light sweep); the camera tilts up to the flag.
Shares: confirms B-light-016 (two-colour light).

### B-people-007 · ★4 · "Character Design Geometric Shapes" (nine body-shape characters)
Idea: people built from simple geometric blocks: a dome torso, rectangle legs, a ball head.
Decision: four colours (rust, mustard, navy, cream) + patterned sweaters; heads are tiny circles; bodies differ by silhouette only.
Eye: across the lineup, silhouette to silhouette.
Feeling: friendly, crafty, modular.
Not generic: no faces, no limbs to animate — people as stacked shapes. This is buildable in SVG without "clip-art hands".
Steal: if B needs people, build them from 3–4 primitive shapes (dome, rectangle, circle) with no face and no hands; identity by silhouette and one pattern.
Moves: a figure "assembles" from its blocks; they hold still.
Shares: A-section 7 (faceless figures, identity by one item).
