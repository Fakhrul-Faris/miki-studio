# M01 — Style B motion notes (GIFs + videos 001–010)

Fields: Idea · Camera · Handover · Timing · Entrances/exits · Steal for Style B. ★1–3 compact.

### B-core-017 · ★3 · tvnet cancer documentary (GIF, 2.7 s)
A painted boy at a table; at 1.5 s the image dissolves in a glitch-pixel smear and re-forms as a tight close-up of his face (eyes glowing). Handover: pixel-dissolve "morph" from wide to close-up in the same position. Steal for B: a texture-dissolve (grain/pixel) as the handover from wide to close, with the subject in the same screen position. (Faces off-limits; use on objects.)

### B-core-023 · ★2 · Forbender VR (GIF, 4 s loop)
Only the light rays pulse and shimmer; figure static. Timing: slow loop. Steal: animate only the light rays on a still image (cheap, effective idle motion).

### B-core-026 · ★2 · GSK Hope (GIF, 5.6 s)
A girl's arm pose eases from one hold to another (hands behind head). Character pose-to-pose. Steal: two held poses with one eased transition — fits "pose only" if used on objects.

### B-core-052 · ★5 · SMM machine (GIF, 7.1 s, 0 cuts)
Idea: the machine works: a ball comes out of the arch onto the belt, opens to show a face, closes and rolls away; the next one, a different colour, follows.
Camera: locked iso.
Handover: loop — each output replaces the last on the belt.
Timing: ~3.5 s per cycle; output slides out, a pause to reveal (lid opens), then exits; calm, no bounce.
Entrances/exits: products emerge from the tunnel and slide off the belt edge (never pop).
Steal for Style B: the invented-machine beat animated as a loop: input sits in the dome, product slides out of the arch on the belt, opens for one beat to show what it is, exits. One product per spoken item.
Shares: A-C4 token / motion-008 build loop.

### B-props-001 · ★3 · Affirm icons (GIF, 3.9 s)
Within a fixed icon grid, small parts animate in place (an umbrella opens, a feather writes, a cupcake candle flickers) — a "living grid". Steal: a static grid where one item at a time does one tiny action = an inventory beat that still moves.

### B-props-007 · ★3 · Flux arcade (GIF, 2.1 s)
Tiles hold; the pencil rocks, the balloon bobs, potion levels slosh — every object idles. Steal: give each prop a 1-parameter idle (rock, bob, slosh) at slow tempo; keeps flat frames alive between beats.

### B-type-002 · ★4 · Habib's pizza names (GIF, 5 s)
Idea: a slot-machine list: the filled white word scrolls through outlined names, stopping on each.
Camera: locked.
Handover: vertical scroll of the list; the fill sits in the centre band.
Timing: ~0.6 s scroll, ~0.6 s hold, repeated; snappy ease-in-out.
Entrances/exits: words slide up through a centre "window"; outlines above/below.
Steal for Style B: a list of options scrolling through a centre window like a slot reel, the chosen one filled; good for "every flavour / every branch / every price" lines.

### B-motion-001 · ★3 · "BAHAR / TU MIL" kinetic type (11 s)
Yellow display words on coral bend and rotate around the frame like a spinning drum, with plasters and stars. Loud kinetic-type loop. Steal: words wrapping a rotating cylinder as a title/poster beat; too loud for explainer pace.

### B-motion-002 · ★3 · drone explainer (84 s, 16 cuts)
Grainy flat explainer in lilac/peach: skyline, captain, presenter on stage, drones, landscapes, rain. Character-led, many cuts. Handover: cuts plus rain/texture transitions. Steal: a full-frame weather texture (rain streaks) as a transition between scenes.

### B-motion-003 · ★1 · blue stock real-estate explainer (127 s, 3 cuts)

### B-motion-004 · ★4 · flowers growing on black (14.6 s, 2 cuts)
Idea: a bouquet assembles stem by stem from nothing on a black void.
Camera: locked, then a re-framed wider shot.
Handover: growth fills the frame; the cut is to a fuller state.
Timing: organic overshoot-free growth; each element grows from its base (stem draws, then the bloom unfolds), staggered ~0.4 s.
Entrances/exits: elements grow out of their base point — never fade or pop.
Steal for B: "grow from the base" entrance for objects (stems, bars, pins, towers): draw the stem/line first, then the head unfolds.

### B-motion-005 · ★3 · walker through giant shoe and type (51 s, 0 cuts, 9:16)
A small figure walks in place while a giant sneaker, big type and flat hills scroll past in layers. Character walk cycle (not ours), but the parallax world over a static figure matches our "move the world" rule. Steal: keep the subject still and scroll multiple flat layers at different speeds behind it.

### B-motion-006 · ★3 · NeverSitStill reel (30 s, 15 cuts)

### B-motion-007 · ★4 · McDonald's McNuggets box becomes a dragon (16 s, 0 cuts, 9:16)
Idea: the product box transforms into a creature in an ornate frame, then returns.
Camera: push from a tray (POV) into a decorated proscenium frame, then pull back to the tray.
Handover: push-in through the product into its "dream" frame and back out (bookend).
Timing: steady push; the transformation (box opens → dragon face → snaps shut with a burst) on a beat.
Entrances/exits: nuggets fall into the scene from above; the box morphs, never cut.
Steal for B: push into the product to enter its "world", tell the beat inside an ornamental frame built from the brand's patterns (logo repeats), pull back out to reality. Brand from frame 1 on the box. (Hands appear here; we'd keep objects only.)

### B-motion-010 · ★4 · lamp in a corner room (22 s, 0 cuts loop)
Idea: one object in a one-point room: a black box with an orange flap becomes an orange table lamp and back.
Camera: locked one-point corner (two thin perspective lines on cream).
Handover: object morph in place (box ↔ lamp), looping every ~5.5 s.
Timing: slow, deliberate; long holds on each state.
Entrances/exits: the shape morphs; nothing enters or leaves.
Steal for B: a quiet "object becomes another object" morph in an almost empty room (a closed shop shutter → an open stall; a jar → a coin pile). Three colours, generous holds.
