# M02 — Style B motion notes (videos 011–026)

### B-motion-011 · ★4 · neon-green runner, balloons, merry-go-round (30 s, 67 cuts)
Idea: anxiety chase told in four saturated colours: a green runner pursued by angry balloons through shifting shapes.
Camera: constant motion, extreme angles, one-point perspective lanes.
Handover: graphic match on the green shape (runner → horse → stair → window); colour fields wipe.
Timing: very fast (~0.45 s cuts), music-driven.
Entrances/exits: shapes slam in from frame edges.
Steal for B: a single-colour hero shape that stays the same colour through every scene so the eye never loses it. Pace too fast for us.

### B-motion-012 · ★4 · monify app (10 s, 0 cuts)
Idea: an app promo where each panel slides in as a full-colour field with one object.
Camera: locked; panels slide/scale to fill the frame.
Handover: the object grows to fill the frame and becomes the next background (screen → purple field → piggy bank scene).
Timing: snappy entries, ~1.2 s per beat.
Entrances/exits: objects slide in from edges; coins drop into the piggy bank.
Steal for B: "object zooms until its colour becomes the next background" — a palette-led handover from scene assets. (Hands are drawn; we'd keep objects.)

### B-motion-016 · ★4 · type with inline logo shapes (3.6 s)
Idea: a sentence builds word by word and a small black logo glyph slots between words, flipping as it moves.
Camera: locked.
Handover: words appear in reading order; the glyph jumps between gaps; the sentence collapses to its last clause.
Timing: ~0.3 s per word, snappy.
Entrances/exits: words drop in from small offsets; the glyph flips (rotate) between positions.
Steal for B: the brand mark hopping through a spoken line like a cursor — logo on screen while the words land. (Our captions stay EX15; this applies to an in-scene title only.)

### B-motion-017 · ★5 · "Are we still friends?" (11.5 s, 15 cuts, 9:16)
Idea: a message left on "Seen" spirals into a cosmic, then crumpled-paper, then cartoon-face metaphor, and returns to the phone.
Camera: tight, mostly locked frames; 9:16.
Handover: shape-led cuts — the red ring around the satellite becomes the red triangle road, the road becomes the red corridor, the paper plane crumples into a ball, the red field becomes the face; bookended by the same phone frame.
Timing: quick (~0.6 s) with a hold on the crumpled paper and the face.
Entrances/exits: shape matches at cuts; grain and soft gradients keep it unified.
Steal for B: a strict three-colour palette (black, cream, one red) where the red shape carries every handover; open and close on the same object (the phone message) — the bookend. Fits 9:16 and our through-line rule.

### B-motion-019 · ★4 · McDonald's app "TAP TAP" delivery (7.4 s, 22 cuts, 9:16)

## M02 digest

**Motion convictions (with M01):**
- **One colour shape carries every handover** (motion-017, 011): B's through-line rule.
- **Object/scale grows into the next background** (motion-012, 022).
- **Bookend on the same object** (motion-017, 007).
- **Brand fills frame 1, then the camera pulls back** (motion-019).
- **Shape kit replaces letters and carries transitions** (motion-022).
- **Morph in place, grow from base** (M01 010, 004).
**Tensions:** fast title/reel pacing (011, 015, 019, 23 at 0.3 s cuts) is too fast for 20–30 s explainers; character-led pieces are off-limits.
