## Sources

- Most McDonald's soft serve runs on Taylor machines (the C602 in ~13,000 of ~40,000 restaurants, 2021). Every night
  the machine heats the mix to **66 °C (151 °F)** to pasteurise it, then refreezes it: **about four hours**. When the
  cycle fails, the usual fix is to run it again, so the machine can be down into opening hours —
  [Wikipedia: McDonald's ice cream machine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McDonald's_ice_cream_machine).
- Digital locks on the machines blocked independent repair; the **US Copyright Office granted a repair exemption in
  October 2024** covering commercial soft-serve machines — [Hackaday, 26 Oct 2024](https://hackaday.com/2024/10/26/mcdonalds-ice-cream-machines-gain-a-dmca-exemption/).
- **McBroken** (since Oct 2020) tries to order a sundae at every US McDonald's every 30 minutes; around **10–15%** come
  back unavailable — [Wikipedia: McBroken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McBroken).
