## Sources

Every fact in the script, with its source. No unverified claims.

- **Posted waits run long.** Actual waits were 55–71 % of posted at the four Walt Disney World parks (Animal Kingdom 65 %,
  EPCOT 55 %, Hollywood Studios 71 %, Magic Kingdom 62 %); 35 million posted and 2 million actual wait times, the 60 days to
  7 Oct 2021. Touring Plans, Becky Gandillon, "The Wait Time Secret That Disney Won't Tell You".
  https://touringplans.com/blog/the-wait-time-secret-that-disney-wont-tell-you/ (checked 2026-11-01)
- **One day's example.** 20 Jul 2021: Pirates of the Caribbean posted 60 min, actual 23; It's a Small World 55 → 16;
  Haunted Mansion 65 → 35. Same source.
- **Smaller gap since.** 3 months to Feb 2023: actuals 60–68 % of posted. Touring Plans, "Posted vs Actual Wait Times".
  https://touringplans.com/blog/posted-vs-actual-wait-times-worst-offenders/ (use the dated 2021 figures)
- **"On purpose" (attributed, not Disney's own words).** MIT queueing expert Richard Larson: signs advertise longer waits
  than the real ones, a "Machiavellian twist": guests feel they've won back minutes. Popular Science, Sara Kiley Watson,
  6 Aug 2020. https://www.popsci.com/story/science/head-trip-disney-wait-lines/ · Also Alex Stone, "Why Waiting Is Torture",
  NYT 2012 (Disney overestimates waits so guests are pleasantly surprised; paywalled, wording not checked).
  **Disney has never said it pads the signs**: the script attributes it.
- **Hidden length.** Space Mountain's queue twists back and forth between walls so you can't see how many people are
  ahead, which keeps you from bowing out. Popular Science, same article.
