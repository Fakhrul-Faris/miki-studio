# EX00 — EffectCraft test (Houston airport opening)

Purpose: prove the look and the core moves in EffectCraft before EX01. Not for posting.
Format: 1080×1920, 24fps. Fonts: Work Sans Black (claims, caps) + Inclusive Sans (detail, caps).
Palette (placeholder, change in post): cream `#F6F1E6`, ink `#141414`, accent red `#D93B3B`, dark `#0E0E0E`.

Facts are approximate for the test only (NYT, Alex Stone, "Why Waiting Is Torture", 2012). Verify before EX01.

## Script (~36 words, ~15s)

| Line | Voice | Beat / pattern | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| L01 | Houston airport fixed its complaints… by making people walk further. | Hook, word-locked headline (D02) | "HOUSTON AIRPORT FIXED ITS COMPLAINTS BY MAKING PEOPLE WALK FURTHER." builds word by word, centred, cream |
| L02 | Passengers walked one minute to baggage claim. Then they stood there for seven. | Chart build (D05) | A timeline axis draws (0–8 MIN). A grey segment "WALKING · 1 MIN", then the red segment "STANDING · 7 MIN" draws |
| L03 | Same eight minutes. But standing still… is the part people hated. | Push-in (D06) + arc pointer (D07) | Camera pushes in on the red segment; arc pointer lands; detail text "7 OF 8 MINUTES DOING NOTHING" types |
| L04 | Now picture your shop. On a Saturday. | Theme flip to dark (D09) | Wipe to dark; "YOUR SHOP. SATURDAY." builds; a small red dot (a customer) waits on a bench line |

## Voice direction (ElevenLabs)

Calm, low, unhurried documentary narrator. Not an ad read.

- **L01:** a slight pause at "…", and land "walk further" as the twist.
- **L02:** matter-of-fact. A short beat between the two sentences.
- **L03:** slower. A pause at "…", with weight on "hated".
- **L04:** quieter, closer. A beat between "your shop" and "On a Saturday."

Export each line as its own file with ~0.3s of silence at both ends:
`audio/L01.wav`, `audio/L02.wav`, `audio/L03.wav`, `audio/L04.wav`.
