## The story

Around 2018–2019, every e-wallet and bank had its own QR code, so a shop counter carried a row of stickers, one per app.
If your app wasn't on the counter, you paid cash or didn't buy. The fix wasn't one app winning. Malaysia made them **share**:
PayNet built one national QR standard, DuitNow QR, that any bank or e-wallet app can scan. One sticker per shop.
Today there are over three million of them, and Bank Negara has ordered the single-app QR networks shut down by 2028.
The turn: your counter still asks questions ("cash only?", "which app?", "transfer and show me the screenshot").

## Sources

- **The problem and the fix (2019):** "merchants would only need to display one QR Code, the DuitNow QR, to accept payments,
  as opposed to the current practice of displaying multiple proprietary QR codes at payment counters." DuitNow QR is
  "a common QR standard that will work for customers of all participating banks and e-wallets."
  RinggitPlus, 5 Aug 2019 (https://ringgitplus.com/en/blog/banking/paynet-s-duitnow-qr-coming-soon-as-a-universal-qr-standard-for-payments.html);
  The Malaysian Reserve, 4 Nov 2019: GrabPay first e-wallet on DuitNow QR (https://themalaysianreserve.com/2019/11/04/grabpay-partners-paynet-to-adopt-duitnow-qr/);
  Borneo Post, 1 Aug 2019: Public Bank first bank (https://www.theborneopost.com/2019/08/01/public-bank-first-to-adopt-national-qr-standard-via-duitnow-qr/).
- **Who:** PayNet was tasked under Bank Negara's interoperability framework to set the national QR standard (same sources).
- **Scale today:** "681,250 new DuitNow QR acceptance points were introduced, including 267,780 among micro, small and medium
  enterprises (MSMEs) … bringing the nationwide total to more than three million registered DuitNow QR touchpoints."
  PayNet press release, 22 Apr 2026 (https://paynet.my/about-us/media-centre/press-release/8-44-billion-transactions-processed-in-2025-as-digital-payments-become-malaysians-preferred-way-to-pay.html).
- **2028:** Bank Negara's Interoperable Fund Transfer Framework (30 Jun 2026): existing proprietary QR networks must be
  discontinued by 30 June 2028; no new merchants can be onboarded onto them in the meantime.
  The Edge Malaysia (https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/808881); RinggitPlus (https://ringgitplus.com/en/blog/banking-technology/bank-negara-phases-out-proprietary-qr-payment-networks-by-june-2028.html);
  Christopher & Lee Ong (https://www.christopherleeong.com/viewpoints/bnm-issues-new-interoperable-fund-transfer-framework/).
  Open: link Bank Negara's own policy document before posting.
- **Not used:** "3 billion DuitNow QR transactions in 2025" (reported by Fintech News Malaysia, attributed to remarks by the
  BNM governor, but not in PayNet's release). Cut unless a primary document turns up.
- The counter questions in the turn are illustrative, not data.
