## Topic gate (`00_bible/script-formula.md` v2)

1. Seen weekly + searched: nasi lemak, every day; "harga nasi lemak" is a perennial news topic.
2. The question viewers already have: why does my nasi lemak keep getting more expensive?
3. One idea: hawker food rose far faster than "inflation"; the coconut is the most picturable cause.
4. Malaysian numbers: all from DOSM / FAMA / Malaysian press.
5. "Wait, really?": 81 % vs 29 %.

**Premise problem with the calendar's question ("Where does your RM10 nasi lemak actually go?"):** no source splits a plate
into ingredients / rent / wages / profit (only a nasi lemak brand's blog: "~35 % ingredients, ~10 % profit", no method), and
DOSM's average plate is RM3.68, not RM10. A breakdown would mean inventing numbers, so the question is reframed below.

## Option B — keep the calendar's question, answered with what can be sourced

"Where does your nasi lemak money go?" told as one stall's day (the Penang seller: 15 kg of santan a day at RM14/kg = RM210
on santan alone, holding his price at RM3). True and concrete, but it's one seller's numbers, and the rest of the split
(rice, rent, wages, profit) would still be missing. Not recommended unless a stall owner can give us real numbers.
