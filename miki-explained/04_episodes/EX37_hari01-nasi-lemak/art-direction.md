# EX37 Hari 1/30 — art direction (frame brief, before any code)

Style B (EX33 baseline: one Three.js world in HyperFrames, two-tone toon, no outlines, grain, light as a hard shape, words
on objects). Script: Option A, approved 2026-11-11. North stars **opened and looked at** for this brief (LESSONS §6):
B-core-033, B-core-010, B-core-031, B-places-007.

## The idea in one line

**The breakfast table is the chart.** Every number is made from the plate itself or sits on the table it's eaten at; the
price tag on the bungkus is the through-line.

## System

| Axis | Decision | Why |

## Frames (Ref → Idea → Decision → Eye → Feeling → Not generic because → Moves → Handover)

**L01 Hook — "Nasi lemak was RM2.03 in 2011. Now it's RM3.68."** · Ref **B-core-031** (top-down 9:16, one huge diagonal shadow, small objects)
- Idea: the same bungkus, two prices, thirteen years apart.
- Decision: top-down on one marble table cut by the awning's stripes; the bungkus sits in a light stripe; its tag reads
  "2011 · RM2.03"; a kopitiam chit beside it: "HARI 1/30".
- Eye: tag → bungkus → chit.
- Moves (frame 0 already moving): the 2011 tag peels off (sambal-red corner lifting) and a new one lands: "2024 · RM3.68";
  the light stripes creep (the sun rising = time passing, one smooth move).
- Not generic: the number lives on the food's own paper; the time passes in the light.
- Handover: the camera tilts up off the table and the bungkus becomes the weight on a see-saw (L02).

**L02–L03 Open loop + Mechanism 1 — "Inflation over those years? Only 29%. But nasi lemak rose 81%. Roti canai, 71%."** · Ref **B-core-010** (a trade-off as two objects on a see-saw)
- Idea: inflation is a basket; your breakfast outweighs it.
- Decision: side view on the table edge; a see-saw made of a kopitiam ruler on a sugar-cube pivot (scale play: objects
  at table scale). Left: a tiny shopping basket labelled "INFLATION +29%" (the CPI is a basket of goods: literal).
  Right: the bungkus, tag "+81%" in sambal red. On "Roti canai, 71%" a roti canai drops onto the right side beside it.
- Eye: the tipping beam → the red +81 % → the basket left up in the air.
- Moves: the beam tips to the right on "81%" (weight, no bounce), tips further on the roti.
- Not generic: a statistics comparison told as a physical imbalance on a breakfast table.
- Handover: the camera drops to the bungkus; it unfolds (banana leaf opening) to show the plate inside.

**L03 (insert, on "81%") — the number made of the plate** · Ref **B-core-033** (chips and a card spell 007 in a beam of light)
- Decision: top-down as the bungkus opens: the boiled-egg halves stacked = "8", a cucumber stick = "1", two peanuts and an
  ikan bilis = "%". One diagonal light beam crosses exactly the 81 %; everything else in the leaf-green shadow.
- Not generic: the dish spells its own price rise.

**L04 Mechanism 2 — "Its santan went from RM7.39 a kilo to RM11.54."** · Ref B-core-061 for the object step (not a machine this time: a pour)
- Idea: the lemak is the santan; the santan is the line that rose.
- Decision: a cracked coconut tips; the santan pours onto the marble and runs as a rising line chart: it starts at a chit
  "2011 RM7.39/kg" and climbs to a chit "2024 RM11.54/kg"; the line's tip is sambal red.
- Moves: the pour leads the line; the line grows on the words.
- Handover: the line runs off the table edge into a market crate (L05).

**L05 Proof — "In late 2024, some markets lost half their coconuts."** · Ref B-places-007 (two colour families split by one light line)
- Decision: a market stall from the front at dawn; a crate full of coconuts; the light line drops down the stall; on
  "half" the coconuts below the line vanish (shrink into the crate) — half a crate left; chalkboard "LATE 2024".
- Not generic: the shortage is the empty half of a crate, cut by light.
- Handover: one coconut rolls out of frame back onto the kopitiam table.

**L06 Payoff — "Inflation is everyone's average. Breakfast is yours."** · Ref B-places-007 + B-core-031
- Idea: an average is a flat line across many tables; your table is not on it.
- Decision: high top-down over the whole kopitiam: a dozen marble tables with different breakfasts; a thin cream string
  (the average) stretched straight across all of them at one height; the light stripes shift and narrow to one table:
  yours, the bungkus with RM3.68, lifted above the string.
- Moves: the camera pulls up to the dozen tables on "everyone's average", then settles on yours on "yours". Hold 1 s.
- Emphasis: large.

**L07 End — "Still worth it at RM3.68?"** · bookend
- The L01 table again; the RM3.68 tag; the sun now full on the table.

**L08 Follow** — Meikigo end card (silent).
