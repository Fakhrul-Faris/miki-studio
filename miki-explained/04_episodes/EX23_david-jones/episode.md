Format: **long-edu (~60 s)** · story type **mechanism** (why department stores die), hung on this week's news · look to be
decided (clay maquette fits: a store you can cut open floor by floor). Figures pose only; EX15 captions. Global, with an

**Angle:** not "a retailer failed", but the maths of the building: a department store is a giant fixed cost (huge floors,
prime malls, long leases) that only works while it's the one place to find everything. Once brands and phones took that
away, the rent stayed.

## Sources

- **The collapse:** David Jones entered voluntary administration on **5 Nov 2026** (McGrathNicol), after talks with its
  biggest landlord, **Westfield** (Scentre Group), broke down; it had **not paid rent since September**; **17 of its 38
  stores** are in Westfield malls — [ABC News, 5 Nov](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-05/david-jones-collapses-into-voluntary-administration/107347878),
  [ABC News, 4 Nov](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-04/david-jones-could-collapse-behind-on-westfield-rent/107345094),
  [Yahoo Finance](https://au.finance.yahoo.com/news/david-jones-collapses-administration-221810575.html).
- **Age:** founded **1838**, 188 years old — ABC; [Retail Gazette](https://www.retailgazette.co.uk/blog/2026/11/australian-department-store-david-jones-collapses-into-administration-after-188-years/).
- **Jobs:** "more than 5,600 employees" ([The Conversation](https://theconversation.com/david-jones-falls-into-administration-and-its-outdated-business-model-explains-why-295349));
  others say "more than 6,000" — the script says **thousands of jobs**.
- **Value:** bought for **A$2.1 billion (2014)**, sold for **about A$100 million (2024)** — The Conversation.
- **Losses:** **$74 million (2023–24)**, **$95.5 million (2024–25)** — Yahoo Finance / ABC live blog.
- **Flagship:** a **$400 million** upgrade of the Sydney Elizabeth Street flagship finished **December 2019**, "just months
  before COVID-19 hit" — The Conversation.
- **Why the model broke:** self-service and malls took the service and convenience edge; brands opened their own stores,
  turning department stores into "mere clearing houses" — The Conversation (Gary Mortimer et al.).
- ⚠ Live story: re-check the job count and store closures the day before posting. All money in Australian dollars.
