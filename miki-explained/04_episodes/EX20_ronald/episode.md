## Sources

- **Life**: born 23 Oct 1921, Dummerston, Vermont; walked or hitchhiked 4 miles to high school, first in his family to
graduate (1940); WWII (North Africa, Italy as a military policeman, Pacific); ~25 years attendant and mechanic at
Haviland's Service Station (retired 1979); 17 years part-time custodial work at J.C. Penney (to 1997); died 2 June 2014
at 92 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronald_Read_(philanthropist)) (citing WSJ), [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/vermont-ex-janitor-bequeaths-secret-millions-library-hospital-n301396).
- **Frugal**: second-hand Toyota Yaris; a safety pin holding his fraying jacket; gathered fallen branches for his wood
stove; someone once paid for his diner breakfast thinking he couldn't afford it — NBC; Wikipedia.
- **How he invested**: "He only invested in what he knew and what paid dividends" (his attorney) — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2015/02/09/heres-how-a-janitor-amassed-an-8m-fortune.html);
held "no fewer than 95 stocks" across healthcare, telecoms, utilities, rail, banks, consumer goods (AT&T, Bank of
America, CVS, Deere, GE, GM); certificates in a safe-deposit box "five inches high"; Lehman Brothers' 2008 failure
barely dented him because he was spread out — Wikipedia (WSJ); read the Wall Street Journal daily — NBC.
- **Estate**: nearly **$8 million**; **$4.8M** to Brattleboro Memorial Hospital and **$1.2M** to Brooks Memorial Library,
the largest gifts either had ever received; $2M to family, friends and caregivers — NBC, CNBC.
- ⚠ "Reinvested his dividends" is widely repeated but not in the sources above; the script says "held on" and "bought
more", not "reinvested".
