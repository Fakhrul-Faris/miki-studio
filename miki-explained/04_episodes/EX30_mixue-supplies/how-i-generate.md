**Style (P1 only):** painterly night illustration, visible grain and stipple, one cool teal-navy hue for the world, the
shop's red-white light the only warm colour, rain-wet ground doubling the light, cinematic composition, no readable text, no faces.

- **P1** — A Malaysian shoplot street at night after rain. A small ice-cream kiosk is the only warm light, red and white, its glow doubled on the wet road. One young customer seen from behind stands at the counter holding a soft-serve cone. Blank signboard, blank menu board.
- **P2** — High aerial view of a Malaysian town at night: rows of terraced shoplots, wet roads. One single shopfront glows red; everything else is dark teal.
- **P3** — The back lane behind the shop at night under one fluorescent tube. A white supply lorry with its tailgate down, stacked plain cartons. The shop owner seen from behind with a hand trolley.
- **P4** — Worm's-eye view of a giant lit factory gate at night, a long line of small white lorries flowing out into the dark. In the foreground, small, one soft-serve cone resting on a low wall.
- **P5** — The same kiosk as the first image, closing: its shutter half down, warm light spilling from under it across the wet road, plain cartons stacked by the door. No people.

## 4 · Motion (Seedance 1 Pro, 5 s, 9:16, first frame = plate, last frame = end keyframe where given) → `media/S1.mp4` … `S5.mp4`
Tail for every prompt: *Silent, no text, people stay completely still.*
- **S1** (P1) — The kiosk's light breathes softly, rain ripples in the puddles, a scooter's headlight passes along the far side of the road. Slow push in.
- **S2** (P2 → P2_end) — Red shopfronts switch on one by one across the town. Camera holds.
- **S3** (P3 → P3_end) — Cartons slide down the tailgate onto the trolley. The fluorescent tube flickers once.
- **S4** (P4) — The lorries stream out of the gate into the night. The cone stays still. Slow tilt down toward the cone.
- **S5** (P5 → P5_end) — The shutter rolls down slowly; the light on the road narrows to a line.
