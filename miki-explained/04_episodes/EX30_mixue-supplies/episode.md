## Sources
- **~97% of revenue from selling goods and equipment to franchisees:** 2024 revenue from sales of goods and equipment
  RMB 24,208.8 m; franchise and related services RMB 620.1 m — MIXUE Group HKEX prospectus/listing documents (Mar 2025),
  [HKEX PDF](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2025/0326/2025032601256.pdf), summarised by
  [KCLau](https://kclau.com/investment/hkse-8-things-to-know-about-mixue-before-you-invest-2025/).
- **Store count:** 45,302 stores at 30 Sep 2024 vs McDonald's ~43,000 (prospectus; [Sherwood News](https://sherwood.news/business/mixue-more-stores-than-mcdonalds-investors-clamor-buy-stock-hot-ipo/),
  [Momentum Works](https://thelowdown.momentum.asia/mixue-overtakes-starbucks-as-worlds-largest-fb-chain-by-store-count/));
  59,823 at end-2025 vs McDonald's 45,356 ([civicintelligence](https://civicintelligence.digital/en/markets/mixue-franchise-model.html)).
  ⚠ Confirm the end-2025 figure in Mixue's 2025 annual report before posting; if not confirmed, the line becomes "over forty-five thousand".
- **Nearly all stores are franchised** (prospectus: a few dozen company-operated).
- **RM2 cone in Malaysia:** menu listings ([syioknya](https://www.syioknya.com/promotion/mixue-menu), [menuzmy](https://menuzmy.com/mixue-menu-malaysia/)).
  ⚠ Unofficial: check an outlet or Mixue Malaysia's own page before posting.
