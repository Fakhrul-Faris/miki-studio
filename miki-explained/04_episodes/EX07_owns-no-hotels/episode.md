## Sources and the numbers

- **Owns <1 %:** "As of year-end 2024, our system included 9,361 properties (1,706,331 rooms) in 144 countries and
  territories"; Marriott owns or leases "less than one percent of our system." — Marriott 10-K for 2024.
  https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1048286/000162828025004818/mar-20241231.htm
- **$5.4 B in fees, 2025:** "For full year 2025, gross fee revenues rose 5 percent, to $5.4 billion." (2024: $5.17 B —
  franchise $3.11 B, base management $1.29 B, incentive $0.77 B.) — Marriott Q4/FY2025 results, 10 Feb 2026.
  https://marriott.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/marriott-international-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025
- **$716 M from banks, 2025:** "co-branded credit card fees rose over 8 percent, to $716 million for the full year";
  guidance for ~35 % growth in 2026. — same release; https://platinumflyer.com/marriott-bonvoy-points-credit-card-fee-revenue/
- **The minibar loses money:** Marriott's CEO: "we don't make money from the minibar"; consultant Bjorn Hanson: of the
  hundreds of hotels with minibars he advised, "not one achieved a profit". — https://www.fastcompany.com/3039540/marriott-ceo-we-dont-make-money-from-the-minibar ·
  https://www.cnn.com/travel/article/hotel-minibars-decline
- Context (not in the script): rooms are a hotel's real profit centre, ~70 % departmental margin vs ~15–30 % for F&B (USALI benchmarks).
