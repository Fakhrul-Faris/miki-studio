# EX07 "The Hotel That Owns No Hotels": what to generate

Only two stills this time: the rest is diagram animation built in EffectCraft. The voice is already in (`audio/`).

Generate at **9:16**, paste the **Negative** box into the negative-prompt field, and save into
`EX07_owns-no-hotels/media/` with the exact **Save as** name.

**Negative (both):**
```
text, letters, words, numbers, typography, logos, signage, brand names, watermark, 3D render, glossy, gradients, clipart, flat vector, cartoon faces, emoji, lens flare, color except ink black, paper and ochre
```

- The hotel cut-out is whole (roof to pavement) on a plain paper background.
