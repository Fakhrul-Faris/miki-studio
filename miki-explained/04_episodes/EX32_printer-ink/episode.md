## Visual — Style B (no outlines, subtle grain, flat shapes with inner detail, light as a flat shape, words on objects)
Palette (new, from the product's meaning): warm paper-grey ground, ink black, and the three process inks — cyan,
magenta, yellow — as the only accents; one alarm (magenta) on the prices. Through-line: **one black ink drop** that
carries every hand-off.

| Line | Viewpoint | Drawn | Hand-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| L01 | frontal elevation, a desk in a window-light patch | the printer (real HP logo, paper tray, control panel), its swing tag RM268; the lid opens to show the cartridges | an ink drop falls from the cartridge |
| L02 | macro cutaway of the cartridge | the cartridge cut open: the ink level, a measuring scale reading 8.5 ml, the RM51 price sticker; pull back: a 1-litre bottle fills drop by drop, its label counts to RM6,000 | the bottle's ink becomes the next scene's ground |
| L03 | top-down, an invented object | HP's money as a cash tray of 3 slots: 2 fill with cartridges and toner, 1 with a printer (≈ two thirds) — "65%" on the tray's label | a coin lifts out of the tray |
| L04 | side elevation | the printer as a coin slot: each page that comes out pulls a coin in ("you keep paying with every page") | the last page slides out |
| L05 | frontal, the desk again (bookend) | the cheap printer beside a tall stack of cartridges; the light patch slides off | — |
