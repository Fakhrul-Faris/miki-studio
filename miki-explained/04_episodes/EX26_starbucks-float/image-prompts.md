# EX26 — plate prompts v2 (Seedream in Dreamina, one image per request, highest resolution)

v1 was called "generic" (2026-11-08). The look the user wants is in `styleframes/refs/1–3.png`:
- **Light does the drawing.** Light shafts and glows are built from stipple grain on flat shapes. There are no smooth gradients.
- **Cool palette, one warm accent.** Navy, cobalt, teal and mint carry the scene, and one small warm accent pulls the eye
  (the orange bucket, the red ring). Here the warm accent is **amber = the money**, always.
- **Scale and angle.** Small people against huge objects (the microscope), seen from high or low (window cleaner,
  escalator). The ground is never flat-on.
- **Layered depth.** A dark foreground frames a lit middle figure in front of a luminous background (the escalator).
- **Storytelling with shadow and reflection.** The window cleaner's shadow is a second character.
- **Quiet space.** Each frame has one idea and plenty of empty area.

Route B stays the same: plates are used whole and never cut out. They carry no text, numbers or logos, and phone screens
glow blank so I can put the numbers on them. The camera moves through each plate, and my vector overlays (coins, counters,
captions, the Starbucks logo) sit on top. Each image draws what its line literally says.
