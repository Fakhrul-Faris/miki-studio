# EX10 "The Popcorn Business": what to generate

Two environment plates, three cut-outs, one documentary photo and the voice. The plates and cut-outs become a Blender paper
cinema (like EX09's supermarket, with real environments this time). Generate at **9:16**, paste the **Negative** box, and
save into `EX10_popcorn/media/` with the exact **Save as** name. Steps 2–5 attach `ENV_auditorium.png` as style reference.

**Negative (all stills):**
```
text, letters, words, numbers, typography, logos, brand names, movie titles, posters with text, menu text, prices, signage, watermark, 3D render, glossy, gradients, clipart, flat vector, cartoon faces, emoji, lens flare, color except ink black, paper and ochre
```
