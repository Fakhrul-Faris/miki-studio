## Sources and the numbers (AMC Entertainment 10-K for 2025)

https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1411579/000141157926000016/amc-20251231x10k.htm

- Admissions revenues **$2,652.8 M**; food and beverage revenues **$1,671.3 M**; attendance **219.4 M** patrons
(→ average ticket ≈ **$12.09**; F&B = 39 % of tickets + F&B).
- "As a percentage of admissions revenues, film exhibition costs were **48.1%**" (U.S. markets 51.2 %) — the studios' cut.
- "As a percentage of food and beverage revenues, food and beverage costs were **19.6%**."
- "Food and beverage per patron increased 5.1% from $7.25 to **$7.62**."
- Per visit: from the ticket AMC keeps ≈ $12.09 × 51.9 % = **$6.28**; from the snacks ≈ $7.62 × 80.4 % = **$6.13** (before
rent, staff and other costs — gross, not profit; the script says "keeps", the pinned comment says "before costs").
- Studio share runs highest in a film's opening weeks (sliding scale): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film_distribution
