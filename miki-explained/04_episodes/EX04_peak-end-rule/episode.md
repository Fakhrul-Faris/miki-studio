## Sources

- **Redelmeier, Katz & Kahneman, "Memories of colonoscopy: a randomized trial", *Pain* (2003).**
  https://yorkspace.library.yorku.ca/xmlui/bitstream/10315/7959/1/KAT073.pdf · https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12855328/ (checked 2026-10-30)
  - "consecutive outpatients undergoing colonoscopy … (n=682)"
  - "half the patients had a short interval added to the end of their procedure"
  - final moments "less painful (1.7 vs. 2.5 on a ten point intensity scale)"
  - "rated the entire experience as less unpleasant (4.4 vs. 4.9 on a 10 cm visual analogue scale)"
  - repeat colonoscopy over a median 5.3 years: "slightly higher (odds ratio 1.41)" for the longer procedure → the script says
    "slightly more of them came back", never a percentage.
- **Redelmeier & Kahneman, "Patients' memories of painful medical treatments", *Pain* 66 (1996): 3–8.** Memories of the
  procedure reflected the pain at the worst moment and at the final moment (the peak-end pattern).
  https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/0304395996029946
