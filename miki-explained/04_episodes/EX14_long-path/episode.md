# EX14 — The Long Path

Status: story ✓ → script ✓ → voice ✓ → build ✓ → **final ✓ (2026-11-01)** → posted
Voice: Nathan, narrator, `v4k7NzxipnuJNJtrKxob`

Lane: Designed on Purpose (series pitch) · Topic type: everyday thing nobody questioned + insider secret (names IKEA)
Hook pattern: counterintuitive claim
Named concept: **The path is the shelf** (title card at Twist/Payoff)
Lands on (Meikigo problem): what your customer walks past on the way to what they came for (retail: a Klang hardware shop)

**Style: new experiment V3, "smooth isometric".** No generated images, no paper, no halftone, no 12 fps stepping. A dark,
clean isometric world (near-black #0E0E10, greys, warm off-white type, ochre #C98A2B the only colour), Isotype pictogram
people, flat shading with soft occlusion, everything at **60 fps** with real easing and motion blur, one continuous camera.
Optional: 1–2 real black-and-white footage inserts on screens inside the world.

## Sources

- IKEA's layout disorients shoppers so they shop the whole store; "the way to the exit is always behind you"; "so well done
  and so cunningly done that I have little doubt that it is intentional" — Prof. Alan Penn, UCL Bartlett (director, VR
  Centre for the Built Environment), lecture "Who enjoys shopping in IKEA?", Jan 2011; quoted by The Times, via
  [IceNews, 27 Jan 2011](https://icenews.is/2011/01/27/ikea-designed-like-a-maze-to-trap-shoppers/).
- "Sixty per cent of what people buy aren't actually on their original shopping list." — Penn, quoted in
  [The Globe and Mail, 7 Apr 2011](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/life/the-hot-button/lost-and-confused-at-ikea-its-all-part-of-their-plan/article613259/).
  **His estimate**, not an IKEA figure: the script says "Penn estimates".
- Marketplace impulse buys (lightbulbs, cheap casserole dishes) because shoppers know they can't easily go back — Penn, via
  [RetailWire](https://retailwire.com/discussion/prof-calls-ikea-layout-tortuous/).
- Small city stores: "We thought we didn't need to guide the customers because the stores are so small… [customers said]
  'No, no, no, this is a big shop!'" — Tolga Öncü, head of retail, Ingka Group, in
  [Fortune, 1 Sep 2023](https://www.fortune.com/europe/2023/09/01/ikea-maze-like-store-layout-shopping).
  ⚠ The article doesn't say exactly what IKEA changed afterwards; the script only says shoppers wanted to be guided.
- 729 million visits to IKEA retail locations in FY24 (Sep 2023–Aug 2024) — Ingka Group Annual Summary FY24, p. 3
  ([PDF](https://www.ingka.com/static/ingkagroup_annualsummary_fy24.pdf)). Ingka is IKEA's largest franchisee (87.8% of IKEA
  retail sales), so the script says "IKEA's biggest operator".
- IKEA denied the layout is designed to baffle (Telegraph, 2011, via secondary reports) — noted for fairness; not in the script.

## Build

- `build/world.py` (the store) + `build/episode.py` (the whole episode, one camera, keyed to `audio/vo_timeline.json` × 60) →
  `out/frames` (2,776 frames, Blender EEVEE, ~0.85 s a frame); `build/caps.py` → `caps.jsx`; `build/MASTER.jsx` (60 fps
  EffectCraft: big words, name card, estimate line, quote card, counter, title, follow card); `build/sfxmix.py` → `sfx/mix.wav`.
- Rebuild: `blender -b --python build/episode.py -- $PWD/out/frames`, then `rm EX14.ecproj; build/run.sh MASTER`, render
  `effectcraft-cli render --project EX14.ecproj --comp MASTER --out export/EX14.mp4 --format h264 --fps 60 --audio on`.
- **Accent and captions (user, 2026-11-01):** the world renders in ochre and MASTER recolours it to green **#57D100** with
  Change to Color (`accent()` in lib.jsx: ochre #DC9830 as rendered → #57D100, hue/lightness/saturation transformed, hue
  tolerance 12, saturation 40, so the cream items and the greys are untouched); all accent type is #57D100 too. Captions follow
  the user's reference recording (`Screen Recording … 9.00.10 PM.mov`): each sentence builds as a one- or two-line block in
  sentence case (Inter Display Medium 46, warm white) on a rounded dark panel (#0E0E10 at 82%) that grows with the words;
  each word fades in and rises 10 px over 14 frames on its spoken frame; the panel's bottom sits at y 1430 (`capBlock()`,
  blocks from `build/caps.py`). The Penn name card moved to the top-left, the estimate line to mid-frame. Follow card: the Meikigo logo
  (`layers/logo.png`, from `02_kit/brand`) above "Follow Meikigo for more." (Inter Display Bold 66), no sound effects.
- Export: `export/EX14.mp4`, 46.3 s, 1080×1920, 60 fps, −19.2 LUFS. Checked: per-frame difference of the world render
  (no spikes; one gentle 0.5 s hold at the overview under the name card), full-size frames on the cards.
