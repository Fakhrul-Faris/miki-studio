# Brand Profiles: Decision Systems in Directive Voice

These profiles are written entirely in Directive Voice. Every line is a decision, not a description. Every line is actionable by a filmmaker or an AI video generator.

---

## NIKE: Shoot the Start, Not the Win

### The Brief
Shoot the start, not the win. Document effort and resilience, not triumph. The story is the decision to begin, not the achievement of victory.

### Camera
Handheld. Not shaky — unsteady. The operator is running too, breathing too. The camera reacts to the moment. Whip pan on the turn. Snap zoom when she stumbles and again when she gets up. Steadicam only for the one shot that earns it (the moment of choice, of commitment). Most of the sequence is operator-present handheld. The camera does not float or drift. It pursues.

### Lens
Long for crowd shots (compress the environment, isolate the subject from context). Wide for isolation (the subject alone fills the frame, the environment is secondary). Never a 50mm — the 50 is a corporate, balanced, middle-ground lens. Reach for 35 when the subject is alone. Reach for 70–85 when the subject is in a crowd.

### Light
Hard. Available. Midday is allowed. Fluorescents are allowed. No fill. Faces are half-black. The light is the world's light, not the camera's light. If the sun is harsh, good. If the gym is fluorescent, good. The light is not soft. The light is not designed. The light is what the location gives.

### Art Dept
Nothing new. Everything owned, used, repaired. Functional clutter only. If there is a water bottle, it is scratched, used, carried. If there is a bench, it is worn, not pristine. Shoes are broken in. The environment shows that someone chose to be here because they had to, not because it was comfortable.

### Wardrobe
Worn and worked. Sweat is a costume element. The fabric shows use. Seams are visible. The fit is tight or loose as the person's body dictates, not as fashion dictates. Hair is not styled; it is functional (tied back, short, out of the way). Makeup is not present. Hands show work — calluses, tape from previous training, or the marks of labor.

### Edit
Cut on movement, cut early. Jump cuts are intentional. Breathless pacing, then one hold — a moment of rest that earns its weight by contrast. The sequence is not smooth. Rhythm is irregular; it mirrors the reality of training (sprint, recover, sprint). Transitions are cut or dissolve, not fade. Fade is rest; you have earned one fade per sequence at most.

### Grade
Crushed blacks. One accent color maximum — volt, crimson, or royal — no pastels. Grain is visible. Saturation is moderate, not oversaturated. Skin is protected (not blown out or desaturated). The image reads as documentary, not designed. No bleach bypass. No extreme color grading. The look is "athlete documenting their own training," not "commercial about athletes."

### Type
Type is a character. Kinetic, bold, custom if possible. Enters and exits on beats, not smoothly. The type is not centered; it is placed to own the frame or interrupt it. Type appears on beat, not fading in. Type does not soften or dissolve; it cuts.

### Call Sheet Block

Handheld, unsteady. Long for crowd, wide for isolation. Hard available light, no fill, faces half-black. Worn wardrobe, owned props, sweat visible. Cut on movement, cut early. Crushed blacks, one accent color maximum (volt, crimson, or royal). Grain. Type is a character. Kinetic, bold, custom. Enters and exits on beats. Nothing polished.

### Refusals

No beauty slow-motion. No product-on-white. No victory pose. No soft light. No smooth corporate camera. No clean surfaces. No aspirational imagery. No metrics or leaderboards (the story is not the time, it is the decision). No sentimental slow-motion. No "inspiring music" cliché. No slow-mo at the moment of achievement; if we linger, we linger on the preparation, not the outcome. No soft-focus transitions between moments. No symmetrical composition.

### Quality Gate

- [ ] Camera is handheld, not smooth or corporate.
- [ ] Operator presence is felt (not shaky, but unsteady).
- [ ] Light is hard and available, not designed.
- [ ] Fill policy is explicit: no fill, faces half-black.
- [ ] Wardrobe shows wear and function.
- [ ] Props are used and owned, not new.
- [ ] Edit is cut-early, with jump cuts intentional.
- [ ] One accent color maximum, no pastels.
- [ ] Grain is visible.
- [ ] Type is kinetic, not soft, enters on beat.
- [ ] No beauty or sentimental moments; the work is the story.
- [ ] Video reads as athlete documentation, not commercial design.

## ONCLOUD: Shoot the Feeling of Fast

### The Brief
Shoot the feeling of fast. Not a time, not a metric. The feeling of precision, control, and breath becoming rhythm. The story is the interior state of flow.

### Camera
Stabilized but alive. Camera rises and falls with the stride. Camera breathes with the breathing. Never floats, never jolts. It is a companion to the runner, not an observer. The camera is Steadicam or gimbal-stabilized. The movement is smooth because the human body's movement is smooth when in flow. The camera is intimate, close, riding alongside.

### Lens
Long, wide open, shallow depth of field. Compress the world. Let it blur. Feet, hands, jaw, breath — these are close and sharp. The environment is soft, barely relevant. The focal length is 70–100mm (on full frame equivalent). The aperture is wide (T1.3 or wider). The depth of field is so shallow that only the runner's immediate form is sharp. The street ahead is soft and irrelevant.

### Light
Soft. Overcast is a gift. Golden hour is perfect if it comes. Never hard midday light. Never fluorescent gym light (if indoors, choose a space with soft, diffused natural light). Bounce only, on the far side of the frame if any. The light is almost imperceptibly soft. The light is clean, without shadow. The light is cool or neutral, never warm harshness.

### Art Dept
Clean. Swiss. Fewer objects than feels natural. Restraint is the design. If there are props, they are minimal, functional, precise (water bottle, watch, one backpack). If the location is a street, it is not cluttered. If the location is indoors, it is spare and clean. The design says: every element here serves the runner's focus. Nothing extraneous. Nothing aspirational. Just precision.

### Wardrobe
Technical, fitted, unremarkable. The fabric is performance fabric: breathable, moisture-wicking, light. The fit is precise, not loose or oversized. Colors are technical neutrals or one muted accent. Hair is tied back, functional. Skin texture is normal, not retouched. The wardrobe is invisible; it is equipment, not fashion. Surreal only if fully committed (e.g., running in formal dress, which is then explained by context, not absurdism).

### Edit
Slower than instinct. Hold past comfort. Wait. The rhythm is not breathless. The rhythm is breath itself — the inhale-exhale of running becomes the edit rhythm. Sound carries the cut — breath, footfall, wind. Silence is a beat. Transitions are held, not quick. One moment lasts long enough to feel the air changing, the body adjusting. This is not documentary pace; it is meditative pace.

### Grade
Cool neutrals, one soft warm accent (very muted, almost not there). Low saturation overall. Gentle grain (like Fujifilm Superia 400, not aggressive). Image protection: protect skin tone, protect the sky, protect the details. No HDR. No crushed blacks. No bold color. The palette is almost monochromatic. When color appears (one muted accent, perhaps the brand), it is felt, not seen.

### Type
Lowercase. Generous spacing. Whispers; does not compete. Type fades in and out, does not cut. Type is integrated into the image, not overlaid. Type appears only when essential (brand, functional text). Type does not call attention to itself.

### Call Sheet Block

Stabilized, breathing with the stride. Long, wide open, shallow depth of field. Soft light only, overcast or bounced. Clean Swiss design, minimal props, restraint. Slow edit, sound-led, breath as rhythm. Cool neutrals, low saturation, gentle grain. Type lowercase, generous, whispers. Skin and sky protected.

### Refusals

No split times. No metrics or leaderboards. No motivational slogans (the feeling is enough). No grit or suffering imagery (no grimace, no struggle). No oversaturation. No hard light. No busy, cluttered frame. No fast, frenetic edit (edit is meditative, not caffeine-fueled). No warm, "energetic" color grading (cool and precise). No motivational music building to climax; sound serves breath, not emotion. No slow-motion beauty; the beauty is in precision and flow, not in the physics of the body. No celebrity or model presence; the story is the feeling, not the person.

### Quality Gate

- [ ] Camera is stabilized and moves with breath, not floats or jolts.
- [ ] Lens is long and wide open, shallow depth of field.
- [ ] Light is soft, overcast or bounced, never hard.
- [ ] Props are minimal, functional, precise.
- [ ] Wardrobe is technical, fitted, unremarkable.
- [ ] Edit is slow, sound-led, breath as rhythm.
- [ ] Palette is cool neutrals, low saturation, one muted accent.
- [ ] Grain is gentle and present.
- [ ] Skin and sky are protected (not blown out or oversaturated).
- [ ] Type is lowercase, whispered, minimal.
- [ ] No metrics, no grit, no suffering imagery.
- [ ] Video reads as meditation on precision, not athletic achievement.

## GRAB: The Joke Has to Land Before the Brand Does

### The Brief
The joke has to land before the brand does. Comedy is the vehicle. Warmth and specificity are non-negotiable. The story is the everyday hero moment, the practical solution, the laugh.

### Camera
Warm, observational, handheld. The camera reacts — it laughs slightly. Small instability on the punchline. The camera is a friend in the room, not a professional observer. The camera moves because life moves, not because the camera is trying to be cinematic. The camera is present and warm. Handheld does not mean shaky; it means human-present and reactive.

### Lens
35 and 50mm. Normal lenses for normal life. These are the lenses of human vision. Do not stylize the street with ultra-wide or ultra-long. The street is the street. The apartment is the apartment. What you see is what the street gives.

### Light
Whatever the street gives. Practicals inside — hawker fluorescents, shop signs, kitchen bulbs, neon. Golden hour if it's there. Hard light if it's midday. Soft light if it's overcast. The light is not controlled. The light is the location's natural condition. The light is honest.

### Art Dept
Nothing aspirational. Mismatched plates. Chairs replaced one at a time. Wear on every surface. Specificity is the authenticity. The locations are real Southeast Asian spaces: a hawker center, a family kitchen, a shared apartment, a shop. The design is not designed. The design is lived. One detail that is uniquely this place, this moment.

### Wardrobe
Everyday. Office shirts, casual, festive attire when the calendar calls. Not styled. Not a lookbook. The character wears what they would wear to work, to a family dinner, to the market. Hair is not styled. Makeup is not present (or present as the character applies it). Skin is not retouched. Age and wear show. The character is real.

### Edit
Comedy timing. Hold the reaction, cut the setup. The pause is the joke. Fourth wall is permitted when it serves the laugh. Cut on the beat, not on the movement. Give the reaction time to land. The edit is not frenetic; it is precise. It waits for the laugh.

### Grade
Warm. Saturated, not glossy. Protected skin (not desaturated, not blown out). Grab green as anchor, not wash (if Grab branding is present, it is a color note, not a filter). Grain is present, moderate. The image reads as warm documentary, as captured life, not as designed commercial. The palette has warmth and saturation because Southeast Asia has warmth and saturation. The grade is not cool or moody.

### Type
Bold, friendly, bilingual or multilingual. The type says the joke out loud. Type is not subtle. Type is part of the comedy. Type cuts on beat. If the language is Malay, Chinese, Tamil, English, or a mixture, the type reflects that. Type is celebratory of language diversity.

### Call Sheet Block

Handheld, warm, observational, reacts to the scene. 35 and 50mm lenses, unstylized. Practical and available light, golden hour if present. Mismatched, lived-in design, real wear. Comedy edit — hold reaction, cut setup. Warm grade, protected skin, Grab green as anchor only. Bold bilingual type. Nothing aspirational. Warmth is earned.

### Refusals

No slick tech-company gloss. No Western minimalism. No generic Asian city that could be anywhere (shoot the specific place, the specific hawker center, the specific family). No aspirational interiors or fashion. No soppy sentiment without a joke to balance it (warmth ≠ sentimentality). No green wash (if Grab branding is present, it is a color note, not a moral statement). No influencer or model presence (cast real people, real families, real communities). No "inspiring" music; let the dialogue and the laugh carry it. No slow-motion. No soft-focus transitions. No cool color grading (warmth is the brand).

### Quality Gate

- [ ] Camera is handheld, warm, observational.
- [ ] Lens is 35–50mm, unstylized.
- [ ] Light is available and practical, honest to location.
- [ ] Locations are real Southeast Asian spaces with specific wear and history.
- [ ] Wardrobe is everyday, not styled.
- [ ] Characters are real people, diverse in age and background.
- [ ] Edit honors comedy timing (hold reaction, cut setup).
- [ ] Palette is warm, saturated, not glossy.
- [ ] Skin is protected and real.
- [ ] Grab green is a color note, not a filter.
- [ ] Type is bold, bilingual, celebratory.
- [ ] Video reads as warm, specific, funny, authentic.
- [ ] No aspirational imagery. No generic "Asia." No Western minimalism.
