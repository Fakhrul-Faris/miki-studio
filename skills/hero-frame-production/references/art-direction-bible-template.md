# Art Direction Bible Template

## How to Use This Template

Fill out one page (500–700 words) for every production. Write in Directive Voice. Do not skip fields. If a field doesn't apply, state why in one sentence and move on.

**Total time to write: 30 minutes.**

---

## Template

### PRODUCTION: [Title]

### ERA, PLACE, TIME, WEATHER

[What is the historical decade or range? What is the geography? What is the social/economic context? What is the current season or weather? How old is this space? What is its primary use? One refusal: what era/place is it NOT?]

**Example:**
"1995. Coastal Portugal, fishing village in Algarve. Working-class, subsistence fishing. Early evening in late autumn. The kitchen is from a 1960s build, additions from the 1980s and 1990s. Used daily for fifty years. Not aspirational. Not a restaurant. Not a vacation rental aesthetic."

### STORY & CLASS

[What is the emotional or narrative context? Who lives or works here and what is their relationship to this space? What does the space say about them? What did they choose and what did they inherit?]

**Example:**
"Rosa has lived here her whole life. Her husband fished from the harbor. The kitchen is where she processes the daily catch and where family eats. Nothing is chosen for beauty; everything is chosen for durability or inherited by necessity. The space speaks of labor and continuity, not comfort."

### PALETTE

[Dominant color (by area). Secondary color. Accent color. All with hex values or reference. What does NOT get saturated? What is muted? Write as directive.]

**Example:**
"Dominant: warm beige linoleum (#D4C5A0). Secondary: pale weathered blue, window frame (#8B9FB8), heavily faded. Accent: rust from hardware and oxidation (#A0533E). Protect skin tones. No saturation boost on the rust; keep it dull. Cool grays in shadow. No bright primaries. No whites (everything is aged)."

### PRODUCTION DESIGN

[Materials, props, wear, clutter, damage, repair, history. What is on every surface? What is broken and how long? What is mismatched? One refusal: what design is NOT here?]

**Example:**
"Linoleum worn to white at high-traffic zones. Tile countertop, yellowed, one cracked corner. Dishes: enamel from the 1950s, plastic from the 1980s, one chipped restaurant plate from a business that closed. One shelf holds water bottles, rope, a broken radio from 1985, a jar of salt. Calendar from 2019 nailed by the door, never updated. A wooden chair with a rush seat, replaced by the occupant, tied with twine. Walls: peeling paint, one patch of wallpaper from the 1970s still adhering. Not new. Not clean. Not a museum piece. Not aspirational."

### WARDROBE, HAIR, MAKEUP

[Fabric, fit, condition. Skin: pores, asymmetry, scars, age, texture. Hair: style, texture, condition. Makeup: none, minimal, or specific reason. Write as directive.]

**Example:**
"Rosa: wool sweater, charcoal, hand-knit, darned at the elbow twice with visible stitching, loose cuffs. Long-sleeved. Worn for ten years. No makeup. Hair: gray, pulled back in a knot with a rubber band, not careful, hairs loose. Face: pores visible, asymmetrical, forehead lined, age showing. Hands: scarred from saltwater and bone knife work. Forearms rope-like. Skin tone: olive, lined, with age spots on the back of the hands."

### LIGHTING & ATMOSPHERE

[Motivated sources (practical or off-frame). Direction (hard/soft, warm/cool, ratio, fill policy). Atmosphere (haze, smoke, dust, rain, steam, condensation, or deliberate absence).]

**Example:**
"One practical source: the window, facing the harbor. Midday sun, hard light, one direction. Color temperature: daylight (5500K). Contrast ratio: 6:1 (bright areas are six times brighter than shadow). Fill policy: no fill. Shadows go dark. Her face is half-lit, half-black. No haze. The air is clear. No smoke. No mist. The dirt on the window is visible; light comes through it anyway."

### CAMERA, LENS, FILM

[Body, format, lens, aperture, grain, grade logic, post-processing. Write as directive.]

**Example:**
"ARRI Alexa. 35mm anamorphic. Lens: Cooke S4 35mm. Aperture: wide open (T1.3). Grain: visible, like Kodak Portra 400, moderate. Grade: Kodak Portra logic. Crush the blacks. Protect skin tones from desaturation. Cool neutrals overall, one soft warm accent (the rust). No HDR. No saturation boost. The image reads as film stock from the 1980s–1990s, not a 2026 render."

### MOOD (NOT EMOTION, ACTION)

[What does the space feel like? But phrase this as visual/physical facts, not feelings. Reference a film or photo if needed.]

**Example:**
"The mood is endurance. Not sadness, not nostalgia. The physical fact is: a space that has absorbed sixty years of work without complaint. The light is honest. The wear is evidence, not patina. Reference: Chema Madoz's still-life photography; Agnes Martin's architectural studies; the 16mm color film of Portuguese fishermen from the 1970s (e.g., 'Brandos Costumes' by João César Monteiro)."

### REFUSALS

[What is explicitly NOT happening in this space? Write as orders, not suggestions.]

**Example:**
"Not a love story. Not sentimental. Not slow-motion beauty. Not a meditation on loss. Not soft focus. Not an advertisement. Not cleaned. Not lit softly. Not a restaurant. Not aspirational. Not photography. Her age shows. Her hands show use. The work is the emotion."

## Worked Example: Portuguese Fisherman's Kitchen

**PRODUCTION:** Rosa's Kitchen, Algarve

**ERA, PLACE, TIME, WEATHER**

1995. Coastal Portugal, fishing village in Algarve. Working-class, subsistence fishing. Early evening in late autumn. The kitchen is from a 1960s build, additions from the 1980s and 1990s. Used daily for fifty years. Not aspirational. Not a restaurant. Not a vacation rental aesthetic.

**STORY & CLASS**

Rosa has lived here her whole life. Her husband fished from the harbor; he is deceased. The kitchen is where she processes the daily catch and where family eats. Nothing is chosen for beauty; everything is chosen for durability or inherited by necessity. She is not wealthy. She is not poor. She is a survivor. The space speaks of labor and continuity, not comfort or design.

**PALETTE**

Dominant: warm beige linoleum (#D4C5A0). Secondary: pale weathered blue, window frame (#8B9FB8), heavily faded, almost gray. Accent: rust from hardware and oxidation (#A0533E), muted, dull. Protect skin tones. No saturation boost on anything. Cool grays in shadow. No bright primaries. No whites; everything aged to tan and gray.

**PRODUCTION DESIGN**

Linoleum worn to white at high-traffic zones (sink, doorway). Tile countertop, yellowed, one cracked corner. Sink: white porcelain, stained. Dishes: enamel from the 1950s (yellow, blue, white), plastic from the 1980s (beige), one chipped restaurant plate from a business that closed, blue rim barely visible. One shelf holds water bottles, rope, a broken radio from 1985, a jar of salt, a tin of tea. Calendar from 2019 nailed by the door, never updated past January. A wooden chair with a rush seat, replaced by hand by Rosa, tied with twine at the back. Walls: peeling paint (pale yellow), one patch of wallpaper from the 1970s (small floral, faded) still adhering at the corner. The window frame is painted blue, the paint cracking. Not new. Not clean. Not a museum piece. Not aspirational.

**WARDROBE, HAIR, MAKEUP**

Rosa: wool sweater, charcoal, hand-knit or very old, darned at the elbow twice with visible darker stitching, cuffs loose from wear. Long-sleeved. Worn for at least ten years. No makeup. Hair: gray, pulled back in a knot with a rubber band, not careful, hairs loose at the temples. Face: pores visible, asymmetrical (stronger on one side), forehead lined, age showing, no botox or smoothing. Hands: scarred from saltwater and bone knife work, forearms rope-like from labor. Skin tone: olive, lined, with age spots on the back of the hands. Stubble shadow on the jaw if male; if female, the jaw is strong and lined.

**LIGHTING & ATMOSPHERE**

One practical source: the window, facing the harbor. Midday sun, hard light, one direction. Color temperature: daylight (5500K). Contrast ratio: 6:1 (bright areas are six times brighter than shadow). Fill policy: no fill. Shadows go dark. The far side of the room is dim. Her face is half-lit, half-black, depending on angle. No haze. The air is clear. No smoke. No mist. The dirt on the window is visible; light comes through it anyway. The light is honest and harsh.

**CAMERA, LENS, FILM**

ARRI Alexa. 35mm anamorphic. Lens: Cooke S4 35mm. Aperture: wide open (T1.3). Grain: visible, like Kodak Portra 400, moderate, not heavy. Grade: Kodak Portra logic. Crush the blacks. Protect skin tones from desaturation. Cool neutrals overall, one soft warm accent (the rust, muted). No HDR. No saturation boost. The image reads as color film stock from the 1980s–1990s, not a 2026 render. The anamorphic character is visible: slight flare on the window highlights, warm glow on the frame edges.

**MOOD**

The mood is endurance. Not sadness. Not nostalgia. The physical fact is: a space that has absorbed sixty years of work without complaint. The light is honest. The wear is evidence, not patina. The kitchen is beautiful because it is true, not because it is designed. Reference: Chema Madoz's still-life photography (quiet, composed, worn objects); Agnes Martin's architectural studies (geometry in decay); the 16mm color film of Portuguese fishermen from the 1970s.

**REFUSALS**

Not a love story. Not sentimental. Not slow-motion beauty. Not a meditation on loss. Not soft focus. Not an advertisement. Not cleaned. Not lit softly. Not a restaurant. Not aspirational. Not photography. Her age shows. Her hands show use. The work is the emotion.
