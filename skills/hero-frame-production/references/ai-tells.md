# AI Tells: Visual Signatures of AI Video

These are the visual signatures that mark a frame as generated by an AI model rather than captured on film or shot with a camera. If you see any of these in your generated still, iterate. Do not animate.

## Skin Texture

**The Tell:** Plastic poreless skin. Oversmoothness. Symmetrical face. Skin tone perfectly even with no freckles, moles, age spots, or texture variation.

**The Fix:**
- Add pores (especially on the nose, forehead, cheeks).
- Add asymmetry (one side of the face slightly different from the other).
- Add texture: freckles, age spots, scars, stubble shadow, sweat.
- Skin should look lived-in, not retouched.
- Reference real people at the age/condition you want, not beauty photography.

**How to Prompt:**
"Pores visible. Face asymmetrical. Age spots on hands. Stubble shadow. Sweat on forehead. Not retouched."

## Composition & Depth

**The Tell:** Everything in sharp focus. Foreground, midground, background all equally sharp. The image reads as a flat photograph, not a space with depth.

**The Fix:**
- Add shallow depth of field (background out of focus).
- Add layers (foreground object, character, background object).
- Don't center everything. Offset the subject.
- Let things go out of frame at the edges.

**How to Prompt:**
"Wide. Shallow depth of field. Background soft. Foreground sharp. Depth of frame: window (out of focus) → character → counter (in focus)."

## Lighting

**The Tell:** Flat, unmotivated lighting. Everything evenly lit. No shadows. No direction. No sense of light source. Or over-dramatic "cinematic" lighting that reads as set-design rather than real light.

**The Fix:**
- One primary light source (motivated, real, named).
- Shadows are dark and directional.
- Fill policy: are shadows filled or do they go black? State it.
- Light has color temperature (warm, cool, mixed).
- Light has quality (hard, soft, specular, diffused).
- Avoid "volumetric lighting" and "god rays" unless earned by story (sunlight through a dusty room, smoke).

**How to Prompt:**
"One practical: the window. Hard light. Midday. No fill. Her face is half-lit, half-black. Contrast ratio 6:1."

## Color & Saturation

**The Tell:** Oversaturated HDR. Every color vibrant. No desaturated or muted tones. The palette reads as too clean or too bright. Skin tones desaturated (a common AI move: increase saturation on everything except skin, making it look painted).

**The Fix:**
- Specify a narrow palette (3–5 colors, not full rainbow).
- Protect specific colors from saturation (especially skin, shadows, highlights).
- Use reference film stocks (Kodak, Fujifilm) for palette logic.
- Crush blacks (darken the shadows, lose detail in darkness).
- Mute one color family if it feels too vibrant.

**How to Prompt:**
"Kodak Portra 400 logic. Crush the blacks. Protect skin. Cool neutrals, one warm accent (rust, muted). No saturation boost."

## Sharpness

**The Tell:** Uniform sharpness across the entire frame. Everything equally detailed. No selective focus. Everything looks "printed" rather than "seen."

**The Fix:**
- Add motion blur (character moving, camera moving).
- Add shallow depth of field (background out of focus).
- Let distant objects be less sharp than close objects (natural parallax).
- Vignette (edges slightly darker/softer) mimics lens characteristics.

**How to Prompt:**
"Shallow depth of field. Background soft, blurred. Close objects sharp. Vignette at frame edges."

## Environments & Props

**The Tell:** Empty, generic environments. "A modern apartment." "A sci-fi corridor." No props, no story, no evidence of use. Everything looks new. Or conversely, generic "cozy" or "rustic" environments that could be anywhere.

**The Fix:**
- Add specific props with history (not new, used, repaired, mismatched).
- Add clutter and function.
- Make the space look like someone actually lives here.
- Add wear: scratches, dents, stains, cracks, fading.
- One detail that is unique to this specific location.

**How to Prompt:**
"Linoleum worn to white at high-traffic zones. Dishes don't match: enamel from 1950s, plastic from 1980s. One cracked restaurant plate. Calendar from 2019 nailed by the door. Wooden chair with hand-replaced rush seat, tied with twine."

## Character & Wardrobe

**The Tell:** Generic attractive person in "cinematic" wardrobe. Clothes look new, perfectly fitted, styled. No evidence of the character's life in their appearance. Beauty photography logic, not character logic.

**The Fix:**
- Age and condition are more important than beauty.
- Wardrobe should show wear, fit loosely or tightly as the character dictates.
- Hair is not styled; it is lived.
- Hands and skin show work, not softness.
- One detail that says "I am not a model."

**How to Prompt:**
"ROSA. Seventies. No makeup. Hair gray, pulled back badly, rubber band, loose hairs. Wool sweater, charcoal, hand-knit, darned at the elbow, cuffs loose. Hands scarred from salt water and bone knife work."

## Motion & Camera Behavior

**The Tell:** Aimless drift. The camera moves but has no intention. Or the scene morphs (characters change, objects shift, composition breaks) during the move.

**The Fix:**
- One named move per shot (push in, dolly out, orbit, track, crane, whip pan).
- The move has a start and an end position (not just "floats around").
- The move is motivated by story or emotion.
- The composition should remain stable (no morphing or shifting).
- Camera moves should preserve parallax and depth (actual motion, not zoom).

**How to Prompt:**
"Push in. Slow. Start wide, she fills frame right. Stop when we see her eyes. Parallax intact."

## Consistency & Coherence

**The Tell:** Shot-to-shot inconsistency. Character's position, size, or appearance changes between keyframes. Lighting direction changes without motivation. Objects appear or disappear. The world doesn't feel cohesive.

**The Fix:**
- Run consistency checks across all keyframes before animation.
- Character position, scale, and appearance should match.
- Lighting direction should be consistent (unless motivated by time passage or scene change).
- Props should remain where they were placed.
- Camera moves should trace logical paths through the space.

**How to Check:**
Generate all hero keyframes. Compare them side-by-side. Do they look like they're in the same space, at the same time, with the same character? If not, iterate each one.

## Language Tells

**The Tell:** Using "cinematic," "dramatic," "moody," "masterpiece," "award-winning," "trending," or any other vague intensity modifier.

**The Fix:**
- Delete these words. They are not decisions; they are wishes.
- Replace with specific choices: "hard light," "crush blacks," "Kodak Portra logic," "one practical."
- If you can't explain why a choice matters, don't include it.

## Checklist: Does This Frame Look AI?

- [ ] Skin is plastic/poreless/symmetrical? Iterate.
- [ ] Lighting is flat or unmotivated? Iterate.
- [ ] Colors are oversaturated or too clean? Iterate.
- [ ] Everything is in sharp focus? Iterate.
- [ ] Environment is generic or empty? Iterate.
- [ ] Character looks styled or new? Iterate.
- [ ] Camera move is aimless or drifty? Iterate.
- [ ] Frame is inconsistent with other keyframes? Iterate.
- [ ] Language is vague ("cinematic," "moody")? Rewrite the brief.

**If you check any box, do not proceed to animation. The still is not ready.**
