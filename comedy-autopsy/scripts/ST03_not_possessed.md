# ST03 — He’s Not Possessed
**Pattern:** P06 False diagnosis · inspired by `vid_046`  
**Format:** 9:16 · ~45s · Grease Pencil  
**Test cast:** Miki (accused) + Roommate Jay + Friend Sam  
**Status:** keep — board taste-pass KEEP (meeting beat parked on board; optional restore)

## Premise
Miki drinks water instead of energy drink once. Jay diagnoses possession. Every normal explanation becomes proof.

## Button (written first)
Jay sprinkles **instant ramen seasoning** as “holy salt.” Miki sneezes. Sam: “See? The demon left.” Miki: “That was pepper.” Jay: “That’s what a demon would say.”

---

## Script

**LOC:** Living room. Miki on couch with a glass of water. Jay and Sam stand over him like a tribunal.

**ON-SCREEN HOOK:** “He drank water”

**JAY**  
Don’t come closer. He’s different.

**MIKI**  
I drank water.

**JAY**  
Exactly.  
(to Sam)  
Yesterday: three energy drinks. Today: *hydration.* Name one innocent reason.

**MIKI**  
I was thirsty?

**SAM**  
(gasps)  
Thirst… for souls.

**MIKI**  
For water.

**JAY**  
Demons hate caffeine. Everyone knows that.

**MIKI**  
I have a headache.

**SAM**  
Withdrawal from the mortal realm.

**MIKI**  
From the energy drinks.

**JAY**  
Why are you speaking so calmly?

**MIKI**  
Because you’re being weird.

**VISUAL:** Jay nods like that settled it. Produces phone flashlight. Shines in Miki’s eyes.

**JAY**  
Pupils normal. Too normal.  
Sam, the kit.

**VISUAL:** Sam dumps a grocery bag: garlic bread, USB cable, instant ramen packets.

**MIKI**  
That’s dinner.

**JAY**  
Ritual components.

**MIKI**  
I’m going to work early tomorrow—

**SAM**  
Leaving before sunrise. Classic.

**MIKI**  
—because of a meeting.

**JAY**  
Meetings are a gateway demon.

**VISUAL:** They loosely “restrain” Miki with a phone charger cable. It’s useless. He could leave. He doesn’t, out of spite.

**JAY**  
Holy salt.

**VISUAL:** Jay opens ramen seasoning. Sprinkles it on Miki’s head like a chef.

**MIKI**  
(sneezes)

**SAM**  
It left!

**MIKI**  
That was pepper.

**JAY**  
(beat, soft, certain)  
That’s what a demon would say.

**BUTTON — VISUAL:** Jay gently takes the water glass away. Replaces it with an energy drink. Pats Miki’s shoulder.

**JAY**  
You’re safe now.

**MIKI**  
(to camera, seasoning in hair)  
I preferred the demon.

**END.**
