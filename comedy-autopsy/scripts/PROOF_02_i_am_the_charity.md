# PROOF_02 — I Am the Charity
**Method:** punchline-first · English · international  
**Knife stolen from:** `vid_068` (NOT a remake)  
**Format:** 9:16 · ~15s · Grease Pencil  
**Status:** KEEP

## The knife (only this)
Soft ask: donate the change. Customer says yes. Thank-yous land. Then: **the asker is the charity.**

## Button (written first)
**MIKI**  
Thanks.  
*(beat, soft)*  
I’m from the shelter.

**VISUAL:** Customer holding drink. Silence. Cut on their face — not on Miki explaining.

---

## Script

**LOC:** Cafe counter. Drink handoff. Change math on screen: total $8 · paid $50 · change **$42**.

**MIKI**  
Here’s your drink.  
Change is forty-two. Want that to go to the shelter?

**CUSTOMER**  
Oh—yeah. Sure.

**MIKI**  
Thanks. Really.

**CUSTOMER**  
Of course.

**VISUAL:** Tiny beat of “good deed complete.” Customer turns half-away.

**MIKI**  
*(same polite tone)*  
Also—I’m from the shelter.

**BUTTON — VISUAL:** Hold customer’s smile dying. Miki does not wink. Cut.

**END.**

---

## Why this isn’t karaoke
- English money ($42) so the “generous yes” hurts internationally.
- No orphanage jargon, no “panti” — **shelter** is enough.
- Punch is identity after thanks — three lines of setup max.

## Cold-read test
If “I’m from the shelter” feels sad instead of funny, add one cash gesture (Miki pockets the $42 while smiling). If still not funny — kill.
