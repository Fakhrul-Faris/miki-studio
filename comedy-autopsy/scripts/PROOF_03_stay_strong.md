## The knife (only this)

## Why this isn’t a remake
- Notifications do setup (English UI). Dialogue only plays the wrong generosity.
- Punch is **misaimed care**, not “he got dumped.”
- No Malay. No “semangat terus” — **Stay strong** is the international twin of the knife.

If you explain the toasts in dialogue, you killed it. If the smile after “Stay strong” doesn’t hurt — kill.
