# ST01 — Remember Me
**Pattern:** P01 Tool hell (inspired by `vid_001` / `vid_009`)  
**Format:** 9:16 · ~35s · Grease Pencil  
**Test cast:** Miki + Password App  
**Status:** draft — kill / keep / rewrite: ___

## Premise
Miki checks “Remember me” on a login. The password app remembers *everything except* the password.

## Button (written first)
VISUAL: App unlocks by recognizing Miki’s **frustrated scream spectrogram** as the new password — then forces a “security upgrade.”

---

## Script

**LOC:** Bedroom desk. Night. Laptop + floating Password App (logo-head, clipboard).

**ON-SCREEN HOOK:** “When you check Remember me”

**MIKI**  
(typing)  
Okay. Email… password… and— Remember me.

**APP**  
(cheerful)  
Remembered!

**MIKI**  
Cool.  
(closes laptop, reopens)  
…Password?

**APP**  
I remembered you! You’re Miki. You like oat milk. You cried during a toothpaste ad in 2019.

**MIKI**  
The *password*.

**APP**  
Ohhh. I don’t store those. For your safety.

**MIKI**  
Then what did “Remember me” remember?

**APP**  
Your shame.  
Also your backup email, which is the same email.  
Also that you clicked Remember me. Twice.

**MIKI**  
Just let me in.

**APP**  
Sure! Security question: What was your first pet’s name?

**MIKI**  
(beat)  
You *are* my first pet.

**APP**  
Incorrect. Hint: it starts with—

**VISUAL:** App displays a progress bar: “Remembering harder…” Photos of Miki’s face cycle: blank, tired, angry.

**APP**  
New method unlocked: Voice ID.

**MIKI**  
Fine.  
(normal)  
It’s me.

**APP**  
Not confident enough.

**MIKI**  
(louder)  
It’s me!

**APP**  
Still hearing hesitation.

**MIKI**  
(full scream)

**VISUAL:** Waveform spikes. Green check. Desktop unlocks.

**APP**  
Welcome back, Miki! We’ve set your password to that sound.  
Please don’t forget it.  
Or scream it in public.  
Or remember me.

**BUTTON — VISUAL:** Popup: **Security upgrade required.** Checkbox already checked: Remember me.

**MIKI**  
(deadpan, to camera)  
I remember.

**END.**
