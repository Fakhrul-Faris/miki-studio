# Vid 058 — Autopsy

## Premise
Labeled **BUKAN POV**: a sales boss gives impossible “do it now” orders; the employee over-complies so hard he physically collapses—“Jat boss!”

## Hook
Third-person office two-shot (explicitly *not* POV). Boss finger-points: “Make sure hari ini sayang kau tinggi, bapak” (ASR; on-screen sense ≈ *sales kau tinggi*). “Boleh?” Staff: “Saya buat sekarang lho boss.” Obedience is instant and dangerous.

## Setup
Boss piles visual chores: “Jalan gerai ini banyak sangat lah gambar. Kau tolong cuci betul-betul keliakan.” Staff: “Dah boss.” Then metric ask: “Aku tak faham lah masjid ini kau breakdown untuk aku boleh?” (ASR heavily garbled—likely *margin/metric/masa* + breakdown). Staff is already mid-sprint.

## Turn
Visual: employee hunched over tablet like life support—boss leans in. The joke is not clever dialogue; it’s **over-performance as body failure**. Camera chase / blur toward glass door sells panic compliance.

## Punchline
Fall / scramble: “Jat boss! Jat boss!” (*jatuh*). The “buat sekarang” promise becomes literal impact with the floor.

## Reaction / Button
No recovery speech—motion-blur heap on office carpet under **BUKAN POV**. The meta title lands: POV trends hide the undignified third-person truth.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Literalization of hustle** — “do it now” → body breaks.
- **Meta frame** — “BUKAN POV” flips TikTok POV comfort into spectator embarrassment.
- **Short runtime** — order → scrub/breakdown → fall; almost no dialogue needed after setup.
- Transcript is thin and ASR-noisy (`sayang`/`sales`, `masjid`/`metric`); trust frames + “Jat boss” as the solid punch.
