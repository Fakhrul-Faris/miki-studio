# Vid 021 — Autopsy

## Premise
A penitent returns to confession after ten years — but treats the sacrament like a deposition, applying **“not without my ___ present”** to every spiritual prompt.

## Hook
Crude doodle beside a confessional lattice photo; text: **“it's been 10 years.”** Penitent opens: *“Hello Father, it's been ten years since my last confession.”* Solemn frame (wooden screen, @DiemArchive / ROY watermark) sets religious tone the bit will vandalize.

## Setup
Priest (voice over lattice still): *“What is it you would like to confess my son?”* Penitent’s first refusal: *“Oh yeah, I don't answer questions without my lawyer present.”* Priest: *“Hello?”* — already one procedural beat behind.

## Turn
Rule-of-three escalation on the same template:
1. *“Son, you must pay for your sins.”* → *“Oh, I don't pay for anything without my financial advisor present.”* → *“Hello?”*
2. *“The answer to salvation lies within your heart.”* → *“Oh, my heart?”* → *“Yeah, I gotta have my doctor present too.”* → *“Hello?”*

Each priest line gets a more absurd professional gatekeeper; penitent stays polite and cooperative in tone while refusing everything.

## Punchline
Priest breaks character: *“Jesus Christ.”* — exasperation, not prayer. The holy name becomes a swear at bureaucracy.

## Reaction / Button
Penitent, oblivious: *“Hello?”* — same word the priest used, now answered as if someone new entered the booth. The button loops the misunderstanding: priest says Jesus Christ; penitent thinks it’s a greeting.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Rule of three** — lawyer → financial advisor → doctor; pattern clear by beat two, payoff on beat three.
- **Register clash** — sacred confession language vs. legal/financial/medical compliance speak.
- **“Hello?” callback** — priest’s confusion becomes penitent’s punchline; symmetrical and stupid.
- **Minimal visuals** — lattice photo + doodle + text captions; joke is entirely dialogue rhythm.
- Penitent never confesses anything; the refusal *is* the content.

## Remake notes
- Steal the machine: **solemn institution asks earnest question → penitent demands third-party rep → repeat until authority snaps → penitent misreads snap as hello.**
- Grease Pencil: static confessional BG; cut to doodle for penitent lines, lattice for priest; on-screen subtitle each line.
- Third gatekeeper should top the second (doctor after financial advisor lands because it literalizes “within your heart”).
- Priest’s *“Jesus Christ”* needs a beat of silence before penitent’s *“Hello?”*
- Swaps: therapy, job interview, airport security — any ritual Q&A where “I need my ___ present” destroys the frame.
