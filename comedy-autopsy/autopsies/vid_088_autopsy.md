# Vid 088 — Autopsy

## Premise
Caption comic about a **“*customer yang dermawan”** (generous customer) at an ojol/delivery door handoff. Spoken ASR is dead — the joke is a word ladder that ends in **KURANG** (not enough) after the tip is quantified as **20**.

## Hook
Closed brown door, silver handle. Top label: generous customer. Big **“BENTAR”** (wait) — anticipation before the rider’s face. Hook is withholding + ironic branding.

## Setup
Courier arrives: helmet, orange reflective jacket, tactical chest rig, fingerless gloves, food bag + phone. Caption **“KAN”** over a wide knowing grin — “see?” confirmation energy. Gig-work transaction is the stage for “generosity.”

## Turn
POV cash handoff: customer’s hand offers a blue note; courier’s gloved hand out — **“TANGAN.”** Reverse: courier holds note + phone, **“AMBIL”** (take) — tip/change/take-back ambiguity. Then self/address beat (**“AKU,”** / possible **BANG.**) Generosity becomes hand choreography + identity.

## Punchline
Push-in on the note with a giant **“20”** — the “dermawan” label collides with a tiny quantified tip. Soft **“UDAH”** (done/already) pretends the transaction is complete. Then motion-blur haul with **“KURANG”** — insufficiency is the kill shot; consequence is physical (dragged), not a speech.

## Reaction / Button
No usable spoken button. Button = **KURANG** + body smeared out of the doorway. Doorframe bookends: enter wait → exit hauled.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Caption comic strip** — dense 1fps recovers full ladder: BENTAR → KAN → TANGAN → AMBIL → AKU → **20** → UDAH → KURANG (richer than first pass).
- **Ironic label** — “generous” title vs tip = 20 vs “kurang.”
- **Number card is the turn inside the punch** — without **20**, KURANG is abstract; with it, math is visible.
- **Gig-economy prop kit** — orange jacket, phone, note, door = instant read.
- Fragile without captions; remake must keep the word ladder on screen.
- ASR note: treat speech as unrecoverable; do not invent farewell dialogue.
