# Vid 090 — Autopsy

## Premise
POV: **"Hp Customer"** at a phone service counter. Customer comes to **pick up a phone serviced yesterday**; staff answers with endless **"bentar"** stalling until the pickup itself is in doubt.

## Hook
Orange/black polo worker (“Ny…ervice Center”) behind glass counter, accessory pegboard, wall fan. Customer (hijab, striped shirt) at the glass. Overlay dialogue: **"BANG MAU AMBIL HP YANG KEMARIN DISERVIS / OH YANG KEMARIN."** Matches transcript open: **"Bang, mau ambil HP yang kemarin di service?"**

## Setup
Confirm beat: **"Mau yang kemarin dia?" / "Ya"** → **"Ya, bentar ya" / "Oke."** Promise of a short wait. Shop looks ready; no visible search yet—stall begins as politeness.

## Turn
Customer pressure: **"Mana bang?"** → **"Ya, bentar, bentar."** Overlay **"IYA BENTAR BENTAR."** Same location, same posture—time passes, object doesn’t appear. Service language replaces service.

## Punchline
**"Udah belum?"** then closing ASR **"Mau dia ambil sama orangnya?"**—garbled; likely “already taken by someone / want it picked up by them” territory. Do not invent a clean reveal line. Functionally the punch is: after all the bentar, possession of the HP is still unclear.

## Reaction / Button
End frame collapses to the shop **logo card** (Ny…ervice Center)—corporate smile after customer limbo. No phone handed over on camera. Button = brand sting instead of resolution.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **"Bentar" as workplace weapon** — delay packaged as courtesy.
- **Client vs. opaque backroom** — repair shops = black boxes (same as freelance “still rendering”).
- **POV customer framing** — viewer shares the wait anxiety.
- Weaker if remade with a big prop reveal; this one thrives on *non*-delivery.
- ASR note: final line uncertain—remake can choose “someone else already took it” or pure infinite wait; don’t claim transcript certainty.

- Button: smash cut to cheerful logo after the unanswered “udah belum?”
