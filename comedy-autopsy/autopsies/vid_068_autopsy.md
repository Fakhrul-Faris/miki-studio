# Vid 068 — Autopsy

## Premise
Cafe checkout soft-asks whether a large change refund should be **donated to an orphanage (panti)**—customer agrees—then the asker reveals **“saya dari panti.”**

## Hook
Industrial cafe counter: barista hands green drink—“ini kak.” Normal hospitality beat. Woman customer + guy waiting in line. Transaction total: Rp25.000; customer pays Rp100.000; change Rp75.000.

## Setup
Barista: “Ini mau didunasikan atau untuk kepanti?” (ASR; frames: donate the 75rb change / for the orphanage). Customer: “Ah iya boleh.” Polite social yes—charity with no friction, no receipt, no NGO logo.

## Turn
Thanks exchange—“Makasih ya kak.” / “Iya mas.” Donation feels complete as abstract goodness. Line guy still hovering; social pressure already spent.

## Punchline
**“Masih kak, saya dari Panti.”** (ASR “Masih”; sense = *Mas* / continue—speaker identifies as from the orphanage.) End frame subtitle: **“saya dari panti.”** The neutral cashier/collector was the beneficiary all along (or the waiting man claims the pot). Soft ask → self-dealing reveal.

## Reaction / Button
No long explanation. Button is the identity line after the thank-you—awkward air, drink still in hand. Cut on the reveal face.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Charity friction comedy** — 75rb is a lot of “spare change”; agreeing feels virtuous until the beneficiary is *this guy*.
- **Status inversion** — customer thought they funded a cause; they funded a person in the queue/behind the counter.
- **ASR note** — “didunasikan/kepanti/Masih” noisy; frames carry “panti” + “75ribu.” Don’t invent NGO names.
- Risk: if “dari panti” reads as tragic overshare instead of punch, add a tiny cash-gesture so the self-deal reads.
