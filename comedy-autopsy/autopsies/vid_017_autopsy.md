# Vid 017 — Autopsy

## Premise
Two wizards in a stone tower run a classic **“I CAST”** duel — except the spell’s target shrugs it off and the caster eats the consequences.

## Hook
Foreground wizard (purple star robe, seen from behind) faces a bearded blue wizard who raises a glowing hand. On-screen text: **“I CAST.”** Yellow energy builds — frame reveals the spell name **“PEANUT ALLERGY!!!”** as a huge aggressive cast. CircleToonsHD dungeon staging: barred window, wooden door, thick outlines.

## Setup
Blue wizard fires the blast — jagged yellow magic straight at the purple wizard. Full fantasy-combat read: big gesture, big SFX energy, confident attack posture. Status: caster is winning the exchange.

## Turn
Target’s only spoken line: *“I feel fine.”* Purple wizard (simple white face, star robe) stands in the yellow aura unharmed — neutral expression, particles floating. The attack **does nothing** to the intended victim. Status flip begins: confidence belongs to the wrong side.

## Punchline
Second **“I CAST”** beat — but the purple wizard **crumples**: gaping mouth, vertical pain eyes, collapsed into a puddle on the teal floor (006_end). The spell hurt the **caster**, not the target. On-screen **“AHAHA”** / laugh beat lands on the target’s reaction while the attacker is destroyed.

## Reaction / Button
Defeated purple blob on the ground with an exclamation mark; mirror and stone room unchanged. The button is physical comedy — the wizard who cast is the one splatted — while the guy who said “I feel fine” outlasts him.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Status inversion** — classic “you hurt yourself” spell trope, compressed to ~11s.
- **On-screen text drives timing** — “I CAST” bookends; the spell name (peanut allergy) is the absurd specificity.
- **Minimal dialogue** — one line (*“I feel fine”*) makes the unfazed target funnier than any comeback.
- **Visual escalation** — confident cast → blank target → caster meltdown; no exposition needed.
- CircleToonsHD contrast: detailed painterly background vs. simple dot-face wizard sells the mismatch.

## Remake notes
- Steal the machine: **confident attack → target unmoved → attacker is the casualty.**
- Grease Pencil: two wizard silhouettes, one big on-screen spell title per cast; hold target’s blank face through the blast VFX.
- Swap peanut allergy for any mundane modern ailment or HR violation cast as epic magic (“I CAST… **MEETING INVITE!!!**”).
- Keep dialogue to one deadpan target line; let collapse + puddle carry the punchline.
- End frame: caster flat on floor, target still standing — status readable without words.
