# Vid 097 — Autopsy

## Premise
Captioned split: **POV: orang lain vs aku kalo nawar harga**—other people grind a thrift-market bargain; “me” folds immediately and buys whatever.

## Hook
Crowded baju stall, woman holds up a pink garment: “Ini berapa bang?” Title promises comparison comedy (skilled haggler vs. soft self).

## Setup
“Orang lain” negotiation ladder (transcript): ask → **100** → counter **50** → “apaan puluh?” → **60** → **75** → “kira 70 / kira-kira 70 / pakai 70 ya.” Cash in hand, sweater on shoulder—confident closer energy. Second item starts the same script: “Ini berapa bang?” → **50**.

## Turn
Counters continue (**30** / **50**) then collapse into surrender tone—not another mathematical step, an exit.

## Punchline
**“iya yaudah boleh ya”** / on-screen end beat **“ya, ya udah, boleh ini.”** Awkward smile while holding a small red item on a hanger. “Aku” doesn’t win the price war; she accepts inventory at face value to end the social stress.

## Reaction / Button
Forced smile to camera with the compromise garment. Bargain theater ends in polite defeat—purchase as conflict avoidance.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Status split in one body/setting** — same market, two bargaining personalities.
- **Number ladder then snap** — rule-of-three haggling teaches the rhythm so the soft “boleh” lands.
- **Freelancer parallel** — clients who negotiate every invoice vs. Miki who accepts the first quote to leave the call.
- Risk: without the POV title, it reads as one long haggle; keep “others vs me” on screen.
