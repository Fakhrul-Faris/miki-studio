# Vid 002 — Autopsy

## Premise
A post-game Pokémon trainer (Elite Four grind) gets forced into a random encounter with a cocky kid — and accidentally vaporizes the kid’s “toughest Pokémon” with a move that shouldn’t exist at that level.

## Hook
Over-the-shoulder forest beat: kid in yellow points at the hero with a pixel **!** over his head — classic RPG ambush energy. Hero is already in Brendan cosplay (red jacket, Poké Ball in hand), so the genre read is instant.

## Setup
Kid refuses the warning (“Oh, are you too scared?”) and flexes **Rattata** as his ace. Hero tries to de-escalate (“I fight the elite four to train now”) but the kid treats line-of-sight as mandatory battle law. Kid’s smug crossed-arms laugh (setup frame) sells that he thinks this is his anime moment.

## Turn
Battle UI reveals the real joke name: **RATHEW ♂ L:3** — a foaming, rabid purple Rattata parody. Kid escalates anyway (“You too… R-Rathew? Use quick attack?”). Reaction frame shows the kid now commanding something Mewtwo-scale — status flip from “easy win” to “why is this child armed?”

## Punchline
Hero panics into absurd overkill: “Use, I don’t know, **hyper beam**?” Rathew dies off-screen; end frame is the kid kneeling in soot, holding a charred spiral-eyed corpse like a war movie.

## Reaction / Button
Melodramatic grief beat (“Rathew, speak to me”) → immediate shakedown: **“Hey kid, you owe me 20 bucks.”** The mourning was real; the invoice is funnier.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Status mismatch** — veteran trainer vs NPC kid, but the kid’s emotional damage is treated as sacred.
- **Naming gag** — “Rathew” makes a weak Pokémon feel like a beloved companion before the kill.
- **Overkill punch** — one-shot hyper beam on a L:3 rat is disproportionate in a way Pokémon fans instantly feel.
- Visual battle UI + creature close-up do heavy lifting; dialogue stays short and game-native.

## Remake notes
- Steal the machine: **warn someone off → they insist → you win too hard → they monetize your guilt.**
- Grease Pencil: one outdoor location; swap in a fake battle HUD overlay (cheap rectangles, pixel font).
- Keep the dead pet tiny and sooty — grief scale should be embarrassingly larger than the victim.
- Button must land as a flat invoice line, not a wink; deadpan kid energy sells it.
