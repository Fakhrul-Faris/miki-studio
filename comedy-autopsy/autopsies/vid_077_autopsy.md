# Vid 077 — Autopsy

## Premise
On a cafe date/hang, both people mime “I’ll get it”—but the cashier already knows who actually pays and walks the EDC straight to that person.

## Hook
Caption: “POV: Kasir lebih tau mana yang sebenarnya harus bayar.” Outdoor Delisa patio, couple + drinks on a tiny black table. Cashier in the doorway already smiling like a referee. Social bill-fight framed as staff omniscience.

## Setup
ASR is heavily garbled (`probability=0.74`): “batu sipu,” “pembalonnya muka atau kris,” “Duna,” “Edisi.” From frames + pattern: payment-method chatter (tunai vs QRIS; EDC offered)—“Duna aja mas? / Duna, silahkan” ≈ tunai; “Edisi ini, silahkan” ≈ EDC. Woman has wallet out (performative pay); cashier orients toward the man.

## Turn
Cashier bypasses the politeness contest and presents the blue EDC to the seated man. Woman’s urgency vs. man’s phone-tap calm. The turn isn’t a spoken roast—it’s **who receives the terminal**.

## Punchline
Transaction succeeds: “Oh, berhasil kan? / Oke, selamat ya.” Congratulating the *correct* payer as if the cashier ran a successful experiment. “Selamat” after a card tap is workplace deadpan blessing.

## Reaction / Button
Repeated “Oke” wind-down—no apology to the woman, no “next time.” Social truth stands; staff walks off with the win.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Institution as mind-reader** — cashier is the system that ignores performative generosity.
- **Prop punchline** — EDC aimed at one body; dialogue can be mush and the joke still reads.
- **ASR note** — do not quote “batu sipu” as real copy; note Whisper mangled payment vocab (tunai/QRIS/EDC). Rely on caption + who holds the machine.
- Business remake gold: vendor knows which stakeholder has budget.
