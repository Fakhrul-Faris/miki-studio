# Vid 055 — Autopsy

## Premise
A sweet “I’ll carry you to bed” offer turns into a slapstick logistics nightmare—doorways, gravity, and cooperation instructions until a tender goodnight after mutual suffering.

## Hook
Partner on green couch, eyes closed, content “Hmm.” Lead: “Hey, honey, let’s go to bed… I know you’re tired. That’s okay. I’ll carry you.” Romance beat established in one line—viewer expects cute, gets engineering problem.

## Setup
Carry begins; hero immediately grunts (close-up: clamped mouth, straining eyes, “(GRUNTING)” caption). Partner asks “What are you doing to me?” He begs “quit it… you’re killing me”—intimacy replaced by exertion before they leave the room.

## Turn
Spatial comedy escalates: “get through the door” repeated; “Your shirt’s slipping! … your pants are slipping too!” Wide shot shows absurd carry—hero’s arms elongated, partner horizontal like furniture. Doorway framing: bent-over struggle, bodies vs. architecture. Status: hero is labor; partner is passive cargo.

## Punchline
Instruction montage as “solution”: “You’re gonna lean with me! Lean with me on the bed! Use your weight! Use your weight! Throw yourself on the bed!” Bed beat—he shoves while she stays limp; cooperation comedy (he needs her to help) replaces romance.

## Reaction / Button
Exhausted tenderness after violence: swollen, baggy-eyed close-up— “Good night, babe.” Same line as the hook’s warmth, now earned through pain. No kiss, no “I love you”—just survival politeness.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Subverted trope** — princess carry → moving couch through a narrow door.
- **Physical exaggeration** — long arms, slipping clothes, grunts sell GP2D-friendly squash-and-stretch.
- **Dialogue as coaching** — “use your weight” reframes the partner as equipment.
- **Button reuses hook language** — “good night” lands funnier when his face is destroyed.
- Risk: without clear door/blocking, it’s just yelling; frames show doorway + bed as required anchors.

## Remake notes
- Steal the machine: **romantic promise → carry fail → cooperative shove → wrecked sincerity.**
- Three locations max: couch → hallway/door threshold → bedroom.
- Hero face library: grunt close-up, doorway panic, bed push strain, final puffy-eye calm.
- Partner stays mostly limp/teal hair/red top—contrast passivity with his escalating instructions.
- Sound/design: fabric slip SFX on shirt/pants lines; grunt captions in yellow paren type.
- Button hold on “Good night, babe” 1 second—eyes swollen shut, tiny smile; cut before rest.
