# Vid 056 — Autopsy

## Premise
POV of a “bos kaki tekan”: the boss walks the office and finds every department unfinished, then lectures that AI + Google could finish the work in under an hour.

## Hook
Title card: **POV : BOS KAKI TEKAN**. Opens on a monitor full of business-card layouts while the boss asks why the design is so bad (“Kenapa buguk sangat desain…” — ASR mangled *buruk*). Staff: “Saya dah usaha dah.” Instant pressure + creative desk = office friction in two lines.

## Setup
Boss demands “bimor kritif” / use “kritif VT japur” (ASR for Canva / design tool jargon) because “benda simple.” Then the same beat repeats across roles: print not ready (“Belum”), report not done, payroll not calculated (“gaji dah siap kira belum? Belum”), new product not ready. Each “Belum” is the same joke with a new desk.

## Turn
Excuses stay tool-shaped, never skill-shaped: “Takat letak-letak… tekan-tekan butang, tu pun slow”; “Benda tekan kalau yang SL japur, tu pun slow”; payroll is “benda tambah tolak japur.” Staff sound like they are describing UI friction while the boss hears laziness. Status: boss is mobile auditor; employees are stationary “Belum” machines.

## Punchline
Product beat: “Benda guna AI dengan Google tak sampai sejam pun boleh siap. Kenapa slow sangat?” Authority weaponizes the AI productivity myth—any unfinished work becomes moral failure.

## Reaction / Button
“So, siap-siap. Make sure jagi siap.” No apology, no help—just a closing squeeze. End frame: staff with POS / laptop still mid-task under the same POV title.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Rule of three+** — design → ops → payroll → product; each “Belum” tightens the spring.
- **Tool-blame vs boss-blame** — employees cite buttons and software; boss cites AI/Google; neither solves the work.
- **POV framing** — viewer *is* the kaki tekan for ~40s; complicity is the gag.
- ASR is heavily garbled Malay/English office slang (`buguk`, `kritif VT japur`, `prim`, `SL japur`); ground remakes in subtitle sense (buruk design, Canva/tool, print, slow systems), not invent cleaner dialogue.
