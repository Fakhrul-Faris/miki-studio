# Vid 059 — Autopsy

## Premise
**POV: BOSS TAKDA COMMON SENSE** — a boss lists staff he wants to fire for praying too long, being 5 minutes late (school drop-off), and using all their EL; the listener threatens labour department (JTK).

## Hook
Boss over glasses: subtitle sense “Georgia… Khalis solat lama” (ASR heard “kali solek”). “Lepas tu? Saya ingat nak buang dia.” Firing intent arrives before any HR logic.

## Setup
Pushback: “Kau jangan sampai aku viral kan kat terat?” / “Mana tahu dia berziki?” (ASR; prayer/zikir context). Boss pivots to Nura: late 5 minutes daily because “hantar anak-anak pergi sekolah.” Still: “Saya nak buang dia. Dia tak on time. Tak suka.”

## Turn
Human appeal fails: “Toleriklah sikit. Kau ini tak ada hati perut ke?” Boss escalates to Amor—crime is finishing EL every year: “tak produktif ah habiskan EL.” Protected leave becomes a performance metric.

## Punchline
Legal nuke: “Eh, itu kan hak dia yang sampai aku report kau kat JTK.” Employee rights reframed as the only language the boss understands.

## Reaction / Button
Boss look back over shoulder under the same POV title—caught, not reformed. No soft landing.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Rule of three firings** — solat → school late → EL; each more absurdly “uncommon sense.”
- **Rights punch** — JTK beat is culturally specific and sharper than “that’s mean.”
- **POV pressure** — viewer is the HR/common-sense foil absorbing the rant.
- ASR mangling (`solek`/`solat`, `terat`, names); ground remake in frame subtitles (solat lama, lambat 5 minit, EL, JTK).
