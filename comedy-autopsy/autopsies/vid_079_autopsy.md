# Vid 079 — Autopsy

## Premise
A Padang-style server runs the full “what’s the portion / sides / sambal / kuah?” checklist; the customer refuses almost everything and leaves with rice plus a single lonely perkedel.

## Hook
Empty banana leaf on branded MANDE paper, server in green uniform + songkok, gloves ready. Formal plating ritual starts before we know the order is pathologically minimal. Service professionalism is the straight man.

## Setup
ASR messy but structure clear: rice yes (“Misi untuk nasinya” ≈ “Masih untuk nasinya” / still for the rice)—“Iya.” Then the menu interrogation begins: sayur, singkong, sayur koha, timun—each met with “Enggak.” Server keeps offering; customer keeps deleting sides. “Serius udah? Bapak kaya?” / “Iya”—status dig: you’re rich but ordering like a monk?

## Turn
Sambal merah/hijau, “mampu merah,” kuah/sampur options—still “Enggak / Enggak usah.” Escalation is length, not volume: the leaf stays almost empty while the checklist grows. Visual turn (frames): mound of white rice alone on the giant leaf.

## Punchline
“Untuk mananya mungkin? / Perkedel aja / Beneran udah, perkedel doang / Perkedel akan nangis.” One fritter on a tiny saucer placed with fine-dining gravity beside a mountain of rice. The side dish is personified as crying—pity punchline for the prop.

## Reaction / Button
“Udahnya sakit udah? / Enggak, amat” then thanks—“Iya, makasih.” Customer unbothered; server completes the ceremony. Outro thank-you for watching. Button: POV of hand pinching a crumb off the single perkedel—budget lunch as ritual.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Rule of many** — not three; a full warung checklist until absurdity.
- **Maximal service vs minimal order** — uniform/gloves vs. one nugget.
- **ASR note** — opening lines (“kisang sebagai galak,” etc.) are garbled; trust the Enggak chain + perkedel landing. Malay outro “kerana” mixed in.
- Remake-friendly for client scope creep in reverse: client declines every add-on.
