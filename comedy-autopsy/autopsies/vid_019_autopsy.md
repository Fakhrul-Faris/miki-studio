# Vid 019 — Autopsy

## Premise
Routine **locker checking day** devolves into a national enforcement story — and the suspects literally escape into students’ pockets.

## Hook
On-screen: **“Locker checking day 🥶🥶🥶”** — hand-drawn inspector (glasses, bow tie, notepad) opens a locker stuffed with real-photo props: Sabrina Carpenter poster, designer bag, **MATH** notebook, and a beagle puppy in a pink cage. Mundane school task + already-unhinged contents.

## Setup
Inspector cycles lockers with clipboard: *“Open it up.”* … *“Hmm. OK, OK, OK, good, good, good. Next one.”* — first pass clears. Second locker: label **“Michael.”** — buck-toothed kid peeks from inside; walls show MrBeast *TIME* cover (white splatter), Lakers poster, locker graffiti **“69” / “DAH.”** Polite exchange: *“Good morning, Mr. Chris.”* / *“Good morning, Michael. Someone can get him.”* Then: *“All right, who's this?”* … *“Hmm.”* — something worse is found off the innocent beat.

## Turn
Hard cut to news VO over glitchy broadcast graphics and tactical doodles: *“Breaking news. A group of teenagers is alleged to have been operating a private illegal club on local school grounds…”* Escalation stack — police surround campus, shutdown, *“one of the largest enforcement actions seen in nearly 20 years,”* all suspects escaped. Caption beats: *“A group of teenagers”* → sniper-peeking doodle → frog-faced anchor on *“the suspects.”*

## Punchline
News continues: *“At this time, authorities say all listed individuals escaped during the raid and remain at large. Here is the list of the suspects you should look out for.”* The scale of the VO treats a school locker find like a federal takedown.

## Reaction / Button
Animated **“SUSPECT”** wanted flyer (crude buck-toothed doodle with lollipop) slides into a **real jeans back pocket** — live-action composite. The “escape” is literal: wanted paper becomes pocket litter. Mundane body, maximum legal drama.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Escalation ladder** — clipboard inspection → polite hellos → breaking news in ~50s.
- **Mundane prop comedy** — puppy, MrBeast, graffiti sell “this locker is a crime scene” without explaining the club.
- **VO contrast** — formal news cadence vs. stick-figure suspects.
- **Physical button** — pocket gag pays off *“escaped”* and *“remain at large”* visually, not verbally.
- Hybrid end (2D flyer + real denim) makes the button stick in memory.

## Remake notes
- Steal the machine: **boring admin task → ominous find → news maximalism → literal pocket escape.**
- Grease Pencil: act 1 all locker inserts (photo collages OK); act 2 news graphics with scanline/glitch overlay; act 3 one composite shot of flyer → pocket.
- Keep inspector calm throughout act 1 — the boredom makes the news cut sharper.
- Don’t explain the illegal club; one “Hmm” + news VO is enough.
- Suspect art should match the kid doodle from act 1 so the wanted poster reads as “they’re all Michael.”
