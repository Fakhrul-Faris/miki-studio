# Vid 067 — Autopsy

## Premise
POV: kitchen staff secretly eating **customer leftovers** behind the counter—then the customer returns mid-bite to change the order to takeaway.

## Hook
Overlay: **POV: MAKAN SISA CUSTOMER**. Worker in red cap + black apron crouches behind stainless counter, yellow steamer basket in hand, already chewing. Bottom text: “HALO KAK MAAF LUPA BILANG”—customer voice arrives while the crime is in progress.

## Setup
Customer (off-screen / OS): forgot to say takeaway—“makanan saya yang tadi di take away aja ya?” Worker, still on the floor with fried mantou dipped in sauce: “Take away?” / “Iya.” Service script continues while body language screams caught.

## Turn
Order confirm under duress: “Man tau goreng?” (ASR; likely *mantau goreng*) → “Ayam telurasin kak?” / “Ayam telurasin.” (Frames clarify **ayam telur asin**.) The turn is logistical, not moral: leftovers he was eating are now a live takeaway ticket.

## Punchline
He has to name the dish—“AYAM TELUR ASIN KAK”—while still crouched with the food in his hands. Professional mouth, criminal posture. No confession line; the visual *is* the punch.

## Reaction / Button
End hold: wide-eyed chew, food still in mouth, same POV title. Button = frozen guilt, not an apology speech.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Illegal path interrupted** — tool-hell cousin: the only free meal is leftovers; customer return closes the loophole mid-bite.
- **Status crush** — apron + POS terminal vs floor crouch = service mask vs hunger.
- **ASR note** — short clip; “telurasin” = telur asin; “Man tau” ≈ mantau. Trust frame captions over Whisper.
- Risk: without the crouch + title, it’s just order confirmation; protect the hide pose.
