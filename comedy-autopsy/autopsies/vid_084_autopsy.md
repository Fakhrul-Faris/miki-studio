# Vid 084 — Autopsy

## Premise
At a night warung counter, a customer asks the total, hears **50 ribu**, then voluntarily pays **double** because the seller is pretty and the noodles are good.

## Hook
Customer (glasses, black tee) at POS tablet: **"Cik, totalnya berapa?"** Hook is pure transaction—no flirt yet. Subtitle mirrors the ask.

## Setup
Worker: **"50 ribu."** Fair, small bill. Setup holds on the customer’s serious face across the green-cased tablet—straight man about to break his own wallet rules.

## Turn
Justification speech: **"Karena cicinya cantik dan misalnya enak."** ASR **"misalnya"** is **"misoa"** (wheat noodles)—on-screen subtitle in turn frame: **"dan misoanya enak."** Flattery + product praise become the “business case” for overpaying.

## Punchline
Customer: **"Aku bayar 2 kali gitu, Pak."** Overlay **"2 kali lipat!"** while he holds out a 50rb note. Vendor’s face reads skeptical/unimpressed—generosity that makes the seller uncomfortable is the laugh.

## Reaction / Button
No extra dialogue in transcript; the button is her deadpan vs. his earnest double-pay. Night stall, hanging bulbs, blue tables stay in background—everyday commerce, not romance montage.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Unsolicited tip logic** — he invents a reason to inflate a fair price.
- **Status flip** — customer performs generosity; worker is the straight man who didn’t ask.
- **Specific props** — misoa + 50rb + “2 kali lipat” text make it remakeable.
- Risk: reads as creepy if flattery is leering; keep it sincere-awkward, not predatory.
- ASR note: **"misalnya" → misoa**; address **"Pak"** after **"Cik"** is mixed register—fine for remake as nervous politeness.
