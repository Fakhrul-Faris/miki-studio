# Vid 086 — Autopsy

## Premise
At a drink stall, a customer orders **brown sugar**, admits a payment problem (ASR-muddy), then offers a **photo of a 100rb note** as if the image is money—and the worker somehow plays along.

## Hook
Worker across the counter (glasses, black tee), menu + POS + Blueprint printer visible: **"Silakan bang mau pesan apa?"** (ASR **"Selakon bang"**). Highlight on **"PESAN"**—normal order intake.

## Setup
Customer: **"Mau pesan brown sugar tapi..."** Trailing “tapi” flags the obstacle. Next line ASR **"Bersedia tinggal"** is garbled (likely money/saldo left behind / can’t pay cash—do not invent a clean line). Setup is “I want the drink *but*…”

## Turn
Workaround: **"Tapi saya ada fotonya."** Turn frame: phone screen shows a clear photo of a pink **100.000** Rupiah note held up to the cashier. Digital payment culture taken one notch too literal—picture of cash, not QRIS.

## Punchline
Worker: **"Bisa-bisa bang"** (sure-sure / yeah-we-can). Ambiguous tone: sarcasm or deadpan acceptance. Punch frame cuts to competent pour with jigger into a branded cup—service continues as if the photo cleared payment.

## Reaction / Button
End: tight reaction on the worker peering at the phone, skeptical focus. No refund speech in transcript—the button is the stare after the “bisa-bisa.”

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Tool literalization** — “pay with phone” → photo of banknote.
- **Service worker as straight man** — still makes the drink; system absurdity wins.
- **Prop clarity** — 100rb image must be readable on the phone screen.
- Soft if “bisa-bisa” reads purely polite without the photo beat; keep photo in frame.
- ASR note: opening **"Selakon"** = **Silakan**; **"Bersedia tinggal"** unreliable—remake with a clear “I didn’t bring cash / saldo empty, but I have a photo.”

## Remake notes
- Steal the machine: **order → can’t pay normally → offers screenshot/photo of money → staff processes it anyway.**
- Miki version: client “pays” with a screenshot of a transfer that never sent; Miki still delivers files.
- Counter set only; phone screen is the star prop (draw the note big).
- Worker line “bisa-bisa” flat, then pour montage, then final side-eye.
- Button: hold on worker face looking at phone; no moral speech.
