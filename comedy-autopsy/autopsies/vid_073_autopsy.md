# Vid 073 — Autopsy

## Premise
Customer asks if there’s a **discount for handsome people**; cashier “checks with a friend”—and returns with a **higher** total.

## Hook
Cashier to camera/customer: **“totalnya jadi 65ribu kak.”** (Transcript “Patatannya” = *totalannya*.) Clean price lock at Rp65.000. Service smile, beaded lanyard, retail counter.

## Setup
Customer (Adidas jersey, hands on hips, performative confidence): **“Ada diskon buat orang ganteng ga?”** Shameless looks-based negotiation—status bid via flirt math.

## Turn
Cashier: **“Bentar ya kak, saya tanya dulu sama temen saya.”** Leaves to “consult.” Pause is the setup for judgment—colleague as beauty tribunal. (Consult frame: coworker glance / mini-fridge beat—process theater.)

## Punchline
Returns: **“Patatannya ini Rp90.000 kak?”** / overlay **“totalnya jadi 90ribu kak.”** Price went **up** 25rb. “Handsome discount” inverted into ugly surcharge. No insult spoken—the number insults.

## Reaction / Button
Hold cashier’s polite smile delivering 90rb. No “you’re not handsome” line. Button = math as roast.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Expectation gap** — ask for discount → get markup.
- **Bureaucracy of shade** — “ask my friend” makes the insult collaborative and official.
- **ASR note** — “Patatannya” = totalannya; otherwise clean Indonesian. Trust 65 → 90 numbers.
- Risk: explaining “because you’re not ganteng” kills it; keep only the new total.

## Remake notes
- Steal the machine: **ask for special discount → colleague consult → price increases.**
- Two staff + one customer; whiteboard or receipt prop that visibly changes 65→90.
- English: “Any discount for handsome folks?” / “One sec—let me ask.” / “That’ll be $90.” (was $65).
- Miki freelancing: client asks for “good vibes discount” → quote comes back higher after “I checked with my partner.”
- Punchline CU on price overlay; cut before customer argues.
