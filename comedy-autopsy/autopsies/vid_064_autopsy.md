# Vid 064 — Autopsy

## Premise
**POV: BOSS INTERVIEW FRESH GRADUATE** — boss lowballs “fresh grad” (ASR: “fresh crack”) at RM1,800 for zero experience, sugarcoats a five-year glow-up, then gets called out for wanting cheap labour.

## Hook
Boss: “You fresh crack leh.” Pushback: “Asal you panggil fresh crack je je. Dia mana ada experience, kosong.” / “Kau bukan mampu pun nak bayar yang ada experience.” Hiring framed as budget confession.

## Setup
Salary war: candidate asks “ribu lapan”; boss: “Mana boleh?” then “Fresh crack, saya akan bayar ribu lapan je. Sebab you kosong.” Counter: “ribu lapan tu minimam. Dia ada degree kot.” Boss: “Dia kena start daripada bawah.”

## Turn
Motivation speech: “Kalau you kerja kat sini, you akan belajar semua benda. So lagi lima tahun, you akan power gila kat dalam industri ni.” Soft-power fantasy replaces pay.

## Punchline
Side voice / foil: “Bos, Bos tak payah nak gula-gulakan dia orang. Bos sebenarnya nak cip lebar dia kan?” (frames: **cheap labour**). The pitch is stripped to extraction.

## Reaction / Button
“Jangan duit kan?” (ASR)—money motive left hanging. Boss still over-glasses into candidate POV; mask off, posture unchanged.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Expectation gap** — degree + minimum wage demand vs “kosong” narrative.
- **Sugarcoat then strip** — five-year “power” speech exists only to be punctured by “cheap labour.”
- **Fresh-grad interview POV** — viewer sits in the lowball chair.
- ASR: `fresh crack` = fresh grad; `cip lebar` ≈ cheap labour (confirm via end-frame subtitle). Don’t invent cleaner boss monologue than transcript allows.
