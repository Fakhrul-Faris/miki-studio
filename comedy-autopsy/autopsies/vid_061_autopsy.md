# Vid 061 — Autopsy

## Premise
**POV: INTERN TULIS MOM BUKAN BUKAN** — boss asks for minutes of meeting; the intern logs every unfiltered rant (Monday blues, bad sales, zikir/rezeki, who’s on MC) until the boss reads the MOM and panics.

## Hook
Whiteboard: **Hourly Meeting**. Boss: “Okay, tolong ambil minit of meeting.” Intern POV from laptop edge—formal task assigned in a joke-frequency meeting.

## Setup
Boss energy check: “kenapa boss macam tak ada semangat?” “Biasa ya, mandi blues.” Intern mishears/misfiles as entertainment: “Oh, boss suka tengok blues.” “Hei!” Then sales: “kit sale… agak teruk”; intern helpfully notes last month was also teruk. Boss spiritual blame: “patut… zikir tak masuk” (ASR; frames: rezeki tak masuk).

## Turn
Attendance gossip enters the record: “Khalis mana…?” “Khalis MC.” Casual HR chatter becomes permanent MOM text. Cut to intern at desk, chin on hand, typing like a court stenographer of chaos.

## Punchline
Boss reads the doc: “Kau ni apa benda menulis minute of meeting begini dulu.” The crime isn’t bad notes—it’s *accurate* notes of things that should never be minutes.

## Reaction / Button
Boss leans into intern POV, confused/annoyed. No rewrite shown—shame is the button.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Literal minutes** — MOM as dump of off-record speech.
- **Intern competence inverted** — too faithful, not too lazy.
- **Hourly Meeting** board = environment gag supporting pointless process.
- ASR messy (`mandi blues`, `Benyaga`, `Bos kanda dengan kades`); structure is clear from frames + closing line about writing MOM “macam tu.”
