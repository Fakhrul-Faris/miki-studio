# Vid 087 — Autopsy

## Premise
Thrift shop promo energy: **"BEBAS AMBIL"** (free to take). Last customer gets **30 seconds** to take anything—then takes the **register cash** (and a bag of clothes) until staff clarifies that wasn’t the deal.

## Hook
Checkout: customer hands black trackpants; clerk in hijab behind white counter + QR stand. Overlay **"BEBAS AMBIL"**; small subtitle **"udah."** **"Udah kak?" / "Iya, udah."**—purchase finished, promo about to drop.

## Setup
Clerk’s gift: **"Karena kakaknya pelanggan terakhir, jadi kakak bebas ambil apa aja selama 30 detik. Oke kak?"** Turn-frame energy: she smiles, hands clasped—genuinely excited about the perk. “Apa aja” is the loaded phrase.

## Turn
Customer interprets “apa aja” as **inventory + cash drawer**. Punch frame: he’s behind the counter digging pink Rupiah from the open till while she stares skeptical from the customer side. Subtitle beat **"nggak"** under the same **"BEBAS AMBIL"** title—policy vs. theft.

## Punchline
Clerk: **"Kak, maksudnya gak gitu."** Soft correction after the fact—authority arrives late. The promo language already licensed the grab.

## Reaction / Button
End: he counts a thick stack; she motion-blurs past with a stuffed clear bag of merch. CCTV “PERHATIAN” sign stays visible—irony, not enforcement. No chase dialogue; the loot sprint is the button.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Ambiguous policy literalization** — “anything for 30 seconds” without a boundary list.
- **Show-don’t-tell theft** — till hands + bag run beat the explanation.
- **Delayed “maksudnya gak gitu”** — classic after-the-damage HR clarification.
- Needs the smile→horror face flip on the clerk; without it, it’s just shoplifting.
- Clean transcript—rare for this batch; keep lines intact.
