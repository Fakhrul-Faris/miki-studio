# Vid 047 — Autopsy

## Premise
Hook: **"When you put a negative tip."** A diner can't pay his bill, so he leaves a negative tip — then the staff argue in loops about whether that's allowed while the cost gets pushed onto the waiter.

## Hook
Waiter in black server shirt stares at receipt, pen in hand, confused brow. Red banner + caption **"Why'd you do that?"** Customer (off-screen beat): thanks for dining, then the negative-tip reveal. Pizza slice on table anchors the restaurant setting.

## Setup
Customer (blonde, olive tee): **"I can't afford to pay for the meal."** Waiter: "That's what you ordered." Customer: "But I can't pay." Waiter: **"You can't put a negative tip."** Customer: **"I just did."** First loop established — rules vs. fait accompli.

## Turn
Manager enters the blame chain: negative tip **"is coming out of your paycheck."** Waiter: "What?" Customer doubles down: **"You said I could tip whatever I want"** (turn frame — emphatic, pizza in foreground). Waiter to customer: "That's not a tip." Customer: **"That's the tip I chose."** Responsibility ping-pongs; nobody can stop the logic.

## Punchline
Customer invents bureaucracy: **"Where's the no negative number sign?"** → **"The sign that says no negative nilly numbers on this receipt."** Manager: **"Because there's no rule that says no negative nilly numbers, Nicholas."** Customer tries to correct his name — manager continues: **"Nicholas better not have no nilly negative bank account because Nicholas got paid for the tip."** Absurd legalese + wrong name = institutional nonsense winning.

## Reaction / Button
Escalation spiral: **"It's just a tip"** repeated until waiter snaps **"No, you stop"** (reaction frame). Customer threatens to sue, offers to pay whole bill sarcastically, then **"Fuck this shit! Fuck this job! I quit!"** Manager: **"You can't do that. You can't even quit."** End frame: violent motion blur at table — chaos button. Every party "just did" the forbidden thing; nobody has authority.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **"I just did" vs. "You can't"** loop — same engine as vid_045 literalization but verbal; rule of three across tip, paycheck deduction, quitting.
- **Service-industry cruelty** lands because manager sides with system, not server (Nicholas pays).
- **"Negative nilly numbers"** — childish phrasing makes fake rules funnier.
- Long (~93s); middle repetition is the joke but tests retention — works as escalating meltdown, not one-liner.
- Pizza prop keeps scene visually static; faces carry it.

## Remake notes
- Steal the machine: **can't pay → exploit loophole language ("tip whatever you want") → staff punish wrong person → everyone claims agency ("I just did").**
- Grease Pencil: three-shot — customer with pizza, waiter with receipt, manager busting in from kitchen door.
- On-screen receipt prop showing **-$XX.XX** tip line; waiter face grows more hollow each loop.
- Manager design: calm bureaucrat who misnames everyone "Nicholas."
- Repeat "It's just a tip" with shrinking pauses until waiter table-flip (end motion blur).
- Variations: negative star review, negative RSVP, refunding a compliment — same "I just did" obstinacy.
