# Vid 083 — Autopsy

## Premise
POV overlay: **"Punya Pasien Nurut"** (having an obedient patient). A clinic exam starts as normal range-of-motion checks, then quietly mutates into unpaid janitorial labor—and ends with the doctor and patient dancing while cash appears.

## Hook
Doctor: **"Keluhannya apa, Pak?"** Patient answer is ASR-garbled (**"Benjala-ngeleng"**—likely a neck/jaw complaint; hook frame shows hand on jawline). Title card already promises obedience; viewer expects medical compliance, not a hire.

## Setup
Legitimate exam instructions land clean: **"Lengok ke atas, Pak"** → **"Angkat tangan ke atas, Pak."** Setup frame: patient sits arms straight up, blank face—hyper-compliant already. **"Tarik kenapa, Pak?"** is ASR-muddy (patient questioning a pull / garbled phrasing)—first friction inside an otherwise obedient loop.

## Turn
Orders leave medicine entirely: **"Melapkaca, Pak"** (wipe the glass/mirror)—turn frame shows patient bent at the sink wiping the mirror while the doctor points at the sanitizer wall. Next: **"Yang ini, Pak"** → **"Nyapuh, Pak"** (sweep). Authority keeps talking like it’s still an exam; labor is the gag.

## Punchline
Choreography as “therapy”: **"Joget gini, Pak"** → **"Lebih lince lagi, Pak"** (ASR **lince** ≈ **lincah**—more agile). End frame: both men dancing in the exam room; patient holds a thick stack of blue Rupiah notes. Obedience has become entertainment + payment spectacle.

## Reaction / Button
No verbal button beyond the dance escalate—cash in hand + synced joget is the button. Clinic desk clutter (stamp, gloves, stapler) stays in frame so the dance reads as professional space violated, not a party venue.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Literal obedience ladder** — medical → cleaning → dancing; each step is still phrased as "Pak" instructions.
- **Status comedy** — doctor never drops authority voice; patient never refuses.
- **Visual mid-beats** — mirror wipe and arms-up pose sell the turn without explaining it.
- Weak if remade as random chaos; the joke needs the *same polite exam tone* across chores.
- ASR note: early complaint + "Tarik kenapa" / "lince" are garbled—ground remakes in clear verbs: look up, arms up, wipe, sweep, dance.
