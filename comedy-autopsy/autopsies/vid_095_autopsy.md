# Vid 095 — Autopsy

## Premise
Silent tenure clock at a **Keykinian / Kekinian** drink stall: captions climb **1 hari → 1 minggu → 1 bulan → 1 tahun kerja** while the worker’s relationship to ice, craft, and bulk milk flips from trauma to shortcut.

## Hook
Empty ASR — comedy is on-screen timestamps + hands/face. **“1 hari kerja”**: ice into steel mug / ice water; cut to staff face — pursed, overwhelmed, tiny pitcher, **“PESAN & BAYAR DISINI / Order & pay here”** behind him. One day already broke him.

## Setup
**“1 minggu kerja”**: careful pink Bandung-style iced drink; metal ice scoop; respectful pour into steel mug. Craft is still sincere.

## Turn
**“1 bulan kerja”**: craft peaks into ritual — pink iced base + dark stream poured through a tip (coffee/tea concentrate), or solemn measuring-cup / “taste” stare with the little steel cup. Tenure buys performative expertise (sommelier energy on a street drink).

## Punchline
Jump to **“1 tahun kerja”**: no precious pour. Counter is a warehouse — Ultra Milk, Indomilk, Milk Life, Sprite, Boba King caramel, syrup row. He bags cartons and bottles into a clear plastic bag at speed. The year’s skill is logistics, not latte art; craft collapsed into takeaway bulk.

## Reaction / Button
Deadpan lift of the heavy bag toward camera/customer. No dialogue; caption + bag weight is the button. Sign still says order & pay — he’s selling milk as a “drink run.”

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Tenure decay / skill inversion** — care peaks at one month, year-one efficiency looks like not caring (but is actually mastery of the real job: inventory).
- **Visual-only joke** — works when ASR fails; captions are the script. Dense 1fps confirmed face beats + brand shelf.
- **F&B tool comedy** — ice scoop → pitcher → milk cartons as career arc props.
- Risk: if time labels are missing, it’s random drink B-roll; keep captions huge.
- Perfect Miki office transplant (slides → Canva ritual → Excel zip).
