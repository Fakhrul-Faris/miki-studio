# Vid 070 — Autopsy

## Premise
Cafe worker mid-shift gets stacked personal disaster notifications (breakup, then overdue PayLater)—then still comps a customer’s bill and hands back a phone with an empty “semangat terus.”

## Hook
Worker mid-shake / at green-tile counter. Phone on charge. Fake WhatsApp from **“ayang ❤️”**: **“KITA PUTUS‼️”** / “sekarang.” Romance nuke as UI overlay—no dialogue needed (transcript silent until ~00:15).

## Setup
Second overlay: Livin’ by Mandiri — **Tagihan PayLater Jatuh Tempo**, Rp1.256.000 overdue. Personal finance pile-on while kitchen gear stays in frame. Then customer beat (transcript): wants to pick up “techware” / pay at the kasir—“Kasinya di sebelah sana kan?”

## Turn
Worker: “Gak apa-apa, gak usah bayar dari saya aja.” Customer: “Ha, serius!” / “Wah, maksi banyak loh.” (ASR *maksi* ≈ *makasih*.) After dump + debt alerts, he spends money he doesn’t have—generosity as dissociation.

## Punchline
Customer: “Saya mau ambil HP saya yang terdisas” (ASR; sense = phone that was charging/left). Worker returns it—“Oh, ini hape-nya, silahkan” / frame: **“OH INI HP-NYA A SILAKAN”**—with a too-wide grin. Pep-talk button: **“Semangatnya, semangat terus.”**

## Reaction / Button
Customer “Oke, makasih ya.” Hold the grin + phone handoff. Encouragement is for the customer; the worker is the one who needs it. No mention of the breakup/PayLater on the spoken track—UI already did that job.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **UI as antagonist** — notifications do setup; dialogue only plays the aftermath.
- **Wrong generosity** — comps bill while PayLater screams; status = financially broken host.
- **ASR note** — early silence is real; “techware/terdisas/maksi” garbled—trust overlays + “gak usah bayar” / “semangat terus.”
- Risk: if notification text is unreadable on mobile, joke collapses—animate huge toast cards.
