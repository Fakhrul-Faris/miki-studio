# Vid 098 — Autopsy

## Premise
Street-cart payment with **kesabaran setipis tisu**—a tiny bill, a large note, and change-counting that burns through everyone’s patience until fanning becomes the only tool left.

## Hook
Caption: **pov : kesabaran setipis tisu**. Customer (yellow glasses, leaf-print shirt) already taut at a roti bakar / topping cart with QRIS on the counter. Heat + wait = premise.

## Setup
“Totalnya jadi berapa pak?” → **“jadi 20 ribu pak.”** Tender: “uangnya 100 ribu ya.” Math is trivial; the emotional load is the wait. Vendor handles the red 100k note under the umbrella.

## Turn
Change announced: **“pembalannya 80 ribu”** (ASR for *kembaliannya*). “80 ribu dilihat si kaka / aji”—ASR mush around watching/counting the change. Tension isn’t the amount; it’s latency on an easy refund.

## Punchline
Visual: vendor still clutching the notes while someone off-camera **fans her face so hard** the air becomes a brown blur. Tissue-thin patience made physical—cooling as harassment/help hybrid.

## Reaction / Button
Eyes half-closed, mouth open, money still in hand—endurance face. No witty line; the fan is the button.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Micro-transaction rage** — 20k order / 100k note is everyday Indonesia payment friction.
- **Tool comedy** — QRIS present but cash path creates the delay; fan is the absurd “solution.”
- **ASR note** — “lika uangnya,” “pembalannya,” “aji” are noisy; keep clear numbers (20 / 100 / 80) in remake subs.
- Risk: without the fan blur, it’s just a normal change handoff.

## Remake notes
- Steal the machine: **small invoice + large tender → change wait → patience prop goes physical.**
- Miki client: pays a 20 invoice with a “100” transfer, stares while finance finds change; intern fans Miki with a printout.
- One cart/counter; QR sticker visible for modern/tool texture.
- Animate the fan as a huge motion arc; hold vendor’s exhausted face through it.
- Button: cut mid-fan, money still uncounted.
