# Vid 091 — Autopsy

## Premise
A diner finishes a good meal, asks what restaurants do with leftovers (doesn’t want waste), hears **"we feed the cats out back"**—then becomes the cat: meowing and eating the scraps outside with the staff.

## Hook
Smiling server (red cap, black apron, tray) leans over the tiled table: **"Halo kak, ada yang bisa dibantu?"** Customer praise + fullness: food was delicious, **"cuman aku udah full,"** but **"gak mau buang-buang makanan."** Soft ethical setup.

## Setup
Customer: **"Biasanya kalau makanan sisa kayak gini, diapain ya kak?"** Server: **"Oh, tenang aja kak, kita ada kucing diari belakang biasa kita kasih makanan."** ASR **"diari"** ≈ **"di area"** belakang—cats in the back. Subtitle beat **"BIASA KITA KASIH MAKAN."** Trust is established: leftovers have a noble destination.

## Turn
Customer: **"Beneran?"** / thanks / **"Oke, sama-sama kak."** Server invite: **"Silahkan…"** (ASR **"Selahkan apa aja?"**). Then animal voice: **"Woos? Woos?"** → **"Mew!" / "Mew!"** Customer doesn’t send food to cats—**auditions as the cat.**

## Punchline
Outdoor huddle: server still in apron with tray, two others crouched around a tiny plate on a concrete ledge. Neon lucky-cat / Chinese signage in the window. Scrap plate becomes the “back area” feeding trough. ASR **"Mew apply!!"** = meow noise garble—treat as continued meowing, not English “apply.”

## Reaction / Button
Three bodies fighting over one small plate; no return to polite table talk. Button is the visual demotion from diner → alley cat meal. Professional tray still in lap = job didn’t end, only the role did.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **False diagnosis / role hijack** — waste-policy answer becomes identity offer.
- **Show the back area** — outdoor scrap huddle pays off “kucing belakang.”
- **Service smile → feral squat** — same server costume across status drop.
- Needs the meow to be committed; half-meow kills it.
- ASR notes: **"diari" → di area**; **"Selahkan" → Silakan**; **"Mew apply"** = meow SFX.
