# Vid 065 — Autopsy

## Premise
POV private-sector boss (“Boss Swasta”) negotiates Raya leave with an employee who cites government holiday rules—until the boss claims he *is* the government.

## Hook
Overlay: **POV : BOSS SWASTA**. Boss leans into camera over low-slung glasses, tropical shirt, stack of cash/papers in hand. Employee open: “Bos, cuti raya setiap hari apa?” Instant office status: viewer = staff; boss owns the frame.

## Setup
Boss sets the calendar: “Setiap hari sabtu… Sabtu sampai ahad.” Employee pushes—“Dua hari je?”—then reaches for public-sector logic: “bukan kerajaan kata kalau raya hari Sabtu… cuti.” (ASR: “cuma dengan istim” / “air bawah lama” / “cuci” = garbled *cuti* / duration talk—sense is holiday-length + replacement-leave when Raya hits Saturday.) Boss keeps the private-sector frame: “Tapi awak kerja-kerjaan je lah?”

## Turn
Employee asks for Friday + Monday off: “saya nak cuci hari Jumaat dengan Isnain. Bolehkah?” Boss shrugs—“Aplailah”—then denies the premise of existing leave: “Bukan memang dah ada cuci? Siapa kata?” Employee: “Kerajaan kata.” Authority clash locked: state calendar vs boss calendar.

## Punchline
Boss: **“Kerajaan? Nama dengan saya.”** (I *am* the government / my name is the government.) End-frame subtitle sense also lands the private-sector rewrite: work under *his* kingdom, not Putrajaya’s. Glasses-over-nose stare + whiteboard buzzwords (“Metamorphosis,” CRM) sell founder-king energy.

## Reaction / Button
Hold the over-glasses glare; no apology, no HR softener. Button is status, not a joke-explain: employee’s legal cite evaporates because the boss declared himself law.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Institution vs founder** — “kerajaan kata” vs “nama dengan saya” is a clean authority swap Miki can reuse with any policy doc.
- **POV + prop** — cash/papers + tropical shirt = boss who is casual and absolute.
- **ASR note** — Whisper `language=ms`; treat “cuci” as *cuti*, ignore “istim/air bawah” as noise; remake from holiday-Saturday + Fri/Mon ask.
- Risk: without the “kerajaan” word, punchline needs another sacred rule (HR handbook, client contract) for the boss to annex.
