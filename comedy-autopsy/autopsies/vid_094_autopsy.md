# Vid 094 — Autopsy

## Premise
POV of a makeup artist with a perfectly obedient bridal client—instructions escalate from lip poses to chores until the fully glam bride is washing a car and scooter-commuting home still in suntiang.

## Hook
Caption: **Pov : Punya Client nurut**. MUA in blue hijab applies lipstick; bottom subtitle **“mangap dikit kak”** (ASR hears “banggap dikit kak”). Fantasy client who never argues.

## Setup
Command montage, each obeyed: “liat bawah kak… liat depan kak…” Phone filming the ornate kebaya/headdress. Soft compliance = power rush for the freelancer.

## Turn
Chores invade the beauty brief: **“nyapu kak… cuci mobil kak…”** Bride in full glam scrubs an SUV with soap while someone holds the beaded train off the wet driveway. Obedience stopped being cute; it’s unpaid labor.

## Punchline
**“pulang kak.”** Bridal client waves from the back of a red scooter—gown, gold headpiece, residential street. The “nurut” client completes the full life-admin checklist, not just the makeup look.

## Reaction / Button
Smile + wave on the pillion seat; no complaint. Button is visual incongruity: ceremony armor on weekday transport.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Rule of escalation** — mouth → eyes → look forward → sweep → wash car → go home; same “kak” cadence.
- **Freelancer wish-fulfillment flipped** — dream client becomes too obedient; status inversion (artist as life coach).
- **ASR note** — Whisper “banggap” = on-screen **mangap**; trust burned-in subtitles for remake copy.
- Risk: without the car-wash/scooter frames, it’s just a cute MUA montage.
