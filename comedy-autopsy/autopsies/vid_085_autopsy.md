# Vid 085 — Autopsy

## Premise
POV: **"Lagi ngajarin anak baru"** (teaching the new hire). A bakery demo of donut icing looks professional—until the trainee “tries it” by eating the product.

## Hook
Title card + stainless kitchen. Senior (black coat, mask, hairnet) pipes neat white zigzags on chocolate donuts while junior (grey coat) stares. Hook line energy: **"Seperti ini ya"**—show, don’t lecture.

## Setup
Demo continues; tray of three donuts, piping bag, ASA GROUP uniforms. Hierarchy is visual (mask vs no mask, black vs grey). Training tone stays serious for a tiny craft task.

## Turn
Senior hands off: **"Ini cobain."** Trainee takes the donut. Expectation: attempt the drizzle. Turn is the choice to treat “try this” as **taste**, not **technique**.

## Punchline
Verbal: **"Oh nooo."** Visual punch: trainee holding a donut with a huge bite missing, eyes shut in bliss over the prep table—hygiene theater next to product theft. Reaction frame locks the mentor’s quiet judgment.

## Reaction / Button
End shifts to **black-and-white** while he still grips/blows-at the pastry—mock-dramatic “career ends here” grade. No lecture line; the grade change *is* the button.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Instruction literalization** — “cobain” means sample-as-eat, not sample-as-practice.
- **Workplace hygiene contrast** — hairnets + mask vs open-mouth bite over parchment.
- **Minimal dialogue** — three lines; comedy is almost all visual (good for GP).
- Thin if the bite isn’t huge/readable; the missing chunk must read in 9:16.
- Transcript is sparse—ground remake in on-screen POV title + eat-fail, not invented mentor rant.
