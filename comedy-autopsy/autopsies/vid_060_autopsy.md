# Vid 060 — Autopsy

## Premise
**POV: BOSS SENANG KENA SCAM** — a boss proudly recounts three scams he fell for (or almost fell for); the employee’s only job is to say “scammer” while the salary disappears.

## Hook
Boss leans in: live stream, woman comments “kalau nak sedap PM.” He PMs and sends RM500. “Agak-agak dapat apa?” Gullible storytelling as flex.

## Setup
Employee: “Jangan, Boss. Skamer itu.” Boss: “Oh, iyakah?” Beat two: parcel-stuck call, pay RM300 to release. He almost pays—“Saya panggilnya RM300. Tapi dia diam betul.” Silence breaks the spell; he gets angry at being treated like an idiot.

## Turn
“Do you? Ya.” call-and-response (ASR; likely “tahu?” / confirmation ping) becomes a ritual: employee verifies each scam story. Beat three: invest/link (“Saya melang sejuta…” ASR)—“Dia bagi link… Saya log in… Betul tak ada apa-apa pun?”

## Punchline
“Habislah gaji aku.” Not almost—actually emptied.

## Reaction / Button
Employee: “Gaji-gaji kau sekali habis.” Pointing finger into frame; boss still mildly concerned, not reformed. Title card still claims the diagnosis.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Rule of three scams** — romance PM → parcel → login link; stakes escalate to full salary.
- **Status inversion** — boss narrates; junior is the only adult.
- **Near-miss then hit** — parcel silence shows he *can* spot a scam, then still loses everything on the next one.
- ASR noise (`Do you`, `melang sejuta`, `Aka memainkan`); keep money numbers (RM500 / RM300 / gaji habis) as anchors.
