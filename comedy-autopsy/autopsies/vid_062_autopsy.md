# Vid 062 — Autopsy

## Premise
**POV: INTERN CUBA INTERVIEW ORANG** — interns run candidate #21; the applicant mirrors “macam saya, saya… intern” flex energy and names a RM3500 expectation that breaks the room.

## Hook
Whiteboard: **Calon ke: 21**. Candidate: “Nama saya Muamad Revki… University of Princeton” (ASR). Intern panel already exhausted; prestige claim lands on tired faces.

## Setup
Side whisper: “Masa dia perkenalkan diri dia?” Interview continues. Experience: “Macam saya, saya sinis sebagai intern, tapi… tolong bisnes development… sales yang hebat.” Candidate sells intern-as-BD like a LinkedIn spiral—same rank as the interviewers.

## Turn
Salary ask: “berapa ekspertisian” (ASR ≈ *expectation*). “Macam saya, ekspertisian saya 3500.” Number is concrete; Princeton + intern + 3500 collide.

## Punchline
Panel fracture: “Itu bukan PSE kaya?” / “Masa dia betul orang? Gaji dia?” (ASR)—shock that he said the number / doubt he’s real. The intern-interviewers lose authority the moment money is spoken aloud.

## Reaction / Button
Over-glasses stare into candidate POV; tablet guy open-mouthed. Title still claims they’re “cuba interview”—they are not in control.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Status inversion** — interns hiring; candidate also “intern” with Princeton flex.
- **Calon 21** — fatigue prop makes any answer feel cursed.
- **Salary as landmine** — 3500 is the sharp object; jargon fluff was safe until then.
- ASR noise (`sinis`/`sini`, `ekspertisian`, `PSE`); keep Princeton + intern + 3500 + panel panic as the spine—don’t invent cleaner lines.
