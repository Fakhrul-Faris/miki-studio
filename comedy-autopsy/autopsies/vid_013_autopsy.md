# Vid 013 — Autopsy

## Premise
A tired dad stages an intervention about his kid’s Pokémon roster — specifically the ones that look less like monsters and more like **people**.

## Hook
Title card **“SUS Pokemon”** over a two-shot: skeptical dad (maroon sweater, **“DAD JUICE”** mug) side-eyes **Thundurus** — blue, muscular, genie-bodied, arms crossed, floating on a cloud. No dialogue yet; the hook is pure accusatory pointing at a design that already reads humanoid.

## Setup
Dad opens with a serious tone: *“We need to have a serious discussion about the type of Pokemon you've been catching lately.”* Status: parent is the authority; the roster is on trial. Thundurus stays smug in frame — the visual case is already made before the verdict.

## Turn
Verdict lands: *“This one's just a straight-up man.”* The category error is stated plainly — not “weird design,” but **literal man**. Dad’s thumb-point (visible across beats) sells that the creature’s posture, build, and attitude count as evidence.

## Punchline
Defense collapses into one weak qualifier: *“No. He has a tail.”* The rebuttal doesn’t deny the man-read; it tries to disqualify it with the thinnest possible Pokémon technicality — a segmented tail visible under the cloud in every frame.

## Reaction / Button
Hold on the tableau: dad still unconvinced, Thundurus still arms-crossed. No third beat — the joke ends on the tail “gotcha” failing to exonerate a clearly sus design. The button is the stalemate face-off.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Category error** — treating a legendary as a dude at a barbecue.
- **Ultra-short** — one accusation, one bad defense; no over-explaining.
- **Visual carries the case** — Thundurus’s humanoid build makes the dialogue redundant; the tail is visibly inadequate as a counterargument.
- **Relatable dad voice** — “serious discussion” + “DAD JUICE” mug = parental exhaustion played straight against absurd fandom.

## Remake notes
- Steal the machine: **solemn lecture → design is obviously wrong category → weakest possible legalistic excuse.**
- Grease Pencil: two-character tableau on flat color; keep the accused creature smug and static while dad does all the emotional work.
- Swap Pokémon for any franchise with “officially a monster but drawn like a person” (Digimon, Yu-Gi-Oh, furry OC).
- End on the same hold — don’t add a third line; the deadpan standoff is the button.
- Prop the mug; it tags the dad role in one read.
