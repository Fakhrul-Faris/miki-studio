# Vid 080 — Autopsy

## Premise
Shoe price 500k, e-wallet balance 498k—customer pays the bulk digital and begs to cover the missing 2k in cash.

## Hook
On-screen math (typo “KETIAK” ≈ “Ketika”): saldo 498.000 vs harga sepatu 500.000. Clerk POV of a guy in a white tee at a sneaker shop, phone ready. Micro-shortage stated before any negotiation—gap is only 2k rupiah.

## Setup
“Mas, bayar emang mau muka apa?” / “Kiris.” / “Muka kris ya?”—ASR for tunai vs **QRIS** (“kiris/keris”). “Nah, ini ya mas. / 500 ya? / Ya.” He commits to the full 500k path knowing the wallet can’t clear it cleanly. Tension = tiny number, public counter.

## Turn
Phone-check face: furrowed, staring at balance while blue BCA EDC waits in the clerk’s hand. The turn isn’t “I can’t pay”—it’s inventing a split: most on QR, remainder as pocket change.

## Punchline
“Mas, kalau 2 ribunya cash bisa ga?” Asking a retailer to accept a hybrid payment for a rounding-error gap. Dignity vs. 2,000 rupiah. The ask is polite, specific, and somehow worse than just being short.

## Reaction / Button
Earnest camera stare holding the phone—waiting for clerk mercy. No transcript of approval; the button is the plea face under the still-visible 498 vs 500 text.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Micro-reality punch** — grand shoe purchase, comic deficit of 2k (paranoia vs tiny crime cousin).
- **Payment-stack comedy** — QRIS + cash for a gap smaller than parking.
- **ASR note** — “muka/kiris” = QRIS; overlay “KETIAK” is Ketika. Don’t invent clerk dialogue not in the transcript.
- Universally remakeable: freelance invoice 500, client pays 498, “can I Venmo the $0.13?”
