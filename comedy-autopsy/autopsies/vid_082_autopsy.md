# Vid 082 — Autopsy

## Premise
Coffee is 50k, but free if the customer says “saya ganteng”; the customer overpays 100k and tips the change specifically to avoid the compliment—then confirms they’re ugly, and the seller agrees.

## Hook
Counter handoff of iced drink; “Jadi kak? / Jadi berapa bang?” Price negotiation energy at a night stall/cafe. Transaction framed as optional humiliation discount.

## Setup
Seller: “50 ribu kak, tapi itu kopinya gratis kalo misalkan kakak bilang saya ganteng.” Clear rule: vanity tax vs. cash. Social dare baked into POS.

## Turn
Customer: “Gw deh 100 ribu, kembaliannya ambil aja.” Pays double and refuses change—buys their way out of saying the line. Status move: money over ego performance. Mid-bit ASR garbles celebration chatter (“Dukan bener katagula, dapet diit lagi”); frames show “pinter lu!” + thumbs-up + cash—crew celebrates the hack.

## Punchline
Customer reality-check: “Jelek berarti gua ya?” Seller: “Iya.” The unpaid compliment path still extracts a status hit—just verbally, after the tip. Avoiding “I’m handsome” earns a confirmed “you’re ugly.”

## Reaction / Button
Deadpan staff face handing the cup; no softener, no “just kidding.” The yes is the button. Short and lethal.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Paid escape hatch** — customer finds an “illegal” path around the game (tool/institution hell cousin).
- **Status inversion** — tipping big still loses the attractiveness frame.
- **Two-beat punch** — clever overpay, then the ugliness confirm.
- **ASR note** — mid celebration line unreliable; punch lines “Jelek berarti gua ya? / Iya” are clear. Don’t invent extra roast lines.
