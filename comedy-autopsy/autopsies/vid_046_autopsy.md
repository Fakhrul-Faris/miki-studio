# Vid 046 — Autopsy

## Premise
Friends decide roommate Cooper is demon-possessed because he's acting slightly normal — then every defense he offers becomes more "proof," culminating in holy water and a breakup meltdown.

## Hook
Blonde friend in mustard work shirt, intense wide eyes, thrusting glasses forward. Caption: **"That's exactly what a demon would want you to think."** Immediate genre parody: exorcism logic applied to a hungover roommate.

## Setup
Cooper is tied up (bag/chair — implied by dialogue). Cycle begins: "He's possessed" / "I'm not possessed" / "That's exactly what a demon would want you to think." One friend admits he doesn't actually believe it — then immediately: "No, trust me. He is." Cooper: "Currie, what the fuck, untie me?" Friend: "See, look how angry he is." Cooper: anger is because **"you hit me on the head and tied me to a fucking bag!"** — dismissed as possession symptom.

## Turn
Evidence montage (each innocent → damning):
- Refused **Baskin-Robbins** → "Maybe I'm trying to watch my figure."
- Friend in bed, hands up: Cooper **woke up early** for a new job — "When has Cooper ever woken up early?"
- Only **one beer** on a Tuesday night.
- Cooper claims **MIT acceptance**, **"current life choices,"** growing up — "Cooper would never say that / sound like a bro."
- Relationship beat: **"Maybe we're not right for each other. Maybe that's okay."** Friend: **"Don't say that."** (reaction frame) — emotional maturity read as demonic.

## Punchline
Holy water assault: **"Hit him with the holy water!"** Cooper: "It's just cold / extremely cold / you're throwing water in my eye." Cold water = "affected by holy water" in their logic. Cooper insists he's **"pissed off about my current life choice"** — phrase alone convicts him.

## Reaction / Button
Girlfriend enters: **"Are you gonna take me to Benihana's tonight? Give me the hot fudge sundae?"** Cooper: "I got an early morning." Friends scream: **"He's possessed! He's possessed! Papa father!"** — refusing dessert and early bedtime = final proof. Circular diagnosis never breaks; Cooper's reasonable boundaries are the horror.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **False diagnosis engine** — every rational explanation feeds the delusion (classic sitcom witch-hunt).
- **Bro-priest vocabulary** ("father," "Baskin-Robbins," "bro") grounds parody in specific friend-group voice.
- Long form (~2 min) earns escalation; MIT/breakup/holy water are distinct act beats.
- Transcript ASR garbles some lines ("ham was doing this really fast," bedroom grunting) — visual context likely physical comedy; remake should clarify or cut.
- Risk: middle sags if exorcism tropes aren't stylized; works when performances sell sincere panic.
