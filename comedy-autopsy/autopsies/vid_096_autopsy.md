# Vid 096 — Autopsy

## Premise
A warung posts a social policy—**resto yang tidak menerima cowok ‘mokondo’** (freeloader / “bring nothing but yourself” date)—and staff enforce it at bill time with pressure, payment theater, and an absurdist bag gag.

## Hook
Ayam penyet interior, couple at table, waiter in green shirt/red apron. Title card names the banned archetype. Pillar gag sign: **MOHON MAAF NGAMEN GRATIS**—even “performance” isn’t a payment method.

## Setup
Bill beat: “Mas, jadi berapa semuanya?” → “Totalnya 55 ribu, mau bayar pakai…” (ASR: “krissa pakai” ≈ card/QRIS noise). Woman offers “Biar aku aja ya”; waiter agrees “Iya, Mbak” then pivots hard: **“Cepet bayar”** at the man. Blue EDC terminal on the table is the prop of judgment.

## Turn
Stalling and crosstalk (ASR messy: “Maka pesaya lopet,” “Micas,” “Pina tem…”) while woman still tries to cover—“Mika aku aja, Mas.” Waiter calls it: “Eh… Disana cowok mau kondok” (ASR for **mokondo**). Policy speech mid-service; romance dinner becomes compliance check.

## Punchline
Visual enforcement: waiter pulls a translucent red plastic bag over the woman’s head—takeaway packaging as “remove the paying party / end this date”—while the man doomscrolls. Absurd literalization of kicking out freeloader economics.

## Reaction / Button
Soft landing: “Maaf ya, Mbak.” / “Makasih.” Polite service voice after the bag violence—policy executed, manners restored.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Institution as moral enforcer** — business rule vs. dating etiquette; waiter is HR + bouncer.
- **Who-pays friction** — classic client/freelancer “I’ll get it” theater, weaponized.
- **ASR note** — heavy garble on payment words and “mokondo”; on-screen POV title is the reliable thesis. Don’t invent cleaner slang lines—keep “cepat bayar” + apology.
- Risk: bag gag can read mean without the title explaining the policy target (the freeloader, not the woman).
