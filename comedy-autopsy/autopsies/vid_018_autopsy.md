# Vid 018 — Autopsy

## Premise
A teacher announces the principal banned quiz help; one student “solves” the test by brute force — and the teacher celebrates the result.

## Hook
Crude charcoal doodle + title fragment **“The bitch ass”** (DiemArchive watermark). Immediately signals unserious, insult-comedy tone before the classroom line lands.

## Setup
Teacher (exhausted stick figure, arms raised): *“Alright, class. The bitch-ass principal said I can't help you guys with your quizzes anymore, so good luck.”* Text overlay **“Quizes anymore”** on a groaning figure. Student reacts: *“Oh, gosh, uh...”* — panic face, **“AAH”** caption — with photo-cutout wig on the doodle head (hybrid collage look).

## Turn
Long visual mid-beat (no dialogue): pencil fills **every bubble — A, B, C, and D** — on the quiz sheet. The “strategy” is visible, not spoken: when help is gone, mark everything. Student’s vacant face + ridiculous wig holds while the pencil works.

## Punchline
Student, amazed: *“Wow, I can't believe I got a hundred percent.”* Somehow the nonsense approach produced a perfect score — the joke is the outcome contradicting the method.

## Reaction / Button
Teacher, deadpan approval: *“Yeah, good shit, Tim.”* End frame: two doodle kids in photo-cutout swim trunks and wigs, side by side — teacher endorses the cheat energy with zero moral follow-up. Button is institutional complicity.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Visual mid-beat carries the joke** — filling all bubbles is the turn; you have to *see* the pencil work.
- **Teacher as ally, not authority** — punchline isn’t “got caught,” it’s “good shit.”
- **Collage ugliness = tone** — photo hair/swim trunks on scribble bodies keeps it meme-stupid, not sitcom-clean.
- **Relatable setup** — lost classroom support → irrational coping → absurd success.

## Remake notes
- Steal the machine: **help removed → visible dumb strategy → impossible win → authority co-signs.**
- Grease Pencil: hold one locked shot on the answer sheet for the fill-every-bubble beat; pencil loop or timelapse scribble.
- Keep the teacher’s insult of the principal in the opener — it tags the world as already unserious.
- End two-shot: student proud, teacher nodding; no lesson learned.
- Optional swap: fill every checkbox on a form, every app notification toggle, every survey “strongly agree.”
