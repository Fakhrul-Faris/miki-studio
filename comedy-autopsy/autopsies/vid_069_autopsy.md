# Vid 069 — Autopsy

## Premise
POV inside a barista’s head: a cute customer asks where to throw trash, gets polite directions—then the barista’s inner monologue rates him on looks **and** not littering.

## Hook
Overlay: **POV: Isi hati barista**. Customer in striped shirt points at a serving tray: “Mas, buang sampannya di sini kan ya?” Classic service pain point—tray ≠ bin—framed as crush-cam.

## Setup
Barista (outward): “Iya, kak, di situ…” Customer thanks him. Professional smile, apron brand visible, cleaning cloth in hand. Surface = perfect service.

## Turn
Inner VO kicks in: “Wah… saya keren banget. Pasti dia orangnya baik, ganteng…” Admiration spiral while hands keep wiping glassware. Romance criteria invade the shift.

## Punchline
Criteria lands on civility: frames/subtitle—“ganteng, terus juga tadi **nggak** buang sampah sembarangan.” (ASR flips it into “kekuatannya buang sampah sembarangan” / “di sampah sembarangan”—Whisper inverted the negation.) The joke: barista’s love language is **basic not-littering**. Self-correct beat in transcript (“Maksudnya dia enggak?”) matches the garble.

## Reaction / Button
Deadpan return to work face (“Jom, ya” / back to task). No confession to the customer. Button = private crush over a trash rule.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Professional mask vs heart HUD** — smile stays; text/VO carries the gag.
- **Micro-standard as romance** — not flowers; correct bin use.
- **ASR note** — critical: transcript says he *does* litter; frames prove *doesn’t*. Remake from frame subtitle, not Whisper.
- Risk: without “isi hati” overlay, it’s just a trash FAQ.
