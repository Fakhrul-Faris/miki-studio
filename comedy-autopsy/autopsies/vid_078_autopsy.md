# Vid 078 — Autopsy

## Premise
At **Edenia Head Spa**, someone forgets they’re nearsighted (“mata minus”) and tries to do precision hair work with a face that looks like a medical emergency — pure visual comedy under one locked caption.

## Hook
Caption: **“Ketika lupa punya mata minus.”** Extreme close-up: brows locked, eyes strained / hunting focus, flat iron in frame. Premise is a physical status: myopia + professional precision task.

## Setup
Hands work fringe/sections with straightener and comb while the **face** does the comedy. Workplace competence requires sight; the body refuses. CU sells the disease before we even clock it’s a spa.

## Turn
Wide two-staff shot: black **EDENIA HEAD SPA** tunics (one mustard hijab + glasses), client in denim jacket, product shelves + arched window. Social turn — someone else can see the face / situation while tools stay live near the client’s head. Still almost no speech (ASR: only “Thank you”).

## Punchline
The face itself is the punch: exaggerated focus that reads as anger, fear, or constipation — audience maps it to “forgot glasses.” Caption already named the disease; no verbal punchline required.

## Reaction / Button
Both staff pull hair sections wide under the same caption — procedure continues as if the face were normal. Button is denial: keep working, never say “where are my glasses?”

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Visual mid-beat** — if you need the laugh explained in VO, it fails; the squint is the gag.
- **Pro vs body** — service uniform + tools vs biological limitation (cousin to tool hell / sleep stapler).
- **Brand-locked set** — Edenia logo makes it feel like real service-biz content, not generic face meme.
- ASR useless; rejuice from caption + CU→wide framing. Do not invent dialogue.
- Weak for audio-only; strong for Grease Pencil face libraries.
