# Vid 023 — Autopsy

## Premise
An introvert eats the wrong Korean dish at a restaurant rather than speak up — then the kitchen discovers the mistake and can't comprehend the silence.

## Hook
On-screen text: **"Introverts at restaurants be like:"** Blue-skinned character with bowl-cut hair hiding the eyes, olive hoodie, hunched over a half-finished plate (rice, green beans, meat) and a bill. The hook frame sells "relatable meme setup" before any dialogue.

## Setup
Cut to kitchen: orange puffy-headed staff in purple overalls slicing kimbap/gimbap at a counter ("Wait — didn't he order bulgogi over rice?"). Second beat: "I think we served him spicy pork over rice instead." The wrong order is established; the customer already ate in silence.

## Turn
Staff spiral: "Why didn't he say anything?" → "We could have just made him a new one." The comedy shifts from kitchen logistics to social incomprehension — they assume a fixable error, not a personality type.

## Punchline
Off-screen voice: "What? Do you know why?" The answer lands as deadpan dismissal: **"You wouldn't get it."** No explanation of introversion — the line refuses to translate the behavior for extrovert logic.

## Reaction / Button
Visual-only outro (no more dialogue): introvert walks away from camera in hoodie, earbuds in, warm sunset cityscape. Hook character reappears facing camera with the same blank mouth and hair-over-eyes — then empty skyline/sunset end card. Status: he left happy; the kitchen never got closure.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Show, don't tell** — wrong plate + walk-off beats a monologue about social anxiety.
- **Two-world structure** — restaurant floor vs. kitchen; audience knows both truths, characters don't share one.
- **"You wouldn't get it"** is a perfect anti-button: denies the audience an easy moral too.
- Animation + photo-textured table food reads tactile; bowl-cut-as-blindfold is a strong visual shorthand for avoidance.
- Slightly long kitchen beat before the walk-off — tension is patience, not punch density.

## Remake notes
- Steal the machine: **mistake happens → institution tries to fix → customer already chose silence → explainer refuses to explain.**
- Grease Pencil: two locations only (table + kitchen pass-through); introvert gets minimal mouth animation, maximum hair/hoodie body language.
- Kitchen staff = one expressive "concerned mom" type (orange blob energy); keep food visually distinct (bulgogi brown vs. spicy pork red).
- End on walk-off with earbuds — no smile change, just city sunset; let the button be absence of confrontation.
- Swap restaurant for pharmacy wrong prescription, wrong haircut, wrong Uber stop — same conflict-avoidance engine.
