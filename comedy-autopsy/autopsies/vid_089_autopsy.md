# Vid 089 — Autopsy

## Premise
POV: mid–online meeting, boss casually asks to **share screen**—while the employee’s browser is a nest of **JobStreet / LinkedIn job tabs** and unread WhatsApp.

## Hook
Middle-aged man (grey hair, glasses, light blue shirt) frozen at laptop. Overlay: **"POV: Ketika lagi online meeting terus bos nyeletuk 'Bisa share screen aja gak'"**. Panic face is the hook; the request is already implied.

## Setup
Spoken stall loop from transcript: **"A, bentar, bentar, wait, wait, wait, wait, wait, wait"** → **"Oke, share screen ya"** → more **"Bentar, bentar… share screen, share screen, bentar"** → **"Bentar ya, Pak"** → **"Oke, white, bentar"** (ASR **"white"** likely UI/“wait” garble). Audio = buying time; visual = the crime scene.

## Turn
Hard cut to the monitor: JobStreet open, tabs for vacancies, career advice, LinkedIn messages **(4)**, WhatsApp **(38)**. Cursor drifts toward closing tabs. The meeting isn’t the joke—**what would appear on share** is.

## Punchline
No witty spoken punch in transcript; the punchline is the **job-hunt desktop** under a share-screen order. Career-advice page (“Temukan tips karir…”) while still employed is the knife twist.

## Reaction / Button
Return to face: deer-in-headlights, hands hovering over keys—still saying variants of bentar. Button is unresolved: we never see him successfully share a clean screen.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Tool/institution hell** — Zoom/Meet share as surveillance.
- **Show the tabs** — dialogue alone (“bentar”) is weak; the browser evidence is the laugh.
- **Boss power in one line** — casual “share screen aja” vs. career-suicide UI.
- Mix of ID/EN **"wait"** is authentic WFH panic code-switch.
- ASR **"white"** unreliable—don’t script “white”; keep “wait / bentar.”
