# Vid 004 — Autopsy

## Premise
Google Maps as a backseat passenger who only speaks when the advice is useless — until it accidentally says something good, then punishes the driver for noticing.

## Hook
Car interior: annoyed driver at the wheel, passenger is a **Google Maps pin head** clutching a folded paper map (hook frame). The navigation app is literally lost while giving directions — instant personification read.

## Setup
Normal ask (“how to get to my destination?”) gets a clean **26-minute ETA**. Then Maps starts narrating non-choices: left is the same time as straight; a U-turn is **1 hour 19 minutes slower**. Driver pushes back (“Why would I do that?” / “I don’t know.”). Setup/reaction frames show Maps buried in a paper map while the driver’s face hardens.

## Turn
Maps commits to the bit: “I’m just gonna stick to giving you ETAs to alternate routes.” Right is 4 minutes slower, left 6 minutes slower — then casually mentions a **dirt road five minutes faster** mid-sentence. Driver finally latches onto the one useful word: “Wait, did you just say **faster**?”

## Punchline
Maps confirms, deadpan: “Sure will.” Driver genuinely thanks Maps — “You’ve actually made a useful suggestion” — rare sincere beat in a clip about useless GPS chatter.

## Reaction / Button
Long beat (~13 seconds of silence in transcript), then Maps: “Hmm, you know, I think we need to update our ETA now.” Driver: **“Get out.”** End frame: dashboard gauge close-up, giant Maps pin looming outside the window — the tool survives; the relationship doesn’t.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Passive-aggressive service character** — helpful tone, hostile content.
- **Buried lede** — the good route is thrown away inside a pile of bad ones; catching it feels like a player skill check.
- **Punish the win** — gratitude triggers eviction, not partnership.
- Car staging stays readable: two seats, one prop (map), one costume head.

## Remake notes
- Steal the machine: **tool floods you with noise → one good answer hidden → you celebrate → tool gets offended.**
- Swap Maps for any assistant (calendar, autocorrect, IT helpdesk) — same sitcom-of-tools cluster as 001/008/009.
- Grease Pencil: single car set; passenger head = logo/icon; ETAs as on-screen subtitle cards.
- Hold the post-thanksgiving pause — the “Get out” lands because Maps waited long enough to feel petty on purpose.
