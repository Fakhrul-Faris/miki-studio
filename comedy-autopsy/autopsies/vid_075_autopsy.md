# Vid 075 — Autopsy

## Premise
Stressed clothing-shop staff get a customer problem about a **seblak** order—food—in a store that only sells clothes, then the ask pivots to hunting a random brand name.

## Hook
Caption: “STRESS SEMUA.” Night boutique, hoodies and jeans behind the counter, handwritten BCA transfer sign. Two staff already dead-eyed (chin-on-hand, hip stance). Burnout established before the complaint lands.

## Setup
Customer (ASR): “Kak, gimana sih? Sayang kan pesanya seblako yang dateng ngala di Samp?”—garbled Indonesian about a seblak order that arrived wrong / at the wrong place. Subtitle beat on frames: “seblak.” Category collision: spicy-food logistics inside a baju shop.

## Turn
Staff cuts in: “Kak, kan kita jualannya baju.” Boundary line—wrong industry, not wrong attitude. “Aduh, lusang, lusang, lusang” (≈ “langsung”)—rushed redirect / go-ahead energy under stress. The shop refuses the food ticket; the customer must reframe the ask.

## Punchline
“Kakaknya cari apa?” → “Sparkwarnio.” After the seblak derail, the customer names what sounds like a clothing/brand hunt (ASR uncertain—treat as nonsense brand). Punch is the pivot: one stress spiral, two mismatched requests.

## Reaction / Button
Wide empty-shop / street-night end beat under the same “STRESS SEMUA” title—no resolution of stock. Stress caption outlives the dialogue; everyone stays fried.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Wrong-channel comedy** — client brings Ticket A (food delivery) to Desk B (apparel). Classic freelancer/office: bug report filed in #random.
- **Burnout frame** — “STRESS SEMUA” is the thesis; dialogue only proves it.
- **ASR note** — “seblako,” “ngala di Samp,” “Sparkwarnio” are Whisper guesses; frames confirm seblak + baju shop. Don’t invent a clean brand line—lean into the garble as “client speaks nonsense SKU.”
- Risk: without the baju line, it’s just three stressed faces.
