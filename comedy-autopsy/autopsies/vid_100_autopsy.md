# Vid 100 — Autopsy

## Premise
Night counter at **The Stone Cafe**: karaoke captions build a pointed question — **KAMU KOK → ANAK BARU → ANAK BARU KAMU DI SINI BAGIAN** — outing the uniformed worker as the confused new hire. ASR is glyph garbage; captions + face are the script.

## Hook
Fluorescent tube, stainless counter, stacks of plates, rice cooker, red-shirt coworker behind. Young staff in beige mandarin-collar uniform (Stone Cafe logo). POV of someone addressing him. First caption: **“KAMU KOK”** (“why do you…?”) — accusation tone without a full sentence yet.

## Setup
Deadpan / listening face while the kitchen keeps moving. Status: he is on trial for some micro-behavior at the counter. Busy service environment makes the “why are you like this?” land as workplace, not random roast.

## Turn
Caption stack continues — **“ANAK BARU”** — the “why” resolves into identity: he’s not being weird on purpose; he’s the new kid. Buffering / mid-speech face sells trainee brain lag.

## Punchline
Full card: **“ANAK BARU KAMU DI SINI BAGIAN”** (“you’re the new hire here in the [section]…”). Explanation-as-punchline: incompetence rebranded as onboarding. Incomplete **“bagian ___”** leaves the department hanging — or the point is the label alone.

## Reaction / Button
Hold expectant/neutral stare at camera while kitchen keeps moving. No clean spoken button — caption + newbie face is the landing.

## Why it works (or doesn't)
- **Status stamp** — one phrase demotes him from “bad at the job” to “new,” which is funnier and kinder-cruel.
- **Caption karaoke** — sentence assembles live; classic short-form reveal. Dense frames confirm ladder: KAMU KOK → ANAK BARU → full line.
- **Brand lock** — Stone Cafe uniform makes it feel like real F&B floor culture.
- ASR note: do not quote Whisper glyph salad; remake from burned-in Indonesian only.
- Risk: if “bagian” never completes and no visual newbie tell, the label feels arbitrary — keep hesitation face.
