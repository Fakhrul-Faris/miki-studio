- Dialogue / OS: (silence / soft chew SFX)

- Timing: establish the hide before any voice
- Status: Miki owns the crime; audience is the POV catcher
- Do not explain: no “I’m eating leftovers” VO

### P02 — To go?
- Beat: setup · ≈3.0s · same angle
- Action: Chew freezes mid-bite. Eyes flick up toward counter.
- Dialogue / OS: **CUSTOMER (OS):** Hey—sorry, forgot. Can you pack my food to go? / **MIKI:** …To go?
- Gag type: status (caught)
- Timing: freeze chew *before* “…To go?”
- Status: Customer unknowingly raids the crime
- Do not explain: don’t show customer face yet (OS only)

### P03 — The food is the order
- Beat: turn · ≈4.0s · MCU hands → Miki face
- Action: Look at food in hands → empty plate slot on pass. Realization: leftovers = the ticket.
- Dialogue / OS: **CUSTOMER (OS):** Yeah. The salted-egg chicken.
- Gag type: prop / status flip
- Timing: hold hands 0.6s after dish name before he speaks
- Status: Prop owns the frame
- Do not explain: no thought bubble “oh no”

### P04 — Salted-egg chicken. To go.
- Beat: punch / button · ≈5.0s · MCU Miki
- Action: Half-stand but still crouched energy. Professional mouth, food still in it. Sauce on lip.
- Dialogue / OS: **MIKI:** Salted-egg chicken. To go.

- Timing: line lands clean; then **1.0s hold** eyes wide as chew restarts against his will
- Status: Service voice wins; body loses
- Do not explain: no apology; cut before full stand

- Action: Chew resumes. Still low. Cut to black.

- Timing: cut mid-chew
