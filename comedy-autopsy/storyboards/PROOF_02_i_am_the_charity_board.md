# PROOF_02 — I Am the Charity — Storyboard
**Script:** `scripts/PROOF_02_i_am_the_charity.md`  
**Knife:** donate the change → thanks → I am the charity  
**Format:** 9:16 · ~15s · Grease Pencil · 1 set (cafe counter)  
**Status:** KEEP — board draft

## Comedy spine (do not dilute)
1. Drink + $42 change math
2. Soft shelter ask → easy yes
3. Thank-you completes the virtue
4. Button: “I’m from the shelter” → customer smile dies

---

## Panels

### P01 — Drink + math
- Beat: hook · ≈3.0s · MS counter
- Action: Miki hands green/clear drink. Register or OS numbers: **$8 · $50 · change $42** readable.
- Dialogue / OS: **MIKI:** Here’s your drink. Change is forty-two. Want that to go to the shelter?
- Gag type: text overlay + staging
- Timing: $42 must read before the ask finishes
- Status: Miki = polite cashier
- Do not explain: no NGO logo, no sad music

### P02 — Easy yes
- Beat: setup · ≈2.5s · MCU customer
- Action: Soft nod/smile. Social yes with zero friction.
- Dialogue / OS: **CUSTOMER:** Oh—yeah. Sure.
- Gag type: status (virtue purchased cheap)
- Timing: no hesitation — easy yes is the trap
- Status: Customer feels good
- Do not explain: —

### P03 — Thanks complete
- Beat: setup · ≈3.0s · two-shot
- Action: Tiny “deed done” beat. Customer half-turns away as if finished.
- Dialogue / OS: **MIKI:** Thanks. Really. / **CUSTOMER:** Of course.
- Gag type: silence / social
- Timing: 0.4s air after “Of course.” before the kill
- Status: Transaction emotionally closed
- Do not explain: don’t show Miki pocketing yet (optional later if sad)

### P04 — I’m from the shelter
- Beat: punch · ≈3.5s · MCU Miki → MCU customer
- Action: Same polite tone. Optional micro cash-into-apron if needed for clarity. Cut to customer smile dying.
- Dialogue / OS: **MIKI:** Also—I’m from the shelter.
- Gag type: status inversion
- Timing: soft delivery; **hold customer face ~0.8s** — button lives here
- Status: Miki still polite; customer owns the reaction
- Do not explain: no wink, no “just kidding,” no speech about poverty

### P05 — Smile dies / cut
- Beat: button · ≈2.0s · MCU customer
- Action: Smile dies. Drink still in hand. Cut to black.
- Dialogue / OS: (silence)
- Gag type: silence
- Timing: cut on the dead smile, not on Miki
- Status: Audience with customer
- Do not explain: —

**END.**
