## Panels

### P01 — Hook: the evidence
- Beat: hook · ≈2.0s · MCU insert · glass of water + OS
- Action: cold open on water glass in Miki’s hand (or on table). OS text snaps on.
- Dialogue / OS: **“He drank water”**
- Gag type: text overlay + prop
- Timing: hold OS long enough to read; no dialogue yet
- Status: the *prop* owns the frame (accusation before faces)
- Do not explain: don’t show energy drinks yet — water alone is the crime

### P02 — Tribunal arrives
- Beat: setup · ≈2.0s · WS · living room
- Action: Jay + Sam stand over Miki on couch (high angle bias on them). Jay’s hand up: “don’t come closer.”
- Dialogue / OS: **JAY:** Don’t come closer. He’s different.
- Gag type: staging (exorcism blocking in a normal apartment)
- Timing: cut in as Jay finishes the line
- Status: Jay owns space; Miki is smaller in frame
- Do not explain: no scary lighting — keep fluorescent / normal

### P03 — Confession of hydration
- Beat: setup · ≈2.5s · MS two-shot Jay→Miki
- Action: Miki lifts the glass a fraction, deadpan. Jay’s eyes widen like a breakthrough.
- Dialogue / OS: **MIKI:** I drank water. / **JAY:** Exactly.
- Gag type: status flip (innocent fact = damning)
- Timing: micro-hold after “Exactly.” before Jay turns to Sam
- Status: Jay takes the win immediately
- Do not explain: Miki does not joke — sincerity sells the machine

### P04 — Yesterday vs today
- Beat: escalate · ≈3.0s · MS Jay + Sam
- Action: Jay counts on fingers to Sam; Sam nods like a juror. Energy-drink cans visible in background trash (readable, not centered).
- Dialogue / OS: **JAY:** Yesterday: three energy drinks. Today: *hydration.* Name one innocent reason.
- Gag type: staging + contrast prop (cans in BG)
- Timing: stress *hydration* with voice + finger count only (no extra OS flash)
- Status: Jay + Sam aligned against Miki
- Do not explain: don’t cut to a flashback of drinking cans — BG cans are enough

### P05 — Thirst for souls
- Beat: escalate · ≈3.0s · MCU Sam → MCU Miki
- Action: Miki shrugs “thirsty?”; Sam gasps (big mouth, hand to chest); cut to Miki’s flat correction.
- Dialogue / OS: **MIKI:** I was thirsty? / **SAM:** Thirst… for souls. / **MIKI:** For water.
- Gag type: cut (gasps vs flat correction)
- Timing: hold Sam’s gasp ~0.4s before Miki’s “For water.”
- Status: Sam briefly owns hysteria; Miki steals it back with literalism
- Do not explain: no soul VFX

### P06 — Demon lore + headache
- Beat: escalate · ≈3.5s · MS three-shot
- Action: Jay lectures with priest confidence; Miki rubs temple; Sam soft-horror nod.
- Dialogue / OS: **JAY:** Demons hate caffeine. Everyone knows that. / **MIKI:** I have a headache. / **SAM:** Withdrawal from the mortal realm. / **MIKI:** From the energy drinks.
- Gag type: status (confident wrong vs pedantic true)
- Timing: Sam overlaps Miki’s headache with zero pause — diagnosis is preloaded
- Status: Sam escalates; Miki is ignored
- Do not explain: no headache cartoon stars

### P07 — Turn: calm = possessed
- Beat: turn · ≈2.5s · MCU Jay · then CU Miki
- Action: Jay leans in, suspicious of Miki’s tone. Miki answers like a tired roommate. **Jay nods once — case closed.**
- Dialogue / OS: **JAY:** Why are you speaking so calmly? / **MIKI:** Because you’re being weird.
- Gag type: status flip + silence after the nod
- Timing: **0.5s hold on Jay’s nod with no dialogue** — this is the turn beat
- Status: Jay’s nod owns the frame; logic is dead
- Do not explain: do not add “see, he’s possessed” — the nod is the line

### P08 — Pupils too normal
- Beat: escalate · ≈3.0s · CU eyes insert + MCU Jay
- Action: Phone flashlight beam in Miki’s eyes. Pupils normal. Jay recoils as if that’s worse.
- Dialogue / OS: **JAY:** Pupils normal. Too normal. / Sam, the kit.
- Gag type: prop (phone as medical tool)
- Timing: flash → beat → “Too normal.”
- Status: Jay controls the exam
- Do not explain: no demonic eye FX — normality is the gag

### P09 — The kit (dinner)
- Beat: escalate · ≈3.0s · high MS · bag dump insert
- Action: Sam upends grocery bag: garlic bread, USB cable, instant ramen packets spill onto couch/coffee table. Miki points at food.
- Dialogue / OS: **MIKI:** That’s dinner. / **JAY:** Ritual components.
- Gag type: prop comedy (mundane inventory as occult kit)
- Timing: dump lands first (~0.6s silent), then “That’s dinner.”
- Status: props take focus; dialogue labels them wrong
- Do not explain: don’t label items with on-screen callouts

### P10 — Useless restraints
- Beat: escalate · ≈2.5s · MS staging
- Action: They loop a phone charger loosely around Miki’s wrists/torso. Cable is clearly not restraining him. He could stand. He doesn’t — spite stillness. (USB cable from the kit can be the same gag prop.)
- Dialogue / OS: (silence — or ambient only)
- Gag type: staging + silence
- Timing: **full silent panel** — laugh is the pathetic cable; cut here straight from “Ritual components.”
- Status: they think they won; Miki’s non-escape is petty power
- Do not explain: no struggle; uselessness must read in silhouette

### P11 — Holy salt
- Beat: punch setup · ≈2.0s · MCU Jay hands
- Action: Jay tears open instant ramen seasoning packet like a sacrament. Sprinkles on Miki’s head (chef wrist flick).
- Dialogue / OS: **JAY:** Holy salt.
- Gag type: prop
- Timing: line then sprinkle; powder visible in hair
- Status: Jay as priest-chef
- Do not explain: packet brand/readable “seasoning” is the joke — keep it readable

### P12 — Sneeze (punch)
- Beat: punch · ≈1.5s · MCU Miki
- Action: Seasoning hits → sneeze. Eyes shut. Specks in hair.
- Dialogue / OS: **(sneeze only)**
- Gag type: prop + involuntary proof
- Timing: sneeze completes; **do not overlap Sam’s line**
- Status: Miki loses control of the frame for one beat
- Do not explain: no demon-exit smoke

### P13 — It left / pepper
- Beat: punch · ≈3.0s · MCU Sam → MCU Miki → MCU Jay
- Action: Sam triumphant point. Miki deadpan wipe. Jay soft, certain — almost gentle.
- Dialogue / OS: **SAM:** It left! / **MIKI:** That was pepper. / **JAY:** (beat) That’s what a demon would say.
- Gag type: cut + status (soft certainty > loud triumph)
- Timing: **hold ~0.6s before Jay’s line** — soft delivery is the kill shot (vid_046 caption DNA)
- Status: Jay ends with quiet authority
- Do not explain: don’t underline with scary sting; dry is funnier

### P14 — Button: safe now
- Beat: button · ≈2.5s · MS
- Action: Jay gently removes water glass. Places energy drink in Miki’s hand. Pats shoulder like a nurse.
- Dialogue / OS: **JAY:** You’re safe now.
- Gag type: prop swap (cure = relapse)
- Timing: glass leave → can plant → pat → line
- Status: caretakers win the worldview
- Do not explain: no celebratory music sting needed; sincerity is the joke

### P15 — I preferred the demon
- Beat: button · ≈2.5s · MCU Miki to camera
- Action: Miki looks into lens; seasoning still in hair; energy drink in hand; flat confession.
- Dialogue / OS: **MIKI:** I preferred the demon.
- Gag type: status (audience alliance) + button
- Timing: hold face ~0.8s after line for end card / cut to black
- Status: Miki finally owns the frame — too late
- Do not explain: no wink; play straight

**END.**
